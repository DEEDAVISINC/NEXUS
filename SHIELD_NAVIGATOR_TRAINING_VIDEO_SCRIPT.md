# SHIELD Navigator Training Video — Production Script

**Type:** Screen recording with voiceover narration
**Audience:** CWC Community Health Worker Navigators (new hires + refresher)
**Companion:** SHIELD_NAVIGATOR_TRAINING_GUIDE.md (written reference)
**Estimated Runtime:** 25–30 minutes (can be split into chapters for on-demand replay)
**Recording Tool:** OBS, Loom, or QuickTime screen recording + separate mic audio

---

## Recording Setup Notes

- **Screen:** Use the SHIELD system at full screen, 1920x1080 resolution
- **Browser:** Chrome, dark mode off, bookmarks bar hidden, no other tabs visible
- **Data:** Seed the demo database first — use the 20 families across 4 counties
- **Login as:** Angela Johnson (angela.johnson@cwcare.org) — Wayne County navigator
- **Mouse:** Move slowly and deliberately. Pause on each element you're describing.
- **Narration:** Conversational but professional. You're a colleague walking a new hire through their first day, not reading a manual.
- **Pauses:** Build in 1-2 second pauses before transitioning between sections so chapters can be cut cleanly.

## CHAPTER 10: Time Tracking (3 min)

**[ACTION: Click "Time Log" in the sidebar]**

**[SCREEN: Time Log section]**

> *"Here's how your time gets tracked — and this is important because your time is billable."*

### 10A: Automatic Tracking

> *"The system automatically records time for most things you do in SHIELD. Open a case? That's logged as case review time. Make a call? The duration is logged. Send a message? Logged. You don't have to do anything for this — it happens in the background."*

**[ACTION: Point to the auto-recorded entries in the activity list]**

> *"See these entries? Each one has a status badge. LOGGED means it's recorded and waiting for your supervisor to review. VERIFIED means the supervisor confirmed it. BILLED means it went to billing. FLAGGED means your supervisor has a question."*

### 10B: Field Work Timer

**[ACTION: Point to the Field Work Timer section]**

> *"For work outside the system — home visits, in-person appointments, community outreach — you use the manual timer."*

**[ACTION: Select an activity type from the dropdown]**

> *"Pick the activity type. CHW Home Visit, NEMT Trip, Benefits Enrollment — each one maps to a billing code automatically."*

**[ACTION: Click Start Timer]**

> *"Start the timer when you begin the activity."*

**[ACTION: Let it run for a few seconds, then click Stop]**

> *"Stop it when you're done. Add a note about what you did. The system records the start time, stop time, duration, CPT code, and billing type."*

> *"Bottom line: your time has value. The system captures it so DDI can bill for it. But your supervisor has to verify it before it goes to billing. So be accurate — if you forget to stop a timer, fix it and let your supervisor know."*

## CHAPTER 11: Service Verification (3 min)

**[SCREEN: Back to a Family Detail View with active services]**

> *"This is the most important part to understand. No service gets billed until it's verified. Let me show you how that works."*

**[ACTION: Point to a service activation with the verification progress bar]**

> *"Each service has verification steps. The progress bar shows how many are complete. Let's say this is a Lead Remediation. The steps might be:"*
>
> *"One — Navigator confirms the contractor was dispatched. That's you."*
>
> *"Two — Contractor confirms completion. The system sends them a text. They reply DONE."*
>
> *"Three — Family confirms they received the service. The system texts them. They reply YES."*

**[ACTION: Point to the Complete button for a navigator-owned step]**

> *"For steps you own, click Complete. Only do this if you've actually confirmed it happened."*

**[ACTION: Point to the Send SMS button for a contractor/family step]**

> *"For steps that need contractor or family confirmation, click Send SMS. The system sends the verification text automatically."*

> *"When all steps are green, the service moves to Verified Complete. A billing record is created automatically. Your supervisor reviews it before payment goes out."*

### What If There's a Problem?

> *"If the contractor says they're done but the family says no — the system flags it. Do not override. Do not verify it manually. Escalate to your supervisor."*
>
> *"If the family doesn't respond to the verification text, call them. Document what they say. If they confirm by phone, you can complete the family verification step."*
>
> *"If you're not sure a service actually happened — do not verify it. It's better to delay billing than to verify something that didn't happen. That's fraud."*

## CHAPTER 12: Escalations (1.5 min)

**[SCREEN: Case with a red SLA bar]**

> *"When a case goes past its SLA deadline, the bar turns red and your supervisor gets an automatic alert. Try not to let this happen, but when it does, document why."*

> *"Outside of SLA, here's when you escalate to your supervisor immediately:"*
>
> *"A child has a blood lead level at or above 15 and no services are active."*
>
> *"A family is being displaced and has nowhere to go."*
>
> *"A contractor doesn't show up."*
>
> *"A family reports a new emergency."*
>
> *"You can't reach a family after three attempts."*
>
> *"You suspect abuse or neglect — and remember, you are a mandatory reporter under Michigan law."*

## CHAPTER 13: Wrap-Up & Daily Routine (1.5 min)

**[SCREEN: Navigator Workspace — My Caseload view]**

> *"Let me leave you with what a typical day looks like."*
>
> *"Start your shift. Set your status to Online. Check your Tasks — handle anything overdue first. Check your Calendar — plan your route for any field visits today."*
>
> *"Open your cases, make your calls, send your follow-up texts. Log everything. If you're heading into the field, start your timer before you leave."*
>
> *"When you get back, stop the timer, update your notes, check for any new referrals. Follow up on any pending verifications."*
>
> *"End of day — set your status to Off Duty."*
>
> *"The system is here to make your job easier, not harder. It tracks things so you don't have to remember them. It automates billing so you can focus on families. But it only works if you use it. Log your calls. Log your time. Verify honestly."*
>
> *"You've got the written training guide as a reference. If you get stuck on anything, talk to your supervisor."*
>
> *"Welcome to the team. Every family deserves a SHIELD — and you're the one holding it."*
