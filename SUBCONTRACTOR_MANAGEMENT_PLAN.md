## 1. COMMUNICATION PLAN

### Communication Cadence

| Frequency | Activity | Method | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Status update | Email (written record) | Sub → DDI |
| Bi-weekly | Performance review call | Phone/video | DDI + Sub |
| Monthly | Formal performance report | Written report | DDI compiles |
| As needed | Issue/incident reporting | Email + phone | Sub → DDI immediately |
| As needed | Contract changes/mods | Email (written) | DDI → Sub |
| Quarterly | Performance evaluation | Meeting + written | DDI |

### Communication Rules

1. **Sub contacts DDI only.** Never the CO, never the agency, never the facility POC.
2. **Issues reported within 4 hours.** Don't wait for the weekly update.
3. **All decisions in writing.** Phone calls followed by confirming email.
4. **DDI handles ALL government reporting.** Monthly reports, invoicing, performance docs.
5. **Sub provides raw data/documentation.** DDI formats for the government.

## 4. PERFORMANCE METRICS

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Target | Measurement Method | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-time task completion | 95%+ | Tasks completed by scheduled date | Weekly |
| Quality (zero rework) | <2% rework rate | Inspection results, CO feedback | Monthly |
| Communication responsiveness | Same business day | Time from DDI request to sub response | Ongoing |
| Safety incidents | Zero | Incident reports | Ongoing |
| Report timeliness | 100% on time | Weekly/monthly reports submitted by deadline | Weekly |
| Staff availability | 98%+ | Scheduled shifts covered | Weekly |
| Insurance compliance | 100% current | COI expiration tracking | Quarterly |

### Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Description | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | Exceeds all targets, proactive | Continue — reference in DDI proposals |
| Satisfactory | Meets targets consistently | Continue — minor improvements noted |
| Marginal | Misses 1-2 targets regularly | Performance Improvement Plan — 30 days |
| Unsatisfactory | Fails multiple targets | Termination consideration — immediate review |

### Performance Review Schedule

- **30 days:** Initial performance check — is the sub meeting expectations?
- **90 days:** First formal review — documented, shared with sub
- **6 months:** Mid-contract review — continue, adjust, or replace decision
- **Annually:** Full evaluation — feeds into option year decision
- **Contract end:** Final evaluation — documented for future reference and proposals
