## ✅ VERIFIED: OPOs Exist and Use Courier Services

**Confirmed:** There are 55 federally designated Organ Procurement Organizations (OPOs) in the United States (Source: OPTN/UNOS)

**Confirmed:** OPOs contract for transportation services including:
- Organ transport (hearts, livers, kidneys, lungs)
- Tissue transport
- Medical team transport
- Lab specimen transport
- 24/7/365 emergency deliveries
