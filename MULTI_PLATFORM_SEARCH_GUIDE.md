## 📊 WHERE TO SEARCH (BY PRIORITY)

### **🥇 TIER 1: Michigan Local (HIGHEST PRIORITY)**

**Why:** Home state, less competition, faster payment, local advantage

#### **BidNet Direct / MITN (Michigan Inter-governmental Trade Network)**
- **URL:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn
- **Coverage:** 200+ Michigan agencies (counties, cities, schools, utilities)
- **Search:** Filter by "Michigan" → "Open Solicitations"
- **Best For:** County bids (Oakland, Wayne, Macomb), city bids, school districts
- **Time:** 5-10 minutes daily
- **Expected Results:** 5-10 new opportunities daily

**Key Michigan Agencies on BidNet:**
- Oakland County: https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/oakland-county
- Wayne County: https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/county-of-wayne
- Macomb County: https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/macomb-county
- **City of Warren:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-warren ⭐ **ADD NOW**
- **City of Sterling Heights:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-sterling-heights
- **City of Dearborn:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-dearborn
- **City of Livonia:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-livonia
- **City of Troy:** https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-troy
- City of Detroit: https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/city-of-detroit
- RCOC (Road Commission): https://www.bidnetdirect.com/mitn/road-commission-for-oakland-county

#### **Michigan SIGMA VMS (State of Michigan)**
- **URL:** https://www.michigan.gov/budget/budget-offices/sigma/doing-business-with-the-state
- **Coverage:** State of Michigan agencies, departments, universities
- **Search:** SIGMA Vendor Self-Service (VSS) portal
- **Best For:** State-level contracts, universities, statewide opportunities
- **Time:** 3-5 minutes daily
- **Expected Results:** 2-5 new opportunities weekly

#### **Michigan Bid Network**
- **URL:** https://www.michiganbids.net/
- **Coverage:** Statewide aggregator
- **Search:** Browse by county or keyword
- **Best For:** Smaller municipalities not on BidNet
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes daily
- **Expected Results:** 3-5 new opportunities daily

### **🥈 TIER 2: Federal EDWOSB/WOSB (MEDIUM PRIORITY)**

**Why:** Set-asides for women-owned businesses, higher value, more competition

#### **SAM.gov (System for Award Management)**
- **URL:** https://sam.gov/search/?index=opp&page=1&sort=-modifiedDate&sfm%5Bstatus%5D%5Bis_active%5D=true
- **Coverage:** All federal agencies
- **Search:**
  - Filter: Set-Aside → "EDWOSB" + "WOSB"
  - Posted Date: "Last 24 hours" or "Last 7 days"
  - Status: "Active"
- **Best For:** Federal contracts $25K-$500K
- **Time:** 5-7 minutes daily
- **Expected Results:** 1-3 new EDWOSB/WOSB opportunities daily

**Key Federal Agencies to Monitor:**
- Defense Logistics Agency (DLA)
- Army Corps of Engineers (USACE)
- Veterans Affairs (VA)
- Navy (NAVSUP)
- Air Force

#### **GSA eBuy**
- **URL:** https://www.ebuy.gsa.gov/ebuy/
- **Coverage:** GSA Schedule contract opportunities
- **Search:** Browse RFQs posted by federal agencies
- **Best For:** If you already have GSA Schedule
- **Time:** 3-5 minutes daily
- **Expected Results:** 2-5 opportunities daily (if GSA registered)

#### **VA Vendor Portal**
- **URL:** https://www.vendorportal.ecms.va.gov/eVP/fco/FCO.aspx
- **Coverage:** Veterans Affairs medical centers, facilities
- **Search:** Forecast of Contract Opportunities
- **Best For:** Medical supplies, equipment, services
- **Time:** 5 minutes weekly
- **Expected Results:** 3-5 VA opportunities monthly

## 🎯 TARGET CRITERIA

**Ideal Opportunity:**
- ✅ Product-based (not service)
- ✅ Deadline: 7-30 days out
- ✅ Value: $10K-$500K
- ✅ Location: Michigan or nearby states
- ✅ Supplier available (Grainger, Zoro, specialist)
- ✅ Municipal/government (good payment terms)
- ✅ Similar to past wins (leverage experience)

**Quick NO-GO:**
- ❌ Less than 5 days to deadline
- ❌ Requires bond (unless value justifies it)
- ❌ Requires specialized certification you don't have
- ❌ Service-based
- ❌ Construction project
