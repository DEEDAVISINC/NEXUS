We are requesting quotes for portable power distribution cables and accessories for a municipal client in Michigan. This equipment will be used for stage and event power distribution at a government venue. All items must meet professional theatrical/stage power standards and be suitable for outdoor event use.

SCOPE OF WORK

Delivery will be required to Metro Detroit area within 30 days of order placement. Supplier must be able to provide all items from a single source or coordinate with approved subcontractors.

Key Requirements:

  • Socapex cables must use genuine SOCO-type 19-pin connectors

  • Camlock connectors must be rated for 350-400 amp service
  • Cat6 cables must use Neutrik Ethercon connectors

  • All items must be suitable for outdoor/weather-resistant use
  • Products must be from established manufacturers with warranty support
