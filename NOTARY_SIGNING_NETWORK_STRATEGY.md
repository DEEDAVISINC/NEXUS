# DDI NOTARY — SIGNING SERVICE NETWORK STRATEGY
**Last Updated:** February 15, 2026

---

## THE OPPORTUNITY

UPS Stores, FedEx Office locations, title companies, escrow offices, and signing service companies **do not employ their own notaries for mobile/field work.** They subcontract to independent notary signing agents. This is a massive, nationwide subcontracting market that DDI can plug into immediately.

**The model:** Signing service companies receive loan closing orders from title companies, lenders, and escrow offices. They dispatch independent notary signing agents (subcontractors) to meet borrowers at their location for document signing. The signing service company gets paid by the title company, the notary gets paid by the signing service.
