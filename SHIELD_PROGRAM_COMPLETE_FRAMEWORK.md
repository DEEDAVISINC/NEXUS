# SHIELD PROGRAM — COMPLETE FRAMEWORK

**Dee Davis Inc. + Cause We Care | June 2026**

---

## PROGRAM DEFINITION

SHIELD is not a lead screening program. SHIELD is a **continuity of care guarantee** anchored to Michigan's universal childhood lead testing mandate (MCL 333.5474d / PA 146 of 2023). Every child under four in Michigan is required to be screened. SHIELD ensures that screening happens — and that every barrier preventing it is removed in real time.

DDI operates as the TPA. CWC provides community trust and CHW workforce. NEXUS tracks the entire chain from referral to clearance.

---

## THE SHIELD DISPATCH MODEL

SHIELD operates exactly like DEPOINTE (NEMT) and 3D Ink (notary network) — DDI controls the network, dispatches the work, and bills the payer. DDI does not own equipment or employ phlebotomists directly. DDI manages the screener network.

**How it works:**

1. Screening referral comes in (from MDHHS, LHD, MCO, MiBridges, pediatrician, school, or community partner)
2. DDI intake coordinator assesses barriers (transport, insurance, language, food, time)
3. DDI dispatches certified mobile screener through NEXUS portal to family's home, daycare, church, school, or community event site
4. Screener conducts fingerstick capillary BLL test using LeadCare II or sends venous sample to Quest/CRL via chain of custody
5. Results returned within 3 minutes (point-of-care) or 24–48 hours (lab-based)
6. DDI logs result in NEXUS, updates MCIR (Michigan Care Improvement Registry), and routes case
7. Non-elevated: education, one-time follow-up, case closed
8. Elevated: SHIELD cascade activates immediately

**Partner structure:**
- Quest Diagnostics or CRL: wholesale lab processing partner (pending confirmed wholesale agreement)
- Michigan mobile phlebotomists: credentialed subcontractor network (2–3 quotes needed — Michigan sourced)
- LHD and MDHHS: referral channel partners
- MCOs: billing partners (Medicaid FFS + MCO vendor contract)

## SHIELD SERVICE CASCADE — CAPACITY BY SERVICE LINE

### Service Line 1: Lead Test Scheduling and Dispatch (TPA Function)

What it is: Pure administrative volume. DDI receives referrals, dispatches screeners, and manages the scheduling portal — exactly like DEPOINTE for NEMT.

Capacity: High. One scheduling coordinator manages hundreds of referrals per month. Technology and workflow problem, not a staffing problem.

Pilot target: 1,500–2,000 screening referrals dispatched in Wayne County Year 1 at 50% capture of mandatory test population.

Staff required: 1 scheduling coordinator + SHIELD/NEXUS platform. Not a CHW function.

Billing: DDI charges per-test coordination fee + mobile event fees (see rate card above). Separate from Medicaid lab reimbursement.

---

### Service Line 2: Barrier Assessment and Removal

What it is: Every family referred for screening receives an immediate barrier assessment. DDI removes whatever is preventing the child from being screened.

Barriers and DDI solutions:

- No transport → DEPOINTE schedules NEMT to screening site OR DDI dispatches mobile screener to family
- Can't afford copay → CWC coordinates Medicaid enrollment or sliding scale
- Needs food before appointment → CWC connects to SNAP, food pantry, or emergency food assistance
- Uninsured or Medicaid gap → CHW corrects enrollment in MiBridges real-time
- Language barrier → DDI provides multilingual CHW or interpreter
- Parent works during hours → DDI dispatches evening or weekend screener
- Child has no pediatrician → CWC connects to FQHC or health department

Capacity: Built into every referral intake. Not a separate staffing function — CHW conducts barrier assessment at first contact.

Pilot target: 95%+ of referred families complete screening within 14 days of referral regardless of initial barriers.

Billing: CHW care coordination codes 98960/98961/98962 through CHAMPS 6309049. Bundled into per-child coordination fee for MCO billing.

---

### Service Line 3: Elevated BLL Case Intake and CHW Assignment

What it is: Activates only when a child tests elevated (BLL 3.5+ micrograms/dL). CHW assigned within 48 hours.

At 4.1% elevation rate: 1,500 tests dispatched → approximately 62 elevated cases. 2,000 tests dispatched → approximately 82 elevated cases.

Capacity: Each CHW carries 15–25 active elevated families simultaneously. 5 CHWs = 75–125 active elevated cases.

Pilot target: 62–82 elevated cases Year 1 depending on referral volume.

Staff required: CHWs — funded through MHEF $300K Capacity Building grant.

Billing: Medicaid CHW codes 98960, 98961, 98962 through CHAMPS 6309049. DDI bills MCOs and Medicaid FFS directly — not through MDHHS.

---

### Service Line 4: Home Visit and Family Navigation

What it is: In-home CHW visit for elevated cases. Not every elevated case requires a home visit — some managed remotely through MiBridges and phone navigation. CHW assessment determines visit need.

Capacity: Each CHW conducts 3–5 home visits per week. 5 CHWs = 15–25 home visits per week across Wayne County.

MDHHS rates:
- CHW home visit: $110.00
- Nurse home visit: $221.74
- DDI admin on subcontracted services: 22.5%

Pilot target: 60–70% of elevated cases require at least one home visit. At 62 elevated cases: approximately 37–43 home visits in Year 1 pilot.

Staff required: CHWs with vehicles, lead-safe kits, MiBridges access, and multilingual capacity.

Billing: CHW codes + NEMT co-pays if transport needed to accompany family.

---

### Service Line 5: CLPPP Referral and Compliance Reporting

What it is: Every elevated BLL case reported to CLPPP (Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention Program) within 48 hours. DDI coordinates referral, tracks case status, and follows up with LHD.

Capacity: Administrative function. Navigator supervisor manages CLPPP reporting for full elevated case load.

Pilot target: 100% of elevated cases reported to CLPPP within 48 hours. Non-negotiable compliance requirement.

Staff required: Navigator supervisor — already budgeted in MHEF grant.

Billing: Included in CHW case management billing — not a separate billable line.

---

### Service Line 6: Abatement Coordination (TPA Function)

What it is: DDI does not perform abatement. DDI coordinates referrals to certified abatement contractors, tracks completion, and documents for MCO and CLPPP compliance.

Capacity: High — coordination only. One coordinator manages referrals for dozens of abatement cases simultaneously.

DDI charges:
- Lead paint inspection coordination: $400–$600 per unit
- Water sampling coordination: $50–$100 per sample
- Abatement vendor management fee: DDI admin rate of 22.5% on subcontracted services

Pilot target: 100% of environmental-source elevated cases receive abatement referral within 5 business days of elevated BLL confirmation.

Staff required: Navigator supervisor in pilot phase. Dedicated abatement coordinator at scale.

Billing: DDI charges MCO a coordination and vendor management fee separate from CHW billing.

---

### Service Line 7: Displacement and HAVEN Activation

What it is: When abatement requires family to vacate the home, HAVEN activates immediately. DDI owns the displacement continuity the same way it owns the screening continuity.

HAVEN services activated:
- Emergency housing coordination (hotel, extended stay, temporary rental)
- Continued transport to school/daycare for children during displacement
- Continued access to medications and DME
- Food assistance coordination through CWC
- Home health reassignment if needed
- Displacement duration tracking and extension management in NEXUS

Capacity: HAVEN displacement coordination runs parallel to SHIELD case management. Same NEXUS system, same CHW, same TPA.

Billing: HAVEN coordination fees (hybrid PMCD model) in addition to SHIELD case management billing. Two separate revenue streams from one family event.

---

### Service Line 8: Sibling Cascade

What it is: When one child tests elevated, DDI automatically identifies and screens all siblings under four in the same household.

Capacity: Built into the elevated case intake workflow. No additional staffing required — scheduling coordinator dispatches sibling screening as part of the original elevated case.

Pilot target: 80%+ of siblings in elevated-case households screened within 30 days of index case identification.

Billing: Per-child screening coordination fee for each sibling screened.

---

### Service Line 9: Post-Abatement Follow-Up and Clearance

What it is: After abatement completion, DDI coordinates repeat BLL screening and clearance testing.

Services:
- Repeat BLL screening at 4 weeks post-abatement
- Home clearance inspection coordination (lead dust wipe test)
- Family education on lead prevention going forward
- Case closure documentation for CLPPP and MCO compliance

Pilot target: 95%+ of elevated cases receive clearance screening. 95%+ achieve BLL decline of at least 2 micrograms/dL within 8 weeks of abatement.

Billing: Repeat screening coordination fee + transport if needed + CHW case management closure billing.
