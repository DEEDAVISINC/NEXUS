## 🏛️ DETROIT'S DIVERSITY PROGRAMS:

### **1. Detroit Workforce Economic Development (WED)** 🔥 **MAJOR PROGRAM**

**What it is:**
- City of Detroit program for local/diverse businesses
- Executive Order 2016-1 (Mayor Duggan)
- Requires city contractors to hire Detroit-based businesses

**Goals:**
- **51% Detroit-based subcontracting** (for construction)
- **25% MBE/WBE participation** (Minority/Woman-Owned)
- Preference for Detroit-based suppliers
- Preference for woman-owned businesses

**Your Advantage:**
- ✅ Michigan-based (meets local requirement)
- ✅ Woman-owned (meets WBE goal!)
- ✅ Can count toward contractor diversity goals
- ✅ Priority in bid evaluation

---

### **2. City of Detroit Local Business Enterprise (LBE)**

**What it is:**
- Certification for Detroit-based businesses
- Preference in city contracts
- Set-aside opportunities

**Eligibility:**
- Business located in Detroit OR
- Owner resides in Detroit OR
- Significant Detroit operations

**Your Status:**
- Michigan-based (close to Detroit)
- Can register even if not Detroit-based
- Still get priority as woman-owned

**Action:** Register at detroitmi.gov/wed

---

### **3. Michigan Unified Certification Program (MUCP)**

**What it is:**
- Statewide MBE/WBE certification
- Recognized by all Michigan agencies
- Includes Detroit

**Your Status:**
- EDWOSB (federal) = similar to WBE
- May qualify for MUCP WBE certification
- Would strengthen Detroit bids

**Portal:** https://www.michigan.gov/mdot/business/certifications
