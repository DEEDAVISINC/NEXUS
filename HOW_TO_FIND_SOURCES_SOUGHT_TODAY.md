# 🔍 HOW TO FIND SOURCES SOUGHT OPPORTUNITIES TODAY
**Manual SAM.gov Search Guide**

**Date:** February 3, 2026  
**Problem:** Web search isn't finding current sources sought  
**Solution:** Manual search directly on SAM.gov

---

## 🎯 STEP-BY-STEP SEARCH PROCESS:

### **STEP 1: Go to SAM.gov**
```
https://sam.gov/
```

### **STEP 2: Click "Contract Opportunities"**
- Top menu bar
- OR direct link: https://sam.gov/search/

### **STEP 3: Apply These Filters**

**Notice Type:**
- ☑ Sources Sought (Type: r)
- ☑ Presolicitation (Type: p)
- ☑ Special Notice (Type: s)

**Set-Aside:**
- ☑ Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Program Set-Aside
- ☑ Economically Disadvantaged WOSB Program Set-Aside

**Status:**
- ☑ Active Only

**Posted Date:**
- Select: Last 30 days
- OR Custom: 01/01/2026 to today

**Sort By:**
- Most Recent

### **STEP 4: Review Results**

Look for notices that say:
- "Sources Sought"
- "Request for Information (RFI)"
- "Market Research"
- "Capabilities Statement Request"
- "Pre-Solicitation"

**Good indicators:**
- ✅ "WOSB" or "EDWOSB" in title
- ✅ Response deadline 7-30 days away
- ✅ Federal agency (DoD, DLA, VA, GSA, etc.)
- ✅ Products/services you can provide

## 📋 WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU FIND ONE:

### **Immediate Actions (5 min):**

1. **Click on the opportunity**
2. **Check these details:**
   - Response deadline
   - Agency/Contracting office
   - Product/service description
   - Set-aside type (WOSB? EDWOSB?)
   - Estimated value (if listed)

3. **Save the key information:**
   - Solicitation number
   - Agency name
   - Contracting officer name/email
   - Response deadline
   - SAM.gov URL

### **Add to NEXUS (2 min):**

1. Open Airtable
2. Go to OFFICER OUTREACH TRACKING table
3. Create new record:
   ```
   OPPORTUNITY TITLE: [From SAM.gov]
   SOLICITATION NUMBER: [Number]
   AGENCY: [Agency name]
   OFFICER NAME: [If listed]
   OFFICER EMAIL: [If listed]
   STATUS: DRAFT
   PRIORITY: HIGH
   DEADLINE: [Response due date]
   TAGS: WOSB, Sources Sought, Federal
   NOTES: [SAM.gov URL + key details]
   ```

### **Generate Capability Statement (10 min):**

**Option 1: NEXUS Frontend**
```
1. Open http://localhost:3000
2. Document Generator → Capability Statements
3. Fill in opportunity details
4. Generate PDF
```

**Option 2: Template**
```
1. Open DEE_DAVIS_CAPABILITY_STATEMENT.md
2. Customize for this opportunity
3. Save as PDF
```

### **Send Response (15 min):**

Use template from `SOURCES_SOUGHT_RESPONSE_SYSTEM.md`

## 🎯 ALTERNATIVE SEARCH STRATEGIES:

### **Strategy 1: Search by Agency**

**High-opportunity agencies:**
- Defense Logistics Agency (DLA)
- Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)
- General Services Administration (GSA)
- Army, Navy, Air Force
- Department of Energy

**On SAM.gov:**
- Filter by Department/Agency
- Select one of the above
- Look for sources sought

### **Strategy 2: Search by Product Category**

**SAM.gov Product Service Codes (PSC):**

**Supplies:**
- 58 - Communications, Detection, Radar
- 59 - Electrical and Electronic Equipment
- 61 - Electric Wire and Power Equipment
- 65 - Medical Equipment
- 66 - Instruments and Lab Equipment
- 79 - Cleaning Equipment and Supplies
- 81 - Containers, Packaging

**Services:**
- R - Professional Services
- S - Utilities and Housekeeping
- T - Photo/Mapping/Printing
- U - Education and Training
- V - Transportation
- W - Lease/Rental

**On SAM.gov:**
- Advanced Filters → Product Service Code
- Enter 2-digit code
- Look for WOSB set-asides

### **Strategy 3: Set Up Email Alerts**

**On SAM.gov:**
1. Create free account (if you haven't)
2. Go to "Saved Searches"
3. Create saved search with filters:
   - Set-Aside: WOSB/EDWOSB
   - Notice Type: Sources Sought
   - Status: Active
4. Turn on email notifications
5. Get alerts when new ones post!

## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING:

**Q: I searched and found nothing?**
- Normal! Not every search will have results
- Try different filters
- Try different agencies
- Try broader product categories
- Check back in 2-3 days

**Q: All the results are expired?**
- Change Posted Date filter to "Last 7 days"
- Sources sought have short windows
- Check more frequently

**Q: Can't find contracting officer email?**
- Look in the full opportunity document
- Check "Point of Contact" section
- May be in attachments
- Call the contracting office phone number

**Q: Opportunity is too technical/specialized?**
- Respond anyway if it's WOSB set-aside
- Focus on your sourcing capability
- Mention partnerships with manufacturers
- Get on the vendor list!
