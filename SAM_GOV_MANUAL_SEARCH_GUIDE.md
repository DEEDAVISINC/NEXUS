## 📋 STEP-BY-STEP MANUAL SEARCH

### **STEP 1: Go to SAM.gov Advanced Search**

**URL:** https://sam.gov/content/opportunities

**Click:** "Advanced Search" button

---

### **STEP 2: Set These Exact Filters**

**1. Set-Aside Type (MOST IMPORTANT):**
- ✅ Check ONLY these two:
  - **EDWOSB** (Economically Disadvantaged Women-Owned Small Business)
  - **WOSB** (Women-Owned Small Business)
- ❌ Uncheck everything else:
  - SDVOSB (Service-Disabled Veterans - NOT eligible)
  - HUBZone (NOT eligible)
  - 8(a) (NOT eligible)
  - SBA (too broad)

**2. Notice Type:**
- ✅ "Solicitation" (active bids you can submit to NOW)
- ✅ "Pre-Solicitation" (coming soon)
- ✅ "Intent to Bundle" (planning stage)
- ✅ "Sources Sought" (capability statements)
- ❌ "Award" (already awarded - too late)
- ❌ "Justification" (sole source - usually not for you)

**3. Posted Date:**
- Select: "Last 7 days" (to see newest opportunities)
- OR: "Last 30 days" (for broader search)

**4. Response Deadline:**
- From: Today (Feb 7, 2026)
- To: March 7, 2026 (30 days out)
- **This filters out expired opportunities!**

**5. Active Status:**
- ✅ "Active" only

---

### **STEP 3: Add Product Keywords (Optional)**

**Search in Title/Description:**

Try these one at a time:
- "supplies"
- "equipment"
- "materials"
- "products"
- "parts"
- "hardware"
- "tools"

**Why:** This filters for product opportunities vs. services

---

### **STEP 4: Review Results**

**For each opportunity found:**

**Quick Screen:**
1. ✅ Is set-aside EDWOSB or WOSB? (check badge)
2. ✅ Is it a product? (check title/description)
3. ✅ Is deadline reasonable? (at least 7 days)
4. ✅ Can you source it? (think: Grainger, Zoro, distributors)

**Click to View Full Details:**
- Read full description
- Check contract value
- Review requirements
- Download attachments if promising

---

### **STEP 5: Evaluate Each Opportunity**

**ASK YOURSELF:**

**Can I Source This?**
- ✅ Standard industrial supplies (Grainger, Zoro, Uline)
- ✅ Medical equipment (McKesson, MOPEC, Henry Schein)
- ✅ Specialty items (find manufacturers on ThomasNet)
- ❌ Custom fabrication (unless can subcontract)
- ❌ Requires specialized certifications

**Is Timeline Workable?**
- ✅ 10+ days: Ideal
- ⚠️ 7-10 days: Tight but doable
- ❌ Under 7 days: Too risky

**Is Contract Size Right?**
- ✅ $10K-$100K: Sweet spot
- ✅ $100K-$500K: Doable if can source
- ⚠️ $500K-$1M: Need strong supplier relationships
- ❌ Over $1M: Too large for now

**Can I Win?**
- ✅ Intent to Solicit: 70-80% win rate
- ✅ Sources Sought: 60-70% win rate
- ✅ Solicitation: 30-50% win rate (depends on competition)
