### 2. RIAC — Paper Card Digitization Service

**Who Needs It:** Any agency with a paper card backlog (police departments, state agencies, federal agencies transitioning to digital)
**How DDI Offers It:** License RIAC from Lakota, buy Fujitsu fi-7600 scanner, offer bulk conversion service
**Revenue:** $5-$20 per card at volume. An agency with 10,000 cards = $50K-$200K contract.
**Why Now:** Federal push to digitize all biometric records. Agencies are drowning in paper.

### 3. Multimodal Biometric Enrollment Station
**What:** Turnkey workstation that captures fingerprints + face + iris + palm + signature
**Who Needs It:** Corrections facilities, military bases, border control, large employers, government agencies
**How DDI Offers It:** Kojak (fingerprints) + Whorl Pro (enrollment software) + camera (face) + iris scanner = complete station
**Revenue:** $5K-$15K per station setup + monthly service contract
**Why Now:** Government moving to multimodal biometrics (not just fingerprints). DoD ABIS, DHS HART, FBI NGI all support multimodal.

### 4. Michigan State Livescan Certification
**What:** Lakota becomes state-certified for Michigan livescan submissions (currently only WV)

**Revenue:** Opens the entire Michigan law enforcement livescan market to DDI + Lakota
**Why Now:** Lakota publicly says they want to pursue more state certifications but need local interest. DDI IS that local interest.

### 5. Managed EFTCreator Service for FFLs
**What:** DDI manages EFTCreator accounts for gun stores — DDI handles the tech, FFL focuses on selling guns
**Who Needs It:** Small/medium FFLs who don't want to deal with EFT file creation themselves
**How DDI Offers It:** DDI sets up the account, provides the scanner, handles conversions on behalf of the FFL
**Revenue:** $50-$100/month managed service + per-conversion fee
**Why Now:** NFA applications surging. Tax stamp eliminated in 2026. More suppressors = more EFT files needed.
