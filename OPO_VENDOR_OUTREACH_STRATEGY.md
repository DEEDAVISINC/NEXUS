## KEY FINDINGS FROM RESEARCH

### OPO Transportation Models

**Model 1: NORA Contract (Most Common)**
- OPO contracts with NORA (Nationwide Organ Recovery Transport Alliance)
- NORA operates in 31 states
- NORA may subcontract to regional couriers for backup/overflow
- **DDI Opportunity:** Become NORA subcontractor OR approach OPOs for backup vendor status

**Model 2: In-House Transportation**
- OPO maintains own fleet and drivers (e.g., Gift of Hope Chicago since 2001)
- **DDI Opportunity:** Backup/overflow vendor for after-hours or surge capacity

**Model 3: Approved Vendor List**
- OPO maintains list of approved courier vendors
- Called on as-needed basis for STAT deliveries
- **DDI Opportunity:** Get on approved vendor list, respond to calls

### Why Vendor Registration Isn't Public

- OPOs don't post procurement on websites (not government RFPs)
- Vendor onboarding happens through:
  1. Direct outreach to operations/logistics department
  2. Referrals from other healthcare partners
  3. RFP/RFQ when replacing incumbent vendor
  4. Emergency situations requiring backup coverage
