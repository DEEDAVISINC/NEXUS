## SCORING METHODOLOGY

Every target agency is scored on 5 criteria (100 points total):

| Criterion | Points | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Service Alignment** | 0-25 | Does this agency actively buy what DDI sells? Active RFPs/contracts? |
| **Set-Aside Potential** | 0-25 | Do they use EDWOSB/WOSB/SB set-asides? History of diversity awards? |
| **Geographic Fit** | 0-15 | Is DDI's location or national network an advantage? |
| **Contract Value** | 0-20 | Annual/total contract value worth the pursuit effort? |
| **Competition Level** | 0-15 | How crowded is the field? Lower competition = higher score. |

**Scoring Guide:**
- **80-100 = TIER 1** — Pursue immediately. High win probability.
- **65-79 = TIER 2** — Strong fit. Pursue when capacity allows.
- **50-64 = TIER 3** — Moderate fit. Monitor for opportunities.
- **Below 50 = PASS** — Not worth the effort right now.

| 561720 | Janitorial Services | Facility services |
| 561730 | Landscaping Services | Grounds maintenance |

**Why this matters:**
- Federal agencies can buy from GSA Schedule with minimal procurement effort
- Simplified ordering procedures for purchases under $250K
- DDI becomes visible to EVERY federal buyer searching GSA Advantage
- EDWOSB filter on GSA Advantage means buyers looking for WOSB find DDI immediately
- One application = access to all agencies

**Process:** 3-6 months. Training required (Pathways to Success). Apply through eOffer.
**Cost:** Minimal filing fees.
**ROI:** Massive. Every federal agency on the hit list could buy through GSA instead of a standalone RFQ.
