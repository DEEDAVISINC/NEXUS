## National — by issue / population

### Children, lead, family health
- Kellogg, Skillman (MI-heavy)
- Robert Wood Johnson Foundation — health equity ($50K–$500K pipeline)
- EPA / environmental health funders
- HUD CDBG (local pass-through)

### Low-income, benefits, SDOH
- HRSA community health
- SAMHSA
- ACL → AAAs (partner/subcontract model)
- NTIA Digital Equity (state pass-through)

### Older adults
- ACL / AoA → **620+ AAAs** (16 in Michigan)
- n4a.org industry opportunities
- MHEF Healthy Aging

### Veterans
- MVAA (MI)
- VA SSVF — homelessness prevention (federal, CWC)
- Local United Way veteran funds

### Disaster / HAVEN
- Center for Disaster Philanthropy
- Primarily **MCO/state contracts**, not foundations

### Women-owned business (DDI for-profit)
- Hello Alice, Amber Grant, IFundWomen, WBENC, Comcast RISE — **capacity**, not population grants
