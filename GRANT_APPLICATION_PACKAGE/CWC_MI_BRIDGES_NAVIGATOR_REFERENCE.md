## Client FAQ (From MDHHS Newsletter)

### Document upload confirmation
After a document is uploaded, the client sees an **"Upload Successful"** screen. They can also go to **View Documents** to see all previously submitted documents.

### Benefit renewal
During renewal or redetermination, clients see a **"Renew Benefits"** button on their MI Bridges dashboard.

### Partner username recovery
On the **MI Bridges for Business** login page → **"Lookup your user ID"**

### Partner password recovery
On the **MI Bridges for Business** login page → **"Forgot your password?"**
