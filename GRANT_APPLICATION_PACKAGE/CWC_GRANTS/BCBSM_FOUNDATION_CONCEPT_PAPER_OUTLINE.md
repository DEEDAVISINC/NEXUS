## Concept paper sections (mirror BCBSM required narrative)

### 1. Purpose of project (2–3 sentences)
Bridge the gap between **Medicaid-enrolled and underserved residents** who miss medical appointments, lab work, and benefits redeterminations because of transportation and navigation barriers — not clinical capacity.

**One-line:** CWC + DDI will deliver a community-based Healthcare Access & Transportation Equity program combining CHW navigation, MiBridges enrollment support, and DDI-managed medical transportation coordination in Wayne and Macomb counties.

---

### 2. Description of the need (data-backed — fill before submit)

**Draft bullets — replace with sourced stats:**
- Southeast Michigan Medicaid members face NEMT authorization friction and missed appointments (cite MCO SDOH/HRSN data if available)
- Dual-eligible and chronically ill adults disproportionately miss care due to transport + digital literacy barriers
- LHDs and MCOs lack turnkey community partner infrastructure for navigation + transport in one program

**Population lane:** MOB-A (plan NEMT / medical access) — not freight, not disaster HAVEN.

---

### 3. How this work addresses the need
- **CWC:** In-person MiBridges navigation, appointment coaching, SDOH screening, family trust
- **DDI:** Program TPA — CHAMPS-enrolled transportation broker (Provider ID 6309049), compliance, billing, outcomes reporting
- **Proof:** MDHHS Community Partner since May 2020 (200+ applications DDI · 50+ CWC); active MCO NEMT credentialing path (HAP CareSource)

---

### 4. Health outcomes & non-medical drivers of health (SDOH)
| SDOH domain | Program response |
|---|---|
| Transportation | Medical appointment access via DDI-managed NEMT coordination |
| Health literacy / digital access | MiBridges enrollment + renewal navigation (CWC) |
| Social support | CHW outreach, referral to community resources |
| Economic stability | Benefits access → coverage continuity → care access |

---

### 6. Evaluation & methodology

**Measurable outcomes (proposed):**
- % reduction in missed appointment rate among enrolled members (baseline → 6 mo → 12 mo)
- # MiBridges applications completed / renewals submitted
- Member satisfaction (brief survey at 90 days)
- Trip completion rate for coordinated NEMT

**Evaluator:** CWC internal + DDI ATLAS PM data export. No external IRB unless BCBSM requires.

---

### 7. Obstacles & mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| MCO authorization delays | DDI existing HAP credentialing + expand payer list |
| CHW staffing | CWC ED + trained navigator bench; cross-train 2 backups |
| Data sharing across entities | DDI-CWC MOU; de-identified aggregate reporting to funder |
| Low family uptake | Partner with LHD + MCO care managers for warm referrals |

---

### 8. Expected impact / measurable outcomes

**12-month targets (draft — finalize numbers):**
- 150+ families receive navigation + transport coordination
- 85%+ trip completion rate for coordinated medical transport
- 200+ MiBridges touchpoints (new + renewal)
- Documented pathway to sustain via MCO SDOH / HRSN billing after grant period

---

### 9. Cost, quality, access implications
- **Access:** Reduces missed care for Medicaid/underserved populations
- **Quality:** Single accountable program vs. fragmented CBO + broker handoffs
- **Cost:** Prevention of ED utilization from missed primary/specialty care (cite literature in full proposal)
