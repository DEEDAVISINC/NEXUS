## Grant narrative — standing language (copyable)

```
Cause We Care provides the community trust relationship, family navigation, and outreach infrastructure required to reach the populations this program serves. Dee Davis Inc., a federally certified contract management third-party administrator, is engaged under a documented services agreement to coordinate the credentialed service networks, compliance framework, and reporting infrastructure the program requires. This division of labor reflects each organization's core competency and ensures funds are directed toward services actually rendered, at fair market value, with outcomes tied directly to program goals.
```

## Cause We Care — Mission (canonical)

**Public purpose:** We connect families to the resources they need — food security, safe housing, education essentials, health navigation, and community support — because every family deserves someone in their corner.

**Formal mission:** To connect Michigan families with the resources, support, and navigation they need to overcome hardship — without barriers, without judgment, and without delay.

**Tagline:** Cause We Care, You Should Too. · **Theme:** Care. Navigate. Transform.

## Standing rules (summary)

- Separate entities — never one company with two names
- CWC→DDI payments = scoped services only, fair market value
- MOB-B (HAVEN) ≠ MOB-A (plan NEMT) in funder copy
- Never name fulfillment partners externally
- WBENC/WOSB renewal in progress (exp. May 28, 2026) — caveat until renewed
- No SWFT claims · ARENA not public-health aligned yet
- MDHHS: **MDHHS refers. CWC navigates. DDI administers.**
