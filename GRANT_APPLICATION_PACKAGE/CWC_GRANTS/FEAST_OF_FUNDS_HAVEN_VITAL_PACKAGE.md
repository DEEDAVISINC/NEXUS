## STEP 1 — PROGRAM SNAPSHOT (copy into Canva workbook)

### Q1 — What program do you want to grow or fund?

```
We want to fund and scale one integrated program: Healthcare Continuity & Logistics.

VITAL — Verified Integrated Transport And Logistics: end-to-end healthcare logistics TPA for lab specimens, pharmacy delivery, and medical equipment under one contract, one compliance framework (HIPAA, DOT biohazard, DEA controlled substance chain, CAP/CLIA specimen handling). Replaces 5–10 fragmented courier vendors with a single program administrator. PRISM modules operational for orders, compliance, SLA (85% on-time), POD, and credentialing.

HAVEN — Housing, Assistance, Vital Emergency Network: member continuity TPA when displacement breaks normal networks — hotel/shelter coordination, evacuation and medical transport, emergency Rx/DME/home health continuity. Pre-credentialed partners activate from a registry; MCOs send displaced-member lists; DDI coordinates (not daily Medicaid rides — disaster and housing-transition mode).

Both run on Dee Davis Inc.’s live Michigan MCO operations (HAP CareSource NEMT, portal active). We are not asking for grant money to “start transport.” We are packaging proven TPA infrastructure into fundable, scalable programs that health plans and foundations can underwrite at $250K–$1M+.
```

### Q2 — Who do you serve?

```
VITAL populations:
• Homebound and chronically ill plan members needing lab draws, pharmacy delivery, and DME
• Hospitals, health systems, and MCOs managing specimen chain-of-custody and Rx compliance
• LTSS / MICH / HIDE SNP members where fragmented couriers create missed care and audit risk

HAVEN populations (MOB-B — separate from routine NEMT):
• Displaced Medicaid/Medicare managed-care members after flood, fire, hurricane, eviction, or facility transition
• Households needing shelter + medical/pharmacy continuity simultaneously
• Plan members whose pharmacies, dialysis centers, or home health agencies are unreachable due to relocation

We name these lanes separately so funders see precision — not generic “transportation services.”
```

### Q3 — What changes when this works?

```
VITAL outcomes:
• One TPA contract replaces multiple courier vendors — unified SLA, unified audit trail
• 85%+ on-time delivery; chain-of-custody and temperature compliance documented per trip
• Fewer missed appointments and specimen rejections for homebound members

HAVEN outcomes:
• Within 24–48 hours of displacement: shelter path, medical transport, and Rx/DME continuity initiated
• MCO quality metrics protected — members do not “fall off” care during housing transitions or disasters
• Pre-staged network — no post-crisis vendor scramble

Combined: everyday logistics (VITAL) + crisis continuity (HAVEN) = one fundable program health plans can budget annually.
```

### Q4 — What makes this fundable (Feast frame)?

```
We are not submitting more small grants. We are selling one structured program:

• Recurring revenue: MCO vendor contracts and hospital system logistics agreements (not fee-for-service chaos)
• Defensible population: dual-eligible, homebound, displaced — aligned to Medicaid quality and HRSN
• Live proof: HAP CareSource operations today — not a concept deck
• EDWOSB prime: Dee Davis Inc. holds contract; Cause We Care provides community navigation where grants require 501(c)(3)
• Technology: NEXUS + PRISM + VERTEX — billing, compliance, and SLA reporting already built

Target: $500K+ annual program revenue within 12 months via 2–3 MCO/hospital contracts — grants accelerate hiring and geographic expansion, not basic existence.
```
