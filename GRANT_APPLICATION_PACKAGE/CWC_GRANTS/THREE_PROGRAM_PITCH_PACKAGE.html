The Undeniable Stack

Executive Pitch

30-Second Pitch

Michigan now requires every child to be blood-lead tested at 12 and 24 months — but only ~20.3% were tested statewide in 2024. When a child tests elevated, families fall through the cracks. SHIELD is a continuity of care guarantee — dispatch screening, remove every barrier in real time, and when BLL is elevated activate the full cascade. When members lose housing or disasters break care, HAVEN keeps plan members connected to shelter, transport, and medications. VITAL runs lab, pharmacy, and DME logistics under one TPA. All three run on infrastructure that is already live: HAP CareSource NEMT (Vendor 100000469269), DDI MDHHS Community Partner since 2020 (CWC since 2024), and MDHHS Environmental Health partnership in motion — SHIELD demo accepted, six-county LHD introductions committed. We are not asking funders to bet on a concept — we are asking for capital to hire navigators and scale what is already operating.

Program Definitions

SHIELD

Family Stability · Healthcare Access

Continuity-of-care guarantee for Michigan's universal childhood BLL screening mandate (MCL 333.5474d) — DEPOINTE-style dispatch of mobile screeners, real-time barrier removal, elevated-BLL cascade through clearance. CHW workforce via Cause We Care; TPA ops and billing via Dee Davis Inc.

Population: All children under 4 in Michigan (MCL 333.5474d) · Wayne pilot first · ~39,460 mandatory tests/yr at full Wayne scale

Billing: CHW navigation billed under Medicaid codes 98960, 98961, 98962 through CHAMPS provider ID 6309049. DDI bills MCOs and Medicaid FFS directly — not through MDHHS.

HAVEN

MOB-B · Displacement Continuity

Member continuity when housing fails or disaster displaces plan members — shelter, dialysis transport, Rx/DME, home health reassignment under one TPA.

Population: Displaced plan members nationally — activates when housing fails or disaster breaks care. MOB-B only. Not routine plan NEMT.

Operational proof (MOB-A, separate lane): HAP CareSource NEMT — Vendor 100000469269 · CHAMPS 6309049 · Wayne + Macomb live. Proves DDI can run MCO contracts at scale.

VITAL

Healthcare Access · Logistics TPA

Healthcare logistics TPA — lab specimen courier, DEA-compliant pharmacy delivery, DME coordination — one HIPAA/DOT/DEA compliance framework for homebound members.

Replaces 5–10 fragmented vendor relationships.

CAPACITY GAP WE ARE SOLVING
Cause We Care has built the systems, state relationships, and live payer contract to run population-scale health navigation — but we lack grant capital to hire community health workers at the volume Michigan's new universal childhood lead testing law requires. We are not seeking funds to design a program. We are seeking funds to staff a program that is already designed, demonstrated, and aligned with MDHHS Environmental Health Bureau priorities.

PROGRAM: SHIELD — LEAD-SAFE NAVIGATION (PRIMARY)
Michigan's universal blood lead testing law (MCL 333.5474d) requires testing at 12 and 24 months. Only ~20.3% of Michigan children under 6 were tested in 2024. When a child tests elevated, families navigate abatement, housing, benefits, transportation, CLPPP, and retesting alone — or overstretched local health departments coordinate piecemeal.

SHIELD provides one door: a continuity-of-care guarantee for Michigan's universal lead testing mandate — dispatch screening, remove every barrier in real time, and when BLL is elevated activate a verified service cascade (CHW home visit, MIBridges enrollment, NEMT, CLPPP referral, remediation coordination, emergency housing, retest) on the SHIELD platform with real-time accountability. Dee Davis Inc. bills MCOs, Medicaid FFS, and private payers — not MDHHS.

SUPPORTING INFRASTRUCTURE (SAME TPA STACK)
• HAVEN — continuity for displaced plan members (housing + medical transport + Rx/DME) — MOB-B disaster/transition mode.
• VITAL — lab, pharmacy, and DME logistics under one HIPAA/DOT/DEA TPA — serves homebound MICH and SHIELD families needing specimen and medication access.
