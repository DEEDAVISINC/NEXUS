Structural & Mission Alignment — CWC × DDI

How a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and a federally certified for-profit TPA share one mission, operate as distinct legal entities, and lawfully contract with one another to deliver public health outcomes.

1 — Two Entities, One Mission

Cause We Care (CWC) and Dee Davis Inc. (DDI) are separate, independently governed organizations. They are not divisions of one company, and CWC is not a fundraising arm of DDI. They are aligned because they were built to solve the same problem from two different legal postures — one as a community trust organization, one as a credentialed contract management firm — and that alignment is what makes the partnership between them lawful, defensible, and fundable.

Why This Structure Is Not "Shady" — It Is the Model

A for-profit operating company and a nonprofit with overlapping mission language are common and lawful when three conditions hold, and CWC/DDI satisfy all three:

  • Documented: Every dollar CWC pays DDI is tied to a written agreement, a defined scope, and a deliverable.
  • Mission-aligned, not mission-identical: CWC raises funds and builds trust for the public good. DDI performs the technical/operational work that public good requires.
  • Fair market value: DDI is paid what any TPA would charge for the same coordination work — not a markup designed to extract nonprofit funds.

CWC does not pay DDI to "help it operate" in a general sense. CWC pays DDI for specific, contracted services — network coordination, compliance management, credentialing, reporting — that CWC does not have the infrastructure to perform itself. That is the entire purpose of a TPA.

Program Alignment

SHIELD
Cause We Care About Safe Homes — Screen, Educate, Prevent

Public health basis: Michigan's universal childhood lead screening mandate (MCL 333.5474d, added by PA 146 of 2023) requires blood lead testing for minors at 12 and 24 months, with catch-up testing through age six if no prior record exists. This is a statewide, statutory public health obligation — not a discretionary program.

Division of labor: CWC's outreach and family navigation network identifies and engages families who need screening, particularly in homes built before 1978 and other high-risk housing. DDI's TPA infrastructure coordinates the mobile screening logistics, lab relationships, CHW workforce, and CHAMPS billing/reporting layer that turns outreach into completed, billable, compliant screenings.

Target audience: MDHHS (CLPPP Care Coordination Section) and state/county health departments.

Positioning: Partner in Michigan's lead-safe ecosystem — referral partnership with MDHHS Environmental Health Bureau. Do not claim MDHHS grantee/subgrantee status unless formally awarded.

Program Alignment

HAVEN
Cause We Care When Disaster Strikes — House, Move, Heal

Public health basis: When disaster displaces health plan members, housing, transportation, and medical continuity fail simultaneously. Members lose stable addresses, miss dialysis and medication, and disappear from care coordination systems — a documented, recurring crisis in declared disaster events nationally.

Division of labor: CWC provides the community trust relationship and family-level navigation during displacement — the human contact families recognize and trust in crisis. DDI's TPA infrastructure provides the credentialed housing, transport, and medical network coordination, compliance documentation, and MCO/state billing relationship that makes continuity of care operationally possible.

Target audience: State emergency management and health agencies in disaster-frequent states — Louisiana, Texas, Tennessee, Florida, and similarly positioned states.

Population note: HAVEN serves displaced health plan members nationally (MOB-B). This population is distinct from, and must never be merged with, routine plan NEMT / enrolled-member daily transport (MOB-A) in funder-facing materials.

Program Alignment

VITAL
Cause We Care About Every Delivery — Specimen to Surgery, No Gaps

Public health basis: Breaks in healthcare logistics — delayed lab specimens, missed medication deliveries, unavailable equipment — directly drive preventable readmissions and care failures. Medication non-adherence alone is linked to roughly 30% of hospital readmissions.

Division of labor: CWC's community presence surfaces logistics gaps at the family and neighborhood level — patients who cannot get to a pharmacy, equipment that never arrives. DDI's TPA infrastructure operates the credentialed courier networks, chain-of-custody compliance (HIPAA, DEA, DOT, OSHA, CAP/CLIA), and health-system contracting that closes those gaps at scale.

Target audience: State health agencies and health systems in the same disaster-relief geographies as HAVEN, where logistics continuity is part of the same continuity-of-care problem.

3 — Why This Structure Holds Up Under Review

What a Grant Reviewer or Funder Sees

  • A 501(c)(3) with a clear, public-facing mission and community navigation track record (CWC)
  • A federally certified, credentialed contract management TPA with active state and MCO relationships (DDI)
  • A written services relationship between the two, scoped to specific deliverables, priced at fair market value
  • No commingling of nonprofit funds into general DDI operations — CWC pays for defined services rendered, the same way it would pay any vendor
  • Outcomes that are documented, tied to the funded program, and reportable back to the funder

Standing Language for Grant Narratives

Cause We Care provides the community trust relationship, family navigation, and outreach infrastructure required to reach the populations this program serves. Dee Davis Inc., a federally certified contract management third-party administrator, is engaged under a documented services agreement to coordinate the credentialed service networks, compliance framework, and reporting infrastructure the program requires. This division of labor reflects each organization's core competency and ensures funds are directed toward services actually rendered, at fair market value, with outcomes tied directly to program goals.

4 — Standing Rules — Never Violate

  • CWC and DDI are always described as separate, independently governed organizations — never as one company with two names
  • Every CWC-to-DDI payment must map to a documented, scoped service — never described as general operating support
  • HAVEN's displaced-member population (MOB-B) is never merged with routine plan NEMT / enrolled-member daily transport (MOB-A) in funder-facing copy
  • Never name fulfillment network partners (labs, couriers, rideshare, etc.) in buyer- or funder-facing materials unless explicitly required by a sealed exhibit
  • WBENC / WOSB (Great Lakes WBC) — renewal in progress; expiration May 28, 2026 — include recertification caveat in external materials until renewed
  • Do not claim DCSA SWFT authorization — per COMPANY_INFO_MASTER.md
  • ARENA is not represented as public-health or CWC-mission-aligned until a specific rationale is developed and approved
  • Operating model for MDHHS materials: MDHHS refers. CWC navigates. DDI administers.
  • Closing language in outreach: "I welcome the opportunity to connect." — no conditions attached
