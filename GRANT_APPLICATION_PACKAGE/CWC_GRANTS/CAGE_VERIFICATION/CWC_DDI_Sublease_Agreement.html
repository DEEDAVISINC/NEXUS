1. PREMISES

Dee Davis Inc. (“DDI”) is the primary leaseholder of office space located at 755 W. Big Beaver Rd., Suite 2020, Troy, Michigan 48084 (the “Premises”). DDI hereby grants Cause We Care (“CWC”) a non-exclusive license to occupy and conduct business operations from the Premises.

2. PURPOSE

CWC shall use the Premises for the conduct of its nonprofit business operations, including but not limited to administrative functions, program coordination, community outreach management, and receipt of business correspondence.

3. RELATIONSHIP OF PARTIES

DDI and CWC share a common founder and principal officer, Dieasha D. Davis. DDI is a for-profit corporation and CWC is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation. They are separate legal entities that operate from the same physical office location. This shared-space arrangement reflects the operational relationship between the two organizations under common leadership.

4. TERM

This Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above and shall continue for a period of one (1) year, automatically renewing for successive one-year terms unless terminated by either party with thirty (30) days’ written notice.

5. CONSIDERATION

CWC’s use of the Premises is provided at no additional cost. DDI maintains the primary lease and bears all rental obligations to the landlord. CWC may contribute to shared operating expenses (utilities, supplies, etc.) as mutually agreed upon by the parties.

6. DESIGNATED SPACE

CWC is authorized to use Suite 2020 as its physical business address for all purposes, including but not limited to federal registrations (SAM.gov, CAGE Code), state filings, grant applications, and business correspondence.

7. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days’ written notice. Upon termination, CWC shall cease using the Premises address for new registrations or correspondence within a reasonable transition period not to exceed sixty (60) days.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan.
