01 Program Identity

DDI and CWC operate as an integrated public health program infrastructure — delivering community-based consultation, navigation, and program administration across environmental health, healthcare access, social determinants of health, and substance use compliance. CWC provides the community trust and navigation layer. DDI provides the operational and administrative backbone. Together they form a two-entity model with proven MDHHS validation and active state-level credentials. Headquartered in Troy, Michigan — program administration scales nationwide under DDI contract management.
