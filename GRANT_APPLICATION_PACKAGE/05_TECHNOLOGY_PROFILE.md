## NEXUS SUBSYSTEMS — 9 INTEGRATED MODULES

### 1. GPSS — Government Procurement Strategic System
**Purpose:** Win government contracts  
**What It Does:**
- Monitors SAM.gov, state portals, and 100+ municipal bid platforms automatically
- Scores every opportunity on a 0–100 scale across 6 criteria: set-aside match, NAICS alignment, service fit, geographic advantage, presolicitation bonus, and deadline urgency
- Auto-classifies as BID NOW / WORTH A LOOK / MAYBE / SKIP
- Researches incumbents via USASpending.gov — identifies who holds the current contract, what they were paid, and where they're vulnerable
- Generates complete bid packages: capability statements, buyer emails, compliance matrices, quote responses — all tailored to the specific solicitation
- Applies ProposalBio (see COMPASS below) to every buyer-facing document
- Manages the full bid folder structure with automated document routing

**Status:** 90% complete — actively used on every DDI bid

### 5. COMPASS — Compliant Optimization & Messaging Performance Assessment System
**Purpose:** Proposal quality assurance  
**What It Does:**
- ProposalBio Engine — scores every buyer-facing document against 10 psychological influence frameworks:
  1. Mirror Neuron (cultural tone matching)
  2. Cognitive Ease (reading level, sentence structure)
  3. Story Arc (challenge → solution → result)
  4. Reciprocity (value given before asking)
  5. Yes Stacking (capabilities mirror RFP requirements)
  6. Familiarity (buyer's exact language echoed back)
  7. Name Recognition (agency name frequency)
  8. Sensory Language (concrete numbers and specifics)
  9. Rhythm (sentence variety and cadence)
  10. Eye Tracking (scannable layout, visual hierarchy)
- Compliance validation — checks every document for correct company info, certification references, and regulatory accuracy
- Win-readiness assessment — gates every proposal before submission

**Status:** Integrated across all NEXUS systems — every buyer-facing output is COMPASS-validated
