## 📝 PARTNERSHIP MODELS:

### **Option 1: Joint Venture (JV)**
- Form new entity together (50/50 or other split)
- JV gets both sets of certifications
- Share profits based on agreement
- Best for: Large, multi-year contracts

### **Option 2: Teaming Agreement**
- One company is prime, other is sub
- Written agreement on roles and revenue split
- Simpler than JV
- Best for: Single contracts

### **Option 3: Mentor-Protégé**
- One company mentors the other
- SBA 8(a) program (if applicable)
- Access to set-asides
- Best for: Long-term relationship building

### **Option 4: Subcontracting**
- You as prime, they as sub (or vice versa)
- Standard subcontractor relationship
- Simplest model
- Best for: Project-specific work
