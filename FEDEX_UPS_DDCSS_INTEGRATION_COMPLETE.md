## 💰 REVENUE TRACKING IN DDCSS

**Fields for Tracking:**

- **Estimated Revenue:** Initial projection ($8,000/month)
- **Contract Value:** Actual signed amount
- **Partnership Status:** Current stage
- **Pilot Start Date:** When pilot begins
- **Contract Start Date:** When contract signed

**Reports Available:**

1. **Total Pipeline:** Sum of Estimated Revenue for all active prospects
2. **Active Contracts:** Sum of Contract Value for signed deals
3. **Conversion Rate:** % of prospects that become contracts
4. **Average Deal Size:** Mean Contract Value
5. **Time to Close:** Days from Research to Contract
