## ✅ WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN RFPs

### **GREEN FLAGS (Pursue These!):**

**Set-Aside Language:**
- ✅ "EDWOSB set-aside"
- ✅ "WOSB eligible"
- ✅ "Restricted to certified WOSBs"
- ✅ "Women-owned business preference"
- ✅ "Evaluation includes diversity"

**Scope:**
- ✅ Equipment/supplies (not services)
- ✅ Clear specifications
- ✅ Known products (can source)
- ✅ Delivery over time (not one-time dump)
- ✅ "Blanket Purchase Agreement" or "IDIQ"

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- ✅ "Technical capability" scored
- ✅ "Past performance" scored
- ✅ "Diversity" scored
- ✅ Not just "lowest price"

**Contract Type:**
- ✅ Firm-fixed-price
- ✅ Multiple award possible
- ✅ Net 30 or Net 60 payment terms

---

### **RED FLAGS (Skip or Be Cautious):**

**Scope Issues:**
- ❌ Requires manufacturing
- ❌ Requires installation/labor
- ❌ Requires specific certifications you lack
- ❌ Proprietary/sole-source items
- ❌ Extreme technical complexity

**Business Risk:**
- ❌ Net 90+ payment terms
- ❌ Cost-plus contracts (complex)
- ❌ Performance bonds required (if you can't get)
- ❌ "Lowest price technically acceptable" only
- ❌ Unrealistic delivery timelines

**Competitive:**
- ❌ Incumbent has strong advantage
- ❌ Evaluation criteria heavily favor experience
- ❌ No set-aside (open competition)
