## STRATEGIC TARGETS BY PRIORITY

### **Tier 1 — Immediate Fit (DDI ready now)**

1. **Lab Courier Services** (State Public Health Labs)
   - Entry: Daily specimen pickup routes
   - DDI advantage: DePointe DNA lab operations = credible lab courier
   - Target states: Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, New York

2. **Medication Delivery Services** (Corrections / Jails)
   - Entry: Same-day pharmacy delivery to county jails
   - DDI advantage: Uber Health network + HIPAA compliance
   - Target: County jail medical units (start Michigan)

3. **Specimen Pickup Routes** (FQHCs / County Clinics)
   - Entry: Recurring clinic-to-lab routes
   - DDI advantage: Existing CHAMPS Medicaid provider status
   - Target: Michigan FQHCs (leverage Medicaid network)

4. **Vaccine Transport (Cold Chain)** (State Immunization Programs)
   - Entry: Temp-controlled vaccine delivery
   - DDI advantage: Rx delivery cold-chain infrastructure
   - Target: State health departments (MI, OH, PA)

5. **Home Health Equipment Delivery** (Medicare / Community Care Networks)
   - Entry: In-home DME deployment
   - DDI advantage: NEMT + Rx delivery = proven home delivery capability
   - Target: Medicare Advantage contractors (CareSource, Molina, UHC)

---

### **Tier 2 — Build Capability (6-12 months)**

6. **Regulated Medical Waste Transport** (Psychiatric Hospitals)
   - Requires: EPA/DOT hazmat certification, special containers
   - Entry: Inter-facility medical waste pickup
   - High margin, less competition

7. **Mobile Imaging Logistics** (Medicaid Imaging Vendors)
   - Requires: Equipment transport insurance, technician routing
   - Entry: X-ray / ultrasound equipment delivery + tech transport
   - Partner with mobile imaging companies

8. **Medical Equipment Routing** (State Medicaid / HHS)
   - Entry: DME delivery & recovery (wheelchairs, oxygen, CPAP)
   - Already aligned with NEMT infrastructure

9. **Behavioral Health Records Transport** (County Health Depts)
   - Entry: Secure document courier for mental health records
   - Low capital, high compliance (HIPAA + county background checks)

10. **Mobile Phlebotomy Support Transport** (Lab Networks)
    - Entry: Route support for mobile blood draw teams
    - Partner with Quest, LabCorp, or state lab networks

---

### **Tier 3 — Specialized / High-Value (12-24 months)**

11. **Organ Procurement Transport (OPO)** (Regional OPOs)
    - Requires: 24/7 emergency response, specialized training
    - Entry: Surgical kit & organ courier
    - **Highest margin, highest barrier to entry**

12. **Clinical Trial Logistics** (University Research Hospitals)
    - Entry: Lab kit & specimen transport for clinical trials
    - Partner with university medical centers (U-M, Wayne State, MSU)

13. **Airport-to-Hospital Medical Delivery** (International Airports)
    - Entry: Time-critical inbound medical cargo
    - Target: DTW (Detroit Metro), ORD (Chicago), CLE (Cleveland)

14. **Hospital Inter-Campus Logistics** (Public Hospital Districts)
    - Entry: STAT internal routing between hospital campuses
    - Target: DMC (Detroit Medical Center), Corewell Health multi-site

15. **Behavioral Crisis Unit Supply Delivery** (County Mental Health)
    - Entry: Mobile crisis team restock routes
    - Partner with county behavioral health programs
