## COMPETITIVE MOAT

### Why DDI Wins

1. **Single Platform, Multiple Sectors**
   - Competitors specialize in one sector (drug testing OR background checks OR environmental)
   - DDI offers integrated compliance across all sectors
   - One contract, one vendor, one invoice

2. **EDWOSB Certification**
   - Government contracts have set-asides
   - DDI qualifies where competitors don't
   - Automatic bidding advantage

3. **AI-Powered Automation**
   - Lower overhead than traditional TPAs
   - Faster service delivery
   - Better accuracy (PRISM Inspection Engine)

4. **Technology-First**
   - Modern platform (NEXUS/PRISM/FleetFlow)
   - API integrations with clients
   - Real-time visibility and reporting

5. **Field Service Capability**
   - Not just software — actual workers in the field
   - Mobile capability for on-site services
   - Geographic coverage through agent network

6. **Unified Compliance Tracking**
   - Expiration alerts across all services
   - Audit-ready documentation
   - Single compliance dashboard for clients
