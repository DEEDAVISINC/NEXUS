## NEXUS COMPLETE CAPABILITIES

### 1. OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Monitor SAM.gov via API (every 2 hours)
- Parse RSS feeds for local/state opportunities
- Scan Michigan portals (MITN, Oakland County, Detroit, etc.)
- Monitor presolicitations and sources sought notices
- Alert on sole source opportunities (EDWOSB advantage)

**Output:** Raw opportunities flowing into Stage 0

---

### 2. AI SCORING & QUALIFICATION
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Extract: Sol #, agency, deadline, NAICS, set-aside, value
- Score on 6 criteria (0-100):
  - Set-aside match (0-30 pts)
  - NAICS alignment (0-20 pts)
  - Product/service fit (0-20 pts)
  - Geographic advantage (0-15 pts)
  - Presolicitation bonus (0-15 pts)
  - Deadline urgency (-5 to +5 pts)
- Assign tier: 🔴 BID NOW / 🟡 WORTH A LOOK / 🔵 MAYBE / ⚪ SKIP

**Output:** Scored, tiered opportunities ready for decision

---

### 3. INCUMBENT INTELLIGENCE
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Search USASpending.gov for current contract
- Identify: Incumbent name, contract value, award date, PIID
- Deep dive FPDS.gov for modifications, option years, task orders
- Check SAM.gov for previous solicitation and pricing (if posted)
- Analyze: How many bidders last time? Pricing history?

**Files Created:**
- INCUMBENT_INTELLIGENCE.md
- COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md (strengths/weaknesses)
- PRICING_INTELLIGENCE.md (historical benchmarks)

**Output:** Context to position against incumbent

---

### 4. PRICING INTELLIGENCE & STRATEGY
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Research historical contract values
- Benchmark unit pricing from USASpending/FPDS
- Research market pricing (ThomasNet, distributors, web search)
- Calculate DDI estimated costs
- Recommend bid pricing with margin targets
- Develop pricing strategy: Match/Beat/Premium based on analysis

**Files Created:**
- PRICING_INTELLIGENCE.md
- PRICING_WORKSHEET.md (cost basis, margins, final pricing)
- WIN_STRATEGY.md (how to position competitively)

**Output:** Data-driven pricing strategy

---

### 5. ACCURATE PRICING (BEFORE & AFTER QUOTES)

#### BEFORE Supplier/Sub Quotes (Intelligence Phase):
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Estimate product costs via market research
- Estimate labor rates via industry benchmarks
- Calculate projected DDI margin at target pricing
- Flag if market pricing suggests unattainable margins

#### AFTER Supplier/Sub Quotes (Resourcing Phase):
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Calculate actual costs from received quotes
- Compare: Projected (Stage 2) vs Actual (Stage 3)
- Adjust bid pricing if needed (maintain competitive position)
- Lock in final DDI margin calculation
- Generate PRICING_WORKSHEET with:
  - Supplier/Sub Cost: $[X]
  - Freight/Other: $[Y]
  - DDI Management Fee: $[Z]
  - **Total Cost to DDI: $[X+Y+Z]**
  - **DDI Bid Price to Govt: $[A]**
  - **DDI Spread/Margin: $[A-(X+Y+Z)] ([X]%)**

**Margin Guardrails:**
- <15% margin = Flag for review
- 15-25% margin = Standard target
- >25% margin = Strong position (verify competitiveness)

**Output:** Accurate, defensible pricing with verified margins

### 6. SUBCONTRACTOR AUTOMATED SOURCING
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Query Airtable SUBCONTRACTORS for existing vetted matches
- Web search for local providers by service type and location
- Check industry directories (eScreen, SWFT, etc.)
- **MANDATORY: USASpending check on each candidate**
  - If they win contracts = CROSS OFF (competition)
  - If no wins = KEEP (ideal partner)
- Generate SUBCONTRACTOR_PROSPECTS.md (Tier 1 & 2)
- Execute 6-pillar vetting framework:
  - PILLAR 1: VET (qualifications, references, SAM)
  - PILLAR 2: PROTECT (NDA, Non-Compete)
  - PILLAR 3: INSURE (COI verification)
  - PILLAR 4: PLAN (staffing, work plan)
  - PILLAR 5: COMMUNICATE (reporting cadence)
  - PILLAR 6: MANAGE (performance metrics)
- Select best qualified sub
- Negotiate internal pricing (not client-facing)

**Files Created:**
- SUBCONTRACTOR_PROSPECTS.md
- SUBCONTRACTOR_VETTING_CHECKLIST.md
- SUBCONTRACTOR_OUTREACH_EMAIL.md
- SUBCONTRACTOR_SCOPE.md
- COI_REQUEST_EMAIL.md

**Output:** Vetted, contracted, ready-to-deploy subcontractor

### 7. SUPPLIER RFQ MANAGEMENT
**What NEXUS Does:**
- Extract product specs from solicitation
- Generate RFQ with buyer protection (NO buyer names revealed)
- DDI-2026-### numbering
- Distribute to 3-5 qualified suppliers
- Track responses and follow up (48hr, 72hr reminders)
- Analyze quotes: pricing, terms, lead times
- Select best value supplier

**Files Created:**
- SUPPLIER_SOURCING_GUIDE.md
- DDI-2026-###_RFQ_[Product].pdf
- SUPPLIER_QUOTES_ANALYSIS.md

**Output:** Secured supply chain at competitive pricing

### 9. STAGE & STEP TRACKING
**What NEXUS Does:**

**7 Stages, Every Opportunity:**
1. **STAGE 0: IDENTIFIED** — Scored, tiered, decision
2. **STAGE 1: INTAKE** — Folder, docs, infrastructure
3. **STAGE 2: INTELLIGENCE** — Incumbent, pricing, strategy
4. **STAGE 3: RESOURCING** — Subs/suppliers secured
5. **STAGE 4: DEVELOPMENT** — Proposal written & priced
6. **STAGE 5: SUBMISSION** — Bid delivered
7. **STAGE 6: AWARD** — Won, lost, or pending

**60 Total Steps Across All Stages:**
- Each step is explicit and trackable
- Status visible in WORKFLOW_CHECKLIST.md
- Airtable reflects current stage
- Dee can query: "Where are we with [bid]?" → Exact stage/step response

**Dashboard View:**
```

🟡 Stage 3 (Resourcing): [X] bids — Sub sourcing active
🟢 Stage 4 (Development): [X] bids — Proposal writing

```

**Output:** Complete visibility into every opportunity's status
