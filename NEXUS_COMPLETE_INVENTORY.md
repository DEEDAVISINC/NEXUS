## 🎯 WHY SOURCES SOUGHT & INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE ARE GOLD!

### **SOURCES SOUGHT = Early Bird Special!** 🐦

**What it means:**
- Agency is doing "market research"
- They're learning what's available
- Requirements NOT finalized yet
- Often determines set-aside type

**Why you love these:**
1. ✅ Get in BEFORE competition starts
2. ✅ Influence requirements (show your capabilities)
3. ✅ Build relationship with CO early
4. ✅ If you're the only EDWOSB → they might set it aside for EDWOSB!
5. ✅ Shows you're proactive (COs remember this!)
6. ✅ Less formal = easier to respond quickly

**Your advantage:**
- Most companies SKIP Sources Sought (think it's not real)
- YOU respond = you're already ahead!
- CO knows your name when RFP comes out
- You helped shape the requirements

---

### **INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE = Hidden Opportunity!** 💎

**What it means:**
- Agency WANTS to use one specific vendor
- But FAR requires them to see if other sources exist
- "We intend to sole source to Company X... unless you can do it too"

**Why you love these:**
1. ✅ Most companies think they can't compete (but you CAN!)
2. ✅ Low competition (self-selecting out)
3. ✅ Urgency = fast decision (NIH patient care!)
4. ✅ If you prove capability → you're in the game!
5. ✅ EDWOSB gives you extra points
6. ✅ Often leads to BPA/IDIQ (long-term relationship)

**Your advantage:**
- "Intent to Sole Source" ≠ "Already awarded"
- FAR requires them to consider capable sources
- Your EDWOSB status is a huge plus
- NIH explicitly mentioned "competed with several supplies in last 2 FY"
- They're LOOKING for alternatives!
