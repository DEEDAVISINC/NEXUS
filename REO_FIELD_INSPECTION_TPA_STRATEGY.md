## WHAT THIS IS

**Real Estate Owned (REO)** properties are bank/agency-owned properties after foreclosure. The government — HUD, VA, USDA, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac — owns thousands of these at any given time and contracts out the full lifecycle of managing them:

- **Field inspections** — occupancy checks, property condition reports, drive-bys
- **Property preservation** — winterization, securing, debris removal, lockouts
- **Maintenance** — lawn care, snow removal, utility activation
- **Inspection reporting** — photo documentation, condition assessments, compliance reports

**DDI's role:** Prime contractor. Sub out fieldwork to licensed inspection networks. Manage compliance, reporting, and CO communication end-to-end.

## DDI TPA MODEL — HOW THIS WORKS

```
GOVERNMENT AGENCY (HUD / VA / USDA)
         ↓  (prime contract)
    DEE DAVIS INC.
    - Contract management
    - CO communication
    - Compliance & reporting
    - QA oversight
    - Invoicing
         ↓  (subcontract)
FIELD INSPECTION SUBCONTRACTOR NETWORK
    - Local licensed inspectors
    - Platforms: iVueit, CS Field Services, VRM, Altisource
    - Physical property visits, photos, condition reports
    - 24-48 hour turnaround
```

**DDI NEVER does the physical inspection.** DDI manages the contract, coordinates the inspector, reviews the deliverable, ensures compliance, and reports to the CO.
