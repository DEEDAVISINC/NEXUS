# PROPOSAL WRITING QUICK REFERENCE
**Keep this open while writing any proposal**

---

## ⚡ THE ONE RULE

**Nobody cares what you've DONE. They care about OUTCOMES.**

---

## 🚫 NEVER SAY THIS

- ❌ "We have X years of experience"
- ❌ "We are qualified and reliable"
- ❌ "We have completed [number] projects"
- ❌ "Our team is experienced"
- ❌ "We can provide [service]"
- ❌ "We have worked with government agencies"

---

## ✅ ALWAYS SAY THIS

- ✅ "When X happens, it costs you $Y annually"
- ✅ "We reduced [problem] by X% for [client]"
- ✅ "You'll achieve [specific outcome] within [timeframe]"
- ✅ "The root cause is Z, which 90% of vendors miss"
- ✅ "This delivered $X savings for [client]"
- ✅ "Here's what most agencies don't understand..."

---

## 📐 PROPOSAL STRUCTURE

### **1. PROBLEM MASTERY** (30% of proposal)
Educate them about what they DON'T understand

**Must include:**
- What the problem ACTUALLY is (root cause)
- Hidden complexities they don't see
- Quantified cost of inaction ($$$)
- What vendors typically get wrong

**Test:** Would the client learn something new?

---

### **2. TRANSFORMATION** (30% of proposal)
What specific outcome they'll get

**Must include:**
- Specific transformation (FROM → TO)
- Quantified outcome (numbers!)
- Timeline (when will they see results)
- How it's different from commodity vendors

**Test:** Can you state the outcome in one sentence with numbers?

---

### **3. PROOF** (30% of proposal)
Show you've delivered this transformation before

**Must include:**
- 2-3 case studies with metrics
- BEFORE and AFTER numbers
- Time to achieve transformation
- Pattern across all clients

**Test:** Do you have specific numbers for each case study?

---

### **4. CREDENTIALS** (10% of proposal)
Brief supporting evidence only

**Only include:**
- Certifications that enable outcomes
- Partnerships that deliver results
- Systems that guarantee performance

**Test:** Can you connect each credential to a specific outcome?

---

## 🔢 NUMBERS YOU MUST INCLUDE

Every proposal needs **at least 5 quantified metrics:**

1. **Cost savings:** $X saved/recovered
2. **Time improvement:** X% faster / X days reduced
3. **Performance metric:** X% improvement
4. **Reliability metric:** X% success rate
5. **Client impact:** X users served / X transactions

**Can't find numbers? Not ready to write the proposal yet.**

---

## ✅ QUICK QUALITY CHECK

**Read any paragraph and ask:**

1. **Is this about THEM or about US?**
   - Should be about THEM (their outcome)

2. **Does this educate them?**
   - Should reveal something they don't know

3. **Are there numbers?**
   - Should have specific metrics

4. **Could a competitor say this?**
   - Should be unique to your insight

5. **Is this a transformation or a resume?**
   - Should be transformation

**If any answer is wrong → REWRITE THE PARAGRAPH**

---

## 🎯 ONE-SENTENCE TEST

**Can you complete this sentence?**

> "We will transform [CLIENT]'s [PROBLEM] into [OUTCOME] within [TIMEFRAME], proven by our [METRIC]% success rate with [CLIENT TYPE]."

**Example:**
> "We will transform Wayne County's $2.1M annual waste from missed NEMT appointments into 43% reduction in no-shows within 6 months, proven by our 98.7% on-time performance with 3 Michigan counties."

**If you can't write this sentence, you're not ready to write the proposal.**

---

## 🚨 EMERGENCY FIXES

### If your proposal sounds like a resume:

**FIND THIS PATTERN:**
> "We are [credential]. We have [experience]. We can [service]."

**REPLACE WITH:**
> "Your [problem] costs $X annually because [root cause]. We delivered [outcome] for [client] by [solution]. You'll achieve [metric] within [timeframe]."

---

### If you're stuck on a section:

**Ask yourself:**
1. What does the client NOT understand about this?
2. What would surprise them?
3. What outcome did we deliver before? (numbers!)
4. What will fail if they pick the wrong vendor?
5. What makes our approach different?

---

## 💡 PHRASE TEMPLATES

### **Problem Mastery:**
- "When [problem happens], [cost/consequence]"
- "The root cause is [X], not [what they think]"
- "What most agencies don't realize is [hidden complexity]"
- "This costs you $[X] annually in [specific impact]"
- "90% of vendors miss [key insight]"

### **Transformation:**
- "We transform [problem] into [outcome]"
- "You'll achieve [metric] within [timeframe]"
- "Our [system] delivers [outcome], not just [service]"
- "Unlike commodity vendors who [X], we [Y]"
- "This means [specific benefit] for your agency"

### **Proof:**
- "[Client] faced [problem]. We delivered [outcome]."
- "Before: [metric]. After: [metric]. Time: [X months]."
- "This saved them $[X] annually"
- "In every case where [problem existed], we achieved [outcome]"
- "The pattern is consistent: [X]% improvement across [Y] clients"

---

## 📋 FINAL CHECKLIST (Before Submitting)

**Every proposal must have:**

- [ ] Education (they learn something about their problem)
- [ ] Root cause identified (not just surface symptoms)
- [ ] Quantified cost of inaction ($$$)
- [ ] Specific transformation stated (FROM → TO)
- [ ] 5+ quantified metrics (%, $, time)
- [ ] 2+ case studies with outcomes
- [ ] Timeline for results
- [ ] Differentiation from commodity vendors
- [ ] Proof pattern across clients

**If missing ANY of these → NOT READY TO SUBMIT**
