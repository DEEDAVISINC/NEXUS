## OVERVIEW

DNA relationship testing is used for:
- **Immigration** (USCIS family reunification)
- **Child support / Paternity establishment**
- **Custody disputes**
- **Inheritance claims**
- **Personal knowledge (informational)**

DDI operates DePointe DNA, providing DNA collection services through the PRISM network.

**Key Distinction:**
- **Legal/Court-Admissible Testing** = Strict chain of custody, AABB-accredited lab required
- **Informational/Peace of Mind Testing** = No chain of custody, results not court-admissible

## REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### AABB (Association for the Advancement of Blood & Biotherapies)
- Sets standards for relationship testing laboratories
- Accreditation required for immigration and most legal cases
- Current standard: AABB Standards for Relationship Testing Laboratories

### USCIS Requirements
- Immigration DNA testing MUST use AABB-accredited laboratory
- Collection MUST follow strict chain of custody
- Results sent directly from lab to USCIS or consulate

### State Courts
- Most states require AABB-accredited lab for court-ordered testing
- Some states have additional requirements (witness signatures, specific forms)

## CHAIN OF CUSTODY — THE FOUNDATION

### What Makes Testing "Legal" vs "Informational"

| Requirement | Legal Testing | Informational Testing |
|---|---|---|
| Chain of custody maintained | YES - mandatory | NO |
| Photo ID verified | YES - all participants | Optional |
| Photographs taken | YES - all participants | Optional |
| Collector is third party | YES - mandatory | Can be self-collected |
| Tamper-evident seals | YES - mandatory | Optional |
| Samples in collector's possession | YES - until shipped | Can mail directly |
| Court admissible | YES | NO |
| Accepted by USCIS | YES | NO |

### Chain of Custody Requirements (Legal Testing)

1. **Collector is neutral third party** — Not related to participants, no interest in outcome
2. **Photo ID verified** — Government-issued photo ID for all participants
3. **Photographs taken** — Each participant photographed with identification sheet
4. **Samples collected under observation** — Collector watches entire collection
5. **Samples labeled during collection** — Not pre-labeled, prevents switching
6. **Samples in collector's possession** — From collection until shipped
7. **Tamper-evident seals applied** — Participant initials seals
8. **Tracked shipping** — Traceable to laboratory
9. **Lab maintains chain** — Documents receipt, handling, testing, disposal

## COLLECTION PROCEDURES

### Step-by-Step Legal DNA Collection

| Step | Action | Documentation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verify appointment** — Confirm all participants are present | Note who is present |
| 2 | **Explain process** — Describe what will happen, answer questions | Verbal acknowledgment |
| 3 | **Verify ID** — Check government-issued photo ID for ALL participants | Record ID type, number, expiration |
| 4 | **Photograph participants** — Each person with their identification sheet | Photo must be suitable for identification |
| 5 | **Complete consent forms** — Each participant signs consent and chain of custody | Signatures required |
| 6 | **Prepare collection area** — Clean surface, organize materials | N/A |
| 7 | **Put on gloves** — Fresh gloves before touching collection materials | Change between participants |
| 8 | **Open collection kit** — In participant's presence | Verify kit is sealed |
| 9 | **Collect sample** — Buccal swabs per lab instructions (usually 4 swabs) | Observe entire collection |
| 10 | **Air dry swabs** — Brief drying before packaging | Prevents mold |
| 11 | **Label envelope** — Label DURING collection, not pre-labeled | One at a time |
| 12 | **Place swabs in envelope** — Paper envelope, NOT plastic | Plastic causes moisture damage |
| 13 | **Seal envelope** — Participant initials seal | Tamper-evident |
| 14 | **Repeat for each participant** — Change gloves between participants | Document each |
| 15 | **Complete paperwork** — All forms signed by participants AND collector | Review for completeness |
| 16 | **Package for shipping** — Sealed outer packaging | Tamper-evident |
| 17 | **Ship via tracked carrier** — FedEx, UPS with tracking | Keep tracking number |
| 18 | **Provide receipt** — Give participant copy of chain of custody | For their records |

### Buccal Swab Collection Technique

1. **Do not eat, drink, smoke, or chew gum** for 30 minutes before collection
2. **Rub swab firmly** against inside of cheek
3. **Rotate swab** to collect cells from multiple areas
4. **Duration:** 30-60 seconds per swab (per lab instructions)
5. **Number of swabs:** Usually 4 swabs per participant
6. **Air dry** swabs for 1-2 minutes before packaging
7. **Place in paper envelope** — NEVER back in plastic wrapper

## PARTICIPANT REQUIREMENTS

### Minors (Under 18)

| Age | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Under 18 | Legal guardian must be present and sign consent |
| Under 18 | Guardian's ID also verified and documented |
| Newborns | Collection typically at hospital before discharge |
| Infants | Use infant buccal swabs (smaller size) |

### Absent Participants

When alleged father or other participant cannot be present:
- **Coordinate with another collector** — Each participant collected separately
- **Same chain of custody standards** — Both collections meet legal requirements
- **Same laboratory** — Both samples sent to same lab
- **Documented connection** — Lab knows samples are related case

### Deceased Participants

- **Medical examiner specimens** — Tissue, blood card, bone
- **Stored samples** — Previously collected DNA
- **Personal items** — Toothbrush, razor (last resort, may not work)
- **Requires lab consultation** — Not all labs can process non-standard samples

## IMMIGRATION DNA TESTING (USCIS)

### Process

1. **Petitioner in US** requests DNA testing through USCIS
2. **USCIS approves** and specifies AABB-accredited lab
3. **Petitioner** tested at AABB-accredited facility in US
4. **Beneficiary** tested at US Embassy/Consulate approved facility abroad
5. **Lab analyzes** both samples
6. **Results sent directly** to USCIS or Embassy (not to participants)

### Requirements

- Lab MUST be AABB-accredited
- Collection in US follows standard legal chain of custody
- Collection abroad follows Embassy/Consulate procedures
- Results go directly to USCIS — not to family
- 99.5%+ probability required to establish relationship

### Common Immigration Relationships Tested

- Parent-child (paternity/maternity)
- Sibling (full or half)
- Grandparent-grandchild
- Aunt/Uncle-Niece/Nephew

## COMMON ERRORS — WHAT VOIDS A TEST

### Fatal Errors (Collection Rejected)

| Error | Why It's Fatal |
|---|---|
| **No collector signature** | Cannot verify who collected |
| **No participant signatures** | No consent documented |
| **Broken tamper-evident seal** | Chain of custody compromised |
| **Samples in plastic** | Mold growth, DNA degradation |
| **Pre-labeled envelopes** | Sample switching risk |
| **No photo ID verification** | Cannot confirm participant identity |
| **No photographs** | Cannot confirm participant identity |
| **Collector related to participant** | Conflict of interest |
| **Samples left with participant** | Chain of custody broken |

### Correctable Issues

| Issue | Correction |
|---|---|
| Incomplete paperwork | Lab contacts collector to complete |
| Missing collection date | Lab contacts collector |
| Insufficient sample | Request recollection |

## QUICK REFERENCE — LEGAL VS INFORMATIONAL

**LEGAL (Court-Admissible):**
- ✅ Third-party collector required
- ✅ Photo ID verified
- ✅ Photographs taken
- ✅ Full chain of custody
- ✅ AABB-accredited lab
- ✅ Tamper-evident seals
- ✅ Tracked shipping
- ✅ Admissible in court

**INFORMATIONAL (Peace of Mind):**
- ❌ Can self-collect at home
- ❌ No ID verification required
- ❌ No chain of custody
- ❌ Not court-admissible
- ❌ Not accepted by USCIS
- ✅ Faster turnaround
- ✅ Lower cost
