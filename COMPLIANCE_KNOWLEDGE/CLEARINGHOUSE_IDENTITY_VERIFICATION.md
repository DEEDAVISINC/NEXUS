# FMCSA Clearinghouse — Identity Verification (Existing Users)

**NEXUS knowledge — not buyer-facing.** Source of truth for FMCSA Clearinghouse ID verification requirement (June 2026).

**Source:** FMCSA Clearinghouse News — posted June 1, 2026  
**URL:** https://clearinghouse.fmcsa.dot.gov (Learn → Expanding Identity Verification: Existing Users)  
**Applies to:** Dee Davis Inc. — registered **Consortium/Third-Party Administrator (C/TPA)**

---

## Deadline

**July 6, 2026**

After this date, affected users **cannot access** their Clearinghouse accounts until identity verification is successfully completed.

---

## Who must complete (DDI = C/TPA ✅)

| Role | Required? |
|------|-----------|
| **Consortium/Third-Party Administrator (C/TPA)** | **YES — DDI** |
| Medical Review Officer (MRO) | YES (if separate account) |
| Substance Abuse Professional (SAP) | YES (if separate account) |
| Employer **without** FMCSA Portal–linked account | YES |
| Assistants invited under another registered entity | YES |
| Employer **linked to FMCSA Portal account** | NO — verified during DOT registration |
| Driver accounts | NO — verified via State systems |

---

## Exact steps (official)

1. **Log in** to the Clearinghouse: https://clearinghouse.fmcsa.dot.gov  
2. Go to **My Dashboard → My Profile**  
3. In the message at the top of the screen, click **Begin Identity Verification**  
4. Follow the on-screen instructions  
5. Optional: download **Clearinghouse Identity Verification job aid** from the Learning Center for step-by-step screenshots

---

## Why FMCSA is doing this

Expanded from April 2026 (new registrants only) to **all existing** C/TPAs, MROs, SAPs, and certain employers — fraud prevention; ensure only authorized users manage safety-sensitive data.
