## CARD 2: POST-ACCIDENT TESTING DECISION TREE

### Step 1: Was there a fatality?
```
YES → TEST REQUIRED (drug + alcohol)
NO  → Go to Step 2
```

### Step 2: Was a citation issued to the DOT-regulated driver?
```
NO  → NO TEST REQUIRED
YES → Go to Step 3
```

### Step 3: Was there bodily injury requiring immediate medical treatment away from the scene?
```
YES → TEST REQUIRED (drug + alcohol)
NO  → Go to Step 4
```

### Step 4: Was any vehicle towed from the scene due to disabling damage?
```
YES → TEST REQUIRED (drug + alcohol)
NO  → NO TEST REQUIRED
```

### Testing Timeframes

| Test | Must Complete Within | Best Practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Alcohol** | 8 hours | Within 2 hours |
| **Drug** | 32 hours | As soon as possible |

### If Timeframe is Missed

- **Document why** the test was not performed
- **Do NOT test** after the window closes
- Keep written record of circumstances

## CARD 3: SHY BLADDER PROTOCOL

### Definition
Donor cannot provide 45mL of urine within a reasonable time.

### Procedure

| Step | Action | Notes |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Discard** the insufficient specimen | Do not save partial specimens |
| 2 | **Start 3-hour clock** | Document exact start time |
| 3 | **Offer fluids** | Up to 40 oz over 3 hours |
| 4 | **Donor remains at site** | Cannot leave (unless emergency) |
| 5 | **Attempt collections** | Donor may try at any time during window |
| 6 | **After 3 hours** | If still insufficient → stop |
| 7 | **Notify employer** | Employer refers for medical evaluation |
| 8 | **Medical evaluation** | Physician determines if legitimate cause |
| 9 | **Determination** | No legitimate cause = REFUSAL |

### Key Rules

- **DO NOT** combine specimens from multiple attempts
- **DO NOT** allow observed collection as substitute for shy bladder
- **DO NOT** allow donor to leave site
- **DOCUMENT** everything — start time, fluid offered, attempts

## CARD 4: TEMPERATURE OUT OF RANGE

### Acceptable Range
**90°F - 100°F (32°C - 38°C)** within 4 minutes of collection

### If Out of Range

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Document actual temperature on CCF |
| 2 | Ask donor if they have an explanation |
| 3 | Note donor's response |
| 4 | **Immediately conduct second collection** |
| 5 | Second collection MUST be **directly observed** |
| 6 | Use NEW CCF for observed collection |
| 7 | Send BOTH specimens to laboratory |
| 8 | Lab tests both independently |

### Common Causes

| Out of Range | Possible Cause |
|---|---|
| Too cold (<90°F) | Substituted specimen |
| Too hot (>100°F) | Specimen was heated |

## CARD 5: DILUTE SPECIMEN HANDLING

### What is Dilute?
- Creatinine 2-20 mg/dL AND
- Specific gravity 1.0010-1.0030

### Result Types

| Lab Result | MRO Reports | Employer Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Negative-Dilute** | Negative-Dilute | May order immediate retest |
| **Positive-Dilute** | Positive | Verified positive — no retest |

### Employer Options for Negative-Dilute

1. **Accept as negative** — No further action
2. **Order immediate retest** — Employer policy determines
3. **Retest is NOT observed** unless other criteria met

### Policy Recommendation
Create written policy on how to handle negative-dilute results BEFORE they occur.

## CARD 6: OBSERVED COLLECTION REQUIREMENTS

### REGULATORY UPDATE — Effective June 10, 2026
**Final Rule published May 11, 2026 (Federal Register)**

Two changes to 49 CFR Part 40:

1. **Oral Fluid Unavailability → Directly Observed Urine Required**
When a situation requires oral fluid testing but the collection site cannot perform oral fluid collection, the employer/C/TPA must default to a **directly observed urine collection**. No exceptions. This closes the gap where oral fluid was authorized but sites hadn't implemented it yet.

2. **Terminology Update per Executive Order 14168**
Observer requirements now use **biological sex** definitions consistent with E.O. 14168 ("Defending Women from Gender Ideology Extremism and Restoring Biological Truth to the Federal Government"). All observer assignment documentation and collector training must reflect updated terminology by June 10, 2026.

**DDI C/TPA Action Items:**
- [ ] Verify Quest/collection site oral fluid capability status
- [ ] Update collector guidance: if oral fluid unavailable → directly observed urine
- [ ] Update observer assignment language to reflect biological sex terminology
- [ ] Update client notification templates before June 10, 2026
- [ ] Reference: https://www.transportation.gov/odapc/frpubs

---

### When Observation is REQUIRED

| Situation | Observation Required? |
|---|---|
| Temperature out of range | YES |
| Previous substituted/adulterated result | YES |
| Return-to-duty test | YES |
| Follow-up test | YES |
| Collector observes suspicious conduct | YES |
| MRO directs observation | YES |
| Employer directs (follow-up) | YES |
| Oral fluid required but unavailable at site | YES (directly observed urine — effective 6/10/2026) |
| Random, pre-employment (routine) | NO |

### Observer Requirements

- **Same biological sex** as donor (updated per E.O. 14168, effective 6/10/2026)
- **Trained collector** (may be different person)
- Must **watch urine leave body** into collection container
- Must have **unobstructed view**
- Document on CCF remarks section

### Donor Refuses Observation
- If required observation is refused = **REFUSAL TO TEST**
- Refusal = treated as positive result

## CARD 7: ALCOHOL TEST RESULTS

### Screening Test Results

| BAC Level | Result | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **<0.02** | Negative | Test complete |
| **≥0.02** | Screen positive | Proceed to confirmation |

### Confirmation Test Results

| BAC Level | Result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **<0.02** | Negative | Return to duty |
| **0.02 - 0.039** | Positive (low) | Remove from safety-sensitive duties 8-24 hours |
| **≥0.04** | Positive (violation) | Immediate removal, SAP evaluation required |

### 15-Minute Wait Rule

Between screening and confirmation:
- **Minimum 15 minutes** wait required
- **Maximum 30 minutes** recommended
- Donor cannot eat, drink, smoke, or put anything in mouth
- If donor does → note on ATF, may affect result

## CARD 8: WHAT IS A REFUSAL?

### Refusal = Same as Positive

**All of these count as REFUSAL:**

| Conduct | Refusal? |
|---|---|
| Fails to appear for test (reasonable time) | YES |
| Leaves collection site before test complete | YES |
| Fails to provide urine specimen | YES |
| Fails to permit observed collection (when required) | YES |
| Shy bladder + no legitimate medical explanation | YES |
| Refuses to take additional test when directed | YES |
| Refuses medical evaluation (shy bladder/insufficient breath) | YES |
| Fails to cooperate with testing process | YES |
| Uses prosthetic device or adulterant | YES |
| Admits specimen was adulterated/substituted | YES |
| Verified adulterated or substituted result | YES |

### Consequences
- Treated same as positive test
- Immediate removal from safety-sensitive duties
- SAP evaluation required before return
- Reported to FMCSA Clearinghouse (if applicable)

## CARD 9: RETURN-TO-DUTY PROCESS

### After Positive Test or Refusal

| Step | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employer | Remove from safety-sensitive duties |
| 2 | Employer | Refer to SAP |
| 3 | SAP | Conduct face-to-face evaluation |
| 4 | SAP | Prescribe education and/or treatment |
| 5 | Employee | Complete prescribed program |
| 6 | SAP | Conduct follow-up evaluation |
| 7 | SAP | Issue return-to-duty recommendation |
| 8 | Employer | Schedule return-to-duty test |
| 9 | Collector | **Directly observed** collection |
| 10 | Lab/MRO | Negative result required |
| 11 | Employer | May return to duty |
| 12 | C/TPA | Begin follow-up testing plan |

### Follow-up Testing Requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|---|---|
| First 12 months | At least 6 tests |
| Maximum duration | Up to 60 months |
| Observation | All follow-up tests are observed |
| Scheduling | Unannounced |

## CARD 12: CCF FORM ERRORS

### Fatal Flaws (Test CANCELLED)

| Error | Can Be Fixed? |
|---|---|

| No donor signature (not corrected) | NO → Cancel |
| Specimen ID doesn't match CCF | NO → Cancel |

| Insufficient specimen volume | NO → Cancel |

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| Missing employer phone | MRO obtains |
| Transposed digits | MRO verifies |
| Missing employer address | MRO obtains |

### Best Practice
- **Review CCF before donor leaves**
- **Double-check specimen ID matches**
- **Ensure all signatures present**
- **Verify seal integrity**

## CARD 13: DOT vs NON-DOT TESTING

### Key Differences

| Aspect | DOT | Non-DOT |
|---|---|---|
| Drug panel | 5 drugs (standardized) | Employer chooses |
| Cutoff levels | Federal standard | Varies |
| Collection procedures | 49 CFR Part 40 | Employer policy |
| CCF form | Federal form required | Varies |
| MRO required | YES | Often (not always) |
| Split specimen | Required | Optional |
| Lab certification | SAMHSA required | May vary |
| Observer gender | Same as donor | May vary |

### CRITICAL RULE
**DOT and Non-DOT tests CANNOT be combined.**
- Separate collection
- Separate CCF
- Separate specimens
- Separate reporting

### If Employer Wants Both
- Complete DOT test first
- Then complete non-DOT test
- Two separate processes

## CARD 15: MRO MARIJUANA VERIFICATION — NO EXCEPTIONS

**Source:** ODAPC Q&A published May 15, 2026 | 49 CFR Part 40

### The Rule

**There is NO legitimate medical explanation for a marijuana positive on a DOT test.**

MRO MUST verify as POSITIVE — regardless of:

| Excuse Given | Valid? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| State medical marijuana card | ❌ NO | Federal law applies — marijuana is Schedule I |
| State recreational legalization | ❌ NO | DOT is federal — state law doesn't matter |
| Foreign country prescription | ❌ NO | Even legally obtained abroad — NO |
| "It was CBD" | ❌ NO | If it tested positive for THC, it's positive |
| "I didn't know it had THC" | ❌ NO | Not a legitimate medical explanation |
| Marinol (synthetic THC prescription) | ⚠️ MAYBE | MRO must verify valid Rx + appropriate use |
| Hemp product claim | ❌ NO | If positive, it's positive |

### Key Citation

> *"Marijuana can never constitute a legitimate medical explanation, even if obtained legally in a foreign country, as it cannot be viewed as having a legitimate medical use."* — 49 CFR § 40.137

### Rescheduling Status (As of May 2026)

| Status | Details |
|---|---|
| Executive Order | Dec 18, 2025 — directed rescheduling Schedule I → III |
| Current Status | **NOT complete** — marijuana STILL Schedule I |
| DOT Testing | **NO CHANGE** — rules remain the same |
| MRO Guidance | Continue following 49 CFR Part 40 |

### What to Tell Employers/Donors

*"DOT drug testing is governed by federal law. Marijuana remains a Schedule I controlled substance under federal law. Regardless of state medical marijuana programs or recreational legalization, a confirmed positive marijuana result on a DOT test will be verified as positive by the MRO. There is no legitimate medical explanation for marijuana under DOT regulations. This has not changed with rescheduling discussions — until the process is complete and DOT updates its regulations, the rules remain the same."*

### Bottom Line

- **Positive THC = Positive result**
- **No medical marijuana exception**
- **No state law exception**
- **No foreign prescription exception**
- **Rescheduling not complete — rules unchanged**
