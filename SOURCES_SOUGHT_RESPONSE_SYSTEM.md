---

## 📋 WHAT IS A SOURCES SOUGHT?

**Type:** Pre-Solicitation / Market Research  
**Purpose:** Government is asking "Who can provide this product/service?"  
**Not a bid:** Just expressing interest and capability  
**Timeline:** 7-30 days to respond  
**Result:** You get on their vendor list for the actual RFP

**Example:**
> "The Department of Defense is conducting market research to identify potential sources for cable assemblies. Interested vendors should submit capability statements."

---

## ✅ WHEN TO RESPOND:

**YES - Respond to these:**
- ✅ WOSB/EDWOSB set-asides
- ✅ Products you can source easily
- ✅ Services you can subcontract
- ✅ Vehicle procurement (wholesale dealer partnerships)
- ✅ Federal agencies (DOD, DLA, GSA, VA, etc.)
- ✅ Contract values $50K+

**MAYBE - Consider these:**
- 🤔 All small business set-asides
- 🤔 Products requiring specialized sourcing
- 🤔 Multi-year contracts
- 🤔 BPAs (Blanket Purchase Agreements)

**NO - Skip these:**
- ❌ Sole source (already awarded)
- ❌ Large business set-aside
- ❌ Highly technical/specialized (unless you have capability)
- ❌ Services you can't subcontract

## 🔍 HOW TO FIND SOURCES SOUGHT OPPORTUNITIES:

### **SAM.gov Search:**

1. Go to https://sam.gov/search/
2. Click "Contract Opportunities"
3. **Filters to set:**
   - **Type:** "p" - Presolicitation
   - **Type:** "r" - Sources Sought
   - **Set-Aside:** "WOSB" or "EDWOSB" or "Small Business"
   - **Posted Date:** Last 7 days
   - **Active Only:** Yes
4. Click "Search"

### **Daily Monitoring:**

**Monday - Friday (15 min/day):**
- [ ] Search SAM.gov for new sources sought
- [ ] Filter by WOSB/EDWOSB set-asides
- [ ] Review requirements
- [ ] If good fit → Respond same day or within 48 hours
