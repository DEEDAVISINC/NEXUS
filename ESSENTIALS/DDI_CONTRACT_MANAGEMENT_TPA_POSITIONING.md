# DDI — Contract Management vs. TPA (Reference)

**Purpose:** One place for NEXUS and outreach to reuse accurate language.  
**Owner:** Dee Davis Inc.  
**Not legal advice** — verify any contract-specific claim with counsel.

**Core distinction**

- **Insurance “third party administrator” (state license):** Claims adjudication / insured plan administration for carriers or self-funded plans — **not** DDI’s primary lane unless a deal explicitly requires it.
- **DOT drug & alcohol “C/TPA”:** Consortium/Third Party Administrator under **49 CFR Part 40** — federal framework, **not** the same as state insurance TPA licensure.
- **Medicaid NEMT:** Michigan **CHAMPS** enrollment + **MCO** credentialing/contracts — **transportation broker / coordinator** role, not insurance claims TPA.

---

## MCO / Medicaid payer (network contracting, NEMT)

Dee Davis Inc. is the **prime**: we **hold the contract**, **credential and manage** transportation partners, and **meet plan reporting and compliance**—we’re not asking to adjudicate your insured benefits as an insurance TPA. For **NEMT**, we’re **CHAMPS-enrolled** in Michigan (**Provider ID 6309049**, **NPI 1538939111**, **Transportation Broker** taxonomy) and we pursue **formal MCO credentialing** so billing and operations match **your** plan rules.

---

## Government CO / procurement (RFP, SAM, agency buyer)

Dee Davis Inc. proposes as **prime contractor and contract manager**: we **own performance risk**, **coordinate qualified subcontractors**, and deliver **reporting, QA, and compliance** under **your** contract. Where drug-and-alcohol testing is in scope, we operate consistent with **DOT Part 40** as a **C/TPA** where applicable—that is **federal program administration**, not state **insurance** third-party administration. Our role is **execution and oversight**, not insurance claims administration.

---

## Harris Health / county health system (Bonfire, employee health screening)

Dee Davis Inc. operates as **contract manager and prime**: we **coordinate** SAMHSA-certified lab and clinical partners, **standardize scheduling and collection**, and **manage** quality and compliance **end-to-end** for your program. We are **not** positioning as a licensed **insurance** third-party administrator; we are your **vendor-of-record** for **occupational health screening services** under the solicitation, with **transparent pricing** and **single-point accountability**.
