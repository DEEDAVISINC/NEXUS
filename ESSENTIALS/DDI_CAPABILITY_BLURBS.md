## USAGE RULES

1. **Always swap in the agency name** — never send a generic blurb. Replace "[Agency]" with the actual name.
2. **Mirror the RFP language** — after pulling the blurb, scan the solicitation for their exact terminology and replace generic terms with their words.
3. **Short blurb** = emails, RFI responses, brief intro paragraphs
4. **Medium blurb** = cap statement overview section, proposal company overview
5. **Differentiator line** = "Why Choose DDI" section or closing paragraph of buyer email
6. **Never use more than one blurb per document** — pick the one that matches the primary service being sold
