**What It Is:**
- Federal employment eligibility verification system

- Confirms employees are authorized to work in the U.S.

**Why It Matters:**
- ✅ **REQUIRED** for most federal contracts over $150K
- ✅ **PREFERRED** by all federal agencies
- ✅ Shows compliance with immigration laws
- ✅ Reduces federal contractor liability
- ✅ Demonstrates operational legitimacy

**Competitive Advantage:**
- Many small businesses are NOT E-Verify certified
- Federal buyers prefer/require E-Verify contractors
- Shows you're "federal-ready"
- Required for DOD, DHS, and most federal work
