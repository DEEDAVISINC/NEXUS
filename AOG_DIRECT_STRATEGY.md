## WHAT IS AOG

**AOG = Aircraft On Ground**
- A plane is grounded and needs a part immediately
- Every hour grounded = lost revenue ($10K-$150K+/hour depending on aircraft)
- Time-critical, premium-rate work
- Requires secure facility access (airports, MRO hangars)

**Why airlines/ports pay premium:**
- Speed matters more than price
- Reliability is non-negotiable
- TWIC/security clearance required
- 24/7 availability expected

## DDI TPA MODEL — HOW IT WORKS

```
AIRLINE / PORT / MRO
         ↓
    DDI (TPA / Coordinator)
    - Holds the contract
    - Receives AOG dispatch requests
    - Verifies carrier availability + credentials
    - Dispatches from Freight 1st Direct network
    - Tracks delivery via PRISM

    - Provides 24/7 availability
```

## TWIC REQUIREMENT

**TWIC = Transportation Worker Identification Credential**
- TSA-issued security credential
- Required for unescorted access to secure areas of airports, ports, vessels
- Valid for 5 years
- Background check + fingerprints

**DDI's TWIC Standard:**
- ALL carriers dispatched to airports/ports must have TWIC-certified drivers
- DDI verifies TWIC status during FleetFlow onboarding
- DDI maintains TWIC expiration tracking for all network drivers

**This is DDI's competitive advantage** — most small courier companies can't guarantee TWIC-certified drivers. DDI can.

## PRICING MODEL

### AOG Industry Benchmarks

| Service Type | Rate Range | When Used |
|-------------|------------|-----------|
| Standard AOG (same-day, 4-8 hrs) | $150-$400 | Part needed today, not emergency |
| Hot AOG (2-4 hrs) | $300-$600 | Plane grounded, crew waiting |
| Critical AOG (<2 hrs) | $500-$1,500+ | Revenue flight delayed, passengers waiting |
| After-hours / Weekend | 1.5x-2x base | Outside business hours |
| Airport-to-airport transfer | $200-$500 | Cross-field or regional |

### DDI Margin Model

| Component | % of Invoice |
|-----------|--------------|
| Subcontractor payout | 60-70% |
| DDI coordination fee | 30-40% |

**Example:**
- Airline pays DDI $500 for hot AOG run
- DDI pays carrier $325 (65%)
- DDI keeps $175 (35%)

**Higher margin than standard freight because:**
- Time-critical = price-insensitive buyers
- TWIC requirement = limited competition
- 24/7 availability = premium for always-on service

## RISKS AND MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Airlines have existing AOG vendors | Position DDI as backup / overflow / diversity supplier |
| Carriers not TWIC-certified | Verify before adding to AOG-eligible roster; require TWIC for airport work |

| Price competition from asset-based couriers | Compete on compliance, diversity, coordination — not just price |
