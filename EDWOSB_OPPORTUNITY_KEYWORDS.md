## 💡 QUICK ASSESSMENT CRITERIA

**When you find an opportunity, ask these 5 questions:**

1. **Is it a product or simple service?**
   - Product = YES (your strength)
   - Simple service (cleaning, transport) = YES (can sub)
   - Complex service (IT, engineering) = NO

2. **Is deadline 7+ days away?**
   - Yes = Pursue
   - No = Only if EDWOSB and very attractive

3. **Can you source/deliver/perform?**
   - Product: Can you find supplier? (Grainger, McMaster, etc.)
   - Service: Can you find subcontractor in that location?

4. **Is set-aside EDWOSB or WOSB?**
   - EDWOSB = HIGH PRIORITY (pursue even if challenging)
   - WOSB = MEDIUM PRIORITY (good opportunity)
   - SBA = LOW PRIORITY (only if perfect fit)

5. **Is contract value worth it?**
   - Even small EDWOSB = Good (builds track record)
   - Large EDWOSB = Excellent
   - Tiny SBA = Skip

**If 4+ YES answers = Add to NEXUS and assess further!**
