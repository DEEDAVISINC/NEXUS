## THE IDEA (Dee)

Reach out to **lenders and loan fulfillment partners** — not to sell notary dispatch first — but to support borrowers who **need in-person help** because they **can't or don't want to complete the loan digitally**.

**Scope is the whole file, not just the closing:**
1. **Application / intake** — portal login, account creation, form completion  
2. **Document collection** — ID, pay stubs, bank statements, uploads  
3. **Disclosures & e-sign** — walkthrough when borrower abandons online  
4. **Scheduling & status** — appointment booking, email/SMS verification  
5. **Closing table** — same navigation support alongside the signing  
6. **Post-close (optional)** — servicer portal, occupancy, benefit cross-enrollment  

**Product types:** Purchase, refi, HELOC, reverse/HECM, FHA, VA, construction, renovation — **any product** where digital-first origination creates fallout for in-person-preference borrowers.

| What signing networks sell | What DDI sells |
|---------------------------|--------------|
| Credentialed notary at the table | **Borrower completes the file** — from application through funding |
| Coverage map + price per signing | **Fallout reduction + digital completion rate** |
| Closing-only | **Origination-adjacent navigation** (without being LO or processor) |

**Borrower profile (not only seniors):**
- Elderly / fixed income  
- Low digital literacy  
- Rural / limited broadband  
- Disability / assistive-tech barriers  
- Language / literacy barriers  
- **Preference** — wants a person, not a portal

## PROBLEM LENDERS ACTUALLY HAVE

| Stage | Friction | Lender cost |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Apply** | Abandoned online apps, bad data entry | Lost leads, LO rework |
| **Intake** | Can't upload docs, wrong file format | Processor delays |
| **Disclosures** | E-sign timeout, identity verification fail | TRID / compliance risk |
| **Pre-close** | Missed appointments, unopened emails | Lock extensions |
| **Close** | Table confusion, ID/Patriot Act errors | Funding delays, rescission issues |
| **Post-close** | Servicer portal never activated | Early delinquency, call volume |

**Pain metric lenders understand:** **cost per funded loan** and **pull-through rate** — not "we need another notary."

## DDI OFFER (POSITIONING)

**Product name (working):** **3D Ink In-Person Borrower Support**  
**Alt names:** Borrower Application-to-Close Navigation · Digital Completion TPA  

**Tagline:** *From first login to final signature — in-person support for borrowers who won't self-serve online.*

**What DDI delivers (TPA-managed):**

| Phase | Service |
|-------|---------|
| **Application assist** | In-person or scheduled video: portal setup, form completion, document checklist |
| **Document intake** | Help gather/upload ID, income, assets; fix common reject reasons |
| **Disclosure walkthrough** | E-sign support before or instead of abandoning the flow |
| **Closing navigation** | Table-side digital steps + notary/signing when licensed act required |
| **Post-close (optional)** | 30-day navigator: servicer login, occupancy cert, MI Bridges / SDOH where relevant |

**Delivery modes:**
- Mobile — borrower's home (signing table model)  
- Community partner site — library, senior center, CWC location  
- Lender branch — by appointment  
- Phone/video — when in-person isn't required but hand-holding is  

**Managed under DDI:** One contract · one invoice · session documentation · EDWOSB prime.

**What this is NOT:**
- ❌ Loan origination, underwriting, or lending advice  
- ❌ Replacement for HUD counseling (HECM) or attorney where required  
- ❌ Replacing the lender's LOS or signing network on day one  

**Teaming:** **CWC (501c3)** for grant-funded or community-site navigation; **DDI** holds lender MSA + billing.
