## WHO IS GTCLBA

The Grand Traverse County Land Bank Authority manages tax-reverted, foreclosed, 
and abandoned properties in Grand Traverse County (Traverse City, MI area). 
They facilitate property rehab, demolition coordination, land sales, and 
neighborhood stabilization programs funded by the State of Michigan and 
HUD/CDBG grants. Contracting needs include:
- Property maintenance and grounds management (mowing, boarding, cleanup)
- Contractor screening (drug testing, background checks, credentialing)
- Program compliance and reporting (HUD, CDBG, Michigan MSHDA regulations)
- Staffing for administrative and field coordination roles
- Notary and document services (deed transfers, closings)
- Logistics for materials and equipment coordination

**Note:** Grand Traverse County is in Northern Michigan — different market than Detroit.
DDI's strength here is the statewide contract management platform, not just local presence.
