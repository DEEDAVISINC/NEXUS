## 1. Two lanes — different buyers, same DDI capabilities

| Lane | What they’re buying | Typical buyer functions (titles vary) | What to prove |
|------|---------------------|----------------------------------------|---------------|
| **A. Lab / specimen / medical courier** | STAT or routed pickup of specimens, supplies, or medical material between sites; often **chain of custody**, temp control, HIPAA | **Lab / ancillary services**, **network development**, **provider contracting** (specialty), sometimes **operations** | CLIA-adjacent process, DOT/MC, insurance, SOPs, SLA windows, after-hours coverage |
| **B. Pharmacy / home delivery** | Member-facing **medication delivery** (retail, specialty, mail-order partner); **cold chain** may apply | **Pharmacy**, **PBM interface**, **specialty pharmacy**, **care management** (sometimes **NEMT** if bundled with benefits) | State pharmacy rules, cold chain, HIPAA, delivery proof, escalation path |

**DDI message:** Same prime contractor model — **you** hold the MCO contract; **subs** execute routes; compliance and reporting sit with DDI.
