### THE PROBLEM DDI SOLVES (60 seconds)

```
"Here's what I keep hearing from employers and government agencies with 
existing drug testing programs:

One — coverage gaps. Their TPA has one lab network. If the nearest site 
is 45 minutes from the donor, that's the employer's problem.

Two — post-accident and reasonable suspicion urgency. It's 9 PM on a 
Friday, there's been an accident, and the TPA says 'find the nearest 
Quest — hope they're open.'

Three — and this is the big one — impaired donor transportation. DOT 
best practices say employees under reasonable suspicion should NOT drive 
themselves to the collection site. But most TPAs don't address that. 
They just say 'here's the nearest site' and leave the transportation 
problem to the employer. If that employee gets behind the wheel and 
something happens on the way, the employer now has a liability exposure 
they could have avoided."
```

### DDI'S THREE-TIER RESPONSE MODEL (the differentiator — 3 minutes)

```
"DDI built a three-tier response model specifically to solve these 
problems:"
```

**TIER 1 — MOBILE COLLECTOR DISPATCH**

```
"For urgent situations — post-accident, remote locations, after-hours — 
we dispatch a certified collector directly to the donor's location. The 
donor never leaves the scene. The test happens on-site. DOT post-accident 
requires testing within 32 hours for drugs and 8 hours for alcohol — we 
don't leave that to chance."
```

**TIER 2 — SECURE DONOR TRANSPORTATION**

```
"For reasonable suspicion situations — when a supervisor believes 
someone may be impaired — we don't tell the donor to drive themselves. 
We send a GPS-tracked ride through our NEMT network directly to their 
location. The donor is picked up, transported to the nearest collection 
site, and the entire ride is time-stamped and documented — pickup, 
drop-off, chain of movement.

The employer's liability exposure is significantly reduced. The donor 
never gets behind the wheel. And the ride record becomes part of the 
compliance file. This is a capability most TPAs don't include in their 
program — we built it because it solves a real problem that employers 
are already supposed to handle but rarely have a solution for."
```

**TIER 3 — MOBILE MASS COLLECTION EVENTS**

```
"For volume testing — annual random programs, pre-employment sweeps, 
athletics testing — we bring a collection team directly to the member's 
location.

A school district with 150 student-athletes doesn't send kids to Quest 
one at a time. We show up on pre-season day with a collection team, 
process everyone in one afternoon, results back in 24-48 hours for 
competition clearance.

A transit authority with 300 bus drivers doesn't schedule 75 individual 
appointments. We set up at the bus terminal, rotate drivers through 
collection stations, and the district's annual DOT compliance is handled 
in a single day."
```
