Dee Davis Inc. (DDI) is a Michigan-based, EDWOSB-certified contract management firm specializing in drug and alcohol testing, occupational screening, and credentialing services for government agencies, school districts, and public sector organizations nationwide. Through our SAMHSA-certified collection network and established testing laboratory partnerships, DDI delivers DOT and non-DOT testing programs, breath alcohol testing (BAT), hair and oral fluid testing, random selection management, and MRO review services — all with the compliance rigor and reporting transparency that Choice Partners members require.

CORE COMPETENCIES — PROVEN PERFORMANCE ACROSS REGULATED SECTORS

★ DOT Drug & Alcohol Testing

Fully compliant 49 CFR Part 40 testing programs including pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, return-to-duty, and follow-up testing.

Non-DOT Workplace Testing

Customized panel configurations (5, 7, 10, 12-panel) for employee screening. Urine, hair follicle, oral fluid, and extended opioid panels available.

★ Breath Alcohol Testing (BAT)

Evidential breath testing (EBT) by certified BATs. DOT and non-DOT protocols. Same-day results with chain of custody documentation.

Random Selection Management

Computerized random selection pools compliant with DOT 50%/10% and client-defined rates. Notification, tracking, and compliance reporting included.

MRO Review Services

Licensed Medical Review Officer services for all positive, adulterated, substituted, and invalid results. Full SAMHSA/DOT compliance.

Athletics Testing Programs

Customized testing programs for K-12 and collegiate athletics. Steroid panels, designer drug testing, and student-athlete education components.

Collection Site Network

Nationwide SAMHSA-certified collection sites. On-site mobile collection available. Electronic chain of custody (eCCF) and rapid result reporting.

Program Management & Reporting

Turn-key TPA services: policy development, supervisor training, employee education, audit-ready compliance reporting, and dedicated account management.
