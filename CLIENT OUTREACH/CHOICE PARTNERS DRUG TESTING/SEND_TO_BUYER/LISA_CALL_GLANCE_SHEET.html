DDI's Three-Tier Response Model (3 min)

Tier 1 — Mobile Collector Dispatch

Urgent / post-accident / after-hours — dispatch a certified collector directly to the donor. Test happens on-site. DOT: 32 hrs drugs, 8 hrs alcohol.

Tier 2 — Secure Donor Transportation

Reasonable suspicion — GPS-tracked ride via NEMT network. Donor never drives. Pickup, drop-off, chain of movement all time-stamped and documented.

Tier 3 — Mass Collection Events

Volume testing — randoms, pre-employment, athletics. Collection team dispatched to the member's location. 150 student-athletes processed in one afternoon.
