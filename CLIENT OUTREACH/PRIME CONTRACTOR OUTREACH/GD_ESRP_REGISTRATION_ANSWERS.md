### Accident/incident reporting — process flow?
**YES**

DDI's incident reporting follows a defined process: (1) Field personnel or subcontractor reports incident immediately to DDI operations, (2) DDI documents incident details including date, time, location, personnel involved, and nature of incident, (3) DDI notifies the contracting officer or client per contract requirements, (4) DDI initiates corrective action and root cause review, (5) DDI files final incident report with resolution and preventive measures. All reports are retained per federal recordkeeping requirements.
