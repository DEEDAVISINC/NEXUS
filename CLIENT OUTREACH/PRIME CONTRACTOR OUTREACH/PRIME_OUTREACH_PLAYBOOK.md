| Category | Typical Goal | DDI Qualifies? |

| Woman-Owned (WOSB) | 5%+ | YES |
| Economically Disadvantaged WOSB | 5%+ | YES |

**If they don't meet these goals, they get dinged on CPARS and risk losing future contracts.**
