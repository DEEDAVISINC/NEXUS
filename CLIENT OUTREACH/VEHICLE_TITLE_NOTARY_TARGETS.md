## WHY THIS LANE FITS DDI

Vehicle title work is **volume + geography + compliance** — exactly what a contract management TPA is built for:

- Dealers, fleets, lenders, and title processors need signers **in every state**, not in one county.
- Rules differ by state; clients want **one contract, one invoice, one ops desk**.

**Michigan HQ is proof of operational discipline — not a service boundary.**

## WHEN CAR / VEHICLE TITLES NEED NOTARY

| Document / situation | Typical notary need |
|---|---|
| Title assignment (seller signature) | **Required in many states** — check destination state DMV rules |
| Power of Attorney for motor vehicle (state POA forms) | **Often required** |
| Odometer disclosure / affidavit | **Sometimes required** with notary |
| Lien release packages | **Often required** for lender/dealer processing |
| Duplicate / bonded / salvage title corrections | **Frequent notary on supporting affidavits** |
| Out-of-state buyer/seller (interstate deal) | **High demand** — title companies need local signers |
| Fleet bulk transfers | **Recurring B2B volume** |

**Note:** Michigan in-state private-party title transfer often uses seller signature on title without notary on the title itself — but **POA, interstate, fleet, and lender workflows still drive notary demand nationally.**
