Operating as a transportation broker and logistics coordinator, we manage comprehensive shuttle operations, campus transportation programs, and specialized event transport services.

We deliver scalable, compliant shuttle services without the overhead of fleet ownership.

🚌 NIST SHUTTLE SERVICES ALIGNMENT

Service Delivery Model for NIST Gaithersburg Campus

  • METRO Shuttle Services: 36/40-passenger bus operations between NIST Building 101 and Shady Grove Metro station with optional Visitor Center routing
  • MARC Shuttle Services: 15-passenger van or 20-passenger mini-bus operations connecting Building 101 to Metropolitan Grove and Gaithersburg MARC stations with scheduled AM/PM runs
  • SURF Program Transportation: 55-passenger coach bus services for summer student transportation between local hotels and NIST laboratory buildings (May-August operations)
  • NCNR Summer School Support: Dedicated 55-passenger coach transportation for NIST Center for Neutron Research educational program attendees
  • Cybersecurity Conference Logistics: Multi-bus coordination (six 55-passenger coaches) for 700+ conference attendees across three hotel locations
  • General Transportation Services: Flexible fleet deployment (57-passenger bus to 6-passenger sedan) for ad-hoc campus transportation needs with 3-day advance notice capability
