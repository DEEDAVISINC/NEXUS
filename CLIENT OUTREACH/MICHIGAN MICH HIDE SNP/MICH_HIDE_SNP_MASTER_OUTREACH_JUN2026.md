## SECTION 1 — PROGRAM OVERVIEW: MICHIGAN MICH HIDE SNP

**HIDE SNP** = Highly Integrated Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan

A HIDE SNP is a Medicare Advantage plan designed for people dually eligible for both Medicare and Medicaid. One plan, one payer — for eligibility, claims, appeals, and grievances.

**Michigan's Program: MI Coordinated Health (MICH)**
Effective January 1, 2026, Michigan's MI Health Link (MHL) program transitioned to MICH. MICH covers all Medicare and most Medicaid benefits including long-term services and supports (LTSS). Members have no co-pays or deductibles for in-network services. Every MICH member receives a Care Coordinator.

**Eligibility:** Members must be 21+, enrolled in both Medicare and full Medicaid, living in an eligible Michigan county.

**Total dual-eligible population in Michigan:** 300,000+

**Rollout:** Regions 1, 8, and 10 launched January 1, 2026. Statewide expansion January 1, 2027.

## SECTION 5 — DDI'S THREE MICH PROGRAM BRANDS

### VITAL — Healthcare Logistics TPA
**Verified • Integrated • Transport • And • Logistics**

End-to-end courier coordination for lab specimens, pharmacy delivery, and medical equipment — under one TPA relationship and unified compliance framework.

**Services:**
- Lab Courier: Chain-of-custody specimen transport, temperature compliance (ambient, refrigerated, frozen), STAT pickup within 60 minutes, routine 2-hour SLA, DOT 49 CFR 173.196 biohazard compliance
- Pharmacy Courier: DEA-tracked controlled substance delivery, same-day discharge Rx, cold chain medications, daily compilation runs (12–2 PM), after-hours emergency Rx
- Medical Equipment: DME inter-facility coordination, surgical instrument transport, sterile processing returns, emergency equipment delivery

**Compliance framework:** HIPAA, DOT 49 CFR 173.196, DEA 21 CFR 1301–1321, OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030, CAP/CLIA

**MICH relevance:** MICH members are predominantly homebound. Fragmented courier vendors create compliance gaps and care failures. VITAL replaces 5–10 vendor relationships with one TPA contract.

---

### HAVEN — Member Continuity TPA (MOB-B)
**Housing • Assistance • Vital • Emergency • Network**

Complete continuity of care coordination for health plan members during displacement or housing transitions.

**Three pillars:**
- Housing: Hotel placement, extended stay, ADA-accessible options, pet-friendly options, duration tracking, FEMA TSA coordination
- Transport: Medical appointment transport, dialysis transport (3x weekly), pharmacy pickup/delivery, wheelchair/stretcher transport, evacuation transport
- Medical Continuity: Rx transfers and emergency fills, prior authorization overrides (Section 1135), DME replacement (oxygen, wheelchairs, CPAP), home health aide reassignment, dialysis center coordination

**MICH relevance:** MCOs remain responsible for member continuity regardless of housing status. MICH members experiencing eviction, hospitalization, nursing facility transition, or displacement do not pause their care obligation. HAVEN keeps members connected to care regardless of where they land. Directly supports MICH quality reporting under health equity and self-determination pillars.

**The math:** One member, one displacement event — $19,000+ in avoided costs vs. ~$800 in coordinated services.
