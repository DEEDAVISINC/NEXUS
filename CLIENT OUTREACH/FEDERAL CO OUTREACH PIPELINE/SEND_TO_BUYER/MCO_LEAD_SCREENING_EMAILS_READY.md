My name is Dieasha D. Davis, President & CEO of Dee Davis Inc. — a Troy, Michigan-based EDWOSB and certified MDHHS Community Partner. I'm reaching out because Dee Davis Inc. can support Molina Healthcare across two critical Medicaid compliance areas: childhood lead screening and non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT).

DDI is a contract management firm that coordinates regulated healthcare services through vetted subcontractor networks — the same model we use for DOT/SAMHSA drug and alcohol testing programs with Quest Diagnostics and 5,100+ collection sites nationwide.

LEAD SCREENING COORDINATION

With Michigan's universal blood lead testing mandate now in effect, the compliance volume facing MCOs is significant. For Molina's Michigan Medicaid members, DDI provides:

- Mobile blood lead level (BLL) testing event coordination in high-risk zip codes
- Specimen collection and transport to MDHHS Bureau of Laboratories
- Community outreach to families with untested children (door-to-door, daycare/Head Start, WIC, faith-based)
- MCIR reporting support to ensure all results are captured
- HEDIS Lead Screening in Children (LSC) compliance tracking and gap closure
- Case management coordination for children with elevated BLL (≥3.5 µg/dL)
- Coverage across Southeast Michigan's designated high-risk cities — Detroit, Pontiac, Hamtramck, Highland Park, Dearborn, and surrounding areas

NON-EMERGENCY MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION (NEMT)

DDI also coordinates NEMT services for Medicaid members who need reliable transportation to and from medical appointments. Our NEMT capabilities include:

- Ride coordination through Uber Health and local NEMT provider networks
- Scheduling, dispatch, and real-time ride tracking
- Wheelchair-accessible and stretcher transport coordination
- Recurring ride programs (dialysis, behavioral health, prenatal care)
- Transportation to lead screening follow-up appointments for children with elevated BLL
- Compliance reporting and member satisfaction tracking
- NPI-active healthcare transportation provider (NPI: 1538939111)

The connection between these two services is direct — DDI screens children for lead, and when follow-up care is needed, DDI coordinates the transportation to get them there. One vendor, two compliance problems solved.

DDI is enrolled in MDHHS CHAMPS, holds official MDHHS Community Partner recognition, and is EDWOSB/WOSB/WBE/MBE certified. We are based in Troy — the same city as Molina's Michigan headquarters — and can mobilize quickly.
