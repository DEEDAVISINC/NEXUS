## WHO IS DLBA

The Detroit Land Bank Authority manages thousands of Detroit-owned vacant, blighted, 
and abandoned properties. They sell properties, manage demolitions, coordinate 
rehabilitation programs, and run neighborhood stabilization efforts. They are a 
public authority with regular contracting needs across:
- Property management and maintenance (mowing, boarding, demolition oversight)
- Contractor credentialing and drug testing (pre-employment for field crews)
- Program management and compliance (HUD, CDBG, NSP funding)
- Transportation coordination for field operations
- Staffing for administrative and field roles
- Notary and document services (deed transfers, closings, legal docs)

DDI is well-positioned for multiple DLBA contract lanes.
