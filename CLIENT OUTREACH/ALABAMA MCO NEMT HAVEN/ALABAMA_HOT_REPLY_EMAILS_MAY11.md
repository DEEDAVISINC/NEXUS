I'm reaching out about HAVEN — DDI's disaster response program for Medicaid and dual-eligible populations.

Alabama sits in one of the most high-risk corridors in the country for tornadoes, hurricanes, and severe weather displacement. When a FEMA disaster is declared, Medicaid members — especially elderly, disabled, and dual-eligible individuals — are often the last to get help and the first to lose continuity of care. HAVEN exists to solve that.

What HAVEN does:
• Automatic member safety activation at the moment of FEMA declaration
• Temporary housing coordination — hotel placement for displaced members
• Transport coordination — emergency medical and non-emergency evacuation
• Pharmacy continuity — medication supply coordination during displacement
• Care coordination handoff — ensuring members stay connected to providers during relocation

What makes DDI's model different:
• Not a crisis hotline — a pre-positioned, contracted program that activates automatically
