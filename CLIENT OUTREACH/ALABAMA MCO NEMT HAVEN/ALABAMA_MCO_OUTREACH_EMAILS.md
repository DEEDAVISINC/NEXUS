## ALABAMA MEDICAID LANDSCAPE

Alabama does NOT operate a traditional Medicaid managed care system like FL, TX, LA, OH, or GA. Instead:

- **Alabama Coordinated Health Network (ACHN)** — Regional care coordination (not full-risk MCOs)
- **My Care Alabama** — The ACHN entity across 3 regions (Northwest, Central, East)
- **NEMT is managed directly by Alabama Medicaid Agency** — Members call 1-800-362-1504 to request rides 5+ days in advance
- **NEMT is NOT carved into MCO contracts** — It's a state-administered benefit

### What This Means for DDI

Alabama's NEMT is state-run, not MCO-managed. The pitch here is different:
1. DDI would need to approach **Alabama Medicaid Agency directly** as an NEMT broker/TPA
2. OR wait for Alabama to transition to full managed care (multiple states are moving this direction)
3. HAVEN disaster response is still relevant — Alabama faces hurricane, tornado, and severe storm risk
