**Your Insurance:** E&O + General Business  
**They Want:** Automotive, Commercial Liability, Workers Comp  
**This Is:** Product resale (NOT a service contract!)  
**The Problem:** They're asking for service contractor insurance for a simple product order

---

## 📋 WHAT THEY'RE ASKING FOR:

### **1. Automotive Liability**
- Covers vehicles used for business
- Needed if YOU are driving/delivering
- **NOT needed if Zoro/supplier ships direct to Warren**

### **2. Commercial Liability**
- This is probably your "General Business" insurance!
- Just might be called different names
- Check your policy - it's likely the same thing

### **3. Workers Compensation**
- Only needed if you have W-2 EMPLOYEES
- NOT needed if you're a sole proprietor with no employees
- NOT needed if you use independent contractors (1099s)

### **4. 30-Day Cancellation Notice**
- This is STANDARD language
- Your insurance company can add this to the certificate
- Just ask your agent to include it on the COI

---

## 🤔 WHAT YOU LIKELY HAVE:

**"E&O" = Errors & Omissions Insurance**
- Professional liability
- Covers mistakes, negligence, missed deadlines
- Good for service businesses
- **This covers you for bid/order errors**

**"General Business" = General Liability Insurance**
- Property damage, bodily injury
- Product liability
- **This IS your "Commercial Liability"** (same thing, different name!)
- **This is what Warren really needs**

---

## ✅ WHAT TO CHECK WITH YOUR INSURANCE AGENT:

**Call your agent NOW and ask these questions:**

### **Question 1: General Liability Coverage**
"Do I have General Liability or Commercial General Liability insurance?"
- If YES → What's the coverage amount? ($1M? $2M?)
- This is what Warren is calling "Commercial Liability"

### **Question 2: Auto Liability**
"Is auto liability included in my policy?"
- If you have a business vehicle on your policy → You have this
- If you don't own/operate vehicles → **You don't need this!**

### **Question 3: Workers Comp**
"Do I have workers compensation insurance?"
- If you have NO employees → **You don't need this!**
- If you're sole proprietor → **Exempt!**

### **Question 4: Certificate Endorsement**
"Can you add a 30-day cancellation notice clause to my certificate?"
- They'll say YES (this is standard)
- Costs $0
