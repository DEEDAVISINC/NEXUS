🎯 APPROACH TO VA MEDICAL COURIER SERVICES

Service Delivery Model

Dee Davis Inc. will leverage our established freight brokerage infrastructure to provide comprehensive medical courier coordination for Orlando VA Health Care System's 15-location network across Central Florida, including 10 VA facilities and 5 external healthcare partners.

1. Carrier Contracting & Management

We will identify and contract with qualified medical courier carriers operating in the Florida service area, applying our proven carrier selection process:

  • Verification of 3+ years medical courier experience
  • Confirmation of appropriate licensing and insurance coverage
  • TWIC certification verification (Transportation Worker Identification Credential for secure facility access)
  • HIPAA certification verification (medical privacy and security protocols)
  • Bloodborne Pathogens certification (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 compliance for specimen handling)
  • Assessment of vehicle fleet capacity (including temperature-controlled transport)
  • Review of safety records and compliance history
  • Reference checks from current and previous clients
  • Ongoing performance monitoring and quality assurance

2. Fifteen-Location Network Coordination

VA Orlando Healthcare System Facilities (10):

✓ Orlando VAMC at Lake Nona (Main Hub)
✓ Lake Baldwin VAMC
✓ Daytona Beach VA Clinic
✓ Viera VA Health Care Center
✓ Tavares VA CBOC
✓ Kissimmee VA CBOC
✓ Clermont VA CBOC
✓ Deltona VA CBOC
✓ Palm Bay VA CBOC
✓ James A Haley Veterans' Hospital (Tampa)

External Healthcare Partners (5):

✓ Florida Hospital Laboratory (Advent Health) - Orlando
✓ Florida Hospital Laboratory (Advent Health HTU Lab) - Winter Park
✓ Southlake Hospital - Clermont
✓ Citrus Memorial Hospital - Inverness
✓ Orlando Regional Medical Center Clinical Lab

Our logistics team will coordinate daily scheduled routes and as-needed pickups across all VA facilities and external healthcare partners, with backup carrier availability to ensure service continuity.

3. Transportation Services

Comprehensive coordination of all required materials:

  • Medical specimens (laboratory samples, tissue specimens)
  • Lab results and documentation transport
  • Sterile and non-sterile medical supplies
  • Medical equipment (portable and stationary)
  • Pharmacy items and medications
  • Temperature-controlled transport for sensitive materials
  • Secure chain of custody documentation
  • Real-time tracking and proof of delivery for all shipments
