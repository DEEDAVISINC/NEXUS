## SIGNING COMPANY PAYMENT TERMS

### Default Rule (Most Companies)
**Most signing companies pay 30–45 days from the SIGNING DATE** unless the order page states otherwise.

**Clock starts:** Day of the signing appointment (not acceptance date).

**Use this to estimate** when no terms are listed on the order — then confirm on first payment.

**Do not use “30–45 days” alone for cash planning.** Most of Dee’s volume is **batched** — use **Deposit Week** below.

---

### Title → signing service → notary (industry rule — Dee confirmed 6/9/26)

**Title pays the signing company at closing** — not on signing appointment date. The signing service then pays the notary per its own terms (batch, Net 45, eCheck, etc.).

**3-day right of rescission applies ONLY to:**
- **Refinance** of **personal home / primary residence**

**Does NOT apply to:** purchases, investment-property refis, reverse mortgages, commercial, most other product types — title can fund and pay the signing service at/after closing without a rescission hold.

**Tracker rule:** For closing-based payers (@SigningAgents/NTS, JP Signings, ASAP Pro), **log closing date** when known. Do **not** start a window from signing date alone.

**Refi + signing-date terms:** If the **signing company pays the notary X days from signing date** (e.g. PSS, First Class batch from signing month), **primary-res refi / 3-day rescission does not move the notary’s clock** — that delay sits between title and the signing company, not in the notary’s stated terms.

- **Do not email invoices** — payment is automatic via batch
- **Deluxe eCheck** to email on file
- **Batch dates:** closings **1st–15th** paid **16th** of next month; **16th–end of month** paid **30th** of next month
- eCheck valid **90 days** — deposit promptly; after 90 days void + **$35** reissue fee taken from notary fee
- Payment questions: **accounting@nationalsigningservices.net** (not order contact email)

### Melio Payments — Key Notes (The Closing Signing Service, AZ Notary)
- Payments arrive via **Melio** — watch for email from Melio with payment link or vendor onboarding
- Set up Melio account / direct deposit when prompted so future payments deposit automatically
- Track which company sent each Melio payment by order number in email subject/body

### ZIL Money — Key Notes (Sensible Signings)
- Payments arrive via **ZIL Money** — watch for ZIL payment notification email
- May require vendor setup or bank link on first payment — complete promptly

### JP Signings LLC — Key Notes
- Clock starts at **closing**, not signing appointment
- Include **picture of signer's ID** at end of signing
- **Witness required** on seller packets
- Do NOT use UPS label to Detroit for Rocket Mortgage

### Premium Closing Solutions — Key Notes
- E-checks valid for **90 days** from issue — must cash within that window or request reissue
- W9 pulled from SigningOrder.com profile at first payment — any changes email accounting@premiumclosingsolutions.com
- Payment stub lists all order numbers for the pay period in one check
