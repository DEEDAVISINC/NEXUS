**Cash Flow Gap:** You're out $3,335 for **45-60 days** before you get paid!

---

## 💳 PO FINANCING OPTIONS:

### **Option 1: Traditional PO Financing**
**How it works:**
- You get awarded the contract
- You show financing company the PO
- They advance you 80-90% of PO value
- You buy from supplier
- Client pays financing company
- You get your profit minus fees

**Cost:**
- 1.5-3% per month
- For 45 days: ~2-4% of invoice
- For Warren ZEP ($3,735): Cost ~$75-150

**Your Net Profit:**
- Gross profit: $400
- PO financing cost: -$100
- **Net profit: $300**

**Still worth it!**

---

### **Option 2: Business Line of Credit**
**How it works:**
- You get a business LOC (like a credit card)
- You use it to buy from supplier
- Client pays you
- You pay off the LOC

**Cost:**
- Interest: ~8-15% APR
- For 45 days: ~1-2% of amount borrowed
- For Warren ZEP ($3,335): Cost ~$33-67

**Your Net Profit:**
- Gross profit: $400
- LOC interest: -$50
- **Net profit: $350**

**Better than PO financing!**

---

### **Option 3: Net Terms from Supplier**
**How it works:**
- Some suppliers offer Net-30 or Net-60 terms
- You order now, pay later
- Gives you time for client to pay you

**Cost:**
- Often FREE (if supplier offers it)
- May require business credit check
- Grainger, Zoro, MSC often offer this for established accounts

**Your Net Profit:**
- Gross profit: $400
- Cost: $0
- **Net profit: $400**

**BEST option if available!**
