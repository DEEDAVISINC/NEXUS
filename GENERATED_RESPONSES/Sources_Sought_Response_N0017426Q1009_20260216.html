Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide Secured Web Fingerprint Transmission (SWFT) services nationwide, certified through DCSA since August 2022. We perform electronic fingerprint capture and submission to OPM, DMDC, and other designated federal agencies in full compliance with SWFT and OPM standards. Our services include FD-258 fingerprint cards, Electronic Fingerprint Transmissions (EFT), federal background investigation support, security clearance processing, and military installation personnel onboarding.

Capability Narrative

Active DCSA SWFT certification since August 2022 with maintained portal access. Direct electronic submission to OPM and Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC). Nationwide fingerprint collection at existing contractor locations. Web-based collection system accessible to government points of contact. Support for up to 900 personnel per year within 40 scans per month threshold. FD-258 cards and Electronic Fingerprint Transmissions for all federal investigation types. Full compliance with SWFT and OPM standards as prescribed by federal mandate.
