# ECEA Scope Definition
## NEXUS Evaluator-Centric Execution Architecture

**Created:** March 9, 2026  
**Owner:** Dee Davis Inc.

---

## Purpose

Define exactly what ECEA includes and excludes so proposal/award infrastructure stays focused and does not get mixed with delivery or motivation systems.

---

## ECEA Includes (In Scope)

ECEA is the buyer-facing proposal and award-positioning stack:

1. Evaluator-centric proposal strategy
2. ProposalBio + scoreability enforcement
3. Rubric mirroring and instant scoreability
4. Proof-first writing standards
5. Clarity/readability as scoring strategy
6. Compliance + evaluator comfort selection logic
7. Defensible-case decision logic
8. Bid infrastructure discipline (ownership, review rigor, win themes)
9. Federal contracting learning loop
10. State registration cost/timing strategy tied to bid responsiveness

---

## ECEA Excludes (Out of Scope)

The following are explicitly NOT part of ECEA:

1. **PRISM execution layer**
   - Field dispatch, chain-of-custody operations, service delivery QA gates

2. **Nightly pipeline tally**
   - Daily motivation / end-of-day revenue recap workflow

3. **General operations dashboards not tied to evaluator scoring**

---

## System Boundary Rule

When referencing ECEA:
- Use it only for pre-award strategy, proposal quality, evaluator scoreability, and award defensibility.
- Do not use ECEA label for post-award delivery execution.

---

## Naming Convention

- Formal: **NEXUS Evaluator-Centric Execution Architecture (ECEA)**
- Short: **ECEA**

---

## Change Control

Any future addition to ECEA must pass this test:
1. Does it improve evaluator scoreability or award defensibility?
2. Is it pre-award, not post-award execution?
3. Does it reduce proposal risk or increase win probability?

If any answer is no, it should not be added to ECEA.
