## SERVICE LINE 6 — MORTGAGE FIELD INSPECTIONS / FORECLOSURE TPA

**DDI's model:** TPA — register with national field services companies, sub inspections to certified inspectors
**NAICS:** 541350 (Building Inspection Services)
**This is NOT a government contract play — it is a private sector B2B play**

### HOW THIS MARKET WORKS

Mortgage servicers (banks, hedge funds, REITs) contract with national field services companies (TPAs) to manage property inspections. DDI registers as a vendor with those TPAs, then builds a network of certified local inspectors to fulfill orders.

### TOP TIER — REGISTER WITH THESE NOW

| Company | Type | Registration Portal | Notes |
|---------|------|---------------------|-------|
| **Mortgage Contracting Services (MCS)** | National TPA | mcs360.com | High volume, nationwide |
| **ServiceLink** | Fidelity National Financial subsidiary | servicelink.com | Highest vetting standards |
| **National Field Representatives (NFR)** | National TPA | nfronline.com | Quality-focused |
| **Safeguard Properties** | National TPA | safeguardproperties.com | High volume REO |
| **Cyprexx Services** | National TPA | cyprexx.com | Growing player |

### REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS (ALL VENDORS)

1. **Aspen Grove ShieldID / ABC#** — mandatory background check for all inspectors ($35–$50/year)
2. **E&O Insurance** — field inspections must be listed as covered activity
3. **General Liability** — minimum $1M/$2M
4. **Business license** — valid in each state where work is performed
5. **InspectorADE or EZInspections app** — mobile reporting platform
