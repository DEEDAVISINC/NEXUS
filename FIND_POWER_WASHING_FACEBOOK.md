## 📱 WHAT TO LOOK FOR:

### **GREEN FLAGS (Good Companies):**
- ✅ Professional business page (not just personal profile)
- ✅ Photos of actual work (before/after)
- ✅ Commercial projects shown
- ✅ Reviews from customers
- ✅ Contact information listed
- ✅ Licensed/insured mentioned
- ✅ Equipment photos (hot water trailer)
- ✅ Response rate to messages

### **RED FLAGS (Skip These):**
- ❌ Brand new page (under 6 months)
- ❌ No photos of work
- ❌ Only residential work shown
- ❌ No insurance mentioned
- ❌ Poor grammar/unprofessional posts
- ❌ No reviews or all negative reviews
- ❌ No equipment shown

## 💡 PRO TIPS:

### **1. Look for Equipment Photos**
- Companies that post photos of their trailer/equipment are serious
- Look for "hot water" systems (not just cold water)
- Professional rigs = professional company

### **2. Check Their Reviews**
- 4+ stars with 10+ reviews = good
- Look at what people say (quality, punctuality, price)
- Recent reviews matter more than old ones

### **3. Message in Batches**
- Contact 5 companies at a time
- Wait for responses
- If low response rate, contact 5 more

### **4. Be Direct About Requirements**
- State it's a municipal contract (some will be excited!)
- Mention insurance requirement upfront (saves time)
- Ask about hot water equipment first (deal-breaker)

### **5. Move Fast**
- Small companies on Facebook respond quickly
- First message → response → send RFQ → get quote (can happen in 24 hours!)
