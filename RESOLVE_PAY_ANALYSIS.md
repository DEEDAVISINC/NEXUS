# 💰 RESOLVE PAY - INVOICE FINANCING FOR GOVERNMENT CONTRACTS

**What it is:** Invoice financing/factoring (different from traditional PO financing)  
**Website:** resolvepay.com  
**Model:** They advance you money against your invoices AFTER you deliver

---

## 🔄 HOW RESOLVE PAY WORKS (DIFFERENT MODEL):

### **Traditional PO Financing (Southstar):**
1. Win bid → Get PO
2. Submit PO to Southstar (wait 5-7 days)
3. Southstar pays supplier
4. You deliver
5. Client pays Southstar
6. You get profit

**Timeline:** 5-7 days from PO to funding

---

### **Invoice Financing (Resolve Pay):**
1. Win bid → Get PO
2. **YOU pay supplier yourself** (need cash upfront!)
3. You deliver to client
4. You invoice client (Net-45)
5. **Upload invoice to Resolve Pay**
6. **Resolve advances you 90% immediately**
7. Client pays Resolve in 45 days
8. Resolve sends you remaining 10% minus fee

**Timeline:** Immediate cash AFTER delivery (but you still need upfront cash!)

---
