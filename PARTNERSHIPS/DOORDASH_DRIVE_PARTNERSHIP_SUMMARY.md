## WHAT IS DOORDASH DRIVE

**DoorDash Drive** is the B2B white-label delivery service — separate from the consumer marketplace.

| Feature | DoorDash Marketplace | DoorDash Drive On-Demand |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| **Model** | Consumer orders from app | Business dispatches deliveries |
| **Pricing** | Commission-based (15-30%) | Flat fee per delivery |
| **Control** | DoorDash owns customer | Business owns customer |
| **Use Case** | Restaurants, retail | Healthcare, pharmacy, B2B |
| **DDI Fit** | ❌ Not applicable | ✅ Perfect for Rx delivery |
