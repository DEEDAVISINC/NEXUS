## KEY TERMS FROM T&Cs (Effective January 1, 2025)

### DDI as "Sender"
- DDI is the "Sender" — entity requesting deliveries
- Must list and disclose all items in each Gig
- Responsible for proper packaging
- Cannot require driver to enter private residences
- Cannot instruct overnight holds (same-day return required)

### Roadie's Role
- Logistics management PLATFORM — NOT a motor carrier
- Does NOT provide delivery services directly
- Drivers are INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS

### Driver Requirements (Sender Requirements)
- 18+ years old
- Proper vehicle, licenses, insurance
- No smoking, no weapons at pickup/drop
- Cannot be accompanied during Rx/pharmaceutical deliveries
- Real-time GPS tracking required
- Photographic proof of pickup/delivery

### Rx/PHI Special Rules
- Open Box Policy does NOT apply to prescription medicines
- Driver cannot be accompanied by anyone during Rx delivery
- Confidential information duty applies (HIPAA-aware)
- PHI handled per Roadie Privacy Policy

### Protection Plan
| Coverage | Amount | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Base Protection** | $100/Gig | Free, included |
| **Additional Protection** | Up to $10,000/Gig | Via UPS Capital partnership |

### Fees
| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| **Cancellation Fee** | Charged if cancel after driver starts driving |
| **Return Fee** | Equal to original Gig cost |
| **Tolls/Parking** | Reimbursed if agreed to in advance |

### Payment Processing
- **Processor:** Stripe
- **Billing:** Per-Gig pricing
