## KEY AGREEMENT TERMS

### Term & Renewal
- Initial term: 1 year from May 4, 2026
- Auto-renews for successive 1-year periods
- 60-day written notice required to terminate

### Termination
- Either party: 60 days advance written notice
- Material breach: 30 days to cure after notice
- Bankruptcy/insolvency: immediate

### Exclusivity
**DDI shall use NCS exclusively and shall not refer Customers to other background screening companies.**

### Pricing Rights
- DDI can REDUCE customer prices below suggested retail
- DDI CANNOT increase prices above suggested retail
- NCS can adjust pricing at their commercial discretion

### Confidentiality
- Agreement terms are confidential
- 2-year survival after termination

### Indemnification
- Mutual indemnification for negligence/breach
- DDI indemnifies NCS for DDI's marketing misrepresentations

### Governing Law
- State of Arkansas
- Venue: Benton County, Arkansas
- No jury trial (waived)
