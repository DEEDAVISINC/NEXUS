Dee Davis Inc. is always building its network of independent contractors, vendors, and subcontractors. We need qualified professionals in the following categories:

INDEPENDENT FREIGHT BROKERS AND CARRIERS: DDI operates Freight 1st Direct (MC-1647572, DOT-4250594). We work with independent freight broker agents and carriers nationwide. If you are a licensed freight broker agent or carrier looking for a brokerage to work under or with, we want to hear from you. Requirements: active MC or DOT authority (for carriers), cargo insurance, clean safety record.

INDEPENDENT PROJECT EXECUTIVE AGENTS: DDI manages government contracts across every sector. We need independent project managers and project executive agents who can represent DDI on contract execution, compliance oversight, and client coordination. If you have government contracting experience, PMP or project management background, and want to work as an independent agent under DDI, contact us.

NOTARY SIGNING AGENTS: DDI operates a nationwide signing agency connected to Snapdocs (100,000+ title companies) and ZigSig (Remote Online Notarization). We need certified notary signing agents in every state. Requirements: active notary commission, signing agent certification (NNA or equivalent), E&O insurance, background check. Mobile notary agents especially needed in Michigan, Ohio, Indiana, Illinois, and nationwide.

DRUG TESTING COLLECTORS AND TECHNICIANS: DDI is Quest Diagnostics certified for DOT and non-DOT drug testing. We need certified drug testing collectors and technicians who can perform mobile collections. Requirements: DOT collector certification, SAMHSA/DOT training, ability to travel to client sites. Especially needed in Michigan and surrounding states.

DNA TESTING COLLECTORS: DDI is an affiliate partner with DDC (DNA Diagnostics Center) for court-admissible DNA testing. We need certified DNA collection agents who can perform mobile sample collection for paternity, immigration (USCIS), and legal DNA testing. Requirements: DDC certification or willingness to get certified, chain of custody training.

FINGERPRINTING TECHNICIANS: DDI operates 3D Ink and Livescan Co for FBI-quality fingerprinting. We need certified fingerprinting technicians for mobile Livescan services. Requirements: fingerprinting certification, Livescan equipment access or willingness to train, background check, ability to travel within Michigan and surrounding states.

COURIER AND DELIVERY DRIVERS: DDI bids on federal and state courier contracts (VA, USPS, state agencies). We need reliable courier drivers with clean driving records, their own vehicle, insurance, and willingness to pass background checks. TWIC certification is a plus for secure facility deliveries.

LICENSED SUBCONTRACTORS (SERVICE CONTRACTS): DDI holds prime contracts for facility services and needs licensed, insured subcontractors in these categories: landscaping and grounds maintenance, janitorial and custodial services, snow and ice removal, HVAC maintenance, pest control, electrical work, plumbing, painting, concrete, roofing, fencing, and general facility maintenance. Requirements: general liability insurance (minimum $1 million), state/local licensing as required, government contracting experience preferred.

MEDICAL AND HEALTHCARE TRANSPORTATION PROVIDERS: DDI is a Medicaid provider (NPI 153893911) in Michigan and Maryland. We need NEMT drivers and transportation providers — ambulatory, wheelchair-accessible, and stretcher vehicles. Requirements: valid driver license, vehicle insurance, Medicaid-compliant vehicle, HIPAA training, background check.
