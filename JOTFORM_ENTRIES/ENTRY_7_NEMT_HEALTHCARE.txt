Dee Davis Inc. is a qualified healthcare provider with NPI 153893911 and active Medicaid provider status in Michigan and Maryland. We maintain a NALI partnership for HIPAA and compliance certification.

We provide end-to-end Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) program development, management, and consulting. Services include: Medicaid/Medicare program design, provider network development, trip coordination and dispatch, eligibility verification, prior authorization management, real-time trip tracking, multi-modal transportation (ambulatory, wheelchair, stretcher, and public transit), member services with language services and accessibility accommodations, compliance and auditing with HIPAA compliance and fraud prevention, and analytics and reporting with utilization analysis and cost per trip metrics.

Our NEMT partnership with Uber Health provides additional transportation platform capability. Having an NPI and active Medicaid provider status makes DDI a qualified healthcare provider, not just a broker — we can contract directly with state Medicaid programs and federal healthcare agencies including the VA, Indian Health Service, and CMS.
