We are a contract management firm and Value-Added Reseller. That means we win government and commercial contracts across every sector, source qualified subcontractors and suppliers to fulfill the work, and manage every aspect of project delivery — compliance, invoicing, quality assurance, reporting, and coordination. Our clients get one accountable point of contact backed by a vetted network of fulfillment partners. We do not specialize in one trade — we specialize in contract management. If there is a contract, we can win it, source it, and manage it.

On product-based contracts, DDI operates as a VAR — we source from manufacturer-authorized distributors and wholesale suppliers at competitive pricing, add procurement management, compliance documentation, quality assurance, logistics coordination, and delivery management, then fulfill the contract to the end client. DDI does not manufacture products. DDI sources, procures, quality-checks, and delivers them with full contract compliance built in. The VAR model combined with our EDWOSB certification gives government agencies a diversity-certified, compliance-ready, single-source procurement solution.
