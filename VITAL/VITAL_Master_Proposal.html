Lab Courier Services

Specimen integrity from collection to analysis — with full chain of custody and temperature compliance.

🧪
Specimen & Lab Transport
Every minute matters for specimen stability

Lab specimens have strict stability windows. A blood culture exposed to incorrect temperatures for even 30 minutes can yield false results. Pathology slides require vibration-free transport. Frozen specimens demand continuous cold chain. VITAL ensures every specimen reaches the lab within its viability window, every time.

Services Provided

  • Routine specimen transport (scheduled routes)
  • STAT specimen pickup (within 60 min)
  • Pathology slide transport (vibration-controlled)
  • Frozen specimen transport (dry ice)
  • Surgical instrument delivery
  • Medical equipment inter-facility transfers

Compliance & Controls

  • Temperature monitoring: ambient, refrigerated (2-8°C), frozen (-20°C)
  • Chain of custody documentation (every handoff)
  • DOT 49 CFR 173.196 biohazard compliance
  • HIPAA-compliant specimen labeling
  • Electronic proof of pickup/delivery

Technology & Compliance

The PRISM/VITAL platform delivers real-time visibility, automated compliance verification, and quarterly performance analytics.

Real-Time Order Tracking

GPS-tracked couriers with live status updates. Health system staff see pickup confirmation, in-transit status, and delivery completion in real time.

Electronic Proof of Delivery

Every delivery captured with photo, electronic signature, and timestamp. Immutable audit trail for regulatory compliance and dispute resolution.

Automated QC Checklists

Pre-dispatch compliance verification: temperature check, packaging integrity, labeling accuracy, chain of custody documentation — all verified before the courier leaves.

SLA Monitoring Dashboard

85% on-time delivery target tracked in real time. Automated escalation when deliveries approach SLA threshold. Exception reporting for every missed target.

Symplr Credentialing Integration

Every courier credentialed through Symplr before setting foot in a facility. Background checks, immunization records, HIPAA training — all verified and current.

Quarterly Performance Reports

Comprehensive analytics: delivery volumes, SLA adherence rates, temperature compliance, incident reports, cost-per-delivery trends, and improvement recommendations.

HIPAA & BAA Coverage

DDI executes a Business Associate Agreement with every health system client. All couriers receive HIPAA privacy and security training. Specimen labeling follows minimum necessary standards. PHI is protected at every handoff point in the VITAL chain.
