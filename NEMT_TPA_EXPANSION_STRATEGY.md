**DDI's edge:** Already inside the program through HAP CareSource. As HIDE expands to more states, DDI has first-mover credibility.

## STRATEGY: MCO-DIRECT (NOT STATE CONTRACTS)

**DDI goes directly to MCOs — not state Medicaid contracts.** The MCO tells DDI what credentials are needed, and DDI enrolls as part of onboarding. This flips the old model:

~~Old:~~ Register foreign entity → Enroll as Medicaid provider → Then pitch MCOs  
**New:** Pitch MCOs with HAP CareSource proof point → Sign contract → MCO tells you what enrollment they need → Enroll as required

**Why MCO-direct wins:**
- MCOs are spending money NOW — no waiting for state RFPs
- One MCO contract = thousands of members = immediate revenue
- HAVEN disaster response is the differentiator no competitor offers
- HAP CareSource HIDE SNP contract is instant credibility
- State Medicaid enrollment follows contracts, not the other way around

**Combined pitch to every MCO:**
> "DDI already manages NEMT for HAP CareSource's HIDE SNP population in Michigan — 4,500 dual-eligible members, unlimited rides, zero-fleet TPA model. We want to bring that to your membership: daily NEMT plus automatic HAVEN disaster response activation when FEMA declares. One vendor, both services, already operational."

## HAVEN + NEMT INTEGRATION

HAVEN disaster response and NEMT are NOT separate programs — they're two sides of the same TPA:

| | NEMT (Daily) | HAVEN (Disaster) |
|---|---|---|
| **When** | Every day | FEMA declarations, hurricanes, floods |
| **Volume** | Steady, predictable | Surge, event-driven |
| **Revenue** | Recurring monthly | High-value bursts |
| **Network** | Same transport partners | Same transport + housing + medical |
| **MCO Value** | Cost management, compliance | Member safety, continuity of care |
| **DDI Role** | TPA — schedule, dispatch, bill, report | TPA — activate, relocate, coordinate, bill |

**One credentialed network. One contract. Two revenue streams. No competitor offers both.**
