## THE MODEL

**Freight 1st Direct is DDI's owner-operator TPA — the bridge between independent truckers and federal freight contracts they'd never access alone.**

DDI is the prime contractor. Owner-operators are credentialed subcontractors in DDI's carrier network. DDI wins the contracts, manages compliance, handles back-office, and dispatches loads. The OO drives and earns.

**DDI is not just a dispatcher.** DDI provides the FULL business infrastructure an OO or small fleet needs to operate:

- **Freight dispatch** — optimized loads, not load board scraps
- **DOT drug & alcohol testing** — random pools, pre-employment, post-accident (TPA 1: Quest + AMRO)
- **Fingerprinting & biometrics** — TWIC, federal credentialing, background prints (TPA 2: Lakota)
- **Background checks** — MVR, criminal, employment verification (TPA 7: NCS)
- **Driver onboarding** — DQ file build, Clearinghouse queries, medical card tracking (TPA 9)
- **DOT physicals** — annual CMV examinations (Concentra network)
- **Factoring** — mandatory Bankers Factoring enrollment for same-day pay (DDI earns broker commission)
- **Detention & TONU recovery** — DDI bills and collects what the OO would normally eat
- **Federal contract access** — FEMA, USPS, DOD loads no solo OO could touch

**Every OO in the network generates revenue across 5+ DDI divisions simultaneously.** That cross-division revenue is what justifies DDI's percentage — and what no dispatcher or load board can compete with.

This is the same model DDI runs in every other division: DDI manages, partners execute.

## WHY THIS WORKS

**The problem for owner-operators:**
- They run load boards earning $1.50–$2.20/mile
- They have no access to federal freight (don't know how, can't navigate procurement)
- They waste hours on back-office: invoicing, compliance, detention recovery, paperwork
- They have zero federal past performance — can't win contracts even if they find them
- They get squeezed by dispatchers taking 10-15% for doing nothing but booking loads

**The problem for federal agencies:**
- They need reliable, compliant carriers
- They deal with large carriers who are expensive and slow
- EDWOSB/WOSB/SB set-asides mean agencies NEED small business carriers but can't find them
- They want one accountable point of contact, not 50 independent truckers

**DDI solves both sides:**
- OOs get federal-rate freight, compliance management, and back-office — in exchange for joining DDI's network
- Federal agencies get a certified EDWOSB prime with a vetted carrier pool, one invoice, one POC
- DDI earns the spread between contract value and OO payout, plus monthly compliance fees
