## 🎯 WHAT IS A QUICK WIN?

**Product resale** + **Simple specs** + **Known suppliers** + **Good deadline** = **QUICK WIN**

### **Quick Win Criteria (ALL must be true):**
1. ✅ Product resale (NOT services, NOT subcontracting)
2. ✅ Simple items (common products, not specialized)
3. ✅ Can get pricing in 1-2 days (Zoro, Grainger, known suppliers)
4. ✅ 5+ days until deadline (time to price & submit)
5. ✅ Profit potential: $1K+ minimum

### **NOT A Quick Win (SKIP):**
- ❌ Services (cleaning, lawn, pressure washing, pest control)
- ❌ Subcontracting (construction, installation, specialized work)
- ❌ Exotic/specialized products (need to find suppliers)
- ❌ < 3 days to deadline (too tight)
- ❌ < $1K profit potential (not worth time)
