## Subcontracting path (no full prime application)

- **Prime OASIS+ holders** issue **task orders**; DDI can **sub**, **team**, or **BOA**-style subcontract per prime’s policy and FAR/GSA ordering.
- **Mentor–protégé** and other SBA programs are **separate** from OASIS+ qualification — useful for capacity, not a substitute for domain NAICS proof.
