## 🔍 VA FORECAST SEARCH — STEP BY STEP

1. Go to: https://www.vendorportal.ecms.va.gov/eVP/fco/EntireVA.aspx?Type=Complete
2. Set filters:
   - **Fiscal Year:** 2026, 2027
   - **Dollar Range:** $150,000.01 to $5,000,000 OR Greater than $5,000,000
   - **Socio-Economic Category:** Women Owned Small Business OR Small Business
   - **Procurement Method:** Total Set-Aside OR Competitive
3. Click Search
4. Export results
5. Review for opportunities DDI can prime (find subs for)

## 🔍 DHS APFS — SET UP EMAIL ALERTS
