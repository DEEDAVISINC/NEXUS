**BEFORE:** Basic bullet list of what to submit

  - Complete company information with EIN/DUNS

- ✅ **PART B: Technical Proposal** (5-10 pages max)

  - Timeline with milestones

  - Safety procedures and OSHA compliance
  - Risk mitigation strategies
  - Project management approach

- ✅ **PART C: Pricing Proposal** (Separate sealed document)
  - Itemized pricing breakdown
  - Unit prices and labor rates
  - Payment schedule
  - 60-day pricing validity

- ✅ **PART D: Qualifications & Experience** (No page limit)
  - Company history and org chart
  - Minimum 3 detailed project examples
  - Key personnel resumes
  - Licenses and certifications
  - Safety record (OSHA, EMR, incidents)

- ✅ **PART E: References** (Minimum 3 required)
  - Specific format requirements
  - Must be from last 3 years
  - Current contact information mandatory

- ✅ **PART F: Insurance & Compliance**
  - Current Certificate of Insurance
  - Additional Insured proof
  - W-9 Form
  - Business licenses
  - Bonding capacity letter

- ✅ **PART G: Required Forms & Certifications**
  - RFP Acknowledgment
  - Non-Collusion Affidavit

- ✅ Coverage for bodily injury, property damage
- ✅ Protection for violations of law
- ✅ Third-party claims coverage

**6.4 Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention - MAJOR ENHANCEMENT:**
**BEFORE:** Basic 4-paragraph confidentiality clause
**AFTER:** Comprehensive legal framework including:

**Confidential Information Defined:**
- ✅ End client identity and contacts
- ✅ Project locations and details
- ✅ DEE DAVIS INC pricing and margins
- ✅ Contract terms
- ✅ Technical specifications
- ✅ Proprietary methodologies

**Specific Obligations (6-point framework):**
1. ✅ Maintain strict confidentiality
2. ✅ Limited use for contract purposes only
3. ✅ No direct contact with end client
4. ✅ No marketing/references without approval
5. ✅ Implement safeguards
6. ✅ Return materials upon completion

**Non-Circumvention Agreement:**
- ✅ No bypassing DEE DAVIS INC
- ✅ 3-year non-solicitation period
- ✅ No interference with client relationship
- ✅ No assistance to third parties

**Remedies for Breach (6 enforcement mechanisms):**
1. ✅ **Injunctive Relief** - Immediate without bond
2. ✅ **Contract Termination** - Immediate for cause
3. ✅ **Withholding Payment** - Offset damages
4. ✅ **Monetary Damages** - Actual, consequential, punitive
   - Loss of profit margins
   - Loss of client relationship
   - Investigation costs
   - Attorneys' fees
5. ✅ **Liquidated Damages** - $50,000 per violation
6. ✅ **Disgorgement** - Forfeit all profits from breach

**Duration:** Obligations survive indefinitely

**6.5 Additional Contract Terms - ENTIRELY NEW SECTION:**
