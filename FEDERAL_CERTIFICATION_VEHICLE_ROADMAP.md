## OASIS+ SCORECARD — HOW YOU SCORE POINTS

OASIS+ uses a **self-scoring model** (Attachment JP-1). You score your own proposal. Higher score = higher chance of award.

### Scoring Categories

| Category | Max Points | What Earns Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualifying Projects** | Up to 5 per domain | Each past performance project that meets domain requirements |
| **Average Annual Value (AAV)** | Tiered | Higher AAV = more points per project |
| **Federal Experience** | Bonus | Projects with federal civilian or DoD agency clients |
| **Multiple Award Environment** | Bonus | Projects performed under IDIQ, GWAC, or BPA vehicles |
| **Past Performance Quality** | Pass/Fail | No negative CPARS ratings |

### To Maximize Your Score
- Use your **highest-value, federal-client projects** as qualifying projects
- If you don't have large past performance, use **multiple smaller projects** (up to 5 per domain)
- Federal clients (VA, DLA, USACE, SBA) earn more points than commercial/state clients
- Document everything precisely — contract number, agency, dollar value, period of performance
