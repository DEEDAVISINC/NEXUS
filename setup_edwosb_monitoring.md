## 🔍 HOW TO FIND MORE EDWOSB OPPORTUNITIES

### **Option 1: SAM.gov Direct Search (Manual)**

**Weekly search routine (Mon/Wed/Fri - 15 min):**

1. Go to: https://sam.gov/search/?index=opp
2. **Advanced Search filters:**
   - **Set-Aside Type:** "Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Economically Disadvantaged"
   - **Posted Date:** "Last 7 days"
   - **Notice Type:** "Solicitation" (not sources sought)
   - **Status:** "Active"

3. **Review results:**
   - Look for product/supply opportunities first
   - Consider service contracts with simple subcontracting
   - Save viable ones to NEXUS

**Time investment:** 15 min, 3x per week = 45 min/week
**Payoff:** First access to EDWOSB opportunities = less competition!

## 📋 EDWOSB OPPORTUNITY CHECKLIST

**When you find an EDWOSB opportunity, ask:**

1. **Product or Service?**
   - Product = Higher priority (easier to bid)
   - Service = Can we subcontract?

2. **Deadline reasonable?**
   - Need at least 7-10 days to prepare quality bid
   - EDWOSB is worth rushing for IF viable

3. **Location/Delivery?**
   - Local/Regional = Easier
   - Remote = Need partners

4. **Contract value?**
   - Even small EDWOSB contracts = good (build federal track record)
   - Large EDWOSB contracts = AMAZING

5. **Requirements?**
   - Can we meet them?
   - Past performance needed?
   - Certifications required?

**If YES to most → PURSUE IT!**

EDWOSB opportunities are rare and valuable!

## 💰 WHY EDWOSB IS WORTH THE EFFORT

**Example scenario:**

**SBA (Small Business) opportunity:**
- 1,000 small businesses can bid
- Win rate: ~1-5%
- High competition

**EDWOSB opportunity:**
- Maybe 50-100 EDWOSB businesses nationally
- Of those, maybe 10-20 actually bid on any given opportunity
- Win rate: ~10-20%
- LOW competition

**Bottom line:** You're 5-10x more likely to win EDWOSB than regular SBA!
