## 🎯 WHAT IS GSA SCHEDULE?

**GSA Schedule (Federal Supply Schedule / MAS - Multiple Award Schedule):**
- Pre-negotiated contracts with federal government
- Agencies can purchase directly WITHOUT full RFP process
- Simplified acquisition (no competition required under $10M SAT)
- Access to ALL federal agencies nationwide
- Contract term: 5 years + 3 five-year options (up to 20 years!)

**Market Size:** $30+ billion in annual purchases through GSA Schedules

**Your Advantage:** EDWOSB businesses get priority and set-aside opportunities

## 📋 GSA SCHEDULE APPLICATION PROCESS:

### **Step 1: Register Prerequisites** (1-2 weeks)
**Must have:**
- [ ] Active SAM.gov registration ✅ (You should have this)
- [ ] DUNS/UEI number ✅
- [ ] CAGE code ✅
- [ ] Active business for 2+ years OR demonstrate financial capability
- [ ] Commercial sales history (can use state/local government sales)
- [ ] EDWOSB certification ✅ (You have this!)

**Check your status:**
- [ ] Verify SAM.gov active
- [ ] Confirm EDWOSB certification current
- [ ] Gather financial documents (tax returns, financial statements)

---

### **Step 2: Choose Schedule(s)** (1-3 days)
**Recommended priority:**
1. **Schedule 84** (Safety & Emergency) - HIGHEST ROI
2. **Schedule 51 V** (Hardware) - Broad products
3. **Schedule 332-293** (Janitorial) - High volume

**Can apply for multiple schedules simultaneously or sequentially**

---

### **Step 3: Prepare Commercial Pricing** (1 week)
**GSA requires:**
- Commercial price list (your catalog)
- Most favored customer (MFC) pricing
- Discount off list price for federal government
- Volume discounts
- Delivery terms

**Your Advantage:**
- You've already priced products (RCOC bids)
- Just need to format as commercial catalog
- Typical GSA discount: 10-30% off commercial

**Example:**
- Commercial price (to businesses): $100
- GSA Schedule price: $80 (20% discount)
- Your cost: $60
- Your GSA profit: $20 (25% margin)

---

### **Step 4: Complete GSA Application** (2-4 weeks)
**Required documents:**
1. **Technical Proposal:**
   - Company overview
   - Product descriptions
   - Technical specifications
   - Quality control processes
   - Delivery capabilities
   - Customer references

2. **Price Proposal:**
   - Commercial price list
   - GSA Schedule pricing
   - Discount structure
   - Terms & conditions
   - Basis of Award (BOA)

3. **Financial Information:**
   - Financial statements (2 years)
   - Tax returns
   - Commercial credit report
   - Banking references

4. **Representations & Certifications:**
   - EDWOSB certification
   - Size standards
   - Compliance certifications
   - System for Award Management (SAM) registration

**GSA provides templates for everything**

---

### **Step 5: GSA Review & Negotiation** (3-6 months)
**Process:**
- GSA Contracting Officer reviews application
- May request additional information
- Price negotiations (expect 10-30% discount off commercial)
- Terms & conditions negotiation
- Contract finalization

**Timeline:**
- Simple application: 3-4 months
- Complex application: 6-9 months
- EDWOSB priority: May expedite

---

### **Step 6: Contract Award & Setup** (1-2 weeks)
**Once awarded:**
- [ ] Sign contract

- [ ] Market to federal agencies
