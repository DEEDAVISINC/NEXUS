## WHAT GSA MAS IS (AND ISN'T)

**What it is:**
- A pre-negotiated contract with the federal government (up to 20 years: 5 base + three 5-year options)
- Once awarded, federal agencies buy from you WITHOUT full competition
- Simplified acquisition — COs can purchase directly under $250K with minimal process
- Access to GSA eBuy (simplified RFQ platform — agencies post needs, schedule holders quote)
- Access to GSA Advantage! (online catalog — agencies search and buy)
- $30+ billion in annual federal purchases flow through MAS

**What it is NOT:**
- Not a guarantee of sales — you still have to market to COs and win task orders
- Not free — there's a 0.75% Industrial Funding Fee (IFF) on all sales, reported quarterly
- Not fast — standard offers take 3-6 months for GSA review and negotiation
- Not a one-time thing — Transactional Data Reporting (TDR) is mandatory, mass mods must be accepted, annual sales reporting required

**Why DDI needs this:**
- EDWOSB on GSA Schedule = agencies can direct-award to DDI to hit WOSB goals
- DDI's services (drug testing C/TPA, courier, fingerprinting, program management) are recurring needs across every federal agency
- Once on Schedule, DDI shows up in GSA Advantage! when COs search — passive deal flow
- Task orders via eBuy are simplified competitions — much less effort than full RFPs

## GSA PRICING INTELLIGENCE — USE THESE TOOLS (buy.gsa.gov)

### Before Setting Your GSA Pricing, Use These Free Tools:

**1. CALC+ Quick Rate (Ceiling Rates)**
- URL: buy.gsa.gov → Pricing Intelligence Suite → Hourly Labor Ceiling Rates
- Search for labor categories matching DDI's services: "Program Manager," "Drug Testing Technician," "Logistics Coordinator," "Background Investigator"
- These are the MAXIMUM hourly rates existing GSA Schedule holders have negotiated
- Your proposed rates should be at or below these ceilings to be competitive
- Use these to validate or adjust rates in `DEE_DAVIS_INC_COMPLETE_SERVICE_CATALOG.md`

**2. BLS (Bureau of Labor Statistics) Wage Data**
- URL: buy.gsa.gov → Pricing Intelligence Suite → BLS
- Search for unburdened wage rates for your labor categories
- This is what workers get paid BEFORE overhead
- COs use BLS data to validate that your labor rates are reasonable
- Build your pricing narrative: "DDI's rate of $X = base labor ($BLS) + overhead (insurance, technology, compliance) + G&A + profit"

**3. SSQ+ Dashboard (Sales Data)**
- URL: buy.gsa.gov → Pricing Intelligence Suite → Data & Analytics Dashboard
- Look up actual sales volume by SIN category
- This validates which SINs have real money flowing
- If a SIN shows $50M+ in annual sales, that's a validated market
- If a SIN shows $500K in sales, maybe DDI shouldn't prioritize it

**4. CALC+ IGCE (Government Cost Estimates)**
- URL: buy.gsa.gov → Pricing Intelligence Suite → CALC+ IGCE
- This is what government program offices use to build their cost estimates before issuing solicitations
- Understanding how COs estimate costs helps you price competitively
- If the IGCE suggests $75/hr for a Program Manager and you're proposing $80/hr, you need to justify the premium

### Pricing Strategy for DDI's MAS Offer

GSA requires "Most Favored Customer" (MFC) pricing — the government must get your best price or a discount off your best commercial price.

| Element | What GSA Wants | DDI Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial Price List** | What you charge non-government clients | Use `DEE_DAVIS_INC_COMPLETE_SERVICE_CATALOG.md` |
| **GSA Discount** | 10-30% off your commercial rates (typical) | Build this into your pricing model — don't set commercial rates so low you can't discount |
| **Price Justification** | Prove your rates are fair and reasonable | BLS wage data + overhead breakdown + market comparables from CALC+ |
| **Volume Discounts** | Optional but strong | Offer tiered pricing for larger task orders |

## TRANSACTIONAL DATA REPORTING (TDR) — MANDATORY

**New as of Refresh 31 (April 2, 2026):** TDR is now mandatory for ALL SINs and ALL MAS vendors.

**What this means for DDI:**
- DDI must report every transaction (task order, delivery order) to GSA
- Required data elements: order type, order date, ship date, customer agency code, zip code, pricing
- Reporting is quarterly through the GSA reporting portal
- Price Reductions Clause liability ends when TDR participation begins
- This replaces the old CSP (Commercial Sales Practices) discount monitoring

**DDI must be prepared to track and report this data from day one of contract award.**
