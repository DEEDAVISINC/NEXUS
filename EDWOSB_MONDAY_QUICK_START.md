## ☕ MORNING SETUP (30 MINUTES)

### **8:00 AM - SAM.gov Setup (15 min)**

**Step 1: Go to sam.gov**
- Click "Contract Opportunities"
- Click "Advanced Search"

**Step 2: Set These Filters:**

**Set-Aside Type (CHECK ALL THREE):**
- ☐ Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Program
- ☐ Economically Disadvantaged WOSB (EDWOSB)
- ☐ Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Sole Source

**Contract Value:**
- Minimum: $100,000
- Maximum: $4,000,000

**Posted Date:**
- Within last: 30 days

**Status:**
- ☐ Active

**Step 3: Save This Search**
- Click "Save Search"
- Name it: "EDWOSB $100K-4M"
- Set email alerts: Daily

## 🔍 OPPORTUNITY HUNTING (1 HOUR)

### **8:30 AM - SAM.gov Search (30 min)**

**Run your saved search, look for:**

**GREEN FLAGS (Click into these!):**
- ✅ Title includes: "Equipment", "Supplies", "BPA", "IDIQ"
- ✅ Set-aside: EDWOSB or WOSB
- ✅ Value: $250K-$2M (sweet spot!)
- ✅ Response deadline: 3+ weeks away

**Add to spreadsheet if:**
- Equipment or supplies (not services/labor)
- You think you can source the items
- Set-aside gives you advantage
- Deadline reasonable (2+ weeks)

**Goal:** Find 5-10 opportunities to track

### **9:00 AM - Michigan SIGMA (20 min)**

**Go to:** sigma.michigan.gov

**Search Strategy:**
1. Click "Search Solicitations"
2. Status: Open
3. Search keywords: "equipment" OR "supplies"
4. Filter results by value (look for $100K+)

**Look for:**
- "WMBE" in description
- "Diversity goals"
- "Certified businesses"
- Large state agencies (MDOT, MDHHS, DTMB)

**Add promising ones to spreadsheet**

## 📋 DEEP DIVE (30 MINUTES)

### **9:30 AM - Review Top 3 Opportunities**

**For each opportunity (10 min each):**

**Step 1: Download the RFP**
- Click "View Details" or "Download"
- Save PDF to: `/EDWOSB OPPORTUNITIES/[Opportunity Name]/`

**Step 2: Quick Read (5 min)**
- What products/equipment needed?
- Can you source these items?
- What's the delivery timeline?
- Any bonding requirements?
- Payment terms (Net 30/60/90)?

**Step 3: Initial Assessment (5 min)**

**Rate each:**
- **Feasibility:** Can I fulfill this? (1-10)
- **Win Probability:** Set-aside? Competition? (1-10)
- **Profit Potential:** Good margins? ($)

**Update spreadsheet with notes**
