## ⚠️ CARRIER VERIFICATION STANDARD — MANDATORY

**ALL carriers in the Freight 1st Direct network are verified through FleetFlow™ (DDI's proprietary logistics and carrier management system) before activation.**

FleetFlow™ verification covers:
- MC authority status (active, no revocations)
- USDOT number confirmation
- Safety rating (Satisfactory or Conditional only — Unsatisfactory = disqualified)
- Insurance on file (liability + cargo minimums)
- TWIC certification status (for AOG/secure facility dispatch eligibility)
- DOT drug & alcohol testing enrollment (C/TPA — required for all FID carriers)
- Carrier agreement signed and on file
- W-9 on file

**No carrier is dispatched until FleetFlow™ verification is complete. No exceptions.**
