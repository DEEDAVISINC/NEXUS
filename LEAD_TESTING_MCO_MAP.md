# Michigan MCO Map — Lead Screening & Blood Lead Level Testing
## DDI Service Lane: Mobile BLL Testing, Community Screening Coordination, EPSDT Compliance Support

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS NOW

**Michigan passed universal blood lead testing law (October 2023, effective April 30, 2025).** Every child under 6 must be tested — mandatory at 12 months and 24 months. Catch-up testing required for ages 24-72 months with no prior test. Additional testing for children ages 4-5 in 82 designated high-risk cities/townships.

**This is a compliance tsunami for MCOs.** The HEDIS Lead Screening in Children (LSC) measure now tracks the percentage of children tested by age 2 as part of the Medicaid Child Core Set. MCOs that miss their numbers get penalized by MDHHS. They NEED vendors who can help them hit compliance targets.

**DDI's angle:** Mobile blood lead testing coordination, community screening event management, specimen collection and transport to MDHHS lab, outreach to families who haven't been tested, MCIR reporting support. Same TPA model as drug testing — DDI primes, phlebotomists and community health workers sub.

## DDI SERVICE OFFERING TO MCOs

### What DDI Provides:
1. **Mobile Blood Lead Level (BLL) Testing Coordination** — Organize and staff community screening events in high-risk zip codes
2. **Specimen Collection & Transport** — Capillary and venous blood draw coordination, chain-of-custody transport to MDHHS Bureau of Laboratories
3. **Community Outreach** — Door-to-door, daycare/Head Start, WIC office, and church-based outreach to get untested children screened
4. **MCIR Reporting Support** — Ensure all results are reported to Michigan Care Improvement Registry
5. **HEDIS Compliance Support** — Help MCOs hit their Lead Screening in Children (LSC) HEDIS measure targets
6. **Case Management Coordination** — For children with elevated BLL (≥3.5 µg/dL), coordinate nurse home visits ($221.74/visit) and community health worker visits ($110/visit)
7. **High-Risk Area Focus** — 82 designated high-risk cities/townships where catch-up testing is mandatory for ages 4-5

### DDI Credentials That Matter Here:
- MDHHS Community Partner (official state recognition)
- NPI: 1538939111 (healthcare provider)
- EDWOSB/WOSB/MBE/WBE (diversity procurement advantage)
- Existing drug testing TPA infrastructure (same logistics model)
- Quest Diagnostics partnership (5,100+ collection sites)

### Pricing Model:
DDI bills the MCO per screening event or per child tested. MCO bills Medicaid. DDI margin = difference between MCO reimbursement rate and DDI's fulfillment cost (phlebotomist, supplies, transport).
