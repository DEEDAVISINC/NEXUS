## HOW TO UPDATE SAM.GOV
1. Log in at https://sam.gov
2. Go to Entity Management
3. Click your entity (Dee Davis Inc.)
4. Edit → NAICS Codes section
5. Add each code above
6. Save and submit
7. Updates usually take 24-48 hours
