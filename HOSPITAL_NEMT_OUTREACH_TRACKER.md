## VALUE PROPOSITION

**For Discharge Planning / Acute Care:**
- Get patients home safely after discharge
- Reduce bed blocking
- Decrease "left without being seen" rates
- Improve HCAHPS scores (patient experience)

**For Readmission Prevention:**
- Transportation to follow-up appointments
- Reduces 30-day readmission penalties (CMS)
- Supports care transitions

**For Population Health / SDOH:**
- Transportation is a social determinant of health
- Supports community benefit programs
- Enables access for underserved populations

**DDI Differentiators:**
- Michigan-based, woman-owned (EDWOSB)
- Zero fleet / TPA model (Uber Health, Lyft Healthcare)
- Already serving HAP CareSource members in Wayne/Macomb
- Scalable, HIPAA-compliant, real-time tracking
