# TRANSLATION & INTERPRETATION SERVICES — Bid Strategy

**Category:** Service Brokering (EDWOSB Prime + Language Service Providers)
**Business Model:** Win language service contracts as EDWOSB, subcontract to interpreters/translators
**NAICS Codes:** 541930 (Translation and Interpretation Services)

---

## WHY THIS FITS

- **Huge federal demand** — DHS/USCIS alone has a $200M language services contract
- **Growing market** — more agencies required to provide language access (Executive Order 13166)
- **EDWOSB set-aside eligible** — NAICS 541930 qualifies for WOSB program
- **No language skills needed** — you manage the contract, certified interpreters do the work
- **Technology-enabled** — over-the-phone interpretation (OPI) can serve agencies nationwide
- **High margins** — interpreters earn $25-$50/hr, agencies pay $75-$150/hr

## HOW IT WORKS

```
Agency needs interpreter ──→ Calls Dee Davis Inc. ──→ You dispatch interpreter
                                    ↑
                          You bill $100/hr
                          Interpreter gets $40/hr
                          You keep $60/hr (60% margin)
```

### Three Service Models

**1. Over-the-Phone Interpretation (OPI)**
- Easiest to set up — no travel, instant availability
- Use a sub platform or independent interpreters
- 24/7 availability for courts, hospitals, police

**2. In-Person Interpretation**
- Courts, schools, government offices
- Higher rates, requires local interpreter pool
- Metro Detroit has strong Arabic, Spanish, Bengali communities

**3. Document Translation**
- Government forms, legal documents, public notices
- Freelance translators, pay per word
- Higher margin on certified translations
