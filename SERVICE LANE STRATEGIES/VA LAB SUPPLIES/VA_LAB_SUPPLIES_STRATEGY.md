## IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS

### VA Specific
- **SAM.gov registration** — must be current (you have this)
- **VetBiz/VetCert registration** — for VA-specific contracts
- **TAA Compliance** — products must be from Trade Agreements Act countries
- **Buy American Act** — preference for US-made products
- **VA FSS (Federal Supply Schedule)** — consider getting on VA Schedule for recurring orders

### Compliance
- Products must be FDA cleared/approved where applicable
- Proper storage and shipping (temperature-sensitive items)
- Lot tracking and expiration date management
- Certificate of Analysis for reagents
