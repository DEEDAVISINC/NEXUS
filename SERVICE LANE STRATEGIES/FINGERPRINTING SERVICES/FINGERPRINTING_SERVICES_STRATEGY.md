## WHY THIS IS A GREAT FIT

- **EDWOSB prime contractor model** — you hold the contract, sub does the fingerprinting
- **Low overhead** — no equipment, no storefront, no special training needed
- **Recurring revenue** — agencies always need fingerprinting (new hires, licensing, background checks)
- **Multiple subcontractor options in Metro Detroit** — proven providers ready to partner
- **Growing demand** — more states requiring fingerprint-based background checks for teachers, healthcare workers, childcare providers

## HOW THE SERVICE BROKERING MODEL WORKS

```
Agency (Client) ──→ Dee Davis Inc. (EDWOSB Prime) ──→ Fingerprinting Vendor (Sub)
     ↑                      ↑                              ↑
   Pays contract price    Takes 15-25% margin         Does the actual work
   Gets EDWOSB credit     Manages relationship         Gets steady business
```

**Your value to the agency:** EDWOSB certification (they get diversity credit), single point of contact, contract management
**Your value to the sub:** You bring them government contracts they can't win on their own (no EDWOSB cert)
