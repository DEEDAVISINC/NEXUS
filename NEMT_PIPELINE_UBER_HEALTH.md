## HOW DDI + UBER HEALTH WINS NEMT CONTRACTS

### The DDI Model:
1. **DDI primes** the government contract as EDWOSB
2. **Uber Health provides** the ride network (ambulatory/sedan trips)
3. **Local wheelchair/stretcher subs** handle ADA and medical transport trips
4. **DDI manages** scheduling, dispatch, compliance, reporting, and billing

### Why This Wins:
- Uber Health = 83% fewer missed appointments (VA data)
- No fleet to maintain = lower overhead = more competitive pricing
- Technology platform already HIPAA-compliant
- Scalable to any city — Uber operates everywhere
- EDWOSB certification gives socioeconomic preference

### What DDI Still Needs:
- Wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV) subcontractors in each market
- Stretcher/gurney transport subs for non-ambulatory patients
- Dispatch coordinator (can be DDI staff or contracted)
