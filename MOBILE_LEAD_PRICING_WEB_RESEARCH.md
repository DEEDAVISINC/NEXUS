- Low: $750 + (30 × $65) = $2,700 → ~$90/child
- High: $750 + (30 × $100) = $3,750 → ~$125/child

That is BEFORE TPA margin, outreach, MCIR, or navigation.

## 8. WEB-BUILT COST FLOORS

**Single child home visit:**
- Mobile collection: $70–$225
- Test consumable/lab: $9–$30 (LeadCare) or $28–$110 (clinic/venous)
- Real floor: ~$100–$250 BEFORE DDI admin, insurance, or margin

**30-child half-day event:**
- Precision DX model: ~$90–$125/child at vendor cost
- Add second staff, registration, QC, transport → ~$130–$180/child cost realistic

## BOTTOM LINE

Nobody credible mobilizes for $50/draw.

Published market:
- $70–$225+ just to show up (collection only)
- $65–$110/test for lead-specific work
- $750–$1,400 event blocks

Old internal rate of $125–$200 all-in mobile is BELOW market when mobilization + test + margin are included.

Fair sub pay for mobile pediatric lead work: **$75–$150+/visit** OR **$150–$250+ half-day** — NOT $35–$55 nursing-home wholesale rates.
