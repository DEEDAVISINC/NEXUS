### DOT Collector Qualifications (49 CFR Part 40)
- Complete qualification training program
- Pass monitored proficiency demonstration
- Stay current on regulatory changes
- No grandfather clause or waiver
- Contact ODAPC: ODAPCWebMail@dot.gov / 202-366-3784
- Reference: [transportation.gov/odapc/collectors](https://www.transportation.gov/odapc/collectors)

### Breath Alcohol Technician (BAT) Certification
- Separate certification from urine collector
- Required for DOT alcohol testing
- Must complete DOT BAT training course

## PERMIT RUNNING — Service Details

> **From the 62-page Notary Permit Runner (NPR) Guide**
> Contractors bill $120+/hour. They HATE standing in line at building departments.
> DDI charges $50-$150 per permit run. Contractors save time, DDI earns margin.

### What NPRs Do
- Pull building permits (electrical, plumbing, mechanical, roofing, solar, pool, etc.)
- Submit plans for review (plan-review permits require 2 trips: drop off + pickup)
- Record documents at courthouses (deeds, notices of commencement)
- File variances on behalf of homeowners
- Handle special permits (flood, multi-dwelling, etc.)

### Target Clients for Permit Running
- Air conditioning companies
- Electrical contractors
- Plumbing contractors
- Roofers
- Home builders
- Solar panel companies
- Pool contractors
- Remodeling companies
- Billboard sign companies
- Concrete contractors
- Driveway installers
- Mobile home dealerships/installers
- Screen enclosure contractors
- Septic system installers
- Private homeowners (where permitted)

### Pricing Model (Updated Feb 2026 — Market-Adjusted)
- **Single permit:** $125
- **Multiple permits (up to 4):** $125 first + $50 each additional
- **5+ permits:** Volume pricing available
- **Plan-review permits (2 trips):** Premium pricing
- **Travel:** Included within service area, surcharge beyond
- **Notarization of permit forms:** Additional fee on top of permit run (most forms require notarization before submission — this is the double-dip)
- *Previous pricing ($75/run) was below market. Full-service permit expediters charge $200-$400+ for residential, $800-$5,000+ commercial. Contractors save $240-$360 in billable time per trip they don't make.*
- **Target:** $30-$100+/hour depending on grouping
- **Document recording:** $50 per document

### How DDI Scales This
1. Dee can personally run permits in Oakland/Wayne/Macomb counties
2. Recruit additional NPRs through notary networks (they already have commissions)
3. Dispatch permit runs through PRISM like any other order
4. QC engine (14 rules) catches incomplete apps BEFORE the agent wastes a trip
5. Group permits by location for maximum efficiency

### Forms That MUST Be Notarized Before Submission
*(From NPR Guide pg 53-54 — this is where DDI double-dips: notary fee + permit run fee)*
- Building Permit Application
- Homeowner Affidavit
- Notice of Commencement
- Roofing Application
- Disclosure Statement
- Air Conditioning Replacement Sheet
- Certificate of Occupancy or Completion
- Change the Contractor of Record
- Easement Encroachment and Acknowledgement
- Hot Water Heater Replacement
- Permit Extension Request
- Swimming Pool/Spa/Hot Tub Safety Act form
- Stand-by Generator Permit form
- 40 Year Building Safety Inspection
