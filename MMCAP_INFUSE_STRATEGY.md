## WHAT IS MMCAP INFUSE

- **Government-run Group Purchasing Organization (GPO)** operated by the State of Minnesota
- **13,000+ member facilities** across all 50 states
- Members include: state/county/municipal government entities, correctional facilities, public health departments, nursing facilities, mental health facilities, public safety/first responders, federal agencies, non-profits
- Contracts cover: pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, equipment, and related services
- Michigan participates via the MiDEAL Extended Purchasing Program
