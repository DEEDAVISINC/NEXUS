Confidentiality / NDA Acknowledgment

Policy ID: DDI-HR-004 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 25, 2026 Applies to: Employees & contractors

This acknowledgment is an electronic confidentiality undertaking for GATEWAY onboarding. It does not replace a separately executed Mutual NDA or Business Associate Agreement when those documents are required for a specific engagement. Where a signed NDA/BAA exists, that written agreement controls on conflicting terms.

1. Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” includes non-public business, technical, financial, pricing, supplier, client, member, solicitation, proposal, and operational information of Dee Davis Inc. and of DDI’s clients that you learn through your work or engagement—whether oral, written, or electronic.

2. Obligations

  • Use Confidential Information only to perform authorized DDI duties.
  • Do not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized persons, including suppliers who do not need buyer identity to quote.
  • Do not disclose end-buyer names, solicitation numbers, specific delivery addresses, or member PHI/PII except as authorized and lawful.
  • Protect credentials, documents, and devices; report suspected breaches immediately to gc@deedavis.biz (and qc@deedavis.biz for operational quality incidents).
  • Return or securely destroy Confidential Information upon request or separation, subject to legal retention duties.

3. PHI / HIPAA

If your role involves PHI, you also must follow DDI-HIPAA-001 and any applicable Business Associate Agreement. Unauthorized PHI disclosure may trigger breach-notification duties and discipline.

4. Duration

Confidentiality obligations continue during your employment or engagement and survive afterward for as long as the information remains non-public, or longer if a separate NDA or law requires.

5. Remedies

Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm. DDI may seek injunctive relief and other remedies available at law or equity, and may pursue employment or contractual discipline.

6. Acknowledgment (E-Sign)

By typing your full legal name in GATEWAY and submitting this acknowledgment for DDI-HR-004, you agree to these confidentiality obligations. Your typed name, timestamp, and IP address are recorded as your electronic signature for onboarding audit purposes.
