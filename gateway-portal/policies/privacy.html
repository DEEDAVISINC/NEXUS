This is the GATEWAY acknowledgment copy of Dee Davis Inc.’s Privacy Policy for workforce onboarding. The formal audit PDF (DDI-PRIV-001) is retained in company records. GATEWAY may collect personally identifiable information (PII) for employment or engagement compliance. Privacy Officer: Dieasha D. Davis — gc@deedavis.biz / info@deedavis.biz.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Dee Davis Inc. (“DDI,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with our websites, services, GATEWAY onboarding portal, and business operations. We are committed to protecting privacy and complying with applicable laws, including HIPAA where PHI is involved, and state privacy requirements where they apply.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Contact and identity — Name, email, phone, address, organization, and similar identifiers provided for services, registration, or onboarding.
  • Employment / engagement onboarding data — Documents and acknowledgments uploaded or e-signed in GATEWAY (for example I-9 supporting documents, W-9, signed offer/IC agreement, policy acknowledgments), personnel numbers, division/role assignments, and training/compliance status.
  • PHI — Only when we create, receive, maintain, or transmit it as permitted by law or contract, in accordance with HIPAA and applicable BAAs.
  • Operational / technical data — IP address, device/browser data, and portal activity logs used for security, audit, and service delivery.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide contracted services; operate workforce onboarding and compliance; communicate about services; bill and administer accounts; meet legal and regulatory obligations; protect safety and security; and detect misuse. We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Uses of PHI follow HIPAA, our policies, and applicable BAAs.

4. Who We Share Information With

We disclose information only as needed and permitted: MCOs and Medicaid programs when required for contracted services; vetted subcontractors/service providers under written safeguards; government/legal when required by law; and professional advisors under confidentiality. Third parties may use information only for authorized purposes.

5. How We Protect Information

DDI maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and our operations, including access controls, workforce training, and secure handling of records and systems we control. No method is perfectly secure; we use commercially reasonable measures and HIPAA Security Rule requirements where we handle e-PHI as a Business Associate.

6. Your Responsibilities as Workforce

  • Use GATEWAY and DDI systems only for authorized purposes.
  • Do not share sign-in links, codes, or credentials.
  • Upload only accurate documents you are authorized to provide.
  • Protect PII/PHI of members, clients, and colleagues — minimum necessary access.
  • Report suspected privacy or security incidents immediately to gc@deedavis.biz.

7. Rights & Complaints

Depending on your relationship with us and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correction, or deletion of certain information, subject to legal retention requirements (including HIPAA and employment/compliance retention). HIPAA-related complaints may also be filed with HHS OCR. Contact the Privacy Officer to exercise rights or file a complaint.
