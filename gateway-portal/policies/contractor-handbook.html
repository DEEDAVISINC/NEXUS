Contractor Obligations Handbook

Policy ID: DDI-HR-002 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 25, 2026 Applies to: 1099 / independent contractors

This handbook describes contractual flow-down obligations for independent contractors engaged by Dee Davis Inc. It is not an employee handbook and does not create an employment relationship. Acknowledgment is completed by typed-name electronic signature in the GATEWAY portal.

1. Purpose & Scope

Dee Davis Inc. (“DDI”) engages independent contractors to perform defined scopes of work under written agreements (MSA / Independent Contractor Agreement). This handbook summarizes obligations that apply in addition to your signed contract, including confidentiality, conduct, systems use, and—when your engagement touches FDR/MCO work—compliance flow-downs.

2. Independent Contractor Status

  • You are not a W-2 employee. DDI does not run E-Verify/I-9 on contractors.
  • Payment is deliverable/milestone-based per your agreement—not an employee-style wage structure.
  • You control your methods within the written scope; DDI does not impose fixed daily schedules that convert the relationship into employment.
  • You may work for other clients unless your agreement says otherwise; disclose conflicts under DDI-HR-003.

3. Scope Discipline

Perform only work within the written scope. Do not expand into member-facing or PHI-related tasks unless the engagement expressly includes them and required training/BAA conditions are met. If scope is unclear, stop and ask the Engagement Manager in writing.

4. Confidentiality & Buyer Protection

Protect DDI confidential information and any PHI/PII you are authorized to access. Never reveal end-buyer, agency names, solicitation numbers, or member identifiers to unauthorized parties. Supplier/subcontractor communications use generic client language only. You must separately open and e-sign DDI-HR-004 (NDA), DDI-HIPAA-001 (HIPAA), and DDI-PRIV-001 (Privacy) in GATEWAY. Where PHI is involved, the applicable Business Associate Agreement also applies.

5. Conduct

Follow DDI’s Code of Conduct / Conflict of Interest Policy (DDI-HR-003) as a contractual flow-down. Professional, non-discriminatory conduct is required in all interactions touching DDI work. Report suspected fraud, waste, abuse, or member harm through the channels in DDI-FDR-001 when your engagement is FDR-adjacent.

6. Systems Access

System access (including GATEWAY and any limited NEXUS/PRISM credentials) is minimum-necessary and engagement-specific. Credentials are personal; do not share. Access ends when the engagement ends. GATEWAY is used for document upload, training acknowledgment, and policy e-sign.

7. Training & Screening

Complete only the compliance training items marked applicable to your engagement. If your assigned division is FDR-adjacent, exclusion screening (OIG LEIE + GSA SAM.gov) and FDR Policies & Procedures acknowledgment (DDI-FDR-001) apply. Currency may be re-verified before renewal.

8. Insurance & Classification Documentation

Provide Certificate of Insurance when required by contract. Cooperate with DDI’s worker-classification documentation process (bounded scope, tools/schedule, other clients, deliverable-based pay). Unclear cases may be routed to counsel.

9. Acknowledgment

By typing your full legal name in GATEWAY and submitting the acknowledgment for DDI-HR-002, you confirm that you have read this Contractor Obligations Handbook (Version 1.0) and agree to these flow-down obligations as part of your engagement with Dee Davis Inc.
