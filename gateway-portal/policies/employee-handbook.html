Dee Davis Inc. · GATEWAY Workforce Policies

Employee Handbook

Policy ID: DDI-HR-001 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 25, 2026 Applies to: W-2 employees

This handbook is company policy for Dee Davis Inc. workforce members. It is not a contract of employment and does not create a guarantee of continued employment. Acknowledgment is completed by typed-name electronic signature in the GATEWAY portal (gateway.deedavis.biz). Counsel review may refine future versions; v1.0 is the controlling version until superseded.

1. Purpose & Scope

Dee Davis Inc. (“DDI,” “the Company”) is a nationwide contract management Third-Party Administrator (TPA) headquartered at 755 W. Big Beaver Rd., Suite 2020, Troy, Michigan 48084. This Employee Handbook sets expectations for W-2 employees who support DDI operations, including divisions that touch Medicaid/Medicare-adjacent work.

Employees must also complete any division-required policies (including FDR Compliance Policies & Procedures where applicable), HIPAA training, and GATEWAY onboarding steps assigned to their role.

2. Employment Relationship

Employment with Dee Davis Inc. is at-will to the extent permitted by applicable law. Either the employee or the Company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or advance notice, subject to applicable law and any written agreement that expressly modifies at-will status.

Nothing in this handbook limits DDI’s right to modify job duties, schedules, compensation structure (consistent with law), or policies.

3. Equal Opportunity & Anti-Harassment

DDI provides equal employment opportunity and prohibits unlawful discrimination and harassment based on protected characteristics under federal and Michigan law. Employees who experience or witness prohibited conduct should report it promptly to their manager, HR, or gc@deedavis.biz. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited.

4. Conduct & Professional Standards

  • Represent DDI professionally in all client, member, vendor, and government interactions.
  • Follow the “coordinate, not provide” standard for TPA work: DDI manages contracts and compliance; do not imply DDI operates client fleets or programs it does not operate.
  • Never disclose end-buyer, agency, or solicitation identity to suppliers or fulfillment partners unless expressly authorized under a signed agreement and operational need.
  • Use systems (NEXUS, GATEWAY, PRISM, email) for authorized business purposes only; protect credentials.
  • Escalate compliance, safety, and member-risk issues immediately—do not wait for a weekly check-in.

5. Confidentiality, PHI & PII

Employees must protect confidential business information and any Protected Health Information (PHI) or Personally Identifiable Information (PII) encountered in their work. You must separately open and e-sign DDI-HIPAA-001 (HIPAA Compliance Policy) and DDI-PRIV-001 (Privacy Policy) in GATEWAY, in addition to the Confidentiality/NDA acknowledgment. Minimum necessary access applies. Unauthorized disclosure may result in discipline up to termination and may trigger legal reporting duties.

6. Timekeeping, Pay & Expenses

Employees must record time accurately as directed by HR/payroll. Falsifying time or expenses is grounds for discipline. Expense reimbursement requires prior approval and original receipts unless a written exception applies. Company email aliases (when provisioned) are for business use and remain Company property.

7. Systems Access & GATEWAY

GATEWAY (gateway.deedavis.biz) is DDI’s workforce onboarding and acknowledgment portal. Employees sign in with their personal email on file. Required training, document uploads, and policy acknowledgments are tracked in GATEWAY and NEXUS. Access is revoked upon separation.

8. Compliance Training & Screening

Employees must complete assigned compliance training within stated deadlines (including any CMS 90-day hard floors for FDR-adjacent roles). Where the employee’s assigned division requires FDR screening, OIG LEIE and GSA SAM.gov exclusion checks apply at hire and on the monthly cadence defined in DDI-FDR-001. Failure to remain current may block work assignment through DDI’s can-work compliance gate.

9. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest (financial, familial, or outside employment that conflicts with DDI duties) under DDI-HR-003 and re-attest as required.

10. Discipline & Separation

DDI may take corrective action up to and including termination for policy violations, compliance failures, dishonesty, or conduct that harms members, clients, or the Company. Upon separation, employees must return Company property and cease use of systems and confidential information.

11. Acknowledgment

By typing your full legal name in GATEWAY and submitting the acknowledgment for DDI-HR-001, you confirm that you have read this Employee Handbook (Version 1.0), understand it, and agree to follow it as a condition of employment.
