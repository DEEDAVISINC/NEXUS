1. Purpose & Scope

This policy establishes how Dee Davis Inc. (“DDI”) protects Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA (including the Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules) and stricter state law where applicable. It applies to all employees, contractors, and others under DDI’s direct control who create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI for or on behalf of DDI. Where DDI acts as a Business Associate, applicable Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) also apply.

2. Definitions (summary)

  • PHI — Individually identifiable health information held or transmitted by a covered entity or business associate, in any form.
  • Business Associate — An entity that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of a covered entity for HIPAA-regulated functions. DDI may act as a Business Associate when performing such services.
  • Minimum necessary — Limit PHI used, disclosed, or requested to what is reasonably necessary for the purpose, except as HIPAA otherwise permits.

4. Permitted Uses & Disclosures

PHI may be used or disclosed only as permitted or required by HIPAA and applicable BAAs (including treatment, payment, and health care operations when applicable; valid authorization; and disclosures required by law). Workforce members must not access, use, or disclose PHI for curiosity, personal reasons, or any purpose outside documented job duties.

5. Minimum Necessary & Safeguards

Role-based access applies. DDI maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards appropriate to the PHI handled, consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule for electronic PHI where applicable. Credentials are personal; do not share. Report suspected incidents immediately to gc@deedavis.biz.

6. Training & Sanctions

Workforce with PHI access must complete HIPAA privacy/security training at hire and at least annually thereafter (tracked in GATEWAY). Violations may result in discipline up to termination of employment or engagement, removal from PHI-related work, and required reporting.

7. Breach Notification

Suspected or confirmed breaches involving PHI are handled under HIPAA breach notification rules and DDI internal procedures. Do not investigate alone — escalate to the Privacy Officer / gc@deedavis.biz immediately.
