FDR Compliance Policies & Procedures

Policy ID: DDI-FDR-001 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 25, 2026 Applies to: FDR-adjacent divisions

Dee Davis Inc. performs work under Medicaid/Medicare Managed Care Organization (MCO) and related contracts. Where DDI is a First Tier, Downstream, or Related Entity (FDR) under 42 CFR 422.504(i) and parallel Medicaid requirements, these Policies & Procedures apply to workforce members in FDR-adjacent divisions. Acknowledgment is required via GATEWAY typed-name e-sign. Related policies: DDI-HIPAA-001, DDI-PRIV-001.

1. Purpose & Scope

This manual establishes Dee Davis Inc.’s compliance program controls for FDR obligations: general compliance and Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) training; exclusion screening; record retention; reporting; and annual attestation.

Division-driven applicability. OIG LEIE + GSA SAM screening and this FDR P&P acknowledgment are required when the person’s assigned division is one of: DEPOINTE (NEMT Coordination), HAVEN, SHIELD, VITAL, or Corporate/HR/Admin. Other divisions (including Freight 1st Direct, 3D Ink Signatures/CNTDA, DEPOINTE DNA, ARENA/PRIME, and unassigned) are not subject to this FDR screening package by default. Changing division via GATEWAY assignment updates the requirement.

2. Definitions

  • FDR — First Tier, Downstream, or Related Entity performing functions on behalf of a Medicare Advantage or applicable Medicaid managed care organization under CMS rules.
  • Workforce — Employees and independent contractors under DDI control for the engagement who support FDR-adjacent work.
  • GATEWAY — DDI’s workforce onboarding and compliance evidence portal (gateway.deedavis.biz), backed by NEXUS HR/GATEWAY records.
  • Can-work gate — Automated compliance check that blocks assignment until first-phase onboarding, required screening, and CMS hard-floor training deadlines are satisfied.

3. Compliance Responsibility & Reporting

Ultimate responsibility for this program rests with the President & CEO. Day-to-day compliance operations are coordinated through HR/Compliance and documented in GATEWAY/NEXUS.

How to report FWA or compliance concerns:

  • Email: gc@deedavis.biz (legal/compliance intake)
  • Quality/ops issues: qc@deedavis.biz
  • Escalate immediately for member safety, suspected fraud, or exclusion matches

Good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation. Reports may also be made to the applicable MCO or to federal/state authorities as required by law or contract.

4. Compliance & FWA Training

Workforce in FDR-adjacent roles must complete assigned CMS FDR curriculum items tracked in GATEWAY, including General Compliance / FWA and Medicare Fraud & Abuse training.

  • DDI internal target: 30 days from hire/engagement start for assigned items.
  • CMS hard floor: General Compliance/FWA and Medicare Fraud & Abuse must be completed within 90 days; missing the 90-day floor is a critical event that fails the can-work gate.
  • Recurrence: As defined in the GATEWAY training catalog (including annual reassignment at 11 months for specified FWA/COI items; member-facing items recur annually when the role is marked member-facing).

Certificates and completion status are retained in GATEWAY/NEXUS as audit evidence.

5. Exclusion Screening (OIG LEIE + GSA SAM.gov)

For FDR-applicable divisions, DDI screens workforce against:

  • HHS OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) — at hire/engagement and monthly thereafter
  • GSA SAM.gov exclusions/debarments — at hire/engagement and monthly thereafter

Screening results are logged in GATEWAY (date, result, notes). Open flagged matches require Compliance escalation and block can-work until resolved. Never-screened and overdue monthly rescreen also block can-work for FDR-applicable records.

6. Record Retention

FDR-related training, screening, acknowledgment, and audit records are retained for a minimum of ten (10) years consistent with 42 CFR 422.504(d) / applicable Medicaid flow-downs. GATEWAY records are archived, not hard-deleted.

7. Annual FDR Compliance Attestation

DDI maintains an organization-level Annual FDR Compliance Attestation on a calendar-year cycle (separate from individual hire dates), tracked in NEXUS HR FDR ATTESTATION and surfaced on the GATEWAY admin dashboard and alerts.

8. Downstream Entities

When DDI uses subcontractors or fulfillment partners on FDR-adjacent work, DDI remains responsible for contract management, compliance oversight, and ensuring applicable flow-downs (training, screening, BAA/PHI controls) are met before work begins. Partners are not disclosed to prospects as a consumer brand stack.

10. Discipline for Non-Compliance

Failure to complete required training, screening, or acknowledgments, or engaging in FWA, may result in removal from FDR-adjacent work, contract termination (for contractors), employment discipline (for employees), and reporting to the MCO or authorities as required.
