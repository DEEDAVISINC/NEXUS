Code of Conduct / Conflict of Interest Policy

Policy ID: DDI-HR-003 Version: 1.0 Effective: July 25, 2026 Applies to: Employees & contractors

For employees, this is a workplace policy. For independent contractors, this is a contractual flow-down obligation—not an employee handbook. Annual re-attestation may be required as assigned in GATEWAY training.

1. Purpose

Dee Davis Inc. expects honest, ethical, and professional conduct from everyone who represents the Company or performs work under its contracts. This policy defines standards of conduct and conflict-of-interest disclosure.

2. Standards of Conduct

  • Comply with applicable laws, contract terms, and DDI policies (including HIPAA and FDR requirements when they apply to your role).
  • Treat members, clients, colleagues, and vendors with respect; no harassment or unlawful discrimination.
  • Do not engage in fraud, waste, or abuse; report suspected FWA promptly (gc@deedavis.biz).
  • Protect confidential information, PHI, and PII; use minimum necessary access.
  • Do not offer or accept improper gifts or kickbacks related to government or MCO business.
  • Do not misrepresent DDI capabilities, certifications, or past performance.
  • Never reveal end-buyer identity to suppliers or unauthorized third parties.

3. Conflicts of Interest

A conflict exists when personal, financial, or outside interests could improperly influence—or appear to influence—decisions made for DDI. Examples include:

  • Financial interest in a vendor or competitor bidding against or supplying DDI
  • Family member working for a client, MCO, or competitor in a decision-making role over DDI work
  • Outside employment that uses DDI confidential information or conflicts with assigned duties
  • Accepting gifts that could influence procurement or referral decisions

Disclose actual or potential conflicts in writing to your manager/Engagement Manager and gc@deedavis.biz before the conflict affects work. Recusal or mitigation may be required.

4. Reporting & Non-Retaliation

Report violations of this policy in good faith without fear of retaliation. Managers who receive reports must escalate appropriately and may not suppress compliance concerns.

5. Violations

Violations may result in discipline up to termination of employment, termination of contractor engagement, removal from FDR-adjacent work, and reporting to clients or authorities as required.

6. Acknowledgment

By typing your full legal name in GATEWAY and submitting the acknowledgment for DDI-HR-003, you confirm that you have read this Code of Conduct / Conflict of Interest Policy (Version 1.0), will comply with it, and will disclose conflicts as required.
