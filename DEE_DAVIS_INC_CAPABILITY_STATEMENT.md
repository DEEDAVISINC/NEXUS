# DEE DAVIS, INC.
## Capability Statement

---

### COMPANY OVERVIEW

**Dee Davis, Inc.** is a certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) specializing in procurement, sourcing, and distribution of electrical equipment, industrial supplies, and materials for government and commercial clients. Based in Michigan, we leverage our extensive supplier network to deliver quality products at competitive prices with reliable, on-time delivery.
