## ✅ Qualification Checklist

For each opportunity, check:

**GREEN LIGHTS (Go for it!):**
- [ ] WOSB set-aside (major advantage!)
- [ ] Contract value < $150K (no bond usually)
- [ ] Michigan-based (local advantage)
- [ ] Janitorial, landscaping, or moving (easiest)
- [ ] Due date > 30 days away (time to prepare)

**YELLOW LIGHTS (Evaluate):**
- [ ] Contract value $150K-$500K (small bond may be needed)
- [ ] Out of state (doable, less advantage)
- [ ] IT or security (higher complexity)
- [ ] Performance bond mentioned (adds cost)

**RED LIGHTS (Skip for now):**
- [ ] Contract value > $1M (too big for first contract)
- [ ] Security clearances required
- [ ] Extensive past performance required
- [ ] Due in < 14 days (not enough time)

**Pick 3-5 opportunities with ALL green lights!**

## 📝 First Bid Preparation (1 Week)

### **Day 1-2: Find Your Sub**

**Janitorial Sub:**
- Google: "commercial janitorial services [your city]"
- Call 5-10 companies
- Ask: "Do you work on government contracts?"
- Get quotes and insurance certificates

**Landscaping Sub:**
- Google: "commercial landscaping [your city]"
- Same process

**What to ask:**
- Do you have workers comp insurance?
- Are you licensed/bonded?
- Have you done government work?
- Can you provide a quote for [project details]?
- Will you team with us as prime contractor?

### **Day 3-4: Teaming Agreement**

**Simple Version:**
```
TEAMING AGREEMENT

Prime Contractor: Dee Davis Inc.
Subcontractor: [Sub Company Name]
Project: [Opportunity Title]
Solicitation: [Number]

1. If Prime wins, Sub agrees to perform work as quoted
2. Sub provides proof of insurance and licenses
3. Prime manages client relationship and invoicing
4. Sub performs all cleaning/landscaping/etc. work
5. Payment terms: Net 30 after Prime receives payment

Signatures:
__________________  __________________
Dee Davis Inc.      [Sub Company]
Date: _______        Date: _______
```

### **Day 5-6: Write Proposal**

**Technical Response:**
```
Prime Contractor Approach:

Dee Davis Inc. (Prime, WOSB/EDWOSB) will serve as prime 
contractor, managing all client communications, scheduling,
quality control, and invoicing.

[Sub Company Name] (Key Subcontractor) will perform all 
[cleaning/landscaping/etc.] services. They bring:
- 10+ years experience (see attached resume)
- Fully licensed and insured (see attached certificates)
- [X] government contracts completed successfully
- All necessary equipment and trained staff

This team provides the client with:
✓ WOSB certification advantages
✓ Experienced service delivery
✓ Single point of contact (Prime)
✓ Proven track record
✓ Local Michigan-based team
```

**Pricing:**
1. Get sub's quote: $80,000
2. Add your margin (15%): $12,000
3. Add small buffer (3%): $2,400
4. **Your bid: $94,400**

### **Day 7: Submit!**

Upload:
- ✅ Technical response
- ✅ Pricing sheet
- ✅ Your insurance certificates
- ✅ Sub's insurance certificates
- ✅ Sub's licenses
- ✅ Teaming agreement
- ✅ Past performance (sub's if you don't have your own)

**SUBMITTED! 🎉**

## 💰 Easiest First Wins

### **Janitorial Services**
**Why:** Most common, many subs available, easy to manage  
**Target:** $75K-$150K contracts (no bond)  
**Where:** Federal buildings, schools, municipal offices
