## 💰 EDWOSB SOLE-SOURCE AUTHORITY

### **Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 19.1505:**

**Contracting Officers can award sole-source contracts to EDWOSB firms when:**
1. EDWOSB is certified and in good standing ✅
2. Contracting officer has reasonable expectation of fair pricing ✅
3. Contract value doesn't exceed $7M threshold ✅
4. EDWOSB is responsible and capable ✅

**Thresholds:**
- **Contracts:** Up to $7,000,000
- **GSA Schedule Orders:** Up to $4,500,000
- **BPAs (Blanket Purchase Agreements):** Up to $7,000,000

**Advantages:**
- ✅ NO competition required
- ✅ Faster award timeline (weeks vs. months)
- ✅ Negotiated pricing (you control margins)
- ✅ Agencies get socioeconomic credit
- ✅ Higher win probability (60-80% vs. 5-10%)

## 📊 BUSINESS MODEL: PRIME CONTRACTOR + SUBCONTRACTORS

### **Dee Davis Inc. Role (Prime Contractor):**

**What We Do:**
- ✅ Win federal contracts (using EDWOSB advantage)
- ✅ Manage client relationships (contracting officer interface)
- ✅ Coordinate service delivery (schedule, route, quality control)
- ✅ Invoice federal agency (get paid directly)
- ✅ Manage subcontractors (performance, payments)
- ✅ Ensure compliance (reporting, insurance, bonding)
- ✅ Provide single point of contact for federal agency

**What We Don't Do:**
- ❌ Direct service delivery (subs do the actual work)
- ❌ Employ specialized technicians (subs provide personnel)
- ❌ Maintain specialized equipment (subs provide)

**Our Value:**
- EDWOSB certification = contract access
- Contract management expertise
- Federal compliance knowledge
- Quality assurance and oversight
- Financial management
- Risk mitigation

### **Subcontractor Role:**

**What They Do:**
- ✅ Provide specialized services (notary, courier, testing, etc.)
- ✅ Supply qualified personnel
- ✅ Maintain equipment and facilities
- ✅ Perform day-to-day service delivery
- ✅ Meet quality and compliance standards

**What They Don't Do:**
- ❌ Interface with federal agency (we manage relationship)
- ❌ Handle federal paperwork (we manage contracts)
- ❌ Get paid directly by agency (we pay them)

### **Financial Model:**

**Example: $500K Federal Contract**

**Revenue:** $500,000 (paid by federal agency to Dee Davis Inc.)

**Costs:**
- Subcontractor services: $400,000 (80% of contract value)
- Insurance/overhead: $25,000 (5%)
- Contract management: $25,000 (5%)

**Gross Profit:** $50,000 (10% margin)

**Or with higher margin:**
- Subcontractor services: $375,000 (75%)
- Overhead: $25,000 (5%)
- **Gross Profit:** $100,000 (20% margin)

**Typical Margins by Service:**
- Courier: 15-20%
- Notary: 20-30%
- Drug screening: 15-20%
- Fingerprinting: 20-25%
- VAR: 10-15% (higher volume, lower margin)
