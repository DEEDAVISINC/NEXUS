## QUALITY CHECK

### Before writing the proposal, verify:

- [ ] **Problem Mastery:** Can I explain the REAL problem better than the RFP does?
- [ ] **Education:** Will the client learn something new about their challenge?
- [ ] **Quantified Outcomes:** Do I have at least 3 specific numbers/metrics to include?
- [ ] **Proof:** Do I have 2+ case studies with measurable outcomes?
- [ ] **Differentiation:** Could a competitor copy this? (If yes, add more unique insight)
- [ ] **Transformation Focus:** Does this describe what THEY'LL GET, not what WE'VE DONE?

### Red Flags (If ANY of these are true, STOP and revise):

- [ ] I don't have quantified outcomes
- [ ] I don't have proof/case studies with metrics
- [ ] I can't articulate the root cause of their problem
- [ ] This could be anyone's proposal
- [ ] I'm listing credentials without connecting to outcomes
- [ ] I can't explain what they DON'T understand about their problem
