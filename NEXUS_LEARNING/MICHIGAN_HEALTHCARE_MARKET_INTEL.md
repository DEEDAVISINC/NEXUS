## ACTIVE DISRUPTIONS

### Michigan Medicine / BCBS Contract Dispute (CRITICAL)

**Date:** May 13, 2026  
**Source:** Small Business Association of Michigan (Brian Calley)  
**Status:** Negotiations ongoing

**Summary:**
Michigan Medicine (University of Michigan Health) announced their Southeast Michigan facilities and physicians will **no longer accept Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan or Blue Care Network** effective **July 1, 2026** — unless a new payment contract is signed.

**Impact:**
- Affects all Michigan Medicine Southeast Michigan facilities
- Employers and employees with BCBS/BCN coverage may lose access to U of M providers
- SBAM released a toolkit for employers to prepare

**DDI Relevance:**

| Area | Impact |
|---|---|
| **VITAL Outreach** | U of M is on target list — disruption may create openings for cost-saving conversations |
| **HAVEN/MCO** | If BCBS members shift away from Michigan Medicine, other health systems (Corewell, Henry Ford, Ascension) may see volume increases |
| **Timing** | Consider reaching out to U of M procurement NOW — before July 1 deadline |

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Monitor for contract resolution or confirmation of termination
- [ ] Consider VITAL outreach to U of M emphasizing cost efficiency
- [ ] Watch for BCBS member migration patterns to other systems

## IMPLICATIONS FOR DDI

### If Michigan Medicine Drops BCBS:

1. **Patient Migration** — BCBS members needing care will shift to Corewell, Henry Ford, Ascension, McLaren
2. **Volume Surge at Other Systems** — More lab specimens, more pharmacy deliveries, more courier needs
3. **Cost Pressure** — Systems absorbing new volume will look for efficiency gains
4. **DDI Opportunity** — Position VITAL as cost-efficient courier solution during transition

### Outreach Strategy Adjustment:

| Target | Timing | Message Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **U of M Health** | NOW (before July 1) | "Cost efficiency during contract negotiations" |
| **Corewell, Henry Ford, Ascension** | June-July | "Capacity support during BCBS member migration" |
| **BCBS Michigan** | Monitor | May need new provider network logistics |
