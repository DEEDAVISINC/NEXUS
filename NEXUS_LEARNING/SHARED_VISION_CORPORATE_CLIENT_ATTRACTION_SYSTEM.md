## Why this matters for DDI

Organizational buyers (agencies, MCOs, health systems, transit authorities, foundations) do not buy from cold capability dumps. They buy after a **low-pressure exploration** confirms shared mission, credible authority, and a path to collaborate.

Safi Media's framework produced **$899K in group coaching sales in under 10 months** using a **15-minute Shared Vision Meeting** as the top of funnel. DDI applies the same mechanics to **contract management TPA** relationships — vendor credentialing, overflow wedges, program pilots, and eventually formal solicitations.

**The math:** More Shared Vision Meetings → more Discovery Meetings → more proposals under review → more revenue.

## The 3-stage pipeline (DDI mapping)

| Safi stage | Duration | DDI name | When to use |
|------------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Shared Vision Meeting™** | 15 min (max 30) | **Shared Vision Meeting** | First touch — explore fit, shared mission, collaboration window |
| **Selling To Corporate Meeting™** | 45–60 min | **Discovery / Scope Meeting** | Second touch — pain points, buying process, wedge or win theme, proposal path |
| **Corporate Close Meeting™** | 45–60 min | **Proposal Review / Close Meeting** | Present proposal to decision-makers; heat check, answer in spirit, or firm next step |

### DDI opportunity-type gate (run BEFORE Stage 1)

| If the buyer situation is… | Skip Shared Vision? | Start at… |
|----------------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Open RFP/RFQ with deadline | Often yes | COMPETITIVE bid track — questions + proposal |
| Micro-purchase / SAT direct ask | Yes | DIRECT quote — perform now |
| Warm intro, presolicitation, MCO credentialing, overflow ask | **No — Shared Vision is perfect** | Stage 1 |
| Incumbent failing + recompete coming | Maybe | ALTERNATIVE — Shared Vision + recompete prep |

**Rule:** Shared Vision is for **RELATIONSHIP** and early **ALTERNATIVE** lanes — not a substitute for submitting a formal bid when a solicitation is open.

## Stage 1 — How to land Shared Vision Meetings

### Step A: Identify organizations with shared vision

Target buyers who **need what DDI sells** AND **signal alignment** with DDI's mission and certs.

**DDI shared-vision signals (look for clues):**

| Buyer type | Shared-vision clues |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Federal agency** | WOSB/EDWOSB contracting goals, small-business office, socioeconomic plans |
| **State/local procurement** | MBE/WBE/EDWOSB preference, local vendor programs, diversity evaluation points |
| **MCO / Medicaid plan** | SDOH/HRSN investments, dual-eligible SNP focus, health equity language |
| **Transit authority** | FTA drug/alcohol compliance, safety culture, DBE goals |
| **Hospital / health system** | Community benefit reports, workforce screening, lab logistics modernization |
| **AAA / aging services** | Older Americans Act programs, senior NEMT, benefits navigation |
| **Foundation / nonprofit funder** | Population served matches DDI lanes (MOB-A, SHIELD, navigation) |

**Reconnaissance checklist:**
- [ ] Annual report / DEI or small-business section
- [ ] Press releases on workforce, health access, or vendor diversity
- [ ] Event sponsorships (women in leadership, health equity, transit safety)
- [ ] SAM.gov / portal registration — are they already a buyer in DDI's NAICS?
- [ ] Mutual connections on LinkedIn for warm intro

**Population lane discipline:** When transport or mobility appears, classify **MOB-A** (plan NEMT), **MOB-B** (HAVEN/disaster continuity), or **MOB-C** (freight) per `DDI_SERVICE_POPULATION_LANES.md` — never merge lanes in outreach copy.

### Step B: Secure warm intro (when possible)

**Subject:** quick favour?

## Stage 1 — Shared Vision Meeting agenda (15 minutes)

**You lead the meeting.** Confirm hard stop at start.

### 1. Open and set expectations (~2 min)

```
[NAME]! I'm glad you made this connection a priority.

We clearly share a commitment to [NICHE — their words].

My goal for this meeting is threefold:
1. Learn more about your goals for [NICHE] in the next 12 months
2. Share a brief insight about how Dee Davis Inc. supports organizations like yours
3. Explore whether there are opportunities to collaborate in the next 3–12 months

Does that sound good?

And just checking — do you have a hard stop at [TIME]? 
```

If no hard stop, agree **no more than 30 minutes total**.

### 2. Discovery — their priorities and how they partner (~5–7 min)

Use **CO question reframes** where natural (see `human-touch-correspondence.mdc`):

- What are your priorities with respect to **[niche]** in the next year?
- How do you measure success in this area?
- What has worked well for you with external partners in the past?
- What are the ways you typically work with vendors or program administrators like us?
- *(Reframe option)* Where does your team need the most support right now?
- *(Reframe option)* What usually delays requirements or vendor onboarding in your shop?

**Listen for:** incumbent vendor, overflow pain, compliance gaps, EDWOSB/socioeconomic pressure, timeline, who else must approve.

### 3. Present DDI as potential partner (~5 min)

**RELATIONSHIP posture (default):**

```
I appreciate you sharing that — it helps me understand how you operate.

Based on what you've described, it sounds like a strong fit for the work we do. We are not asking [ORGANIZATION] to replace [incumbent category]. Dee Davis Inc. is a nationwide contract management TPA — EDWOSB — available as additional capacity for [ONE WEDGE: overflow geography / rush post-accident testing / SNP slice / credentialing surge / etc.].

Here's what we do:
- [One sentence — program administration under single prime contract]
- [One proof — HAP live / MI Bridges 6+ years / SAM active / real-time NEXUS ops]
- [One example — similar buyer type, no confidential names if sensitive]

I can't say for sure yet, but it sounds like there could be meaningful ways to partner around [SHARED VISION] in the next 12 months.

I've got some ideas, but I'd like to flesh them out properly. Would you be open to a longer working session to walk through them?
```

**Do NOT name fulfillment partners/subvendors** in this meeting (`never-name-subvendors-network.mdc`).

### 4. Book Stage 2 — Discovery Meeting (~2 min)

```
Awesome. I need a little time to prepare something specific to your priorities. 
Let's set up a 45-minute working session. When might work for you after 12:00 PM ET?
```

Capture: date, attendees, their prep expectations, internal buying steps they mention.

## Stage 2 — Discovery / Scope Meeting (45–60 min)

**Purpose:** Expertly guide buyer through structured discovery — pain, process, stakeholders, budget signals, proposal path.

### DDI discovery agenda

| Block | Time | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| Recap Shared Vision | 5 min | Confirm priorities still accurate |
| Deep pain / risk | 15 min | Operational failures, compliance exposure, member/population impact |
| Buying process map | 10 min | Who decides, procurement vehicle, timeline, set-aside, incumbent |
| Solution sketch | 15 min | DDI approach — **one contract, one invoice**, national TPA, EDWOSB value |
| Scope & next step | 10 min | Pilot vs RFP vs credentialing packet vs micro-purchase |
| Close | 5 min | Proposal review date, data needed from them, internal champions |

**Questions that show homework (pick 4–6):**
- Where do vendors usually fall short on contracts like this?
- What constraints matter most — budget, timeline, or audit/compliance?
- Is there an incumbent, and are you looking for overflow capacity or a formal recompete path?
- Would a micro-purchase pilot under $15K or SAT path under $350K help you move faster?
- Who else should be in the room for a proposal review?

**Output:** Internal brief → bid folder if competitive, or custom one-pager / pilot SOW for relationship track.

## Stage 3 — Proposal Review / Close Meeting (45–60 min)

**Purpose:** Present proposal to **all key decision-makers** when possible. Secure one of:
- **Heat check** — strong interest, minor edits
- **Answer in spirit** — verbal yes pending procurement mechanics
- **Firm next step** — award timeline, contract signature, or formal submission confirmation

### Agenda

1. **Executive summary** — client outcomes + risk reduction (see `executive-summary-selection-justification.mdc`)
2. **Scope alignment** — map to their stated priorities from Stage 1–2
3. **Pricing / vehicle** — agency rates, EDWOSB advantage, SAT/micro-purchase if applicable
4. **Q&A** — objections = fear; answer with ownership, QA, escalation
5. **Close** — "What would you need to see to move this forward internally?"

**Federal CO closing line (when relevant):**
> For immediate needs under $15,000, Dee Davis Inc. is available for direct micro-purchase — no competition required, SAM-registered and ready. For needs up to $350,000, we're available under simplified acquisition procedures with fast turnaround and reduced competition requirements.
