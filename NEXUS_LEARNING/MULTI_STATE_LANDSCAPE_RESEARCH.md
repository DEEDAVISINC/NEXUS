## North Carolina — Reference Model

**Healthy Opportunities Pilots**
- $650M authorized; 29 evidence-based services via CBOs
- Outcomes: −22 ER visits/1,000 (12+ mo participants); ~$85/member/month savings
- **JAMA**, March 2025

| NC role | DDI/CWC equivalent |
|---------|-------------------|
| Network Leads | **CWC** — community trust, coordination |
| Human Service Organizations | CBOs needing TPA support → **DDI** |
| Prepaid Health Plans | MCOs → DDI existing relationships (HAP, BCC target) |
