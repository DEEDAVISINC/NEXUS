# TEXAS INTEGRATED D-SNP — INTEL SUMMARY

## WHAT IT IS

Texas ended Medicare-Medicaid Plans (MMP) on December 31, 2025 (CMS Financial Alignment Initiative sunset). As of January 1, 2026, Texas runs Integrated Dual-Eligible Special Needs Plans (Integrated D-SNPs) in 5 counties only.

Integration tier: Texas = HIDE SNP (Highly Integrated Dual Eligible), NOT FIDE — per Aurrera Health / CMS state transition classification. (Molina provider FAQ says "FIDE" — use HHSC HIDE/Integrated D-SNP language in buyer-facing docs.)

Texas vs Michigan:
- Michigan: One state-awarded HIDE SNP contract per MCO (Medicare + Medicaid + LTSS integrated).
- Texas: Two linked plans from same parent:
  - Integrated D-SNP = Medicare (CMS)
  - Aligned STAR+PLUS MCO = Medicaid (HHSC)
- One ID card, integrated appeals/grievances — but TWO contracts, not single three-way MMP.

## TEXAS vs MICHIGAN HIDE

Michigan MICH/HIDE
  Launch: Jan 2026, 9 MCO awardees
  Geography: Regional → statewide 2027
  Model: State HIDE SNP award (DTMB RFP 240000002334)
  DDI: HAP CareSource live (~4,500 Wayne/Macomb)

Texas Integrated D-SNP
  Launch: Jan 2026, 3 parents, 5 counties
  Geography: Regional only — no statewide Integrated tier announced
  Model: CMS D-SNP + aligned STAR+PLUS (EAE)
  DDI: None — Molina TX declined TPA May 2026
