# Michigan HIDE SNP — NEMT Contract Language (Appendix M & N)

**Created:** June 12, 2026  
**Purpose:** Source-of-truth excerpts for MCO outreach, Brian/HAP call prep, and rebuttal when plans claim “brokers only.”  
**Applies to:** All **9** MDHHS-awarded HIDE SNP contractors under RFP **240000002334** — same contract template, same appendices.

---

## Executive summary

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does HIDE SNP **ban** NEMT brokers? | **No** — no such language in Appendix N or elsewhere |
| Can MCOs use subcontractors for NEMT? | **Yes** — “transportation Subcontractors,” “NEMT Subcontractor” explicitly referenced |
| Can DDI contract **direct** with any awardee? | **Yes** — as credentialed NEMT provider and/or subcontractor/administrator in plan network |
| Does “Agents and Brokers” (§2.5) block trip brokers? | **No** — that section is **plan enrollment/marketing** (42 CFR 422.2274), not NEMT |
| Are Uber/Lyft/TNCs allowed? | **Yes** — Appendix N § C.e |
| Same rules for all 9 MCOs? | **Yes** — one state contract framework; plans differ only in **operational** vendor choice |

**Rebuttal line:** *“We only use our broker”* = plan **habit**, not HIDE SNP contract requirement.

## Section 2.5 — “Agents and Brokers” (NOT NEMT)

**Contract § 2.5 Methods of Enrollment — A. Using a Medicare Advantage Agent or Broker**

> If Contractor uses Agents and Brokers or makes payments to third party marketing organizations to sell its HIDE SNP plan, Contractor must adhere to all applicable Michigan laws and regulations related to the licensure and activities of Agents and Brokers and follow all requirements set forth in 42 CFR 422.2274 and 423.2274 as applicable.

> Contractor must ensure brokers have access to trainings specific to LTSS.

**NEXUS note:** This governs **Medicare Advantage enrollment marketing** — not MTM, ModivCare, SafeRide, or other **NEMT trip administration** vendors.

## Appendix N — Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (full text)

*Verbatim from HIDE SNP contract; line breaks preserved for searchability.*

### A. General

**a.** Contractor must provide non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) to and from medically necessary Medicare services, Medicaid Covered Services, and Medicaid services covered outside of this Contract in accordance with Medicaid policy.

**b.** NEMT includes transportation services as well as reimbursement for travel expenses incurred by or on behalf of the Enrollee.

**c.** Contractor must ensure NEMT is available 24 hours per day, 7 days a week, 365 days per year.

**d.** Contractor failure to adhere to NEMT requirements as outlined in this Contract may lead to MDHHS pursuit of contract remedies, including but not limited to monetary Sanctions and other Sanctions, such as corrective action.

**e.** Contractor must develop and maintain a **network of qualified NEMT providers**.

**f.** Contractor must ensure that its network has a sufficient number of vehicles and drivers available to provide safe, appropriate, and reliable transportation services to meet the requirements set forth in this Contract.

**g.** Contractor must have ability to schedule and provide Enrollee NEMT services for:

- **i.** Ongoing services (dialysis, chemotherapy, SUD office services, PT, speech, OT)
- **ii.** Regularly scheduled services — Contractor may require reasonable advance notice (**e.g., 48 – 72 hours**)
- **iii.** Urgent services — same day as or day following the request

**h.** Contractor must ensure NEMT providers comply with federal and State transportation safety standards (seatbelts, child seats, heating/AC, driver assists with seatbelts, capacity limits, safe parking at destination, vehicle visible to driver).

**i.** Driver must park safely and notify dispatcher if passenger behavior impedes safe operation; behavior issues reported to Contractor.

**j.** Contractor must establish and maintain a **toll-free telephone number** available **24/7** for NEMT requests.

**k.** May share main member line but must have a **dedicated line for NEMT requests**.

**l.** NEMT number must be on the **Enrollee HIDE SNP ID card**.

**m.** Contractor must submit to MDHHS at least annually its NEMT policies and procedures, including reimbursement of Enrollee travel expenses. Contractor must submit **equivalent policies and procedures for transportation Subcontractors**. Contractor must provide monitoring documentation for **Subcontractors** (passenger safety, timely access, complaints, mileage reimbursement, vehicle inspections).

**n.** Contractor/**subcontractor** policies must include provisions for:

- **o.** Notifying Enrollees of NEMT benefit and mileage reimbursement when self/family transport is appropriate
- **p.** Receiving requests, approving services, **scheduling, assigning, and dispatching NEMT providers**
- **q.**–**r.** Mode selection for medically fragile, I/DD, pregnancy, infants, escorts, car seats, housing/confidentiality needs
- **s.** Special transport (wheelchair lift, Medi-Van, attendants, etc.)
- **t.** Ask about special transport needs at scheduling
- **u.** Notify arrangements; **24-hour reminder** call when applicable
- **v.** No excessive multi-loading — not **>30 minutes** beyond average direct travel time
- **w.** Pick-up/drop-off standards:
  - Pre-arranged times with provider and Enrollee/representative
  - **≤15 minutes** wait past scheduled pick-up; driver waits **15 minutes**, notifies dispatcher before leaving
  - Not required at appointment **>1 hour** early
  - Not dropped off before facility opens (unless requested)
  - Picked up within **1 hour** if no prearranged time
  - Not picked up from appointment **>15 minutes** after close (unless requested)
  - Contingency plans; delay notification; alternative transport; documented rescheduling
- **xiii.** Mileage reimbursement method
- **xiv.** Escort/attendant policies per Medicaid Provider Manual

### B. NEMT complaint resolution and escalation processes

**a.** Prior auth for overnight travel if **<50 miles** — may not deny on distance alone. CSHCS accommodations required. Contractor must document each trip: Enrollee ID, service reason, destination, scheduled/actual pick-up and drop-off.

**b.**–**c.** Delays, missed trips, complaints (name, date, reason, resolution).

**d.** **Annual NEMT evaluation report** to MDHHS — must include **NEMT Subcontractor non-compliance** findings and corrective action.

**e.–f.** Delayed/missed trip counts and percentages.

**g.** Grievance/appeal documentation; driver/subcontractor corrective action.

**h.** Grievance log must include: complaint reason, complainant name, NEMT service date, **transportation provider name**, **driver name and driver’s license number**, resolution, resolution date (provided to MDHHS on request).

**i.** Consolidated Appropriations Act 2021 Div. CC Title II §209 — NEMT provider/driver verification.

**j.** Driver vetting (exclusions, valid license, controlled substance felonies, driving history, OWI/DUI/moving violation rules with family-driver exceptions).

### C. NEMT Provision Applicability

**a.** Not applicable to public transit authority.

**b.** Not applicable to the Enrollee.

**c.** Applicable to drivers with vested interest (family, caretaker, friend, neighbor).

**d.** Applicable to taxicab drivers.

**e.** Applicable to **transportation network companies, including but not limited to Uber, Lyft and other rideshare companies.**

## What Appendix N does NOT require

- Single statewide NEMT broker only
- Prohibition on brokers, TPAs, or third-party administrators
- Direct fleet ownership by the MCO
- Exclusion of credentialed direct providers from the network

**What it DOES require:** MCO **accountability** — qualified network, 24/7 access, dispatch/SLA standards, documentation, annual report, subcontractor monitoring.
