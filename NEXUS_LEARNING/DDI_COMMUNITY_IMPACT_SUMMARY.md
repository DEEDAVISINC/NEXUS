**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

## One paragraph

Dee Davis Inc. and Cause We Care connect **law and benefits on paper** to **real outcomes** for people: children get lead tests and follow-through when levels are high; Medicaid members get rides to medical care; after disasters, displaced families keep shelter, trips to care, and medications. NEXUS, PRISM, SHIELD, HAVEN, and VERTEX are how one **national EDWOSB prime** coordinates that work — so families are not left calling ten agencies alone.
