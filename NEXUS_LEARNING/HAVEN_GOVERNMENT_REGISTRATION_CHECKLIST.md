# HAVEN Government Registration Checklist
**Created:** May 9, 2026
**Purpose:** Get DDI registered as a disaster response contractor with FEMA and all 4 target state emergency management agencies

---

## STEP 1: SAM.gov — Disaster Response Registry ⚠️ CRITICAL FIRST

DDI is already registered on SAM.gov — but needs to **opt into the Disaster Response Registry**.

**Action:**
1. Log into sam.gov → Entity Registrations → Dee Davis Inc. → Edit Registration
2. Navigate to **Assertions** module
3. Find **"Disaster Response Information"** page
4. Check the box to participate in the **Disaster Response Registry**
5. Select relevant disaster response services:
   - Emergency shelter/housing coordination
   - Emergency transportation services
   - Medical services coordination
   - Temporary staffing
   - Logistics and supply chain
6. Save and submit

**Why:** FEMA and other federal agencies search this registry when they need contractors during disasters. If DDI isn't opted in, they won't find us.

**Time:** 15 minutes
**Cost:** Free

## STEP 3: FEMA — Doing Business Registration

**Portal:** https://www.fema.gov/doing-business

**FEMA doesn't have a separate vendor portal** — they use SAM.gov (Step 1 above). The key actions:

1. ✅ SAM.gov registration (DDI already has this)
2. ⚠️ Opt into Disaster Response Registry (Step 1 above)
3. ⚠️ Register for FEMA Industry Day notifications at: https://www.fema.gov/doing-business
4. ⚠️ Follow FEMA on SAM.gov for solicitation alerts
5. ⚠️ Contact local APEX Accelerator for FEMA-specific guidance (free)

**FEMA Transportation Service Provider (TSP):**
- If DDI wants to provide evacuation transport directly to FEMA
- 2026 registration deadline has passed (Feb 20, 2026) — **target 2027 cycle**
- Opens February 2027 for 2027 hurricane season

**Time:** 30 minutes
**Cost:** Free

## STEP 4: Florida — Division of Emergency Management (FDEM)

**Two-step registration:**

### Step 4A: MyFloridaMarketPlace (MFMP)
- **Portal:** https://vendor.myfloridamarketplace.com/
- Register as a vendor to access FL state solicitations
- **Help:** 1-866-352-3776

### Step 4B: Substitute W-9 with FL DFS
- **Portal:** https://flvendor.myfloridacfo.com/
- Submit W-9 so FL can issue payments
- **Help:** 850-413-5519 or FLW9@myfloridacfo.com

### Procurement Contact:
- **Director of Procurement:** Tara Walters — 850-815-4606
- **Website:** https://prod.floridadisaster.org/dem/procurement-and-contract-management/

**Note:** FL FDEM had an Emergency Response & Disaster Recovery Services bid closing April 29, 2026 — check if there are follow-up opportunities.

**Time:** 45 minutes
**Cost:** Free

## STEP 5: Texas — Division of Emergency Management (TDEM)

### Step 5A: AggieBid Registration
- TDEM is part of Texas A&M University System
- **Portal:** AggieBid (Jaggaer) Supplier Portal
- Register to access TDEM solicitations (ITBs, RFPs, RFIs, RFQs)

### Step 5B: Texas CMBL (Certified Master Bidder List)
- Register on the State of Texas CMBL (recommended)
- Receives automatic bid notifications for disaster contracts

**Website:** https://www.tdem.texas.gov/about-us/finance

**Time:** 30 minutes
**Cost:** Free
