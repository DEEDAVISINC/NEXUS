# NEMT & Large Program Contracts — Multi-Vendor Slice Strategy

**Added:** May 29, 2026  
**Updated:** May 29, 2026 — owner clarification  

**Owner decision:** DDI does **not** need the whole statewide broker contract. DDI wants to be **one of the companies on the contract** — alongside 2–3 other awardees — not ModivCare-scale sole prime.

**Applies to:** NEMT, Medicaid NET brokers, MCO programs, any megacontract where multiple vendors share the work.

**Related:** `DDI_BUSINESS_MODEL.md` · `MAINE NEMT TEAMING/` · `MODIVCARE_COMPETITIVE_INTEL.md`

---

## Core Rule

> **The contract is often split among several companies. DDI wants to be one of them.**

Not:
- ❌ Sole statewide broker ($750M Maine / $103M Michigan headline)
- ❌ Replacing ModivCare as the only name on the award

Yes:
- ✅ **Named awardee** for a region, CLIN, or service line
- ✅ **One vendor on a multi-company panel** (2–3+ primes sharing the program)
- ✅ **Credentialed network participant** under the broker (if RFP structure is single broker + vendor network)
- ✅ **Teaming partner inside a winning bid** (e.g., EDWOSB + TPA on Penquis’s regional proposal — DDI is on the contract as part of that team)

## Maine — how it actually worked (2023 RFP)

**MaineCare NET is not inherently one company.**

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Structure** | **8 transportation regions** — bidders submitted **per region** |
| **Historical operators** | Penquis, Waldo CAP, ModivCare, and others served **different regions** before consolidation attempt |
| **2023 bidders** | ModivCare (all 8 regions), Penquis (4 regions), Waldo CAP (1 region), Verida (3 regions), plus EPICMD, MTM, WellTrans |
| **Flawed outcome** | State awarded **ModivCare statewide** — consolidating what had been **multi-vendor / regional** |
| **Recompete (0520260310)** | Political pressure to **reissue** — expect return toward **multiple vendors**, not one Colorado broker for everything |

**DDI goal on Maine recompete:** Be **one of the awarded companies** — ideally by:
1. **Teaming with Penquis/Waldo** on their regional bid (DDI on the proposal = on the contract), or  
2. **Bidding a region** directly if RFP allows zero-fleet TPA/broker model, or  
3. **Network/vendor role** under whichever brokers win each region  

Headline **$750M / 10 yr** = **entire program**. DDI revenue = **DDI’s region(s) or role only**.

## GO / NO-GO

**Bid as named awardee (region or panel) only if:**
- [ ] RFP allows DDI’s model (zero-fleet TPA/broker or teaming structure)
- [ ] Operator partner locked if fleet required
- [ ] Margin on **DDI’s award share** meets floor
- [ ] Dee approves

**Default:** Find the path to be **on the contract** — not **the** contract.

## NEXUS behavior

1. **Read RFP:** Single broker vs **multi-region awards** vs **vendor network**?
2. **Count awardees:** How many companies can win?
3. **Revenue analysis:** DDI’s share only — never whole-program headline as DDI revenue
4. **Outreach:** Teaming emails = path to be **on** partner’s award, not just advisory
