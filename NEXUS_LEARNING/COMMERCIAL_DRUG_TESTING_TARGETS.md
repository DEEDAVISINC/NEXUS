## HOW TO FIND TRUCKING COMPANIES

### Data Sources
| Source | What It Provides | How to Access |
|---|---|---|
| **FMCSA SAFER** | Carrier name, DOT#, fleet size, safety rating | safer.fmcsa.dot.gov (free) |
| **FMCSA Licensing & Insurance** | Active carriers by state | li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov |
| **State Trucking Associations** | Member directories | Google "[State] Trucking Association" |
| **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** | Owner/operator contacts | Paid subscription |
| **ZoomInfo/Apollo** | Company + contact data | Paid subscription |
| **DAT/Truckstop Directories** | Carrier search | Industry platforms |
| **Local Business Directories** | Small fleets | Google, Yelp, Yellow Pages |

### FMCSA SAFER Search Strategy
1. Go to safer.fmcsa.dot.gov
2. Search by state (start with MI, TX, OH, FL, GA)
3. Filter: "Authorized For Hire" + "Property" carrier
4. Target: 10-200 power units (sweet spot for C/TPA)
5. Export list → build outreach campaign

### Search Keywords for Carriers

- "[City] LTL carrier"
- "[City] flatbed trucking"

- "[City] intermodal"
