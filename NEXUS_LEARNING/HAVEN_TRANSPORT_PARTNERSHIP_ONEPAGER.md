## WHAT WE'RE BUILDING: HAVEN

**HAVEN** (Housing, Assistance, Vital Emergency Network) is DDI's disaster response TPA platform.

When disasters strike, health plan members are displaced. They need:
- **Housing** — Temporary shelter coordination
- **Transportation** — Evacuation and medical transport
- **Medical Continuity** — Rx, DME, home health services

MCOs are responsible for member continuity but lack disaster response infrastructure. **DDI provides the coordination layer.**

---

## THE TRANSPORT NEED

**Pre-Disaster:**
- Evacuation of vulnerable members (dialysis patients, mobility-impaired, nursing home residents)
- Transport to staging areas and shelters

**Post-Disaster:**
- Medical appointment transport for displaced members
- Pharmacy pickup and delivery coordination
- Return transport when areas reopen

**Volume Potential:**
- Single hurricane event: 2,000-10,000+ transport requests
- Ongoing displacement period: 30-90 days of elevated demand
- Geographic scope: Gulf Coast, Florida, Texas, Southeast initially

---

## PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

**DDI's Role:**
- MCO relationships and contracts
- Member intake and needs assessment
- Dispatch coordination and case management
- Billing and reporting to payers
- Quality monitoring and compliance

**Transport Partner's Role:**
- Ride fulfillment (drivers, vehicles)
- Surge capacity during disasters
- HIPAA-compliant service delivery
- Integration via API or concierge dispatch

**Revenue Model:**
- DDI bills MCO for transport services
- DDI pays transport partner per-ride or negotiated rate
- DDI retains coordination margin
