# DDI / CWC Four Pillars Brand Framework

**Established:** May 30, 2026  
**Source session:** `SESSION_SUMMARY_MAY_30_2026.md`  
**Use in:** Website, program narrative headers, funder-facing materials, capability statements (content only — not layout)

---

## Philosophy

**Stability. Accessibility. Capability. Equitability.**

All DDI/CWC programs operate under this unified brand. CWC is the **equitability layer**. DDI is the **stability, accessibility, and capability engine**.

---

## Pillar Definitions

| Pillar | Definition | DDI/CWC Services |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Stability** | The foundation is secure | Housing TPA, drug testing compliance, family navigation, recovery support |
| **Accessibility** | You can get to what you need | DEPOINTE NEMT, credentialed courier network, specimen courier |
| **Capability** | You have the tools to succeed | MI Bridges digital navigation, NEXUS systems, CWC navigators, occupational health |
| **Equitability** | You have the right to receive it | CWC community trust layer, Medicaid enrollment, benefits coordination, program advocacy |

---

## Entity Split

| Entity | Pillars owned |
|--------|----------------|
| **Cause We Care (501c3)** | Equitability — community trust, population access, advocacy |
| **Dee Davis Inc.** | Stability + Accessibility + Capability — TPA operations, compliance, delivery, reporting |
