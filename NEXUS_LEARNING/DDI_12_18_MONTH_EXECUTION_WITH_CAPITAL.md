# 12–18 Month Execution — Multiple Six-Figure Packages

**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026  
**Scenario:** DDI secures **several $100K–$500K+ annual programs** (plus HAP-scale NEMT).  
**Goal:** Operate without breaking; bill without leaking; scale only what’s proven.

---

## What “multiple six-figure packages” means

| Annual secured | What changes |
|----------------|--------------|
| **$300K–$600K** | 1 ops lead + billing discipline; still Dee-heavy |
| **$600K–$1.2M** | Program managers + SHIELD pilot possible |
| **$1.2M–$2M+** | Navigator team + multi-state entity work + HAVEN Phase 2 |

**HAP (~$540K)** counts as one package. “Multiple” = **2–4 more** in same band.

---

## North star

Turn NEXUS from architecture into **repeatable delivery**: **PRISM → program ops → VERTEX**, headcount matched to **live volume**, not pipeline fantasy.

---

## Months 0–6 — Stabilize

### VERTEX (first)
- Every contract in VERTEX; weekly invoicing; program P&L monthly.
- Factoring path clear for government AR.
- Monday dashboard: cash, AR>60d, margin by program.

### PRISM
- Close gaps for active contracts (background module, NEMT trip closeout, freight if needed).
- Standard order types per contract.

### NEMT / HAP
- Document HAP case file (trips, SLA, complaints) → **HAVEN proof deck**.
- Uber Health: ACH, 3P wheelchair, pricing locked.
- **1–2 more MCO slices** — not 40-state spray.
- HAVEN: FL/LA corp if pitching there; **contract clause language** only — not full build.

### SHIELD (if MDHHS path real)
- Vendor intros (abatement, nurse visit, emergency housing).
- Wayne: **3–5 navigators + 1 supervisor** (CWC).
- Airtable + SLA automations; **25% capture ramp**, not 50% day one.

### Stop / defer
- Mass MCO email without signed slice.
- HAVEN Phase 4 before Phase 1 partners signed.
- TPA 8/9 marketing without anchor client.
- 3D Ink 100-target blast before NEMT billing is boring-reliable.

---

## Months 6–12 — Scale what works

### SHIELD (if live)
- Hit KPIs on MDHHS portal.
- Oakland only after Wayne stable.
- VERTEX ↔ SHIELD billing automation.
- Full-time navigator supervisor.

### NEMT + HAVEN
- 2–3 six-figure MCO relationships.
- **HAVEN rider** in every MCO contract.
- HAVEN Phase 2: manual displaced-member list dispatch.
- LA/FL foreign corp where pitching.

### Other six-figure mix
- **Transit C/TPA** — low touch, high margin.
- **Courier** (if UH-scale) — **separate PM**, not SHIELD navigators.
- **3D Ink B2B** — part-time scheduler; Dee on CNTDA only.

### Systems priority
1. VERTEX program P&L  
2. SHIELD MDHHS portal outcomes from Airtable  
3. HAVEN registry + manual dispatch  
4. PRISM background + freight if mix requires  

**Target month 12:** $1.5–3M run-rate; 2 program managers; VERTEX trusted.

## Two horses rule (12 months)

**Pick 2 operational focuses:**
1. **NEMT stack** (HAP → second slice → HAVEN clause)  
2. **SHIELD OR C/TPA cluster** (transit + county) — not both full scale without **$1.2M+ margin** and supervisor.

**University Health–scale courier** = third company — dedicated PM.
