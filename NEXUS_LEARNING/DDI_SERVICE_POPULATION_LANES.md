# DDI Service Population Lanes — Funding & Program Alignment

**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026  
**Purpose:** Precise definition of **who DDI programs serve** (end populations), separate from **who signs the contract** (buyers). Required for grants, Medicaid/MCO proposals, FEMA/state EM narratives, foundation applications, and capability statements.  
**Rule:** `.cursor/rules/service-population-lanes-funding.mdc`

---

## Why precision matters for funding

Funders score **population fit**, **need**, and **continuity of care** — not generic “transportation services.”

| If NEXUS writes… | Funder risk |
|------------------|-------------|
| Broad “we serve transportation needs” | Wrong NAICS/SAM narrative; confused with freight or rideshare |
| HAVEN = general mobility | Misses disaster continuity, Medicaid waiver, MCO continuity angles |
| All Medicaid members | Overstates enrollment; weakens HIDE SNP / county-slice proof |
| One blob “healthcare” | Loses DNA vs NEMT vs credentialing vs drug testing program eligibility |

**Correct lane → correct funder, correct statutory language, defensible past performance.**

## Golden rule: three mobility lanes (never merge)

| Lane ID | Name | End population (who is served) | Primary payers / funders |
|---------|------|----------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **MOB-A** | **Plan NEMT (TPA 5)** | Enrolled **Medicaid/Medicare managed-care members** with authorized **non-emergency medical trips** (HIDE SNP dual-eligible, LTSS/SNP lines, county-enrolled populations) | MCOs, state Medicaid, CHAMPS; CMS HIDE/LTSS narratives |
| **MOB-B** | **HAVEN continuity transport** | **Displaced plan members** and crisis populations needing **evacuation, continuity trips, pharmacy access** when disaster breaks normal networks — *not* routine benefit trips | MCO disaster clauses, Medicaid emergency waivers, FEMA, state EM, health plans (Rx/DME/home health continuity) |
| **MOB-C** | **Freight & logistics (TPA 6)** | **No patient NEMT population** — shippers, agencies, **owner-operators**, disaster **supply chain** / critical cargo | FEMA logistics, USPS HCR, DOD, GSA, freight programs — **not** ACL/AAA senior mobility grants |

**HAVEN full model (three pillars — funding hooks):**

| Pillar | Population | Funding angle |
|--------|------------|---------------|
| Housing | Displaced households (shelter/ALE/TSA) | FEMA TSA, property insurance ALE, state housing/emergency funds |
| Transport (MOB-B) | Displaced members — medical/pharmacy continuity mobility | MCO continuity, Medicaid waiver, state EM |
| Medical continuity | Displaced **patients** — Rx, DME, home health interruption | Health plan benefits, PBM emergency, HRSA-style continuity |

**Proof anchor (live):** HAP CareSource NEMT — Vendor ID 100000469269, Wayne + Macomb, ~4,500 dual-eligible members (per partnership docs). HAVEN pitches **extend this infrastructure** — not a startup transport company.

## Grant & narrative language — DO / DON'T

### DO
- Name the **specific population**: “dual-eligible HIDE SNP members,” “displaced MCO members during declared disasters,” “DOT-regulated transit operators.”
- Tie **HAVEN** to **continuity of care** and **pre-credentialed MCO operations**.
- Separate **Cause We Care (501c3)** community footprint from **DDI national TPA** scope when the application is entity-specific.
- Cite **live contracts** only when documented (`PARTNERSHIPS/`, bid folders, `COMPANY_INFO_MASTER.md`).

### DON'T
- “DDI provides transportation services” without MOB-A / MOB-B / MOB-C tag.
- Use HAVEN to mean daily Medicaid rides.
- Claim SWFT or unverified member counts.
- Mix Freight 1st Direct OO program into senior NEMT or AAA grant narratives.
