**Established:** May 30, 2026  
**Source session:** `SESSION_SUMMARY_MAY_30_2026.md`

## Framework Origin

Reframe clinical services as **clinical programs**. Find entities distributing money across chronic disease, behavioral health, healthcare access, and primary care. Pitch with outcomes, partners, delivery model, and 3–5 year roadmap. Target: 7-figure awards from funders with $50B+ nationally.

**Proven model:** MDHHS CLPPP / Environmental Health — CWC = community trust; DDI = program administration.

---

## Operating Model (Every Program)

| Layer | Entity | Role |
|-------|--------|------|
| Community trust | **Cause We Care** | Credibility, relationships, population access, equitability |
| Program administration | **Dee Davis Inc.** | Operations, compliance, delivery, reporting, billing |

---

## Program Priority Order (Confirmed May 30, 2026)

1. **Healthcare Access & Transportation Equity** — warmest; Medicaid provider infrastructure live (HAP)
2. **Family Stability & Child Welfare Support** — underserved; MI Bridges + DNA unique in MI
3. **Substance Use Monitoring & Recovery Support** — C/TPA operational; court track record
4. **Housing Stability** — identified future; HRSN + opioid settlement pathway
