## SHIELD — lead-safe navigation

**Buyers:** MDHHS, LHDs, MCOs (payers). **Population:** Children 12/24 mo + families with elevated BLL.

### Population
- Mandatory tests **scheduled and completed** at scale (targets: 90%+ referred tested within 14 days; Wayne 50% capture ≈ ~19,730 tests/yr).
- Elevated children enter **full cascade** (~809/yr at 50% capture) — not pamphlet-only.
- Referral to first contact **< 48 hours**.
- Referral to retest **< 90 days** average.
- BLL trending down; remediation, filters, CLPPP/Medicaid/SNAP connected where applicable.
- Family satisfaction **4.0+ / 5.0** at closure; **Cause We Care** as single door.

### System
- Michigan testing volume moves toward statutory need (~39,460 mandatory tests/yr Wayne birth data vs ~20% statewide tested today).
- MDHHS **live portal** — no quarterly spreadsheet chasing.
- MDHHS **does not pay ops** — DDI bills MCOs, Medicaid FFS, private.

### Operations (DDI)
- **85%+** activated services “Verified Complete” (two-way SMS).
- **95%+** clean claims — **VERTEX**.
- **80%+** navigator utilization; 15–25 active families per CHW.
- All **9 service lines** routable with vendor network complete.

**Working well in one line:** Every referred child tested on time; every elevated child on a verified path to retest; MDHHS sees it live.

*Source: `MDHHS_SHIELD_PILOT_PROPOSAL.md`, `SHIELD_REVENUE_MODEL.md`*

## Plan NEMT (MOB-A) — e.g. HAP CareSource

**Buyers:** MCOs, state Medicaid. **Population:** Enrolled members with authorized **medical** trips (HIDE SNP dual-eligible, etc.).

### Population
- Members **reach appointments** (dialysis, therapy, PCP, pharmacy-related).
- Fewer no-shows driven by transportation gaps.

### System
- One **TPA desk**: schedule, authorize, verify, bill.
- Documented SLA proof for expansion and **HAVEN** pitch.

### Operations
- Trip completion, on-time metrics, complaint rate tracked for HAVEN case file.

**Working well in one line:** Members use the benefit they already have — reliably.

## HAVEN — disaster continuity (MOB-B)

**Not daily NEMT.** **Buyers:** MCOs, health plans, state EM, FEMA paths. **Population:** **Displaced** plan members + crisis populations.

### Population
- Care continues after relocation: **housing**, **rides to care**, **Rx / DME / home health** at new location.
- “Triangle” covered: shelter + mobility + medical survival.

### System
- **Pre-credentialed network** — no post-disaster vendor scramble.
- MCO sends **displaced member lists**; DDI coordinates continuity.
- **Push-button activation** on declaration (roadmap Phase 4).

### Operations (phases)
| Phase | Outcome |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Registry + signed partners |
| 2 | Cases dispatch from member lists |
| 3 | Insurance/MCO billing flows |
| 4 | Monitoring + auto-activation |

**Working well in one line:** After FEMA declares, displaced members still get shelter, care trips, and meds — network was live before the storm.

*Source: `HAVEN/STRATEGY/HAVEN_DISASTER_RECOVERY_TPA_STRATEGY.md`*

## PRISM — field execution quality

**Population:** Same families/members — indirect.

### When working
- Field orders dispatched; scanbacks clean; rejections caught **before** ship.
- Fewer redo trips and broken claims downstream for SHIELD/NEMT/testing/notary.

**Working well in one line:** Services complete right the first time.
