## What CCAM is

**Coordinating Council on Access and Mobility (CCAM)** — federal interagency body (FTA-led) coordinating **132 programs across 9 agencies** that fund human services transportation (seniors, disabilities, low income).

**Contact (reference only):** CCAM@dot.gov · 202-366-2053  
**Page:** https://www.transit.dot.gov/regulations-and-programs/ccam

**CCAM-TAC (Technical Assistance Center):** FTA/CCAM program administered by **CTAA** (Community Transportation Association of America). Resource library, training, **Grants and Opportunities** section, newsletter.  
**Site:** https://www.ccam-tac.org · **TA contact:** info@ccam-tac.org  
**Passive monitor only** — newsletter signup OK for grant/opportunity alerts; not a buyer relationship lane.

**CCAM definition of full coordination (Resource Central):** Coordinated services may evolve into an **integrated brokerage** — *one entity schedules and collects payment for trips performed by several providers* — or full consolidation under one agency. **This is DDI/DEPOINTE's TPA model in federal policy language.**

## Top 5 — DDI-relevant extracts (use in proposals / grant copy)

### 1. Trip brokering — federally recognized model

FTA's **Feb 2026 guidance clarification** addresses eligible **National Transit Database (NTD) reporting** for **trip brokering** (alongside paratransit, charter, incidental use).

**DDI use:** Validates **DEPOINTE / DDI TPA broker model** in federal framework — program-level coordination and trip brokering, not fleet-as-transit-agency. Cite when MCO, AAA, or transit evaluators question whether a non-fleet prime fits human-services transport policy.

**Lane:** **MOB-A** (plan NEMT TPA)

---

### 2. QHSO charter exception — CWC positioning

Under FTA's Charter Service Regulation (49 C.F.R. Part 604), FTA grant recipients are generally restricted from charter service. **Exception:** **Qualified Human Service Organizations (QHSOs)** funded by one or more programs on the **CCAM Program Inventory** may receive services from FTA-funded transit providers without charter violations. **Registration on FTA's charter service website is not required** for QHSOs meeting that funding criterion.

**DDI use:** Relevant when **Cause We Care** (501c3) teams with transit agencies or AAA grantees on senior/disabled/low-income transport — CWC as community-facing human-services partner, **DDI** as contract/TPA layer. Verify CWC's specific grant sources against CCAM Program Inventory before claiming QHSO status in a bid.

**Lane:** CWC grants + **MOB-A** / AAA coordination (not MOB-C freight)

---

### 3. Cost allocation technology for NEMT

CCAM actively promotes **cost-sharing** and **cost allocation technology** for NEMT — frameworks for multiple programs/partners to share transport resources and expenses.

**DDI use:** Aligns with DDI's **spread model** (MCO/agency reimbursement minus fulfillment cost = program margin under TPA management). Language for MCO pitches and federal-facing capability sections: coordinated NEMT with transparent cost allocation across programs — not duplicate brokers.

**Lane:** **MOB-A** · `DDI_BUSINESS_MODEL.md`

---

### 4. Mobility management — eligible capital (5307 / 5310 / 5311 / ICAM)

**Mobility management** is an eligible **capital** expense under FTA Sections **5307**, **5310**, **5311**, and the **ICAM Pilot Program** — regional coordination, one-call/one-click, coordination tech deployment.

**DDI use:** Potential **federal funding lane for NEXUS/PRISM coordination infrastructure** (not vehicles). **DEPOINTE** = operational NEMT proof; **NEXUS/PRISM** = the capital/coordination story when ICAM or 5310-linked NOFOs open. **ARENA (MOB-E)** = tangential unless scoped as coordination platform in a human-services pilot.

**Lane:** Future **ICAM** watch · `NEXUS_WATCH_LIST.md`

---

### 5. Federal fund braiding — grant stacking

CCAM encourages **federal fund braiding** — using federal funds from one program toward the **local match** of another federal grant, when both programs' statutory/regulatory requirements are met.

**DDI use:** Relevant for **HAVEN** and **SHIELD** multi-funder stacks (ACL/OAA, MCO, state HHS, FEMA continuity) where transport + navigation + housing/medical continuity are bundled. Requires explicit eligibility check per program pair — do not assume braid without reading CCAM **Federal Fund Braiding Guide** and both grant terms.

**Lane:** **MOB-B** (HAVEN) + SHIELD grant packages · `GRANT_APPLICATION_PACKAGE/CWC_GRANTS/`

## Flag for future pipeline: ICAM Pilot Program

**Program:** **Innovative Coordinated Access and Mobility (ICAM) Pilot Program**  
**Authority:** FAST Act § 3006(b) · continued under BIL/IIJA (~**$5M/year** appropriation)  
**FTA hub:** https://www.transit.dot.gov/grants/innovative-coordinated-access-and-mobility-pilot-program

### Why it matters to DDI

- Explicitly funds **coordination of public transit + NEMT** for transportation-disadvantaged populations.
- Eligible project types include **regional/statewide mobility management**, **coordination technology deployment**, and **one-call/one-click centers** — aligns with NEXUS/PRISM coordination infrastructure, not fleet ownership.
- CCAM page lists ICAM alongside Sections **5307, 5310, 5311** as mobility-management-eligible FTA lanes.

### DDI fit notes (when reviewing — not now)

| Asset | ICAM relevance |
|---|---|
| **DEPOINTE / plan NEMT TPA** | Strong — NEMT + coordination is the program purpose |
| **NEXUS / PRISM** | Strong — coordination technology, trip brokering, QA gates |
| **ARENA (MOB-E)** | Weak primary fit — event mobility unless bundled as coordination platform in a human-services pilot |
| **Cause We Care** | Possible partner for human-services population narrative; **DDI** holds SAM/contracts for TPA layer |

### Eligibility constraint (important)

**Direct applicants** are typically:
- State DOTs
- Section **5310** designated recipients (seniors/disabled formula)
- Local governmental entities operating public transit
- Their eligible **subrecipients** with regional/statewide cost-allocation pilot capacity

**DDI as EDWOSB prime** likely needs a **teaming path** (5310 recipient, transit authority, or state DOT as applicant) unless a future NOFO broadens eligibility. Do not assume prime without reading the NOFO.

### Funding mechanics (from FTA fact sheet — verify each NOFO)

- **Capital expenditures only** (not operating/admin as the grant purpose)
- Federal share up to **80%** · minimum **20% local match**
- Competitive awards; pool often ~$4–5M per cycle (many small projects)
- Last documented NOFO: **FTA-2024-006-TPM-ICAM** — closed **Feb 13, 2024**
