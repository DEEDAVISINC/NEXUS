# DDI Buyers & Markets — Who We Get Business From

**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026  
**Purpose:** Organizations DDI sells **to** (contracting buyers). **Not** fulfillment partners (Quest, Uber, Roadie, etc.).  
**Index:** `DDI_STRATEGIC_KNOWLEDGE_INDEX.md` · Detailed NAICS lists: `ALL_BUYERS_BY_SECTOR.md`

---

## Core idea

DDI pursues **organizations that buy programs** for vulnerable or regulated populations and need a **national EDWOSB prime** to run compliance, coordination, and billing.

**Lead strength:** **Systems in place** (NEXUS, SHIELD, PRISM, VERTEX, HAVEN) — real-time program ops, not spreadsheets. Prove with **HAP live** + SHIELD/portal. See `DDI_STRATEGIC_KNOWLEDGE_INDEX.md`.

## By DDI system

### SHIELD (lead-safe children & families)
- **MDHHS** — Environmental Health Bureau, CLPPP
- **Local health departments** — Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Genesee
- **Medicaid MCOs** — pay for tests, CHW, NEMT, elevated-case services
- **Private payers** — elevated-case billing per program design

### NEMT / plan medical transport (MOB-A)
- **Medicaid MCOs** — CareSource, Molina, UHC Community Plan, Blue Cross Complete, etc.
- **State Medicaid** — MDHHS, other state HHS
- **VA** — medical transport programs
- **Area Agencies on Aging** — senior medical transport
- **County health / CMH** — behavioral health transport

### HAVEN (disaster continuity — MOB-B)
- **MCOs / health plans** — disaster rider on NEMT
- **Medicare Advantage plans**
- **State emergency management** — FL, TX, LA, etc.
- **FEMA-related programs** — housing/continuity when declared
- **Large health systems** — discharge continuity in disaster zones

### Drug testing / C-TPA
- **Transit authorities** — DDOT, SMART, VIA, RTA, etc.
- **State procurement** — e.g. Michigan DTMB statewide TPA
- **Federal** — VA, DHS/CBP, DOJ/BOP, DoD
- **Utilities & pipelines** — PHMSA/DOT workers
- **Hospitals & universities** — employee screening
- **Trucking fleets** — DOT random pools

### Fingerprinting / background
- **Federal** — VA, DHS, defense health, credentialing solicitations
- **State police / licensing boards** — LARA, teacher cert, healthcare licensing
- **School districts** — staff & volunteers
- **Transit, housing authorities, gaming commissions**
- **Hospitals & nursing homes**

### DNA (DePointe)
- **Courts & child support agencies**
- **Immigration legal market** — USCIS-related collections
- **Family law** — referral volume

### Notary / 3D Ink
- **Title companies & lenders**
- **Law firms** — estate, immigration, real estate
- **Senior living** — POA, advance directives
- **Government** — RON/signing where contracted
- **Ports / secure facilities** — TWIC escort contracts

### Courier / medical logistics
- **Hospital & health systems**
- **VA medical centers**
- **Reference labs** — specimen routes

### Benefits navigation / SDOH
- **MDHHS** — MI Bridges community partner ecosystem
- **MCOs** — SDOH / HRSN
- **AAAs & state aging units**
- **Grant funders** — ACL, NTIA pass-through, United Way
- **County social services**

## Buyer vs partner (do not confuse)

| Signs **buyer** | Signs **fulfillment partner** |
|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| Issues RFP / contract / MCO agreement | Executes trips, tests, lab work under DDI |
| Pays DDI prime | Paid by DDI as sub/vendor |
| Cares about EDWOSB, reporting, SLA | Cares about rates, coverage, API |
