# Dual-Eligible Navigation — National Opportunity Scan

**Scan date:** May 31, 2026  
**Scope:** **United States — all 50 states + DC** (DDI national contract management TPA; HQ Troy, MI)  
**Population:** Dual-eligible (Medicare + Medicaid), D-SNP / FIDE / HIDE members, seniors 60+, low-income benefits enrollees  
**Proof anchor (replicable nationally):** MDHHS MI Bridges Community Partner since **May 15, 2020** · **250+** applications facilitated · HAP CareSource NEMT live · EDWOSB **CAGE 8UMX3**

**Entity model:** **DDI prime TPA nationwide** · **CWC 501(c)(3)** when grant requires nonprofit applicant · Local fulfillment partners per state (never named in buyer outreach)

---

## National thesis

Dual-eligible navigation is **not a Michigan program**. It is a **national market** driven by:

1. **~12M+ full-benefit dual-eligible individuals** in the U.S. (Medicare + Medicaid overlap — plan/market varies by year; use as market-size order of magnitude only)
2. **D-SNPs, FIDE SNPs, HIDE SNPs** — CMS requires Medicaid agency coordination; plans invest in **care coordination + SDOH** (transport, food, housing navigation)
3. **25 states with approved Medicaid 1115 HRSN waivers** (existing approvals preserved post–March 2025 federal guidance change per `MULTI_STATE_LANDSCAPE_RESEARCH.md`)
4. **620+ Area Agencies on Aging** — **SHIP** (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) in **every state**, delivered locally through AAAs
5. **State benefits enrollment ecosystems** — every state has Medicaid/Medicare enrollment touchpoints (MI Bridges is DDI's **proven model**, not the geographic limit)
6. **NTIA Digital Equity Act** — navigator networks in **all states**
7. **ACL / CMS pass-through grants** — SHIP, MIPPA, Options Counseling, ACA Navigator grants to community orgs

**DDI sells to:** MCOs, state HHS, AAAs, ACL grantees, counties, VA, 211 networks, FQHC systems — **one national prime contract**, local delivery partners under DDI management.

## National MCO / dual-plan lane

D-SNPs must hold **State Medicaid Agency Contracts (SMACs)** with coordination requirements (CMS CY2026 instructions). Plans need vendors for:

- Member enrollment / retention (expanded SEPs for duals in 2025+)
- SDOH supplemental benefits (transport, food, housing **navigation** — not always separate RFP)
- HRSN service delivery in waiver states
- NEMT (mandatory Medicaid benefit — DDI already operates this lane)

| National targets (examples — not exhaustive) | Why |
|---|---|
| Centene, Molina, UHC, Humana, Aetna, Elevance, Cigna | Largest Medicaid + D-SNP footprint |
| State-specific plans (CareSource, BCBS state plans, etc.) | Regional procurement · relationship entry |
| **FIDE / HIDE integrated plans** | Highest dual-eligible integration — navigation + transport bundle |

**DDI posture:** Relationship/complement default — **on file for SDOH navigation + NEMT TPA** in every state where plan operates. Never name fulfillment partners in outreach.
