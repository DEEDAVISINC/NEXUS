## #1 strength (official positioning)

**DDI’s super strength is the systems already in place** — NEXUS, SHIELD, PRISM, VERTEX, HAVEN (not a generic TPA brochure).

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Lead with** | Program-grade infrastructure: schedule, verify, bill, report in **real time** |
| **Prove with** | **HAP NEMT live** + **SHIELD** portal/demo (same rails for MCOs, MDHHS, funders) |
| **Support with** | EDWOSB certs, national TPA model, fulfillment network (behind the curtain) |

**One line:** *We built the operating system for population programs — and we’re already running part of it in Michigan.*

**Not the lead for:** commodity LPTA supply bids where price-only wins.
