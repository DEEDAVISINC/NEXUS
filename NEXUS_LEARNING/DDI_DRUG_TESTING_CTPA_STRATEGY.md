# DDI Drug Testing C/TPA Strategy — Collection Models & Pricing

**Created:** May 18, 2026
**Purpose:** Define DDI's C/TPA business model for drug testing — how DDI wins contracts, dispatches collections, and maintains 30-50%+ margin.

---

## DDI'S ROLE: C/TPA (Consortium/Third Party Administrator)

**DDI is the broker. DDI doesn't collect — DDI dispatches and bills.**

| DDI Does | DDI Does NOT Do |
|---|---|
| Win trucking/fleet contracts | Perform collections |
| Manage DOT compliance programs | Own collection sites |
| Schedule and dispatch collections | Employ collectors (W-2) |
| Coordinate MRO (AMRO) | Process lab specimens |
| Handle Clearinghouse queries | Make medical determinations |
| Bill clients | Touch specimens |
| Keep 30-50%+ margin | |

## THREE COLLECTION FULFILLMENT MODELS

### Model 1: Sub to Small Collection Company (B2B)

**DDI subcontracts to a local collection business**

| DDI | Collection Company (Sub) |
|---|---|
| Prime contractor | Subcontractor |
| Gets the trucking contract | Does all collections |
| Schedules, dispatches | Sends their collectors |
| Bills client | Invoices DDI monthly |
| Handles compliance, MRO, Clearinghouse | Handles collection operations |

**Pros:**
- ✅ No collector management
- ✅ They have their own insurance, training, supplies
- ✅ Scalable (they have multiple collectors)
- ✅ One invoice, one relationship
- ✅ Professional — they do this for a living

**Cons:**
- ❌ Lower margin (they take their cut)
- ❌ Less control over service quality
- ❌ Dependent on their availability

**DDI pays:** $40-50/collection or $300-400/collective event
**DDI margin:** 40-50%

**Best for:** New markets, testing the waters, overflow, guaranteed coverage

---

### Model 2: 1099 Individual Collectors (Direct)

**DDI contracts directly with individual DOT-certified collectors**

| DDI | Collector (1099) |
|---|---|
| Dispatches jobs | Accepts jobs |
| Provides/reimburses supplies | Does the collection |
| Manages scheduling | Shows up |
| Bills client | Invoices DDI per job |

**Pros:**
- ✅ Higher margin (no company markup)
- ✅ Direct control over scheduling
- ✅ Build your own network

**Cons:**
- ❌ More management overhead
- ❌ Verify certifications, insurance yourself
- ❌ Reliability varies by individual
- ❌ Backup coverage if someone cancels

**DDI pays:** $25-40/collection or $150-250/collective event
**DDI margin:** 55-70%

**Best for:** Established areas, high volume, scheduled collective events

---

### Model 3: National Collection Network (Aggregator)

**DDI routes orders to nationwide networks**

| Network | Sites | How It Works | DDI Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eScreen (Abbott)** | 5,100+ | Online portal, electronic CCF | $30-40/test |
| **Quest Employer Solutions** | 2,200+ | Employer portal, nationwide PSCs | $30-35/test |
| **24/7 Onsite** | Nationwide mobile | Dispatch like Uber | $50-70/test mobile |
| **On-Time Screening** | Fleet specialists | Group events, trucking focus | $40-60/test |
| **National Drug Screening** | Network aggregator | Routes to nearest site | $35-45/test |

**Pros:**
- ✅ Nationwide coverage
- ✅ No management
- ✅ Instant scalability
- ✅ Good for out-of-area contracts

**Cons:**
- ❌ Lowest margin (pay their markup)
- ❌ Less control

**DDI margin:** 30-45%

**Best for:** Out-of-state contracts, overflow, urgent/post-accident

## COLLECTION TYPES & PRICING

### On-Demand Mobile (Single Collection)

Collector goes to driver/site for one test.

| Component | DDI Pays | DDI Charges | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1099 Collector | $35 | — | — |
| Collection Company Sub | $45-50 | — | — |
| Lab processing | $20-25 | — | — |
| MRO (if needed, ~5%) | $0.20 avg | — | — |
| **Total DDI cost** | $55-75 | $100-125 | **$25-70 (25-56%)** |

### Collective Event (Scheduled Group)

Collector goes to site, tests multiple drivers at once.

| Component | DDI Pays | DDI Charges | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1099 Collector (25 tests) | $200-250 flat | — | — |
| Collection Company Sub (25 tests) | $300-400 flat | — | — |
| Lab processing (25 × $20) | $500 | — | — |
| Supplies (if DDI provides) | $125 | — | — |
| **Total DDI cost (1099)** | $825-875 | $1,875 (25 × $75) | **$1,000-1,050 (53-56%)** |
| **Total DDI cost (Sub)** | $925-1,025 | $1,875 | **$850-950 (45-51%)** |

### Post-Accident / Urgent

Must test within 32 hours (drugs) / 8 hours (alcohol). Premium pricing.

| Component | DDI Pays | DDI Charges | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1099 Collector (urgent) | $75 | — | — |
| Collection Company Sub (urgent) | $100 | — | — |
| Lab processing | $25 | — | — |
| **Total DDI cost** | $100-125 | $150-200 | **$50-75 (33-38%)** |

## NEGOTIATION RULE: DDI SETS THE RATE

**DDI is the prime. DDI has the contract. DDI sets the terms.**

| Who | Approach |
|---|---|
| **1099 Collector** | "DDI pays $35/collection. Interested?" — Take it or leave it |
| **Collection Company** | "DDI pays $45/collection. Can you service this area?" — Negotiate from your number |

**If they push back:**
> "That's our rate for ongoing C/TPA work. We're offering steady volume. If that doesn't work, no problem."

**Know your floor:**
- Client pays $100 → Lab $20 + MRO $4 + DDI margin $30 = **$46 max to collector**
- If they want more than your floor, walk.
