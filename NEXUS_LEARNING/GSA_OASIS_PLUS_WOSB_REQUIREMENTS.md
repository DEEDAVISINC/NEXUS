## QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

### Minimum Score to Qualify

| Contract Type | Qualifying Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Small Business / WOSB** | **36 points** out of 50 |
| Unrestricted | 42 points out of 50 |
| Enterprise Solutions (Unrestricted) | 45 points out of 50 |

### How Points Are Earned

| Category | Max Points | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **QP Relevance** | Up to 15 | Qualifying Projects relevant to the domain |
| **QP Scale** | Up to 10 | Size/value of qualifying projects |
| **Integrated Experience** | Up to 5 | Multi-domain project experience |
| **Management & Staffing** | Up to 5 | Key personnel qualifications |
| **Past Performance** | Up to 5 | CPARS/past performance ratings |
| **Federal Experience Projects (FEPs)** | Up to 5 | Additional federal contract experience |
| **Systems & Certifications** | Up to 5 | Accounting systems, ISO certs, clearances |
| **TOTAL** | **50** | Need **36** for WOSB |

## QUALIFYING PROJECT (QP) REQUIREMENTS

To count as a Qualifying Project, each project must meet ALL of these:

### Mandatory Criteria

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Contract Type** | Must be a contract for services (FAR Part 37) |
| **Minimum Value (AAV)** | Meet domain-specific Average Annual Value threshold |
| **Timeframe** | Completed within 5 years of June 15, 2023, OR ongoing with 6+ months performance |
| **Past Performance** | No negative past performance (average score must be ≥3.0 on 5-point scale) |
| **Max Projects** | Up to 5 QPs per domain |

### Average Annual Value (AAV) by Domain

| Domain | Minimum AAV (Full) | Minimum AAV (Protégé) |
|---|---|---|
| Management & Advisory | $1,000,000 | $500,000 |
| Technical & Engineering | $500,000 | $250,000 |
| **Facilities** | **$500,000** | **$250,000** |
| **Logistics** | **$500,000** | **$250,000** |
| Business Administration | $250,000 | $125,000 |
| Human Capital | $500,000 | $250,000 |

### AAV Calculation

For projects >12 months:
```
AAV = (Total Project Value ÷ Total Days of Performance) × 366
```

**Example:** $3M project over 450 days = ($3M ÷ 450) × 366 = **$2.44M AAV**

## FEDERAL EXPERIENCE PROJECT (FEP) REQUIREMENTS

FEPs are OPTIONAL but provide bonus points. Requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Prime federal contract, grant, or cooperative agreement |
| **Minimum AAV** | $250,000 (or $125,000 for protégé) |
| **Timeframe** | Same as QP — within 5 years of June 15, 2023, or ongoing |
| **Documentation** | FPDS report or contract award document |

## WHAT WOULD IT TAKE TO QUALIFY?

### Path 1: Build Organically (12-24 months)

1. Win 3-5 contracts at $500K+ AAV in Facilities or Logistics
2. Build CPARS history with positive ratings
3. Get accounting system reviewed for adequacy
4. Consider ISO 9001 certification

### Path 2: Mentor-Protégé Joint Venture

1. Find an OASIS+ prime contractor willing to mentor
2. Form 8(a) Mentor-Protégé JV
3. Protégé thresholds are 50% lower ($250K AAV instead of $500K)
4. Leverage mentor's past performance and systems

### Path 3: Wait and Focus on Direct Awards

1. Skip OASIS+ for now
2. Focus on direct agency solicitations (VA, DLA, state/local)
3. Build track record and revenue
4. Revisit OASIS+ in 2027-2028 with stronger position
