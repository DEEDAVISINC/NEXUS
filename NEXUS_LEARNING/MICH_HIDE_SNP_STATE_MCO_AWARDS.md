### How to read these numbers (important)

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Payment model** | **Capitated PMPM** — state pays per enrolled member per month, not a fixed lump sum |
| **Why DTMB contracts show $0** | Executed MCO contracts (e.g. Priority Health DTMB file) list **“Estimated Contract Value at Execution: $0.00”** because real dollars flow through **monthly capitation** tied to enrollment |
| **What the table is** | **Actuarial 7-year projection** at award time if enrollment matches bid assumptions across awarded regions |
| **Not equal to cash in hand** | Actual plan revenue **scales with dual-eligible enrollment** in each plan's counties — winner plans with more members capture more capitation |
| **Same $ for 4 plans** | Meridian, Molina, Priority, UHC share **identical** 7-year estimates because they received **overlapping broad regional awards** with similar projected member share in the actuarial model |
| **2026 vs 2027** | 2026 launch counties = smaller pool; **statewide 2027** increases addressable population toward full ~**300,000+** dual-eligible statewide figure cited by MDHHS/plans |
| **DDI NEMT slice** | DDI's HAP contract is **one LTSS service line** inside this capitation — NEMT is a **small fraction** of total plan spend (orientation cited ~4,500 members Wayne+Macomb for HAP) |

**Do not cite these as guaranteed contract minimums.** Use for **market sizing and outreach prioritization** only.
