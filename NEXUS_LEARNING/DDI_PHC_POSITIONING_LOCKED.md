## Identity

**DDI is a Contract Management TPA.** Not a NEMT vendor. Not a service provider.

DDI designs, administers, and operates community-based public health programs — coordinating credentialed networks across transportation, environmental health, housing stability, and compliance services. **NEMT is one service line DDI administers. It is not what DDI is.**
