# SERVICE VS. SUPPLY: WHICH FRAMEWORK TO USE?
**Quick Decision Guide**

---

## 🔀 THE DECISION TREE

```
NEW RFP RECEIVED
↓
What type of opportunity is this?
├─ SUPPLIES/PRODUCTS
│  │
│  ├─ Examples:
│  │  • Industrial supplies

   │
```

## 🎯 QUICK IDENTIFICATION

### **It's a SUPPLY bid if:**
- ✅ RFP lists product part numbers/SKUs
- ✅ Specifications are about products (dimensions, materials, specs)
- ✅ Delivery is about shipping products
- ✅ You'll order from distributors (Grainger, Fastenal, etc.)
- ✅ No labor/service component
- ✅ Pricing is per-unit or per-case

**→ Use GRAINGER CALL SCRIPT FRAMEWORK**

---

### **It's a SERVICE bid if:**
- ✅ RFP describes work to be performed
- ✅ Specifications are about labor/service delivery
- ✅ Requires on-site personnel
- ✅ You'll need subcontractors to perform work
- ✅ Pricing is per-hour, per-visit, or per-service
- ✅ Insurance requirements for workers

**→ Use SERVICE CONTRACT SUB FRAMEWORK**

## 📋 SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON

| Aspect | SUPPLY BIDS | SERVICE BIDS |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **What you provide** | Products/materials | Labor/services |
| **Who delivers** | Distributors (Grainger) | Subcontractors (local companies) |
| **Framework** | Grainger Call Script | Service Sub Framework |
| **Negotiation** | Supplier discount (5-10%) | Sub pricing + terms (15-25% margin) |
| **Key leverage** | Volume, EDWOSB, guaranteed payment | Volume, duration, portfolio building |
| **Documentation** | Quote, tax exemption | Letter of Intent, insurance certs |
| **Margin target** | 10-20% | 15-25% |
| **Risk** | Pricing, delivery time | Quality, reliability, capacity |
| **Transformation proof** | Cost savings, delivery speed | Response time, completion rate, satisfaction |

## 🔄 HYBRID BIDS (Both Supply + Service)

**Example:** Landscaping contract that includes both:
- Materials (mulch, plants, fertilizer) ← SUPPLY
- Labor (installation, maintenance) ← SERVICE

**How to handle:**

1. **Separate the components:**
   - Materials: Use Grainger/supplier framework
   - Labor: Use Service Sub framework

2. **Get quotes for each:**
   - Supplier quote for materials
   - Sub quote for labor
   - Calculate combined margin

3. **Build transformation story:**
   - Materials: Cost savings (supplier negotiation)
   - Labor: Quality outcomes (sub performance)
   - Combined: Total value proposition

## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES

### **Mistake 1: Using Wrong Framework**
**Problem:** Treating service bid like supply bid (or vice versa)  
**Solution:** Use decision tree above to identify correctly

### **Mistake 2: Not Vetting Subs Thoroughly**
**Problem:** Sub can't deliver, you're liable  
**Solution:** Always vet 3-5 subs, get Letter of Intent, check references

### **Mistake 3: Wrong Margin Expectations**
**Problem:** Expecting 20% margin on supply bid (unrealistic) or 10% on service bid (too low)  
**Solution:** Supply: 10-20%, Service: 15-25%

### **Mistake 4: Resume-Style Proposal for Either**
**Problem:** "Our sub has 10 years experience" or "Grainger is a leading distributor"  
**Solution:** Use Transformation Framework for BOTH - outcomes not credentials!
