## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do NOT use stock photos on any platform. Real photos or branded graphics only.
- Do NOT post the same caption on LinkedIn that you post on Instagram. Rewrite for the platform.
- Do NOT use more than 5 hashtags on LinkedIn or Facebook. Save the volume for Instagram.
- Do NOT turn the personal Facebook page into a business page. That kills authenticity.
- Do NOT post and ghost. Engage with comments within 2 hours on business accounts.
- Do NOT reveal client/agency names in social media posts (same buyer protection rules apply).
- Do NOT post pricing, sub costs, or internal strategy on any platform.
