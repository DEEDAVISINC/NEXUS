# NAICS codes for DDI — SAM.gov (jet fuel + freight)

Use this to **select** and **justify** NAICS on **SAM.gov** and **FPDS** searches. **Confirm with your CPA or SBA**—primary business activity drives the **primary** NAICS.

**Official NAICS reference:** [https://www.census.gov/naics/](https://www.census.gov/naics/)

---

## Primary candidates (pick one primary)

| NAICS | Title | When to use for DDI / JETA |
|-------|--------|-----------------------------|
| **488510** | Freight Transportation Arrangement | **DDI freight brokerage** (arranging motor freight; no carrier authority as broker). **Keep** if freight is still core revenue. |
| **425120** | Wholesale Trade Agents and Brokers | **Broker-only** refined products / **petroleum** deals: you **facilitate** buyer/seller, **commission/fee**, **do not take title**. Index explicitly lists **petroleum brokers**. |
| **424720** | Petroleum and Petroleum Products Merchant Wholesalers (except Bulk Stations and Terminals) | You **take title** to petroleum products and resell (trader/marketer model). **Not** classic intro-only brokerage. |

**Typical split:**

- **Freight + jet fuel intro (broker, no title):** primary **488510**, secondary **425120**.
- **If jet fuel becomes merchant resale:** discuss **424720** with counsel/accountant—may become co-primary or primary depending on revenue mix.

## Secondary / situational (federal opportunity matching)

| NAICS | Title | Notes |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **424710** | Petroleum Bulk Stations and Terminals | Only if you **operate** or **contract as** terminal/bulk station activity (unusual for pure broker). |
| **324110** | Petroleum Refineries | Only if you **are** a refiner (almost certainly **no** for DDI as broker). Sometimes appears on **supplier** RFPs—not your firm’s code unless you refine. |
| **488119** | Other Airport Operations | Airport **operations** vendors—not a default for **fuel brokerage**; might appear on **very** broad airport ITBs. Use only if you truly perform those services. |
| **488190** | Other Support Activities for Air Transportation | Broad; use only if activities match **census definition** (often **not** pure fuel brokering). |

---

## SAM.gov usage

1. **Entity registration** — list **all** NAICS that reflect **actual or planned** services (honest scope).
2. **Opportunity search** — search **425120** and **424720** for **fuel supply** / **petroleum** solicitations; **488510** for **freight**; add **324110** only to **find** refiner-side RFPs if you **partner** with refiners (search, not necessarily your code).
3. **Size standards** — differ by NAICS (e.g. **425120** often **125 employees**; verify current **SBA table**).
