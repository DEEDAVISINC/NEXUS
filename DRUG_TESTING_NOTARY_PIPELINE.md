## PART 2: NOTARY SERVICES — THE REAL PICTURE

### THE TRUTH ABOUT NOTARY GOVERNMENT RFPS

After searching all 50 states + federal, here's the reality:

**Standalone notary RFPs are RARE.** Notary services at the government level are typically:

1. **Bundled inside larger contracts** — legal services, admin support, document processing
2. **Handled in-house** — most government agencies have notaries on staff
3. **Per-transaction basis** — not contracted, just paid per notarization as needed
4. **Part of real estate/closing services** — HUD, VA, USDA foreclosure closings

### WHERE NOTARY GOVERNMENT MONEY ACTUALLY IS:

| Channel | How DDI Gets In | Revenue Potential |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **HUD/VA Foreclosure Closings** | Register as signing agent with title companies that handle HUD/VA REO | $75-$200/signing, recurring |
| **County Jail/Prison** | Contact county sheriff — inmates need legal doc notarization | Per-transaction, steady |
| **State Court Systems** | Contact court administrator — notary for court filings | Per-transaction |
| **Federal Agency On-Site** | Respond to admin support RFPs that include notary | Part of larger contract |
| **Military Installations** | Legal assistance offices sometimes need overflow notary | Part of admin support |
| **Title Company Overflow** | Sign up with Snapdocs, NotaryCafe, SigningOrder | $75-$200/signing |
