I had simplified the RFQ too far, making it sound like commodity purchasing:
- ❌ "Provide products/services as specified"
- ❌ "Delivery/completion timeline"
- ❌ "Optional" experience section
- ❌ Tone appropriate for buying products, not hiring contractors

### **The Fix:**

Recalibrated for **SERVICE SUBCONTRACTING:**
- ✅ "What do you charge to PERFORM this work?"
- ✅ "Labor pricing" (hourly, per-unit, or lump sum)
- ✅ Required: Brief description of HOW you'll perform the service
- ✅ Required: Equipment, tools, and CREW you'll use
- ✅ Experience section is helpful/expected (not optional)

   - Certificate of Insurance (CGL $1M/$2M, Workers' Comp, Auto Liability)
   - W-9 Form
   - Contractor license
   - Can add DDI as Additional Insured ✓

4. **Experience & References:**
   - Pressure washed City of XYZ municipal buildings (2024)
   - Pressure washed ABC Park facilities (2023)
   - References: John Smith, Public Works Director, 555-1234
