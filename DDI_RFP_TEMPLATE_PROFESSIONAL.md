## SECTION 4: EVALUATION CRITERIA

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
               SECTION 4: EVALUATION CRITERIA
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 EVALUATION PROCESS

All proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

Criteria                                Weight
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Technical Capability & Approach         30%
Pricing                                 30%
Experience & Qualifications             25%
References                              10%
Insurance & Compliance                   5%
──────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL                                   100%

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.2 TECHNICAL CAPABILITY (30 points)

Evaluation of:
• Understanding of project requirements
• Proposed methodology and approach
• Quality control measures
• Equipment and resources
• Timeline and ability to meet deadlines

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.3 PRICING (30 points)

Evaluation of:
• Total project cost
• Reasonableness of pricing
• Completeness of pricing breakdown
• Value for money
• Payment terms flexibility

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4 EXPERIENCE & QUALIFICATIONS (25 points)

Evaluation of:
• Years in business
• Experience with similar projects
• Relevant certifications and licenses
• Key personnel qualifications
• Track record of successful project completion

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.5 REFERENCES (10 points)

Evaluation of:
• Quality of references provided
• Similarity of reference projects to current project
• Feedback from reference contacts

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.6 INSURANCE & COMPLIANCE (5 points)

Evaluation of:
• Adequate insurance coverage
• Compliance with all requirements
• Completeness of documentation

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.7 SELECTION PROCESS

Step 1: Initial Review
All proposals will be reviewed for completeness. Incomplete 
proposals will be rejected.

Step 2: Technical & Pricing Evaluation
Complete proposals will be scored according to criteria above.

Step 3: Short List
Top-scoring proposers may be invited for interviews or 
clarification discussions.

Step 4: Final Selection
DEE DAVIS INC will select the subcontractor that provides the 
best overall value.

Step 5: Contract Negotiation
Selected subcontractor will enter into contract negotiations 
with DEE DAVIS INC.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.8 RIGHTS RESERVED

DEE DAVIS INC reserves the right to:
• Accept or reject any or all proposals
• Waive minor irregularities
• Request clarifications or additional information
• Negotiate with any proposer
• Cancel this RFP at any time
• Award to other than the lowest priced proposer
```
