# OASIS+ & Federal Professional Services — Strategic Picture (NEXUS)

**Purpose:** Capture where **federal professional services** buying is moving, why **OASIS+** matters for DDI, and what to verify on official sources before bid decisions.

**Last updated:** March 29, 2026

---

## 1. Policy direction — consolidation through GSA vehicles

Federal **procurement consolidation** policy directs agencies toward **government-wide acquisition** (including GSA **GWACs** and Best-in-Class vehicles) for overlapping categories of spend, to reduce duplication and improve leverage.

**Public anchor:** Executive Order **14240** (March 2025) — *Eliminating Waste and Saving Taxpayer Dollars by Consolidating Procurement* — expands GSA’s role in consolidated buying; agencies and OMB/GSA issue implementation plans and memos (e.g. category management / procurement consolidation guidance). **Read the current EO text and OMB/GSA implementation before citing in proposals.**

**Operational read (for DDI planning):** Contracting organizations are steered to use **established GSA-wide vehicles** (e.g. **OASIS+**) for **professional services** where those vehicles meet the requirement — instead of standing up or extending **agency-specific** boutique IDIQs for the same categories of work.

---

## 2. What happens to agency “boutique” professional services IDIQs

**Industry thesis (verify per agency):** Management consulting, program support, and similar **agency-unique IDIQs** are increasingly **not renewed** at recompete in favor of routing work through **GSA GWACs** (especially **OASIS+**). Framing used in the market: **retired**, not merely “restructured.”

**DDI implication:** Past performance and relationships tied to **legacy agency vehicles** still matter for **teaming and past performance narratives**, but **pipeline strategy** should assume **new** professional services work in core domains flows toward **OASIS+** (and related GSA vehicles) over time.

---

## 3. OASIS+ Phase II — 13 service domains (January 2026)

GSA expanded **OASIS+** so professional services coverage is **broader** — public reporting describes **Phase II** with **13 domains** (up from **8**), with **five new domains** intended to absorb categories of spend that were fragmented or under-managed.

### Original eight domains (Phase I family)

- Technical and Engineering  
- Research and Development  
- Management and Advisory  
- Environmental  
- Intelligence Services  
- Enterprise Solutions  
- Facilities Services  
- Logistics  

### Five new domains (Phase II — public list)

1. **Business Administration**  
2. **Human Capital**  
3. **Financial Services**  
4. **Marketing & Public Relations**  
5. **Social Services**  

**Timing:** Phase II / open enrollment timelines are described in GSA releases (e.g. continuous enrollment concepts in **early 2026**). **Confirm solicitation status, pool you’re eligible for, and evaluation queue on [GSA OASIS+](https://www.gsa.gov/oasis-plus) before committing proposal resources.**

---

## 4. Small business pools — structural, not cosmetic

**OASIS+** includes **set-aside pools** (e.g. Total Small Business, **WOSB**, **8(a)**, HUBZone, SDVOSB) that are **restricted to eligible certified firms**. **Unrestricted** pools are where **large businesses** prime.

**For DDI:** **EDWOSB / WOSB** certification is not a “preference point” only — on the **small business restricted pools**, **large primes cannot displace you as the prime** on that pool. That is a **lane advantage** for qualified small businesses in a way unrestricted competitions do not offer.

**Action:** Map **which OASIS+ domain(s)** match DDI’s NAICS and service lines; confirm **which pool(s)** you will pursue; align **past performance** and **teaming** to those domains.

---

## 5. How this ties to other vehicles (e.g. HCaTS)

**HCaTS** (Human Capital and Training Solutions) is a **separate GSA human-capital/training** family. Consolidation still pushes buyers toward **GSA-managed vehicles**; **OASIS+** domains such as **Human Capital** and **Business Administration** overlap **some** of that spend profile. **Do not assume** one vehicle replaces the other without reading the **specific** requirement and GSA ordering rules — use **SOW + agency policy** as the tie-breaker.
