## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

DDI is building lead testing TPA services. The same model applies to **all environmental testing** — asbestos, mold, radon, water quality, air quality, and soil contamination. Government funding is massive: EPA, HUD, state environmental agencies, and private sector remediation projects.

**The Play:** DDI coordinates environmental testing the same way it coordinates lead testing — schedule inspections, coordinate sampling, manage lab analysis, track compliance, bill the client. Certified inspectors and labs do the actual work.

### Order Flow

```
1. Client requests environmental testing (property address, service type)
2. PRISM creates order, identifies service requirements
3. AI matches to qualified inspector (certifications, distance)
4. Inspector receives order, schedules appointment
5. Inspector performs testing/sampling
6. Samples shipped to certified lab
7. Lab results returned to PRISM
8. AI validates results, generates report
9. Report delivered to client
10. VERTEX invoices
11. Compliance tracking (re-test dates, remediation follow-up)
```
