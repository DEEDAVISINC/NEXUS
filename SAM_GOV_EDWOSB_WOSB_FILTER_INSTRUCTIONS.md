# 🎯 SAM.GOV - EDWOSB/WOSB FILTER SETUP
## Step-by-Step: Find ONLY What You Can Bid On!

**Date:** February 6, 2026  
**Purpose:** Filter SAM.gov to show ONLY EDWOSB and WOSB opportunities  
**Time:** 5 minutes to set up, then search forever!

---

## 🚀 STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

### **STEP 1: Go to SAM.gov**
Open browser and go to: **https://sam.gov/search/?index=opp**

---

### **STEP 2: Click "Filter" Button**
Look for the **"Filter"** or **"Advanced Search"** button on the left side

---

### **STEP 3: Set "Set-Aside" Filter** 🚨 MOST IMPORTANT!

**Find the "Set-Aside" section and CHECK ONLY THESE 2:**

✅ **EDWOSB** - Economically Disadvantaged Women-Owned Small Business  
✅ **WOSB** - Women-Owned Small Business

**UNCHECK EVERYTHING ELSE:**
❌ SDVOSB  
❌ HUBZone  
❌ 8(a)  
❌ Veteran-Owned  
❌ Small Business (too broad)  
❌ Partial Small Business  
❌ Emerging Small Business

**This is THE key filter that prevents wasted time!**

---

### **STEP 4: Set "Notice Type" Filter**

**CHECK ALL OF THESE:**

✅ **Solicitation** (full RFPs)  
✅ **Pre-Solicitation** (advance notice)  
✅ **Sources Sought** 💎 (early bird!)  
✅ **Special Notice** 💎  
✅ **Intent to Bundle Requirements** 💎 (intent to sole source!)  
✅ **Combined Synopsis/Solicitation**

---

### **STEP 5: Set "Response Date" Filter**

**Date Range:**
- **From:** February 7, 2026 (tomorrow)
- **To:** April 30, 2026 (90 days out)

---

### **STEP 6: Set "Active" Filter**

✅ **Active opportunities only**

---

### **STEP 7: Add Keywords (Optional but Helpful)**

**In the search box, type keywords for your services:**

**Examples:**
- "courier"
- "medical transportation"
- "NEMT"
- "drug screening"
- "janitorial"
- "IT support"
- "medical supplies"
- "staffing"

**OR leave blank to see ALL EDWOSB/WOSB opportunities!**

---

### **STEP 8: Click "Search" or "Apply Filters"**

**Result:** You'll now see ONLY opportunities you can bid on!

---

## 💎 SAVE YOUR SEARCH!

**After running the search:**

1. Click **"Save This Search"** button
2. Name it: **"EDWOSB_WOSB_All_Active"**
3. Set email alerts: **Daily**
4. Click **"Save"**

**Result:** You'll get daily emails when new EDWOSB/WOSB opportunities are posted!
