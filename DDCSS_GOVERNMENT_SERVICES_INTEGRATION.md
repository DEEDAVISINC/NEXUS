## 🎯 OVERVIEW

**What This Is:**
Expand DDCSS beyond just corporate partnerships (FedEx/UPS) to include **proactive government client prospecting** for all DEE DAVIS INC services.

**Business Model:**
- Identify cities/counties/agencies that need services
- Research decision-makers and pain points
- Generate customized proposals
- Send proactive outreach (not waiting for RFPs)
- Build relationships → Win contracts

**Target Clients:**
- Cities (all sizes)
- Counties
- Transit agencies
- School districts
- Special districts (water, fire, parks)
- Federal agencies (smaller offices)

## 📋 NEXUS WORKFLOW

### **Step 1: Research & Add Prospect**

**In DDCSS:**
1. Click "Add Government Services Prospect"
2. Enter basic info:
   - Name: City of Auburn Hills
   - Type: City
   - Service Type: DOT Testing, Fingerprinting
   - Population: 24,000
   - Status: Research

3. Save to Airtable

---

### **Step 2: Run ProposalBio Analysis**

**In DDCSS:**
1. Click prospect → "Run ProposalBio Analysis"
2. Select template: "DOT Testing Services"
3. System generates analysis (uses template above)
4. Research and fill in:
   - Decision-maker name
   - Current provider (if known)
   - Fleet size estimate
   - Budget cycle
   - Pain points

5. Save analysis

---

### **Step 3: Generate Service Proposal**

**In NEXUS Documents:**
1. Click "Government Services Proposal" tab
2. Select service: DOT Testing
3. Auto-fills from ProposalBio analysis:
   - Prospect name
   - Service details
   - Pricing options
   - DDI credentials
   - Partnership details (Quest)

4. Click "Generate Proposal PDF"
5. Professional PDF created with DDI branding

---

### **Step 4: Send Outreach Email**

**In DDCSS:**
1. Click "Generate SalesScripts Email"
2. Select template: "DOT Testing - Initial Outreach"
3. Auto-fills with:
   - Decision-maker name
   - City/county name
   - Personalized details

4. Copy email → Send via Gmail/Outlook
5. Mark in DDCSS: "Outreach Sent" + date

---

### **Step 5: Track Follow-Up**

**In DDCSS:**
1. Set "Next Action": Follow up in 5 business days
2. If no response: Send Follow-Up #2
3. If response: Move to "Meeting Scheduled"
4. After meeting: Move to "Proposal Under Review"
5. Win or lose: Update status
