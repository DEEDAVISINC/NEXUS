## 🎯 What Are Service Contracts?

**Service contracts = You manage, subs perform**

Instead of selling products, you're selling **managed services**:
- Janitorial & custodial
- Landscaping & grounds maintenance
- Facility maintenance & repair
- IT services & support
- Security services
- Construction & renovation
- Moving & relocation
- Event services

**Your Role:** Prime contractor (bid winner, client relationship, project management)  
**Sub's Role:** Perform the actual work (with their licenses, insurance, expertise)  
**Your Profit:** 10-25% of contract value

You already have:
- ✅ General Liability (required for prime)
- ✅ E&O Insurance (HUGE advantage for IT/professional services!)

**Most small contractors don't have E&O!**

### **3. Michigan-Based** 🏠
- Local preference in Michigan contracts

### **4. Low Overhead** 💼

**What It Is:**
- Building cleaning
- Floor maintenance (stripping, waxing)
- Window cleaning
- Restroom sanitation
- Trash/recycling removal
- Commercial office cleaning

**Contract Examples:**
- Federal building janitorial: $300K-$1M annually
- School district cleaning: $200K-$500K annually
- Multi-building campus: $500K-$1M+ annually

**Why It's Good:**
- Very common contracts (governments need cleaning everywhere)
- Easy to find qualified subs
- Steady recurring revenue
- Low complexity to manage

**What You Need:**
- Your insurance (general liability)
- Sub's workers comp insurance
- Sub's cleaning business license
- Background checks (sub provides)

**Typical Margins:** 12-20%

**SAM.gov Searches:**
```
"janitorial services" WOSB
"custodial services" small business
"building cleaning" EDWOSB
"facility cleaning" woman owned
```

**Easy First Wins:**

- Municipal office cleaning ($50K-$100K)

## ✅ Qualification Quick Reference

### **Green Lights (Pursue These!):**
- ✅ WOSB or EDWOSB set-aside
- ✅ Contract value under $500K (start range)
- ✅ Michigan location
- ✅ No specialized clearances required
- ✅ Standard insurance requirements
- ✅ Small Business set-aside
- ✅ Performance period realistic

### **Yellow Lights (Evaluate Carefully):**
- ⚠️ Performance bond required (possible but adds cost)
- ⚠️ Contract value $500K-$1M (need past performance)
- ⚠️ Out of state (can do but less advantage)
- ⚠️ Extensive past performance required (use sub's via teaming)
- ⚠️ Security clearances (time consuming)

### **Red Lights (Skip or JV Required):**
- ❌ Contract value >$2M (for first year)
- ❌ Extensive clearances you don't have
- ❌ Highly specialized technical requirements
- ❌ Requires large bonding capacity you can't get
- ❌ Unrealistic timelines
- ❌ Requires equipment/facilities you don't have
