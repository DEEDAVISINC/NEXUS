The Problem

The $48B Problem Nobody Owns

Healthcare logistics is fragmented, expensive, and non-compliant. Hospitals juggle 5–10 courier vendors with no unified oversight, no single compliance framework, and no accountable partner.

🧪

Lab Specimens Delayed

Critical specimens miss testing windows, requiring costly recollections and delayed diagnoses. Temperature excursions void results. Recollection rates climb 15–20%. Chain of custody gaps invite audit exposure.

💊

Pharmacy Deliveries Missed

Patients discharged without medications are 2x more likely to return within 30 days. Controlled substances unaccounted for. Cold chain broken. Readmission rates spike 30%.

🏥

Equipment Stranded

Surgical instruments delayed, sterile supplies backlogged, patient care stalls. $2,400+ per cancelled surgery hour. Inter-facility transfers backlogged. Sterile processing bottlenecked.

The Regulatory Reality: HIPAA requires documented chain of custody for PHI-bearing specimens. DOT 49 CFR 173.196 governs biohazard transport. DEA mandates controlled substance tracking. Compliance isn’t optional — and most multi-vendor courier setups have gaps that invite audit exposure.

The Solution

One TPA. Complete Healthcare Logistics Coverage.

DDI serves as the single coordination point — one contract, one compliance framework, one invoice — managing credentialed courier networks across all three service pillars nationwide.

Technology & Compliance

Real-Time Visibility. Automated Compliance.

The PRISM/VITAL platform delivers GPS tracking, electronic proof of delivery, automated QC checklists, and quarterly performance analytics — across every courier in every pillar.

Real-Time Order Tracking

GPS-tracked couriers with live status updates. Pickup confirmation, in-transit status, and delivery completion visible to health system staff in real time.

Electronic Proof of Delivery

Every delivery captured with photo, electronic signature, and timestamp. Immutable audit trail for regulatory compliance and dispute resolution.

Automated QC Checklists

Pre-dispatch compliance verification: temperature check, packaging integrity, labeling accuracy, chain of custody — all verified before the courier leaves.

SLA Monitoring Dashboard

85% on-time delivery target tracked in real time. Automated escalation when deliveries approach SLA threshold. Exception reporting for every missed target.

Symplr Credentialing Integration

Every courier credentialed through Symplr before entering a facility. Background checks, immunization records, HIPAA training — verified and current.
