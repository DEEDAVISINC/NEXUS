How Pricing Works

Dynamic Pricing — Too Many Variables for Flat Rates.

ARENA programs are scoped per event. We do not publish rate sheets — every engagement has a different cost profile driven by the variables below.

Share your event details in a consultation. DDI builds a custom proposal with a line-item breakdown — trip mobility, program management, and optional PRIME Valet if needed — under one EDWOSB contract and one invoice.

Trip & Mobility Variables

  • Mileage — trip distance drives fulfillment cost on the mobility network
  • Event timing — surge pricing, peak arrival/departure windows, after-hours load-out
  • Who’s paying — passenger-pay vs. organizer-pay vs. split / sponsor model
  • Event scale — 500 attendees vs. 50,000 attendees changes volume and surge exposure

Program & Operations Variables

  • PUDO complexity — single gate vs. multi-entrance venue, staging lots, and geofence design
  • DDI program management — communications plan, day-of command staffing, engagement scope and duration
  • PRIME Valet (optional) — staffing headcount, hours on site, venue size and entry points
  • Program type — single event vs. recurring season or annual venue program
