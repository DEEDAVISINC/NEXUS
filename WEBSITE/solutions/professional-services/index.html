Sector Overview

Multiple Service Lines. One TPA Relationship.

Professional services buyers — title companies, law firms, HR departments, health systems, and government agencies — often juggle separate vendors for notary, fingerprinting, I-9, apostille, and credentialing. DDI consolidates program administration under a single nationwide contract with credentialed fulfillment dispatched where each act is performed.

“Dee Davis Inc. is a nationwide contract management TPA for professional and legal services — notary and signing agency dispatch, biometric credentialing, I-9/E-Verify program administration, medical professional credentialing, apostille coordination, and legal document courier — headquartered in Troy, Michigan, performing in all 50 states + DC.”
