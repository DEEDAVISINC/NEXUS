## OUTREACH RULES — WHAT NOT TO SAY

- **NEVER** name the specific federal agency (DHA, DeCA, SSA) — same buyer protection rules as suppliers
- **NEVER** share solicitation numbers
- **NEVER** say "we're bidding on a contract" — say "we're pursuing active federal contracts"
- **NEVER** name Lakota by name in recruitment materials — say "our technology partner"
- **DO** say "federal contracts," "SWFT submission," "DCSA" — operators know what these mean
- **DO** emphasize "independent" and "no franchise fees" — this is the differentiator
- **DO** emphasize higher pay — this closes deals
