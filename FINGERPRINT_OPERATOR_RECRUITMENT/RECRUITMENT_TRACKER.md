## OPERATOR QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

### Minimum Requirements (Must Have)
- [ ] Owns or has access to FBI Appendix F compliant livescan equipment
- [ ] State-certified/authorized fingerprint roller (in their state)
- [ ] Background check cleared (clean criminal history)
- [ ] Willing to sign DDI Operator Agreement (NDA + non-compete + service terms)
- [ ] Reliable — can respond to scheduling within 24 hours
- [ ] Professional — will represent DDI to federal employees

### Preferred (Nice to Have)
- [ ] Mobile capability (can travel to applicant's location)
- [ ] Hard card scanning capable (FD-258 ink cards + scan)
- [ ] Experience with federal background checks
- [ ] Multiple locations or staff (covers wider area)
- [ ] Also a notary (dual-service = more value per operator)
- [ ] NLSA certified
- [ ] Existing business insurance (general liability)

### Equipment Standards
- Livescan device must meet FBI IQS (Image Quality Specification)
- NFIQ (NIST Fingerprint Image Quality) score capability
- Compatible with Lakota WHORL software OR willing to install
- Internet connection for electronic submission

## COMPENSATION MODEL OPTIONS (Discuss with Sam)

| Model | How It Works | Operator Gets | DDI Gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Per-Transaction Fee** | DDI pays operator flat fee per fingerprint session | $25-$50 per session | Contract rate minus operator fee |
| **Revenue Share** | Percentage split of contract rate | 40-60% of DDI's rate | 40-60% of DDI's rate |
| **Guaranteed Minimum + Bonus** | Base pay per session + bonus for volume/quality | Base $30 + quality bonus | Remainder after operator pay |
| **Tiered Rate** | Higher rate for harder-to-service areas (rural, territories) | $25-$75 depending on location | Contract rate minus tiered fee |

**Recommended:** Per-transaction fee with tiered rates for rural/territory locations. Simple, predictable, easy to explain in recruitment.

**Benchmark:** Fieldprint pays operators roughly $15-$25 per session. DDI should beat that to attract operators away from the majors.
