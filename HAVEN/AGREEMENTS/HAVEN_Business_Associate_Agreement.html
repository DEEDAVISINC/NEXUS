Purpose: This BAA is entered into in connection with the HAVEN Partner Service Agreement between the Parties (the "Service Agreement") and governs the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information by Business Associate in connection with services provided under the HAVEN program.

Recitals

WHEREAS, Covered Entity operates the HAVEN disaster response third-party administration program and is a "covered entity" or "business associate" under HIPAA;

WHEREAS, Business Associate provides services to Covered Entity that involve access to Protected Health Information;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to comply with the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA"), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH"), and implementing regulations at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, the "HIPAA Rules");

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants herein, the Parties agree as follows:

Article 1 — Definitions

1.1 General. Terms used but not defined in this BAA shall have the meanings set forth in the HIPAA Rules.

1.2 "Breach" means the acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of PHI in a manner not permitted by the Privacy Rule that compromises the security or privacy of the PHI, as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402.

1.3 "Designated Record Set" means a group of records maintained by or for a covered entity as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.501.

1.4 "Electronic Protected Health Information" or "ePHI" means PHI that is transmitted or maintained in electronic media.

1.5 "Individual" means the person who is the subject of PHI and shall include a person who qualifies as a personal representative under 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(g).

1.6 "Protected Health Information" or "PHI" means individually identifiable health information as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103, limited to information created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

1.7 "Required by Law" has the meaning set forth in 45 C.F.R. § 164.103.

1.8 "Security Incident" means the attempted or successful unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of information or interference with system operations in an information system, as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.304.

1.9 "Subcontractor" means a person to whom Business Associate delegates a function, activity, or service, other than in the capacity of a workforce member.

Article 2 — Permitted Uses and Disclosures of PHI

2.1 Performance of Services. Business Associate may use and disclose PHI solely to perform services for Covered Entity as specified in the Service Agreement, provided such use or disclosure would not violate the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity.

2.2 Business Associate's Operations. Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, provided:

  1. The disclosure is Required by Law; or
  2. Business Associate obtains reasonable assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially and used or further disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and the recipient agrees to notify Business Associate of any breach of confidentiality.

2.3 Minimum Necessary. Business Associate shall use, disclose, or request only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.502(b) and the minimum necessary policies and procedures of Covered Entity.

2.4 Prohibited Uses and Disclosures. Business Associate shall NOT:

  1. Use or disclose PHI for any purpose not authorized by this BAA or the Service Agreement;
  2. Use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity;
  3. Use or disclose PHI for marketing purposes without authorization;
  4. Sell PHI without authorization;
  5. Use or disclose genetic information for underwriting purposes.
Article 3 — Safeguards

3.1 Administrative Safeguards. Business Associate shall implement administrative safeguards as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.308, including but not limited to:

  1. Security management process with risk analysis and risk management;
  2. Assigned security responsibility;
  3. Workforce security, including authorization and supervision;
  4. Information access management;
  5. Security awareness and training;
  6. Security incident procedures;
  7. Contingency plan for data backup, disaster recovery, and emergency operations;
  8. Periodic evaluation of security measures.

3.2 Physical Safeguards. Business Associate shall implement physical safeguards as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.310, including:

  1. Facility access controls;
  2. Workstation use and security policies;
  3. Device and media controls for disposal, re-use, and removal.

3.3 Technical Safeguards. Business Associate shall implement technical safeguards as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.312, including:

  1. Access controls (unique user identification, emergency access, automatic logoff, encryption);
  2. Audit controls;
  3. Integrity controls;
  4. Person or entity authentication;
  5. Transmission security (encryption of ePHI in transit).

3.4 Policies and Procedures. Business Associate shall maintain reasonable and appropriate policies and procedures to comply with the Security Rule, as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.316.

3.5 Encryption. Business Associate shall encrypt all ePHI at rest and in transit using encryption standards consistent with NIST guidelines.

Article 4 — Reporting Requirements

4.1 Breach Notification. Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Breach of Unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and in no event later than 24 hours after discovery. The report shall include:

  1. Identification of each Individual whose PHI has been or is reasonably believed to have been accessed, acquired, used, or disclosed;
  2. A brief description of what happened, including the date of the Breach and date of discovery;
  3. Description of the types of PHI involved;
  4. Steps Business Associate is taking to investigate, mitigate harm, and prevent future breaches;
  5. Contact information for questions.

4.2 Security Incidents. Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Security Incident of which it becomes aware within 48 hours. The Parties acknowledge that unsuccessful Security Incidents (e.g., pings, port scans, unsuccessful log-on attempts) occur regularly and agree that notification of such unsuccessful incidents is not required.

4.3 Mitigation. Business Associate shall mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to Business Associate of a use or disclosure of PHI in violation of this BAA.

4.4 Cooperation. Business Associate shall cooperate with Covered Entity's investigation of any Breach or Security Incident and shall provide all information and documentation necessary for Covered Entity to comply with its breach notification obligations.

4.5 Notification Costs. If a Breach is caused by Business Associate's acts or omissions, Business Associate shall bear the costs of notification to affected Individuals, credit monitoring services (if appropriate), and other remediation measures as determined by Covered Entity.

Article 5 — Subcontractors

5.1 Subcontractor Agreements. Business Associate shall ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this BAA.

5.2 Responsibility. Business Associate shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subcontractors as if performed by Business Associate itself.

5.3 Notification. Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity of any Subcontractor that will have access to PHI prior to providing such access.

Article 6 — Individual Rights

6.1 Access. Business Associate shall make PHI maintained in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity or, as directed by Covered Entity, to an Individual, within 15 days of a request, to enable Covered Entity to fulfill its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524.

6.2 Amendment. Business Associate shall make PHI available for amendment and incorporate any amendments to PHI as directed by Covered Entity within 30 days, to enable Covered Entity to fulfill its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526.

6.3 Accounting of Disclosures. Business Associate shall maintain and make available information required for an accounting of disclosures within 30 days of a request, to enable Covered Entity to fulfill its obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528. Business Associate shall maintain such information for at least six (6) years from the date of disclosure.

6.4 Restrictions. Business Associate shall comply with any restrictions on the use or disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, provided Covered Entity notifies Business Associate of such restrictions.

6.5 Confidential Communications. Business Associate shall accommodate reasonable requests for confidential communications as directed by Covered Entity.

Article 7 — Covered Entity Obligations

7.1 Notice of Privacy Practices. Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitations in its Notice of Privacy Practices that may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

7.2 Restrictions. Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any changes in, or revocation of, permission by an Individual to use or disclose PHI, to the extent such changes affect Business Associate's permitted uses and disclosures.

7.3 Permissible Requests. Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity.

Article 8 — Term and Termination

8.1 Term. This BAA shall be effective as of the Effective Date and shall continue in effect until the Service Agreement terminates or until all PHI is returned or destroyed.

8.2 Termination for Breach. Covered Entity may terminate this BAA and the Service Agreement immediately if:

  1. Business Associate materially breaches this BAA and fails to cure within 30 days of notice; or
  2. Business Associate has breached a material term of this BAA and cure is not possible.

8.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination:

  1. Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI received from Covered Entity or created or received on behalf of Covered Entity, if feasible;
  2. If return or destruction is not feasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this BAA to such PHI and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible;
  3. Business Associate shall certify in writing to Covered Entity that all PHI has been returned or destroyed, or that return or destruction is not feasible, within 30 days of termination.

8.4 Survival. The obligations of Business Associate under this Article 8 shall survive termination of this BAA.

Article 9 — General Provisions

9.1 Regulatory References. A reference to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.

9.2 Amendment. The Parties agree to amend this BAA as necessary to comply with changes in the HIPAA Rules or other applicable law. If the Parties cannot agree on an amendment, either Party may terminate this BAA upon 30 days written notice.

9.3 Interpretation. Any ambiguity in this BAA shall be interpreted to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

9.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries. Nothing in this BAA shall confer upon any person other than the Parties any rights or remedies.

9.5 Governing Law. This BAA shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan and applicable federal law.

9.6 Indemnification. Business Associate shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Covered Entity from any claims, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Business Associate's breach of this BAA or violation of the HIPAA Rules.

9.7 Audit Rights. Covered Entity shall have the right to audit Business Associate's compliance with this BAA and the HIPAA Rules upon reasonable notice. Business Associate shall cooperate fully with such audits.

9.8 Government Access. Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of HHS for purposes of determining Covered Entity's compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

9.9 Entire Agreement. This BAA constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

9.10 Counterparts. This BAA may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
