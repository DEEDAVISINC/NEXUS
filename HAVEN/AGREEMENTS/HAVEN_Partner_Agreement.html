RECITALS

A. DDI operates the HAVEN (Housing, Assistance, Vital Emergency Network) disaster response third-party administration program, coordinating housing, transportation, and medical continuity services for managed care organization (MCO) members displaced by disasters.

B. Partner has the capability and capacity to provide services within the HAVEN program as indicated above.

C. The parties wish to establish a partnership whereby Partner will provide services to HAVEN program members under DDI's coordination and management.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Partner agrees to provide services as described in Exhibit A (Scope of Services) attached hereto. Services shall be provided in the geographic areas and during the time periods specified in Exhibit A.

2. DDI RESPONSIBILITIES

DDI shall:

  1. Maintain MCO client relationships and contracts
  2. Perform member intake, needs assessment, and case management
  3. Coordinate service dispatch and scheduling with Partner
  4. Handle all billing and reimbursement with MCO clients
  5. Provide quality monitoring and compliance oversight
  6. Maintain 24/7 disaster activation hotline
3. PARTNER RESPONSIBILITIES

Partner shall:

  1. Provide services in accordance with Exhibit A and all applicable laws
  2. Maintain surge capacity as specified in Exhibit A
  3. Ensure all personnel are properly credentialed, trained, and background-checked
  4. Comply with HIPAA and all healthcare privacy regulations
  5. Respond to service requests within timeframes specified in Exhibit B (Service Levels)
  6. Maintain required insurance coverage as specified in Exhibit C (Insurance Requirements)
  7. Report service completion and issues to DDI within 24 hours
4. COMPENSATION

DDI shall compensate Partner according to the rate schedule in Exhibit D (Rate Schedule). Payment terms:

  1. Partner shall submit invoices weekly during active disaster events, monthly otherwise
  2. DDI shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt
  3. DDI's payment is contingent upon DDI's receipt of payment from MCO clients
  4. Disputed charges shall be resolved within fifteen (15) business days
5. SERVICE LEVELS

Partner agrees to meet the service levels specified in Exhibit B, including but not limited to:

Metric Standard
Response to activation request Within 2 hours
Service fulfillment (routine) Within 24 hours of request
Service fulfillment (urgent) Within 4 hours of request
Issue reporting Within 24 hours of occurrence
Quality compliance 95% or higher
6. INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

Partner shall maintain the insurance coverage specified in Exhibit C, including at minimum:

  1. General Liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate
  2. Workers' Compensation: State statutory limits
  3. Professional Liability (if applicable): $1,000,000 per occurrence
  4. Commercial Auto (if applicable): $1,000,000 per occurrence

DDI shall be named as Additional Insured on all policies. Partner shall provide Certificates of Insurance prior to commencing services and upon each policy renewal.

7. HIPAA COMPLIANCE

Partner acknowledges it may receive Protected Health Information (PHI) and agrees to:

  1. Execute DDI's Business Associate Agreement (BAA) prior to receiving PHI
  2. Comply with all applicable HIPAA and HITECH requirements
  3. Implement appropriate safeguards for PHI
  4. Report any breach or suspected breach within 24 hours
  5. Train all personnel on HIPAA requirements
8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-CIRCUMVENTION

Partner agrees to the terms of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement executed between the parties. Additionally, Partner agrees NOT to:

  1. Contact DDI's MCO clients directly for any purpose
  2. Solicit business from DDI's MCO clients or their members
  3. Use information obtained through HAVEN to compete with DDI
  4. Disclose DDI's client relationships to any third party

This obligation survives termination for two (2) years.

9. TERM AND TERMINATION
  1. Term: This Agreement shall be effective for one (1) year from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless terminated.
  2. Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate with sixty (60) days written notice.
  3. Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice.
  4. Effect of Termination: Partner shall complete all services in progress at the time of termination and shall be compensated for services properly rendered.
10. INDEMNIFICATION

Partner shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless DDI from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from Partner's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. DDI shall indemnify Partner from claims arising from DDI's negligence or breach.

11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

Partner is an independent contractor. Nothing herein creates an employment, agency, joint venture, or partnership relationship. Partner is solely responsible for its taxes, benefits, and compliance obligations.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This Agreement shall be governed by Michigan law. Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Oakland County, Michigan.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, including all Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
