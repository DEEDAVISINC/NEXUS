Thank you for your interest in joining the HAVEN disaster response network. Prior to activating your partnership, we require proof of insurance coverage as outlined below. Please have your insurance provider submit a Certificate of Insurance (COI) meeting these requirements.

Insurance Requirements by Partner Type

🏠 Housing Partners

  • General Liability: $1M/$2M
  • Property Coverage: As required
  • Workers' Comp: State statutory

🚗 Transport Partners

  • General Liability: $1M/$2M
  • Commercial Auto: $1M+
  • Workers' Comp: State statutory
  • Professional Liability: $1M (NEMT)

💊 Medical Partners

  • General Liability: $1M/$2M
  • Professional Liability: $1M+
  • Workers' Comp: State statutory
  • Product Liability: As applicable
