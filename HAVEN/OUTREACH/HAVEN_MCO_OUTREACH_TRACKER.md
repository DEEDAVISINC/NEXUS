## OUTREACH RULES (REVISED)

**OLD RULE (May 9):** Wait for state registrations before MCO outreach
**NEW RULE (May 25):** Active emergencies override registration gates — reach out NOW, sort paperwork after

**Emergency Outreach Triggers:**
- FEMA disaster declaration
- State emergency declaration
- NWS Flood Watch/Warning in service area
- Hurricane Watch/Warning in service area
- Major weather event displacing residents

**When triggered:** 
1. Identify affected MCOs by state
2. Send emergency follow-up within 24 hours
3. Call if email doesn't get response within 4 hours
4. Document everything in this tracker
