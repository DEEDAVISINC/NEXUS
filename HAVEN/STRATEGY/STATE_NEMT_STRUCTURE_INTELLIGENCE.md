# STATE NEMT STRUCTURE INTELLIGENCE
**Updated:** May 14, 2026
**Purpose:** Understand how NEMT is structured in each state — determines DDI's path (TPA prime vs MCO-only)

---

## THE KEY INSIGHT

**Most states do NOT have a Texas-style FFS open enrollment.** NEMT is handled through:
1. **Statewide Brokers** (Verida, Modivcare) — who ARE the TPAs
2. **MCOs** — who manage their own NEMT networks

**DDI's TPA model only works as a state-level prime in states with FFS open enrollment.**
In broker/MCO states, DDI's path is **MCO contracts for HAVEN services**.

### TEXAS — TPA PRIME OPPORTUNITY ⭐

**Structure:** Dual-path — FFS through HHSC + MCO managed care

**HHSC NEMT Open Enrollment (HHS0016482):**
- **Status:** OPEN NOW — closes September 15, 2026
- **Type:** Rolling applications, reviewed continuously
- **Contract:** 3 years base + 2 one-year extensions (5 years total)
- **Subcontracting:** ✅ Allowed — can subcontract "all or part" of transportation
- **DDI Model:** TPA prime, subcontract to Uber Health + local NEMT
- **Reimbursement Rates:**
  - Metro (urban): $32.38/one-way leg
  - Micro (suburban): $59.64/one-way leg
  - Rural: $76.22/one-way leg

**Requirements for DDI:**
- TX Secretary of State registration (~$750)
- TMHP enrollment (required to bill Medicaid)
- Insurance: CGL $1M/$2M, Auto $1M, Cyber $1M, Umbrella $1M, Workers' Comp
- Physical TX office (can use sub's address)
- Key personnel: Project Manager, Transport Supervisor, Dispatcher, Vehicle Safety Supervisor

**DDI Path:** Apply for HHSC OE as TPA prime + continue MCO outreach for HAVEN
