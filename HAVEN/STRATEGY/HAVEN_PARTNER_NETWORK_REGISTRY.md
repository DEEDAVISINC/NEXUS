### FEMA TRANSITIONAL SHELTERING ASSISTANCE (TSA) PROGRAM

**How it works:** FEMA directly pays room costs + taxes to participating hotels for disaster survivors. Hotels enroll via Corporate Lodging Consultants (CLC) at ela.corplodging.com.

**DDI's role:** Coordinate member placement at TSA-participating hotels during FEMA-declared disasters. DDI handles scheduling, transport to hotel, and continuity tracking — MCO pays DDI for coordination.

**Participating hotel search:** femaemergencyhotels.com (for survivors with FEMA registration number)

## HAVEN PARTNER VALUE PROPOSITION

### What DDI Offers Partners:

**Transport Partners:**
- Guaranteed trip volume from MCO contracts (not dependent on member calls)
- Payment within 30 days (DDI as intermediary, not Medicaid direct)
- Disaster activation protocol = surge volume during FEMA events
- No marketing costs — DDI brings the members

**Housing Partners:**
- Block room reservations during hurricane season
- Guaranteed payment within 30 days (DDI as intermediary)
- No per-diem tracking — DDI handles all member coordination
- Disaster-specific rates negotiated in advance

**Medical Partners:**
- Member continuity referrals during displacement events
- Coordination with MCO care managers (DDI handles communication)
- Portable care plans — DDI ensures records follow the member
- No claims delays — DDI manages MCO billing

**Personal Care Platforms:**
- Guaranteed volume from MCO contracts (authorization-based)
- B2B invoicing (DDI as intermediary, reliable payment)
- Scalable across 10 states (DDI's expansion footprint)
- Single relationship = national coverage
