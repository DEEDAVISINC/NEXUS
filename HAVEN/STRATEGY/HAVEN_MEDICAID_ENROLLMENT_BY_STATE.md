# HAVEN — Medicaid Provider Enrollment: Foreign Entity Requirements by State
**Updated:** May 10, 2026
**Purpose:** Does DDI need to register as a foreign entity before enrolling as a Medicaid provider?

---

## THE SHORT ANSWER

**No state explicitly requires foreign entity registration as a condition of Medicaid provider enrollment in the enrollment forms themselves.** The Medicaid enrollment process asks for NPI, EIN, ownership disclosure, and domicile state provider numbers — not Secretary of State filings.

However, **operating in a state without foreign entity registration may violate that state's business laws separately** — meaning you could get enrolled but face legal issues later. These are two separate compliance tracks.

---

## LOUISIANA — NO FOREIGN ENTITY REQUIRED FOR ENROLLMENT

**Source:** La. Admin. Code tit. 50, § I-1511 (effective 7/1/2024)

Louisiana has a clear out-of-state provider enrollment rule:
- Out-of-state providers CAN enroll by submitting a provider enrollment application
- The enrollment form asks: "Is this enrolling entity/business located out-of-state?" → Yes/No
- If yes, it asks: "Has this out-of-state entity/business been issued any Medicaid or Medicare provider numbers by the domicile state?" → Yes/No
- **DDI answers: Yes — Michigan CHAMPS Provider ID 6309049, NPI 1538939111**
- No mention of Secretary of State foreign entity filing in the enrollment packet

**BONUS: Louisiana also has an exemption for limited services:**
If a provider is enrolled in Medicare OR another state's Medicaid, AND the services are a single instance or to a single beneficiary over 180 days — enrollment may not even be required (Section F of § I-1511). But for ongoing TPA work, full enrollment is the right path.

**VERDICT: DDI can apply for LA Medicaid enrollment NOW without foreign entity registration.**

## FLORIDA — CONDITIONAL, NO FOREIGN ENTITY FOR ENROLLMENT

**Source:** Florida Medicaid Out-of-State Provider Enrollment (FLMMIS Portal)

Florida reimburses out-of-state providers under specific circumstances:
- Emergency services
- Health would be endangered if postponed
- Prior-authorized services
- Foster care/adoption

Florida's Medicaid enrollment form does NOT require Florida Secretary of State registration as a prerequisite. It requires NPI, federal tax ID, and applicable licenses.

**However:** Florida is more restrictive about WHEN they'll enroll out-of-state providers. DDI may need to demonstrate a specific service need (disaster response qualifies under emergency services) or get prior authorization from AHCA.

**VERDICT: DDI can likely apply without foreign entity registration, but may face scrutiny on the "why" — disaster response TPA is a valid reason.**

## TEXAS — NO FOREIGN ENTITY REQUIRED FOR ENROLLMENT

**Source:** Texas Medicaid Provider Procedures Manual, Section 1.01

Texas allows out-of-state providers to enroll:
- Must meet all applicable eligibility criteria
- Must be approved by Texas HHS Commission
- Must have NPI
- Must file complete Texas Medicaid enrollment application
- Processing takes up to 60 days

The Texas enrollment application asks for business information and NPI — no Secretary of State filing required as a condition of enrollment.

**VERDICT: DDI can apply for TX Medicaid enrollment without foreign entity registration.**

**NEXUS note (May 2026):** TMHP may issue **portal access** and an **Enrollment Transaction** (e.g. **D19273048**) **before** a full application is filed. Dee confirmed **Texas Medicaid enrollment not yet submitted** — next step is to complete TMHP enrollment when prioritized.

## MARYLAND — FOREIGN ENTITY REQUIRED ⚠️

**Source:** Direct communication from Maryland Medicaid enrollment office to DDI

Maryland explicitly told DDI that foreign entity registration with the Maryland Secretary of State is required BEFORE applying as a Medicaid provider.

This is a state-by-state variance. While LA, TX, and FL do not gate enrollment behind SOS filing, **Maryland does.** This means:
- DDI must file as a foreign entity with the Maryland SDAT (State Department of Assessments & Taxation)
- Get a registered agent in Maryland
- THEN apply for Maryland Medicaid enrollment

**Cost:** ~$100 filing fee + registered agent (~$100-150/year)
**URL:** https://dat.maryland.gov/businesses
**VERDICT: Foreign entity first, then Medicaid enrollment. No shortcut.**
