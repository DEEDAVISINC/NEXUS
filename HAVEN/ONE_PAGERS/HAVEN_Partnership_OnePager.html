HAVEN Program

Disaster Response TPA

When disasters strike, health plan members are displaced. MCOs are responsible for continuity of care but lack disaster response infrastructure.

DDI provides the coordination layer — pre-built partner networks, instant activation, seamless member services across housing, transport, and medical continuity.
