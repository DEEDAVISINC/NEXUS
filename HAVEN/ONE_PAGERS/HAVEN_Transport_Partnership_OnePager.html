What We Need

Transport Partner Network

When disasters hit, MCO members need transport immediately — evacuation before the storm, medical rides after. Standard NEMT networks are overwhelmed or shut down.

DDI coordinates the transport layer — pre-positioned partners ready to activate the moment a disaster declaration drops. No scrambling. No cold calls. Network is built before it's needed.

Three Transport Phases
🚨

Pre-Disaster

Evacuation of high-risk members — dialysis patients, mobility-impaired, oxygen-dependent. Transport to shelters and staging areas.

⛑️

Active Disaster

Shelter-to-clinic transport. Emergency pharmacy runs. Medical appointment continuity in displacement locations.

🏠

Recovery Phase

Return transport when areas reopen. Ongoing NEMT for displaced members resettled in new locations. 30-90 days of elevated volume.
