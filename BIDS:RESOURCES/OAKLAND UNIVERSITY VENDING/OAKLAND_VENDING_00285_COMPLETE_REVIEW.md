## 📝 WHAT THEY NEED

### **Scope of Services:**

This is a **FULL-SERVICE VENDING OPERATION** - NOT just supplying machines!

**Requirements:**
1. **Furnish & Install** vending machines (owned by vendor, not OU)
2. **Operate** all machines across campus
3. **Maintain & Service** all equipment
4. **Stock & Replenish** products regularly
5. **Handle** routine and emergency maintenance
6. **Provide** parts and replacements at NO cost to university
7. **Staff** the operation with your employees
8. **Monitor** inventory, sales, and machine health remotely
9. **Respond** to service calls within 4 business hours
10. **Maintain** 98% uptime per machine per month

### **Products to Vend:**
- Snacks (candy, nuts, gum, cookies, chips, etc.)
- Coffee
- **Optional:** Ice cream/frozen novelties

### **Requirements:**
- Cashless payment (credit/debit, mobile wallets, EMV)
- Bill/coin acceptance with change-making
- Remote telemetry for monitoring
- Contemporary product mix (gluten-free, nut-free, vegan, low-sugar options)
- University approval rights over product menus

## ❌ WHY YOU SHOULD PASS

**This is NOT your business model:**

1. **Capital Intensive**
   - You need to OWN the vending machines (not just supply them)
   - Machines cost $3,000-8,000 EACH
   - Need dozens of machines for campus-wide coverage
   - Total investment: $100,000-300,000+ in equipment

2. **Ongoing Operations**
   - Need employees to restock machines regularly
   - Need service technicians for repairs
   - Need inventory management system
   - Need delivery trucks for restocking
   - This is a FULL-TIME BUSINESS, not a one-time product sale

3. **Revenue Share Model**
   - You pay OU a commission on sales (typically 10-30%)
   - You absorb product costs, labor, equipment
   - Profit comes from sales margin minus all costs
   - This is NOT a simple markup - it's running a vending business

4. **Service Level Requirements**
   - 98% uptime required
   - 4-hour response time to service calls
   - After-hours emergency service required
   - SLA penalties if you fail to meet standards

5. **5-Year Commitment**
   - This is a long-term operational contract
   - Can't just "buy and deliver" - you run the business for 5 years
   - Exit requires equipment removal within 10 days

## 💰 FINANCIAL REALITY

**What this actually requires:**

### **Startup Costs:**
- Vending machines: $100,000-300,000
- Initial product inventory: $20,000-40,000
- Delivery vehicles: $40,000-80,000
- Telemetry systems: $10,000-20,000
- **Total Startup:** $170,000-440,000

### **Monthly Operating Costs:**
- Restocking labor: $5,000-10,000/month
- Product costs: $15,000-30,000/month
- Maintenance & repairs: $2,000-5,000/month
- Insurance, admin, overhead: $3,000-6,000/month
- **Total Monthly:** $25,000-51,000/month

### **Revenue Model:**
- Gross sales: $50,000-100,000/month (estimate)
- Minus commission to OU (10-30%): -$5,000-30,000
- Minus product costs (50-60%): -$25,000-60,000
- Minus operating costs: -$25,000-51,000
- **Net Profit:** Highly variable, often break-even first 2-3 years

**This is NOT a simple product resale - it's operating a vending business!**
