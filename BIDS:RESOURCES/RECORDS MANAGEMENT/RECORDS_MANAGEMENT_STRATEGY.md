# RECORDS MANAGEMENT & DOCUMENT SCANNING — Bid Strategy

**Category:** Service Brokering (EDWOSB Prime + Scanning Sub)
**Business Model:** Win records management contracts as EDWOSB, subcontract scanning/storage to specialists
**NAICS Codes:** 518210 (Computing Infrastructure Providers, Data Processing), 561410 (Document Preparation Services)

---

## WHY THIS FITS

- **Government is drowning in paper** — every agency has backlog of records to digitize
- **Mandatory compliance** — records retention laws force agencies to manage documents
- **Pairs with shredding** — scan first, then shred (double revenue from same client)
- **EDWOSB advantage** — records management companies rarely have diversity certs
- **Growing market** — more agencies moving to digital, COVID accelerated this
- **Project-based + recurring** — big scanning projects + ongoing storage/management

## THE SHREDDING + SCANNING COMBO

**Win a scanning contract → scan documents → then shred the originals**

```
Step 1: Win records management contract
Step 2: Scan all documents (sub does scanning)
Step 3: After retention period, shred originals (your shredding sub)
Step 4: Ongoing digital management (you maintain)

Revenue: Scanning fee + Shredding fee + Ongoing management fee
= Triple revenue from one relationship
```

## PROFIT MODEL

**Example: Court Records Scanning Project**
- 200,000 pages of case files
- Sub cost: 200,000 x $0.05 = $10,000
- Contract price: 200,000 x $0.12 = $24,000
- **Gross Profit: $14,000 (58% margin)**

**Example: Annual Records Storage + Scanning**
- 500 boxes stored, 50,000 pages scanned per year
- Storage sub cost: 500 x $0.35/mo x 12 = $2,100
- Scanning sub cost: 50,000 x $0.05 = $2,500
- Total sub: $4,600
- Contract price: $9,500
- **Gross Profit: $4,900 (52% margin)**
