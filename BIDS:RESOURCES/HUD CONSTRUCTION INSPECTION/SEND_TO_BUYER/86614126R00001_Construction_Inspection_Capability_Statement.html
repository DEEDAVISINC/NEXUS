Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide construction inspection, quality assurance, and project management support services to federal agencies throughout the Great Lakes region. Our Michigan base and network of ICC-certified inspectors positions us for reliable, responsive support to HUD multifamily housing projects across the Chicago regional office coverage area.

Service Approach — Construction Inspection
  • Onsite construction inspection for new multifamily housing projects
  • Substantial rehabilitation project monitoring and inspection
  • HUD building code and standards compliance verification
  • Progress reporting and documentation
  • Deficiency identification and corrective action tracking
  • ADA and Fair Housing Act accessibility compliance review
  • Fire/life safety systems inspection
  • Structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing inspection
  • Energy efficiency and green building standard verification
  • Final inspection and punch list management
Key Qualifications
  • SBA-certified EDWOSB / WOSB — supports HUD socioeconomic goals
  • Michigan-based with coverage across HUD Chicago region (IL, IN, MI, MN, OH, WI)
  • Network of ICC-certified building inspectors
  • Licensed professional engineers available for complex inspections
  • Understanding of HUD multifamily housing standards and protocols
  • Experience with federal construction documentation requirements
  • Digital reporting and photo documentation capabilities
  • Rapid deployment — inspectors available within 48 hours

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As a Michigan-based EDWOSB, DDI offers HUD both socioeconomic contracting value and regional operational coverage. Michigan has significant multifamily housing development and rehabilitation activity, particularly in Detroit, Grand Rapids, Flint, and Lansing. Our team of certified inspectors understands Michigan building codes, local conditions, and HUD-specific requirements for multifamily projects.
