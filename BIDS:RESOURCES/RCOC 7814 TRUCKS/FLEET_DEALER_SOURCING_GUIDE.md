## 💰 PRICING STRATEGY:

**Fleet dealers typically offer:**
- MSRP minus 10-15% for fleet sales
- Additional 2-5% for government/municipal
- Volume discounts for multiple units

**Your markup:**
- Get their best fleet price
- Add 5-10% margin
- Still be competitive vs. retail

**Example:**
- Truck MSRP: $40,000
- Fleet discount (12%): $35,200
- Government discount (3%): $34,144
- Your price (+7%): $36,534
- Your profit per truck: $2,390
