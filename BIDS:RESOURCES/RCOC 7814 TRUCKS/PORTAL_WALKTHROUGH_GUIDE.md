## STEP 2: SELECT THE RIGHT CONTRACT

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard.

- Look for **Contract 081325** — this is Class 1-3 vehicles (pickup trucks)
- Contract 032824 is for Class 4-8 (medium/heavy duty) — you don't need this one for RCOC 7814

---

## STEP 3: BUILD YOUR FIRST TRUCK (Inspector Configuration)

1. Click **"Build a Vehicle"** or **"Request a Quote"**
2. Select the **make** — start with Chevrolet Silverado 1500 (or Ford F-150, or GMC Sierra 1500)
3. Select **model year** — must be current (2026)
4. **Configure the base truck:**

   **Inspector Truck specs:**
   - Cab: **Extended Cab** (SuperCab for Ford)
   - Bed length: **6.7 foot** (or closest available — 6.5' is OK)
   - Drivetrain: **4x4**
   - Engine: **V6 Turbo or V8** (minimum 300hp / 350 ft-lb torque)
   - Color: **Red**
   - Limited slip differential: **Yes**

5. **Add options/packages:**
   - Tow package
   - Trailer brake controller
   - Running boards
   - Any fleet/work truck package available

6. **Note the base truck price** — this is your Sourcewell contract price (already discounted)

---

## STEP 4: ADD UPFITTING (Critical)

This is where the money is. The portal may have upfitting options, or you may need to request them separately.

**Look for sections like:**
- "Accessories"
- "Upfits"
- "Utility Body"
- "Vehicle Accessories and Uplifts"

**Inspector truck upfits needed:**
- ARE truck cap (Deluxe Commercial Unit — DCU or equivalent)
- CargoGlide sliding cargo tray (CG1500XL or similar)
- Amber/Green LED strobe light package (roof mount + grille)
- Traffic advisor light bar
- Weatherguard toolbox (cross-bed)
- LINE-X spray-in bed liner
- Running boards (if not included in base)
- Back rack with work lights

**If the portal doesn't have upfitting options:**
- Note the base truck price
- You'll need to get upfitting quoted separately (more on this below)
- OR email Jesse and ask him to add upfitting to the build

## KEY THINGS TO REMEMBER

- **Sourcewell pricing is already discounted** — it's pre-negotiated government cooperative pricing
- **You're buying at wholesale** and bidding at wholesale + your margin
- **Your markup (10-15%) is your profit** — this is standard for fleet coordinators/prime contractors
- **Save screenshots of every quote** from the portal as backup documentation
- **Pricing may not include upfitting** — check carefully and get upfit quotes separately if needed
- **RCOC values turnkey pricing** — one number for a fully equipped, ready-to-work truck
