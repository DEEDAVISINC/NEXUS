Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide marine construction, dredging, and heavy civil engineering support services to federal agencies throughout the Great Lakes region. Our established relationships with experienced Great Lakes dredging contractors enable us to deliver reliable, cost-effective maintenance dredging services for USACE navigation projects.

Service Approach — Maintenance Dredging
  • Maintenance dredging of harbors, channels, and navigation facilities
  • Mechanical and hydraulic dredging operations
  • Confined disposal facility (CDF) management and placement
  • Sediment testing and environmental compliance coordination
  • Dredged material transport and placement
  • Project management and USACE coordination
  • Environmental monitoring and reporting
  • Quality control and survey verification
  • Great Lakes seasonal operations planning
Key Qualifications
  • SBA-certified EDWOSB / WOSB — supports USACE socioeconomic goals

  • Network of experienced Great Lakes dredging contractors
  • Understanding of USACE dredging specifications and standards

  • Project management for marine construction operations
  • Safety management (EM 385-1-1 compliance)
  • Familiar with Great Lakes navigation infrastructure

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As a Michigan-based EDWOSB, DDI offers the USACE Detroit District both socioeconomic contracting value and Great Lakes regional expertise. Our dredging subcontractor partners have decades of experience maintaining harbors and channels throughout the Great Lakes, including the Upper Peninsula ports. DDI's proximity to the Detroit District office ensures responsive project coordination and communication.
