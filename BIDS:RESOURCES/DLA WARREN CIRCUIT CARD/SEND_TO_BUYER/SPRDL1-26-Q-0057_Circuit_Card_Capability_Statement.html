Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan — located minutes from DLA Land and Maritime in Warren. We provide defense-related component supply, electronics procurement, and manufacturing coordination services to DoD agencies. Our established supply chain relationships and proximity to DLA Land Warren enable responsive, reliable support for critical defense electronics requirements.

Capability — Circuit Card Assemblies
  • Circuit card assembly (CCA) procurement and supply chain management
  • Coordination with IPC-certified electronics manufacturers
  • Technical data package (TDP) review and compliance verification
  • First article testing (FAT) coordination and documentation
  • MIL-STD and defense specification compliance
  • Component obsolescence management and alternative sourcing
  • Supply chain risk management (SCRM) and DFARS compliance
  • Counterfeit parts prevention (AS6171/AS6081 standards)
  • Quality management and inspection coordination
  • Packaging, marking, and shipping per MIL-STD-2073

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As an EDWOSB headquartered just miles from DLA Land Warren, DDI offers the ideal combination of socioeconomic contracting value and operational proximity. Our manufacturing partner network includes multiple IPC-certified circuit card assembly houses capable of meeting defense specifications, ensuring competitive pricing and reliable delivery for DLA requirements.
