Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide construction, facility maintenance, and specialty trade services to federal agencies throughout Michigan and the Great Lakes region. Our established network of licensed Michigan contractors enables us to deliver quality construction services with local expertise and rapid response.

Service Approach — Snow Guard Installation
  • Complete snow guard system design, supply, and installation
  • Site assessment and measurement for optimal snow guard placement
  • Metal roof snow retention system expertise (S-5!, SnoBlox, Alpine SnowGuards)
  • Compliance with building codes and manufacturer specifications
  • Licensed and insured Michigan contractors
  • Fall protection and OSHA-compliant work practices
  • Material procurement through authorized distributors
  • Post-installation inspection and warranty documentation
  • Project management and timeline coordination with facility operations

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As a Michigan-based EDWOSB, DDI provides the Coast Guard both socioeconomic value and local operational advantage for the Grand Haven SFO project. Our network of experienced roofing and construction professionals in West Michigan ensures quality installation with rapid mobilization, local material sourcing, and familiarity with Michigan weather conditions that drive snow guard requirements.
