🏢 Company Overview
Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is a Michigan-based, SBA-certified EDWOSB headquartered in Troy. We provide comprehensive drug and alcohol testing program administration aligned with the State of Michigan's employee screening requirements under Civil Service Regulation 2.07. Our services support DTMB's mission to "Help. Connect. Solve." by delivering reliable, statewide testing infrastructure that connects state agencies with SAMHSA-certified laboratory services, Medical Review Officers, and collection sites across all 83 Michigan counties.
