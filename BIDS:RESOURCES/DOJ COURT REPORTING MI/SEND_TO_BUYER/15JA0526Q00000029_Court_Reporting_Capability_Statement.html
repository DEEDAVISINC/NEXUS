Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide professional support services to federal agencies, including court reporting, legal support, and administrative services. Our network of certified court reporters throughout Michigan — including the Upper Peninsula — enables us to provide reliable coverage for federal court proceedings statewide.

Service Approach — Court Reporting
  • Certified court reporting for grand jury proceedings
  • Real-time stenographic reporting and transcription
  • Verbatim transcript production meeting federal standards
  • Expedited and daily transcript delivery as required
  • Secure handling of classified and sealed proceedings
  • Digital audio recording backup
  • Rough draft and final transcript delivery
  • Statewide coverage including Upper Peninsula (Marquette)
  • Compliance with USAO and federal court transcript format requirements
Key Qualifications
  • SBA-certified EDWOSB / WOSB — supports DOJ socioeconomic goals
  • Michigan-based with Upper Peninsula court reporter network
  • NCRA-certified reporters (RPR, RMR, RDR credentials)
  • Experience with federal grand jury confidentiality requirements
  • Secure transcript storage and handling protocols
  • Reliable scheduling and backup reporter availability
  • Understanding of Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure
  • Quality control: proofreading and accuracy verification

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As a Michigan-based EDWOSB, DDI provides the US Attorney's Office both socioeconomic value and local operational advantage for the Western District of Michigan. Our reporters understand the unique logistics of serving the Upper Peninsula and can provide consistent, reliable coverage for grand jury proceedings in Marquette with backup support to ensure no session goes uncovered.
