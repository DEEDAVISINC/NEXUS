## WHAT IS AN INDIRECT COST ALLOCATION PLAN (ICAP)?

**Definition:**
An Indirect Cost Allocation Plan is a specialized accounting document that distributes shared/overhead costs across different government programs, departments, or grants according to federal cost principles.

**Purpose:**
- Required for government agencies to claim indirect costs on federal grants
- Ensures compliance with 2 CFR 200 (Uniform Guidance)
- Allocates overhead costs fairly across programs
- Maximizes federal reimbursement for allowable costs

**Typical Services:**
- Cost accounting analysis
- Indirect cost rate calculation
- Cost allocation methodology development
- Federal compliance documentation
- Annual ICAP updates and submissions
- Grant cost recovery optimization
