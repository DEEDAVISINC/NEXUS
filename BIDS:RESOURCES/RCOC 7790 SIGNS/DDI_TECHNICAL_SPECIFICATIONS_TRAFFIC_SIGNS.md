# DEE DAVIS INC
## Technical Specifications - Traffic Signs
### RFQ #: DDI-2026-007

---

## A. General Requirements

All supplied traffic signs shall meet the requirements of the **Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT) 2020 Standard Specifications For Construction, Section 919**.

### Sign Panel Materials
All sign panels shall be either **Type III or Type V**, except for the following signs which shall be **0.063" aluminum sheets**:
- DEAD END – RIGHT SPECIAL 36"x9" FLUORESCENT YELLOW
- DEAD END – LEFT SPECIAL 36"x9" FLUORESCENT YELLOW
- NO OUTLET – RIGHT W14-2AR(MOD) 36"X9" FLUORESCENT YELLOW
- NO OUTLET – LEFT W14-2AR(MOD) 36"X9" FLUORESCENT YELLOW

### Sign Sheeting
Sign sheeting shall comply with **MDOT Section 919.02B and Table 919-3**. Sign reflective sheeting is for highway signs and other traffic control devices to assure optimum day and night visibility when exposed to a light source in dry and wet weather conditions.

**Bidders must specify:**
- Brand name and manufacturer of sheeting used
- Confirmation that materials are listed on **MDOT's Qualified Products List (QPL)**

---

## B. Fabrication

Signs shall be fabricated in accordance with:
- Current **MDOT Traffic Safety Standard Highway Signs Manual**
- **MDOT Reflective Sign Sheeting Guidelines**
- All sheeting materials must be listed on **MDOT QPL**

---

## C. Sign Face Requirements

### CRITICAL: Required Markings on ALL Signs

**ALL fabricated signs shall bear the following information in characters not to exceed one half inch in height:**

1. **The initials "RCOC" in uppercase letters**
2. **The 2-digit month/2-digit year of fabrication** (e.g., "02/26" for February 2026)
3. **Uppercase initials of the sheeting manufacturer** (e.g., "3M" for 3M Company)
4. **Uppercase initials (4-character max) representing the supplier fabricating the sign**

**Location:** This information string shall be on or near the sign border at the bottom, lower left portion of the sign face.

**Example marking:** `RCOC 02/26 3M ABCD`

### Additional Requirements
- No splices shall be in the sign face
- Design shall conform to detailed drawings in the current **Michigan Manual of Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MMUTCD)**
- Holes in aluminum as specified
- Sign faces must conform to all standards specified herein

---

## D. Packaging and Shipping

### Protection Requirements
Fabricated signs shall be packaged to ensure:
- Protection from damage in transit
- Readiness for use upon delivery
- Sheeting, decals, and signs shall NOT become wet in transit

### Packaging Standards
- **Packaged cartons:** Maximum weight 60 pounds
- **Plastic banded** with each sign face protected
- **Slip sheet paper (glossy side)** against each sign face
- **Pallet skids:** Protected and plastic banded with slip sheet protection
- Banding, stacking, or crating in accord with commercially accepted standards
- Adequate support and protection to prevent movement and chafing during transit

### Carton/Pallet Marking
Each carton or pallet skid shall be clearly marked with:
- Contents description
- Quantity
- Item stock code number (as shown on DEE DAVIS INC purchase order)
- Any special packaging or handling requirements

## E. Certification Requirements

The supplier shall certify:
- All materials furnished are **new or refurbished aluminum like new**
- All work will be **good quality, free from faults and defects**
- All work conforms to these technical specifications
- All materials and work conform to MDOT standards and MMUTCD requirements

**Samples may be requested to confirm acceptability.**

All work not conforming to these specifications, including substitutions not approved and authorized, may be considered defective.
