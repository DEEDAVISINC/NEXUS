# DOCUMENT SHREDDING SERVICES — Bid Strategy

**Category:** Service Brokering (EDWOSB Prime + Subcontractor)
**Business Model:** Win contract as EDWOSB prime, subcontract to licensed shredding company
**NAICS Code:** 561990 (All Other Support Services)

---

## WHY THIS IS A GREAT FIT

- **Every government agency needs shredding** — it's mandatory (HIPAA, FACTA, GLBA compliance)
- **Recurring contracts** — biweekly or monthly service, multi-year deals
- **Simple service to manage** — sub shows up, shreds documents, provides certificate of destruction
- **EDWOSB advantage** — VA and other federal agencies actively set aside shredding contracts for small business
- **Multiple proven subs in Metro Detroit** — no shortage of shredding companies to partner with
- **VA Detroit already had a shredding contract** — it will need renewal

## HOW IT WORKS

```
Agency ──→ Dee Davis Inc. (EDWOSB Prime) ──→ Shredding Company (Sub)
  ↑                    ↑                              ↑
Pays monthly       Takes 15-25%                  Brings the truck
Gets compliance    Manages contract              Shreds the paper
Gets diversity     Handles billing               Provides cert of destruction
```

## WHAT THE CONTRACT TYPICALLY REQUIRES

1. **Locked containers** — provided by contractor (your sub provides these)
2. **On-site shredding** — mobile truck comes to the location
3. **Certificate of destruction** — issued after each service
4. **Background checks** — for all employees entering government facility
5. **Insurance** — general liability + professional liability
6. **Compliance** — NIST 800-88 for media, HIPAA for healthcare
7. **Scheduling** — typically biweekly, some monthly

## PROFIT MODEL

**Example: VA Regional Office Contract (Annual)**
- Biweekly service, 5 bins
- Sub cost: $300/mo x 12 = $3,600
- Contract price: $500/mo x 12 = $6,000
- **Gross Profit: $2,400/year (40% margin)**

**Example: County Government + Courts (Annual)**
- Weekly service, 10 bins
- Sub cost: $600/mo x 12 = $7,200
- Contract price: $1,000/mo x 12 = $12,000
- **Gross Profit: $4,800/year (40% margin)**

**Example: One-Time Purge (Records Retention Cleanup)**
- 500 boxes at a state agency
- Sub cost: 500 x $4 = $2,000

- **Gross Profit: $2,000 (50% margin)**
