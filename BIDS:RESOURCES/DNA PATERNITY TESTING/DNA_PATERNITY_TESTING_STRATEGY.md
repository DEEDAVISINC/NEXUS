## THE OPPORTUNITY — IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Every state in the US has a child support enforcement program. When paternity is disputed, the state orders DNA testing. That testing is done by a contracted lab — and those contracts are procured through RFPs, usually every 3-5 years.

**DDC already holds contracts with dozens of states.** You're their affiliate partner. The play:

1. You bid as the EDWOSB prime contractor
2. DDC does the actual lab work (they already do it)
3. The state gets EDWOSB credit for the contract
4. You manage the relationship, invoicing, and compliance
5. Revenue flows through you

**This isn't speculative. DDC is the incumbent in most of these states. You're adding the EDWOSB wrapper.**

```

         ↓
Dee Davis Inc. bids as EDWOSB prime (NAICS 621511)
```

## PRICING INTELLIGENCE

### Typical State Pricing
- **Per-test pricing:** $25-$80 per individual tested
- **Trio testing** (mother, child, alleged father): $75-$240 per case
- **Average state contract value:** $200K-$2M annually (varies by state population)

### Your Margin
- Negotiate a DDC sub-rate (they do this at scale already)
- Bid competitively to the state (slightly under LabCorp's pricing)
- Your margin = state contract rate minus DDC sub-rate
- Even 10-15% margin on a $500K/year contract = $50-75K/year passive income
- **Stack 5-10 states = $250K-$750K/year**
