**Category:** Service Brokering + Freight Brokerage (EDWOSB Prime)
**Business Model:** Win moving contracts as EDWOSB prime, sub-broker execution nationwide
**NAICS Codes:** 484210 (Used Household and Office Goods Moving), 488510 (Freight Transportation Arrangement)
**GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE: NATIONWIDE — Not limited to Michigan**
**NOTE:** Broker duties will be subbed out. See also `FREIGHT TRANSPORTATION/FREIGHT_TRANSPORTATION_STRATEGY.md`
**COMPETITIVE EDGE: FREIGHT BROKER + TRUCKINGPLANETNETWORK.COM**

---

## WHY THIS FITS

- **Freight broker = moving broker** — same logistics skill, different cargo
- **TruckingPlanetNetwork access** — find box trucks, movers, carriers instantly
- **Government offices relocate constantly** — lease changes, renovations, consolidations
- **Big contract values** — Michigan state moving contract was $1M estimated
- **EDWOSB differentiator** — moving companies are almost never EDWOSB certified

## THE OPPORTUNITY

Government moving is NOT like residential moving. It's:
- Office furniture and equipment relocation
- Cubicle/workstation disassembly and reassembly
- IT equipment and server moves
- Records/file box relocation
- Between government buildings (not homes)

You don't need to own trucks or employ movers. You need to:
1. Win the contract (EDWOSB prime)
2. Dispatch carriers from your network
3. Hire local moving labor (day labor or moving company sub)
4. Coordinate and manage the project
