### 1. PURPOSE

The Parties are entering into discussions regarding potential teaming, subcontracting, or joint venture arrangements for the purpose of pursuing government contract opportunities (the "Purpose"). During these discussions, the Parties may share confidential and proprietary information. This Agreement governs the protection of such information.

---

### 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

(a) **Business Information:** Pricing strategies, cost structures, profit margins, supplier relationships, supplier pricing, client lists, and business plans

(b) **Proposal Information:** Bid strategies, technical approaches, past performance data, pricing volumes, labor rates, teaming arrangements, and proposal content

(c) **Technical Information:** Proprietary processes, methodologies, software, systems, trade secrets, and know-how

(d) **Government Contract Information:** Solicitation strategies, evaluation intelligence, relationship information with government personnel, and competitive intelligence

(e) **Financial Information:** Revenue data, financial statements, contract values, and pricing models

---

### 3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party

(b) Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records

(c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information

(d) Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure

(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice to allow time to seek protective measures

---

### 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

Each Party, when receiving Confidential Information, agrees to:

(a) **Protect** the information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care

(b) **Limit access** to only those employees, agents, or subcontractors who have a need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement

(c) **Not disclose** the information to any third party without prior written consent of the Disclosing Party

(d) **Not use** the information for any purpose other than the Purpose described in Section 1

(e) **Not use** the information to compete against the Disclosing Party, solicit the Disclosing Party's clients or suppliers, or gain any unfair business advantage

---

### 5. SPECIFIC GOVCON PROTECTIONS

In addition to the general obligations above, the Parties specifically agree:

(a) **No End-Run:** Neither Party shall use information learned through this relationship to independently pursue, bid on, or otherwise compete for any specific government opportunity that was identified or discussed as a potential teaming opportunity under this Agreement

(b) **No Supplier Poaching:** Neither Party shall contact, solicit, or engage the other Party's suppliers, subcontractors, or teaming partners that were disclosed as Confidential Information for the purpose of bypassing the Disclosing Party

(c) **No Client Disclosure to Suppliers:** Neither Party shall reveal the identity of end-use government clients or agencies to suppliers, vendors, or other third parties unless specifically authorized in writing

(d) **Proposal Integrity:** Any proposal content, pricing, or technical approaches shared for joint bid preparation shall not be reused, repurposed, or referenced in any other proposal without written consent

(e) **Past Performance Protection:** Past performance information shared for proposal purposes shall not be disclosed to any third party or used for any purpose other than the specific proposal being prepared

---

### 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

(a) **Term:** This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for two (2) years, unless terminated earlier by either Party with thirty (30) days written notice

(b) **Survival:** The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement shall survive termination for a period of three (3) years following termination or expiration

(c) **Return of Information:** Upon termination or upon request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and any copies, notes, or materials derived from it, and shall certify in writing that it has done so

---

### 7. REMEDIES

(a) The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate

(b) In the event of a breach or threatened breach, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity

(c) The prevailing party in any action to enforce this Agreement shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs

---

### 8. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

(a) This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any teaming agreement, subcontract, joint venture, or other business arrangement

(b) This Agreement does not grant either Party any license or rights to the other Party's intellectual property

(c) Either Party may terminate teaming discussions at any time for any reason without liability (subject to the continuing confidentiality obligations)

---

### 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

(a) **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Michigan

(b) **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior discussions, representations, or agreements on this subject

(c) **Amendments:** This Agreement may only be modified by written agreement signed by both Parties

(d) **Assignment:** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party

(e) **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect

(f) **Counterparts:** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original

(g) **Electronic Signatures:** Electronic signatures shall be deemed original signatures for all purposes

## HOW TO USE THIS NDA

### When to Send:
- **Before** sharing pricing strategies, supplier names, or bid approaches with a potential teaming partner
- **Before** discussing specific opportunities in detail with a potential subcontractor
- **Before** sharing past performance data or proprietary proposal content
- **Before** any joint bid preparation work begins

### When You DON'T Need It:
- General capability discussions ("What NAICS codes do you work in?")
- Public information exchanges (SAM.gov profiles, capability statements)
- Initial introductory calls where no proprietary information is shared

### How to Send:
1. Fill in Party B's information
2. Email as PDF with a brief note: "Before we get into specifics on this opportunity, I'd like us both to sign a standard NDA. This protects both of us during our discussions."
3. Use DocuSign, Adobe Sign, or Rocket Lawyer for e-signatures
4. Store signed copy in the relevant bid folder AND in `COMPANY_DOCUMENTS/NDAs/`

### Track in NEXUS:
- Add to `GPSS SUBCONTRACTOR COMPLIANCE` table
- Document Type: "NDA"
- Status: Sent / Signed / Expired
- Expiration Date: 2 years from effective date

**IMPORTANT:** This template is a starting point. Have your attorney review it before first use. Government contracting has specific nuances around data rights (DFARS 252.227-7013/7014) and CUI handling that may need to be added depending on the nature of the work.
