## SUPPLIES — DETAILED

### VA Medical/Surgical Supplies
The VA buys medical supplies through two main channels:
1. **Medical/Surgical Prime Vendor (MSPV)** — large distribution contracts (Medline, Cardinal Health run these)
2. **Electronic Catalog (ECAT)** — get your products listed and VA hospitals order directly
3. **Open Market** — individual VA medical centers post solicitations for specific needs

**Michigan VA Medical Centers (filter VA Vendor Portal by WOSB):**
- Ann Arbor VAHCS
- Battle Creek VAMC
- Detroit VAMC
- Iron Mountain VAMC
- Saginaw VAMC

**How to search:** vendorportal.ecms.va.gov → State FCO Query → Michigan → Filter "Women Owned Small Business" → Classification Code 65 (Medical, Dental & Veterinary Equipment & Supplies)

### DLA Troop Support Medical
DLA Troop Support procures medical commodities including:
- Pharmaceuticals, surgical items, dental items
- Lab consumables, critical care supplies
- Safety supplies, surgical dressings

**How to find opportunities:** Search SAM.gov using DoDAAC SPE2D (DLA Troop Support Medical). Also check DLA DIBBS (Internet Bid Board System).

### General Supplies (Office, Safety, Industrial)
- **DLA** — DLA buys everything. Search by commodity on DIBBS.
- **GSA Schedule** — Best long-term play. Get on a GSA MAS schedule and agencies order from you directly.
- **VA** — Classification Code 75 (Office Supplies), 79 (Cleaning Equipment), 65 (Medical Supplies)

## NEMT (NON-EMERGENCY MEDICAL TRANSPORTATION) — DETAILED

### How NEMT Works
Every state Medicaid program must provide transportation to medical appointments. Most states hire a **broker** who manages a network of transportation providers (sedans, wheelchair vans, stretchers). The broker gets the calls, dispatches drivers, handles billing.

### The Two Plays

**Play 1: Become the NEMT Broker (Big Play)**
- States issue RFPs every 3-7 years for NEMT brokerage contracts
- These are massive — Michigan's tri-county contract is worth $128M+
- Current players: ModivCare (formerly LogistiCare), MTM, Access2Care
- Your freight broker infrastructure (TruckingPlanetNetwork, dispatching, etc.) translates directly to NEMT brokerage

**Play 2: Become a Network Provider (Faster Play)**
- The existing broker (ModivCare in Michigan) needs transportation providers in their network
- You sign up as a provider, they dispatch rides to you, you sub-broker to local drivers
- Lower barrier to entry, cash flow starts faster
