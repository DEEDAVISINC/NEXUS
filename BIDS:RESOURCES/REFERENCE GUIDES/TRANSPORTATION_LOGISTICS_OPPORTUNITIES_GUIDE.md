**Types of USPS Contracts:**

**Mailing Supplies:**

- Shipping labels
- Forms and documentation

**Mailroom Equipment Supplies:**
- Postal scales (replacement parts, supplies)
- Label printers and supplies
- Mail carts and hampers
- Sorting equipment supplies
- Processing equipment supplies

**Facility Supplies:**
- Office supplies
- Janitorial supplies
- Cleaning supplies
- Paper products
- Safety equipment
- Maintenance supplies

**Postal Operations:**
- Uniforms and PPE
- Delivery vehicle supplies
- Route carrier supplies
- Mailbox supplies
- PO Box hardware and supplies

**How to Find USPS Opportunities:**

1. **SAM.gov Primary Method:**
   - Search: "postal" OR "mail processing" OR "USPS"
