## 🎯 WHAT IS NEMT?

**NEMT = Non-Emergency Medical Transportation**

Transportation services for patients to/from:
- Doctor appointments
- Dialysis treatments
- Chemotherapy sessions
- Physical therapy
- Pharmacy visits
- Hospital discharges
- Mental health appointments
- Dental visits
- Vision appointments

**Who Pays:**
- Medicaid (state programs)
- Medicare Advantage plans
- Managed Care Organizations (MCOs)
- Hospitals and healthcare systems
- Veterans Affairs (VA)
- Insurance companies
