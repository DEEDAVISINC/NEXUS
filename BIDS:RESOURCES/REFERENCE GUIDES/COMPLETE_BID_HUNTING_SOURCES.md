## 🚨 RED FLAGS (Skip These - Save Time!)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Services (you sell products, not services)
- ❌ Equipment installation (too technical)
- ❌ Software/IT (not your market)
- ❌ Construction services (you supply, don't build)
- ❌ Professional services (consulting, engineering)
- ❌ Due in <7 days (too rushed, quality suffers)
- ❌ 100+ page RFPs (too complex, low ROI)
- ❌ Highly specialized technical (unless you have expertise)
- ❌ Sole source/incumbent only (waste of time)
- ❌ Pre-qualified vendors only (if you're not on list)
