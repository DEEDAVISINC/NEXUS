 # State Foreign Registration Cost Tracker
 
 **Purpose:** Quick cost and compliance guide for Dee Davis Inc. when deciding whether to foreign-qualify in bid states.
 
 **Last updated:** March 9, 2026  
 **Legal structure assumed:** Michigan for-profit corporation (Dee Davis Inc.)
 
 ---
 
 ## Important Reality Check
 
 - There is generally **no reciprocity** for foreign qualification.  
 - Being in good standing in Michigan does **not** waive foreign registration in other states.  
 - Some states allow limited exemptions for isolated transactions, but procurement contracts are usually treated as doing business in-state.

 ## State-by-State Notes
 
 ### Illinois
 - IPG registration can be returned unless you are authorized in Illinois.
 - Additional procurement onboarding may be required (e.g., BOE registration).
 - Budget extra admin time for compliance forms and ownership disclosures.
 
 ### Kentucky
 - Low-cost entry state.
 - Annual report due each year; failure can revoke authority.
 
 ### Georgia
 - Moderate setup cost.
 - Annual registration must be kept current for bid eligibility and good standing.
 
 ### California
 - High-carry state due to minimum franchise tax.
 - Only register when award probability is high or award requires qualification.
 
 ### Texas
 - High initial filing fee for foreign for-profit corporations.
 - Annual franchise filing obligations apply even when tax due is $0 under threshold rules.
 
 ### Ohio
 - Low setup cost relative to other states.
 - Good candidate for early registration if pipeline volume increases.
 
 ### Pennsylvania
 - Moderate setup cost.
 - Low annual report fee; manageable long-term carry cost.
 
 ---
 
 ## Registration Trigger Rule (Cost-Control Strategy)
 
 Register only when one of these triggers is true:
 
 1. **Award trigger:** The solicitation or contract requires in-state authorization before award.
 2. **Probability trigger:** Win probability >= 35% and projected annual margin >= $50K.
 3. **Portfolio trigger:** Repeated opportunities in the same state (3+ active bids in 6 months).
 4. **Vehicle trigger:** Statewide or cooperative vehicle that can generate recurring task orders.
