### ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 **Purpose.** The Parties agree to team together for the sole purpose of pursuing, proposing on, and performing (if awarded) the government contract opportunity described above (the "Opportunity").

1.2 **Prime Contractor.** Party A (Dee Davis Inc.) shall serve as the Prime Contractor and will:
- Submit the proposal to the government
- Hold the prime contract if awarded
- Serve as the primary point of contact with the Contracting Officer
- Manage overall contract performance and compliance
- Handle invoicing to the government and payment to Party B

1.3 **Team Member.** Party B shall serve as a subcontractor and will:
- Provide the capabilities, personnel, and resources described in Article 2
- Support proposal preparation as described in Article 3
- Perform the work described in the workshare allocation
- Report to Party A on performance and deliverables

---

### ARTICLE 3: PROPOSAL PREPARATION

3.1 **Proposal Lead.** Party A shall lead the proposal effort, including:
- Overall proposal strategy and approach
- Proposal coordination and schedule
- Final review and submission to the government
- All communications with the Contracting Officer regarding the proposal

3.2 **Party B's Proposal Responsibilities.** Party B shall:
- Provide technical content for their workshare areas within agreed timelines
- Provide past performance references relevant to the Opportunity
- Provide resumes/qualifications of key personnel proposed for this work
- Provide pricing/cost data for their portion of the work
- Review and approve proposal content describing their work before submission
- Provide a Letter of Intent or commitment letter if required by the solicitation

3.3 **Proposal Costs.** Each Party shall bear its own costs for proposal preparation. Neither Party shall be entitled to reimbursement from the other for proposal preparation costs, regardless of whether a proposal is submitted or an award is made.

3.4 **Timeline.** Party B shall provide all requested proposal materials within [___] business days of Party A's request, unless a different timeline is mutually agreed.

---

### ARTICLE 4: PRICING

4.1 **Pricing Approach.** Party B shall provide firm pricing for their workshare to Party A within [___] business days of receiving the final solicitation.

4.2 **Pricing Format.** Party B's pricing shall include:
- Fully-burdened labor rates by labor category (if applicable)
- Material/equipment costs (if applicable)
- Travel costs (if applicable)
- Other direct costs
- Any applicable escalation rates for option years

4.3 **Pricing Confidentiality.** Each Party's pricing information is confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party, including other team members, without prior written consent.

4.4 **Pricing Adjustments.** Pricing may be adjusted only by mutual written agreement based on:
- Changes to the government's requirements
- Changes to the workshare allocation
- Material changes in market conditions affecting cost

---

### ARTICLE 5: SUBCONTRACT TERMS (If Awarded)

5.1 **Subcontract.** If Party A is awarded the prime contract, the Parties shall negotiate and execute a subcontract consistent with this Teaming Agreement within [30] days of prime contract award.

5.2 **Flow-Down Clauses.** The subcontract shall include all applicable flow-down provisions required by the prime contract, including:
- FAR and DFARS clauses
- Agency-specific clauses
- Reporting requirements
- Quality standards
- Security requirements (if applicable)

5.3 **Payment Terms.** Party A shall pay Party B within [___] days of receiving payment from the government for Party B's work, or Net [30/45] from receipt of a proper invoice, whichever is sooner.

5.4 **Key Personnel.** If Party B has proposed specific key personnel in the proposal, those individuals shall be assigned to the contract. Substitution of key personnel requires Party A's prior written approval.

---

### ARTICLE 6: EXCLUSIVITY

6.1 **Exclusive Teaming.** During the term of this Agreement, Party B agrees not to:
- Submit a proposal for the Opportunity as a prime contractor
- Join another team pursuing the Opportunity as a subcontractor
- Assist any other offeror in pursuing the Opportunity

6.2 **Scope.** This exclusivity applies only to the specific Opportunity identified in this Agreement. It does not restrict either Party from pursuing other opportunities independently or with other partners.

---

### ARTICLE 7: CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 **Incorporation.** If the Parties have executed a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), its terms are incorporated herein by reference.

7.2 **If No Separate NDA.** If no separate NDA exists, the Parties agree that all information exchanged in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to pricing, technical approaches, past performance data, and business strategies, shall be treated as confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent.

7.3 **Specific Protections.** Regardless of any other provision:
- Neither Party shall reveal the identity of government end-clients to suppliers or vendors
- Neither Party shall use information gained through this teaming to independently pursue the Opportunity
- Neither Party shall solicit the other Party's employees, subcontractors, or suppliers

---

### ARTICLE 8: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Pre-Existing IP.** Each Party retains all rights to its pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses pre-existing IP to the other Party.

8.2 **Proposal Content.** Proposal content created jointly shall be jointly owned. Proposal content created solely by one Party shall be owned by that Party.

8.3 **Contract Deliverables.** IP rights in contract deliverables shall be determined by the prime contract terms (which typically grant the government unlimited rights in data developed under the contract) and the subcontract.

---

### ARTICLE 9: TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 **Term.** This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and shall remain in effect until:
- The government awards the contract and the Parties execute a subcontract; or
- The government cancels the solicitation; or
- The Parties mutually agree to terminate; or
- [18] months from the Effective Date, whichever occurs first

9.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:
- Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within [15] days of written notice
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy
- Violates the exclusivity provisions of Article 6
- Breaches confidentiality obligations

9.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
- Confidentiality obligations survive for [3] years
- Each Party returns or destroys the other's confidential information
- Neither Party is liable for proposal preparation costs incurred by the other
- Neither Party may use the other's proprietary proposal content

---

### ARTICLE 10: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Each Party represents and warrants that:

10.1 It has the authority to enter into this Agreement

10.2 It is not currently debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment from government contracting

10.3 Its SAM.gov registration is active (or will be active before proposal submission)

10.4 It has no organizational conflicts of interest related to the Opportunity

10.5 The information provided regarding its certifications, capabilities, and experience is accurate

10.6 It will comply with all applicable laws and regulations in performing under this Agreement

11.2 **Mediation.** If negotiation fails within [30] days, the Parties agree to attempt mediation before pursuing litigation.

12.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent.

12.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

## HOW TO USE THIS TEAMING AGREEMENT

### When to Use:
- **After** you've had initial conversations and both parties want to pursue an opportunity together
- **After** the NDA is signed (if sharing confidential info)
- **Before** you start writing the proposal together
- **Before** any significant work is done on bid prep

### Typical Flow:
```
1. Initial outreach (email template 5A or 5B)
2. Introductory call — is there mutual fit?
3. Sign NDA (GOVCON_NDA_TEMPLATE.md)
4. Discuss specific opportunity and workshare
5. Sign Teaming Agreement (THIS DOCUMENT)
6. Begin proposal preparation
7. Submit proposal
8. If awarded → Negotiate formal subcontract
```

### Customization Needed:
- Fill in all blanks (Party B info, workshare percentages, pricing timelines)
- Adjust workshare tasks to match the specific opportunity
- Add any opportunity-specific terms
- Review with attorney before first use

### Where to Store:
- Signed copy in the relevant bid folder: `BIDS:RESOURCES/[CLIENT NAME]/`
- Master copy in: `COMPANY_DOCUMENTS/TEAMING_AGREEMENTS/`
- Track in NEXUS: `GPSS SUBCONTRACTOR COMPLIANCE` table (Document Type: "Teaming Agreement")

---

**IMPORTANT:** This is a starting template designed for government contracting teaming relationships. Have your attorney review before first use. Specific opportunities may require additional provisions (security clearances, CUI handling, ITAR compliance, etc.).
