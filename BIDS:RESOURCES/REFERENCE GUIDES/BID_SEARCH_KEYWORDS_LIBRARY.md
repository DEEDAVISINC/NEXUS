## 💡 PRO SEARCH TIPS

### **Broaden Results:**
- Remove location filters
- Use fewer keywords
- Use "OR" between terms: "salt OR deicing"
- Use broader categories: "supplies" instead of "office supplies"

### **Narrow Results:**
- Add location: Michigan, Texas, your county
- Add set-aside: WOSB, EDWOSB, Small Business
- Add price range: $25000 to $100000
- Add date range: Posted last 30 days

### **Find Hidden Gems:**
- Search misspellings: "janitoral" "maintainence"
- Search abbreviations: "PPE" "MRO" "HVAC"
- Search brand names: "Grainger" "Rubbermaid"
- Search product codes: "NAICS 423840"

### **Avoid Junk:**
- Exclude: -services -consulting -professional
- Exclude: -software -IT -technology
- Exclude: -construction (the service, not materials)
- Exclude: -installation -maintenance (the service)
