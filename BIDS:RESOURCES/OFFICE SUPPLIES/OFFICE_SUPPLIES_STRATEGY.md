# OFFICE SUPPLIES — Bid Strategy

**Category:** Product Resale (Core Competency)
**Business Model:** Buy wholesale office supplies, sell to government agencies at contract prices
**NAICS Codes:** 424120 (Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers), 453210 (Office Supplies, Stationery Stores)

---

## WHY THIS FITS

- **Pure product resale** — your bread and butter
- **Massive recurring market** — every government office buys supplies monthly
- **EDWOSB advantage** — set-asides exist for office supply contracts
- **Easy to source** — wholesale accounts with major distributors
- **High volume = high total profit** — even at lower margins, volume makes it work
- **Gateway contract** — once you're their office supply vendor, upsell everything else

## PROFIT MODEL

**Example: County Office Supply Contract (Annual)**
- 50 employees ordering monthly supplies
- Wholesale cost: $2,000/month = $24,000/year
- Contract price: $3,200/month = $38,400/year
- **Gross Profit: $14,400/year (38% margin)**

**Example: School District (Annual)**
- 10 schools ordering quarterly
- Wholesale cost: $5,000/quarter = $20,000/year
- Contract price: $7,500/quarter = $30,000/year
- **Gross Profit: $10,000/year (33% margin)**

## THE GATEWAY STRATEGY

Office supplies gets your foot in the door. Once you're the vendor:
- Upsell **breakroom supplies** (coffee, cups, napkins)
- Upsell **janitorial supplies** (cleaning chemicals, paper products)
- Upsell **printer toner** (highest margin item)
- Upsell **technology supplies** (cables, drives, peripherals)
- Cross-sell **drug testing supplies** (if they need them)
- Cross-sell **safety supplies** (gloves, PPE, first aid)

**One vendor, multiple categories = less procurement work for the agency = they love you.**
