## DDI STRATEGY

**⚠️ SOW check:** The contract requires **Chicago court-site collectors**, **statewide kit/supply logistics**, **secure portal + daily reporting**, **training**, and **no-cost testimony** — not lab-only. Confirm DDC + any collection/IT partners can cover **every** line in **SOW HOT SPOTS** before final GO.

**DDI primes as contract management TPA. DDC (DNA Diagnostics Center) subs as AABB-accredited lab.**

This is the same prime/sub model DDI uses for drug testing — DDI manages the contract, coordinates collections, handles compliance and reporting. DDC performs the actual DNA testing as a qualified subcontractor. **Additional subs or DDC-stretched services may be required** for Chicago staffing, portal, and performance SLAs — map in compliance matrix.

**Why DDI wins this:**
- WBE/MBE = Illinois BEP (Business Enterprise Program) eligible — this is their #1 selection criteria
- Contract management prime model = DDI's exact lane
- Existing DDC distributor agreement = ready-made sub partnership
- Title IV-D child support experience (Kentucky OAG bid in progress)
- DDI brings diversity certification + DDC brings AABB accreditation = complete team

**Why this works for DDC:**
- DDC gets a statewide IL contract without competing for it themselves
- DDI handles all government compliance, reporting, billing
- DDC just processes specimens — their core work
