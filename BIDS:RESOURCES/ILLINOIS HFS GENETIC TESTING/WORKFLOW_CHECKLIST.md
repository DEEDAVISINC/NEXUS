## DDI STRATEGY

**DDI primes as contract management TPA. DDC (DNA Diagnostics Center) subs as AABB-accredited lab.**

This is the same prime/sub model DDI uses for drug testing — DDI manages the contract, coordinates collections, handles compliance and reporting. DDC performs the actual DNA testing as a qualified subcontractor.

**Why DDI wins this:**
- WBE/MBE = Illinois BEP (Business Enterprise Program) eligible — this is their #1 selection criteria
- Contract management prime model = DDI's exact lane
- Existing DDC distributor agreement = ready-made sub partnership
- Title IV-D child support experience (Kentucky OAG bid in progress)
- DDI brings diversity certification + DDC brings AABB accreditation = complete team

**Why this works for DDC:**
- DDC gets a statewide IL contract without competing for it themselves
- DDI handles all government compliance, reporting, billing
- DDC just processes specimens — their core work

## SCOPE OF WORK

- DNA paternity testing for child support cases under Title IV-D
- Specimen collection coordination (buccal swabs — collection sites across IL)
- Testing in compliance with AABB Standards for Relationship Testing Laboratories
- Maintaining confidentiality of test results
- Expert testimony if required in court proceedings
- Compliance with all applicable IL state regulations
- BEP/diversity participation (utilization plan required)
