## 🚨 CRITICAL LEARNINGS FROM 7799

### **Brand-Specific Items:**

**When you see "NO SUBSTITUTIONS":**
1. Document exact brand/part number required
2. Source directly if possible
3. May have higher costs (premium brands)
4. Lower markup acceptable (25-28% vs 33-35%)
5. Cannot bid if brand unavailable

**This bid taught us:**
- Aeroquip/Eaton hydraulic couplers = specialized, expensive
- LOCKNLUBE = direct order from manufacturer
- Brand restrictions MUST be met or mark "No Bid"

### **Product Categories:**

**Hydraulic Fittings:**
- Premium pricing (Aeroquip female = $42.60!)
- Specialized suppliers
- Industrial/heavy equipment applications

**Pneumatic Fittings:**
- More commodity pricing
- Widely available (Milton, etc.)
- Various "styles" (A, G, T, H) = NOT interchangeable!

**Lubrication Equipment:**
- Specialty brands (LOCKNLUBE)
- Direct-to-consumer often available
- Premium features = higher pricing
