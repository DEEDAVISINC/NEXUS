## 💰 PRICING IMPACT

**What this means for quotes:**
- Need to quote for 9,000 packs (not cases of packs)
- Each pack contains 250 napkins
- Suppliers must provide pricing per pack
- Verify if suppliers sell by pack or by case (and how many packs per case)

**Example:**
- If supplier sells in cases of 20 packs each
- Would need 450 cases (9,000 packs ÷ 20 packs/case)
