### How to Contact (PROTECT THE BUYER)
**CRITICAL: Never reveal VA Illiana, the solicitation number, or that this is a VA contract.**

**Script for initial outreach:**
> "Hi, calling from Dee Davis Inc. We're a freight brokerage looking at a potential government medical courier contract in central Illinois. We'd need coverage across about 9 locations between Danville and Springfield. Before I go further — do you handle medical courier subcontracting? Specifically daily routes, specimens, DME transport?"

**What to ask:**
1. Do you cover the Danville-to-Springfield corridor? All major cities in between?
2. How many years of medical courier experience?
3. HIPAA-compliant? Bloodborne pathogen trained?
4. Can you handle DME (durable medical equipment) transport?
5. Do you have temperature-controlled vehicles?
6. What's your general rate structure? (Per route? Per mile? Per stop?)
7. Are you open to working as a subcontractor under a prime contract?
8. Insurance — what coverage do you carry? (Auto, cargo, general liability amounts)
9. Any government/federal facility experience?
10. Any TWIC-certified drivers?

- ❌ VA Illiana
- ❌ Department of Veterans Affairs
- ❌ Solicitation 36C25226Q0235
