## APPROACH TO VA MEDICAL COURIER SERVICES

### **Service Delivery Model**

Dee Davis Inc. will leverage our established freight brokerage infrastructure to provide comprehensive medical courier services for VA Illiana Health Care System's 9-location network across central Illinois.

**1. Carrier Contracting & Management**

We will identify and contract with qualified medical courier carriers operating in the Illinois service area. Our carrier selection process includes:

- Verification of minimum 3 years medical courier experience
- Confirmation of appropriate licensing and insurance coverage
- Assessment of vehicle fleet capacity, including specialized vehicles for DME transport
- Review of safety records and compliance history
- Reference checks from current and previous clients
- Ongoing performance monitoring and quality assurance

**2. Route Coordination & Network Management**

Our logistics team will coordinate comprehensive courier services connecting all 9 VA facilities:

**Primary Service Locations:**
- Danville Illiana VA Medical Center (Main Campus) - Hub facility
- Peoria CBOC, Bloomington CBOC, Springfield CBOC
- Mattoon CBOC, Decatur CBOC, Champaign Primary Care Clinic
- Peoria Vet Center, Springfield Vet Center

**Service Coordination:**
- Daily scheduled routes between main campus and CBOCs
- As-needed pickups for time-sensitive or specialized deliveries
- Dedicated vehicle availability for large DME equipment requiring specialized transport
- Backup carrier availability to ensure service continuity
- Route optimization for efficiency while maintaining required service levels

**3. Cargo Handling & Transportation**

We will coordinate transport of all required materials:
- Sterile medical supplies (temperature-controlled transport when needed)
- Non-sterile medical supplies and office supplies
- Medical equipment (portable and stationary)
- Durable Medical Equipment (DME) requiring specialized vehicles
- Documentation and tracking for all shipments

**4. Communication & Reporting**

- Single point of contact (Dee Davis Inc.) for VA coordination
- Real-time tracking and status updates
- Scheduled reporting on deliveries, exceptions, and performance metrics
- 24/7 coordination capability for urgent needs
- Issue resolution and contingency management

**5. Quality Assurance & Compliance**

- Regular carrier performance evaluation
- Compliance monitoring for all regulatory requirements
- Service level adherence tracking
- Continuous improvement processes
- Incident reporting and corrective action procedures
