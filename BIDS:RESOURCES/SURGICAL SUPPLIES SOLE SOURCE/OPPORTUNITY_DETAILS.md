## 🎯 WHAT "NOTICE OF INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE" MEANS

**Critical Understanding:**

"Notice of Intent to Sole Source" means:
- Agency WANTS to award to a specific vendor without competition
- **BUT they're required to post publicly first**
- Other companies can object/respond
- **EDWOSB has standing to challenge sole-source awards!**

**Your Opportunity:**
1. Agency posts intent to sole-source
2. **You respond as qualified EDWOSB alternative**
3. Agency may:
   - Award to you instead (EDWOSB preference)
