## 3. HOW DDI WILL DELIVER SCOPE OF WORK (RFP SECTION 3)

### 3.1 Scope of Work

**3.1.1 General Requirements**

**Requirement 1:** Pick up specimen samples from local public health jurisdictions, clinics, hospitals, and other state agencies located throughout the 88 counties in Ohio up to five (5) days a week Sunday through Thursday and deliver up to five (5) days a week to BPHL, Monday through Friday, at a time mutually agreed upon.

**DDI Approach:**  
DDI will coordinate with ODH BPHL to establish optimized pickup routes covering all 88 Ohio counties. Our subcontractor will operate:
- **Pickup days:** Sunday - Thursday (up to 5 days/week as needed)
- **Delivery days:** Monday - Friday (up to 5 days/week)
- **Delivery window:** 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM (mutually agreed time with BPHL)
- **Route optimization:** Grouping pickups by region to ensure same-day or next-day delivery
- **Flexible scheduling:** Adjusting routes based on specimen volume and urgency

DDI will serve as the single point of contact for BPHL to request pickups, adjust schedules, or add new pickup locations.

**Requirement 2:** Pack and ship all specimen samples in accordance with DOT 49 CFR Parts 171-180 and IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations.

**DDI Approach:**  
Our subcontractor's drivers are trained in DOT and IATA hazardous materials regulations for biological specimens. All specimens will be:
- Verified for proper packaging at pickup (double-bagged, sealed, labeled)
- Transported in compliance with DOT 49 CFR 171-180 (Hazardous Materials Regulations)
- Handled per IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (Category B biological substances)
- Documented with shipping labels, biohazard markings, and chain of custody forms

If a specimen is improperly packaged at pickup, the driver will notify DDI immediately, and DDI will contact the submitting facility for correction before transport.

**Requirement 3:** Protect specimen samples from heat in summer months.

**DDI Approach:**  
Summer heat protection protocols include:
- **Pre-cooled vehicles:** All vehicles pre-cooled to 2-8°C before pickup
- **Insulated containers:** Specimens placed in insulated coolers with frozen ice packs
- **Avoid direct sunlight:** Specimens never left in direct sunlight or hot vehicles
- **Expedited delivery:** Priority routing during heat waves (>90°F)
- **Temperature monitoring:** Continuous data logging to detect any heat exposure

**Requirement 4:** Ensure that specimen samples are not exposed to weather conditions exceeding 90 degrees Fahrenheit.

**DDI Approach:**  
Temperature compliance is guaranteed through:
- **Vehicle climate control:** Refrigerated compartments maintain <90°F even in extreme heat
- **Real-time alerts:** Temperature monitoring systems alert driver and DDI if temperature approaches 90°F
- **Immediate action:** If temperature exceeds 90°F, specimens are transferred to backup cooling, and ODH is notified immediately

**Requirement 5:** Deliver each specimen sample in the same condition and temperature as it was when picked up, with no variances during transit.

**DDI Approach:**  
Chain of custody and temperature integrity are maintained through:
- **Pickup verification:** Driver documents specimen condition and temperature at pickup (photo + signature)
- **Sealed transport:** Specimens remain in locked, temperature-controlled compartments (no access during transit)
- **Delivery verification:** BPHL staff verifies specimen condition and temperature at delivery (photo + signature)
- **No variances:** If any variance is detected (damage, temperature excursion), DDI notifies ODH immediately and documents the incident

**Requirement 6:** Specimen samples must be kept dry.

**DDI Approach:**  
All specimens are transported in:
- **Waterproof containers:** Sealed plastic bags and insulated coolers
- **Dry compartments:** Vehicle compartments are climate-controlled and dry (no moisture exposure)
- **Leak protection:** If ice packs are used, they are sealed separately to prevent condensation contact with specimens

**Requirement 7:** Specimen samples cannot be put through conveyor systems which could result in damage and/or loss.

**DDI Approach:**  
All specimens are **hand-delivered directly from pickup location to BPHL**. No conveyor systems, no sorting facilities, no third-party handling. Driver maintains custody from pickup to delivery.

**Requirement 8:** Provide on-time deliveries. Notification of not making the delivery on time is to be communicated prior to the delivery time.

**DDI Approach:**  
On-time delivery is guaranteed through:
- **Route optimization:** Efficient routing to meet delivery windows
- **Real-time tracking:** GPS monitoring allows early detection of delays
- **Proactive communication:** If delay is anticipated (traffic, weather, vehicle issue), DDI notifies BPHL immediately (minimum 1 hour advance notice)
- **Performance target:** 99%+ on-time delivery rate

**Requirement 9:** Deliver specimen samples to the BPHL, Monday-Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

**DDI Approach:**  
All deliveries will occur within the 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM window, Monday - Friday. DDI will coordinate with BPHL to establish a preferred delivery time (e.g., 9:00 AM daily) to ensure consistency and predictability for BPHL staff.

**Requirement 10:** Deliver specimen samples and supplies on demand when requested, and on weekends or holidays when requested.

### 3.1.5 Storage and Handling of Medical Specimen Samples

**Requirement 1:** The Contractor must store medical specimen samples that cannot be delivered due to Level 3 snow emergencies and must deliver at 7:00 a.m. the next available day the BPHL opens after the snow emergency is cancelled.

**DDI Approach:**  
Our subcontractor maintains a **temperature-controlled storage facility** in the Columbus area for emergency specimen storage. During Level 3 snow emergencies:
- Specimens are stored in refrigerated units (2-8°C)
- Temperature monitoring continues during storage
- Specimens are delivered at 7:00 AM the next day BPHL opens
- DDI notifies BPHL of storage and expected delivery time

**Requirement 2:** The Contractor must never leave a shipment at the BPHL unattended. If BPHL personnel are not available to receive shipments, the Contractor must store shipments and redeliver at 7:00 a.m. the next day the BPHL opens.

**DDI Approach:**  
Drivers will **never leave specimens unattended**. If BPHL personnel are unavailable:
- Driver contacts DDI immediately
- DDI contacts BPHL to arrange alternative delivery time
- If no alternative available, specimens are returned to temperature-controlled storage
- Redelivery at 7:00 AM the next business day BPHL opens
- Incident documented and reported to BPHL

**Requirements 3-6:** Specific handling protocols for Rabies, Tuberculosis, Microbiology, and Mosquito specimens.

**DDI Approach:**  
Drivers are trained in specimen-specific handling protocols:

**Rabies Specimens (Animal Heads/Brain Tissue):**
- Refrigerated (not frozen) during transport
- Double-bagged in heavy plastic, sealed (no metal twist ties)
- Sharp protruding parts wrapped in newspaper
- Insulated container with frozen ice packs
- Aluminum foil barrier if dry ice used (prevent freezing)
- eLIMS requisition form included (sealed in waterproof bag)
- No Friday or pre-holiday shipping (held until next weekday)

**Tuberculosis (TB) Clinical Specimens:**
- Refrigerated on cold packs (preferred)
- Ambient temperature acceptable if overnight delivery
- Stability: Ambient 24 hours, refrigerated 1 week
- If held >24 hours, must refrigerate
- No Friday or pre-holiday shipping

**Microbiology Specimens:**
- Refrigerated temperature on cold packs (or frozen on dry ice if needed)
- Stability: Refrigerated 3 days, frozen indefinite
- No Friday or pre-holiday shipping

**Mosquito Specimens:**
- Stored frozen until shipped
- Placed in 5-mL polypropylene tubes (labeled with agency #, location #, date, trap type)
- Grouped by collection date in zippered plastic bags
- Insulated container with multiple frozen ice packs
- Field collection record included (sealed in plastic bag)
- Room temperature transit acceptable (avoid extreme heat)
- No Friday or pre-holiday shipping
