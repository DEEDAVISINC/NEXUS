## UNDERSTANDING THE REQUIREMENT

The ODH BPHL processes specimens and samples for disease diagnosis, environmental surveillance, and epidemiological support. These specimens come from local public health jurisdictions, clinics, hospitals, and other state agencies across Ohio.

**Critical Success Factors:**
1. **Time Sensitivity** — Many tests are time-critical. Late delivery = invalid results.
2. **Temperature Control** — Specimens must maintain proper temperature throughout transport.
3. **Chain of Custody** — Documentation must be complete and traceable for regulatory compliance.
4. **Reliability** — Public health depends on consistent, predictable service.
5. **Communication** — Real-time updates on pickup/delivery status.

DDI's approach addresses each of these factors through active contract management and a vetted subcontractor partnership.

## TEMPERATURE CONTROL & SPECIMEN INTEGRITY

**Our Process:**
1. **Pre-Transport:** Verify specimen packaging and temperature requirements before pickup
2. **During Transport:** Temperature-controlled vehicles maintain 2-8°C (refrigerated) or ambient as required
3. **Monitoring:** Temperature data loggers track conditions throughout transport
4. **Delivery:** Specimens delivered within specified time windows, documented on chain of custody
5. **Verification:** BPHL staff sign for receipt, confirming specimen condition

**Equipment:**
- Refrigerated vehicles for temperature-sensitive specimens
- Insulated coolers with ice packs for backup
- Temperature monitoring devices (digital thermometers, data loggers)
- Secure specimen transport containers (leak-proof, labeled)
