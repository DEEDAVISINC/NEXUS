## 4. SERVICE DELIVERY MODEL

### 4.1 DDI Role (Prime Contractor)

**DDI provides active contract management, NOT a pass-through arrangement:**

- **Contract Compliance:** Ensure all ODH requirements, timelines, and protocols are met
- **Quality Assurance:** Weekly performance reviews, monthly audits, quarterly reporting to ODH
- **Subcontractor Oversight:** Daily communication, performance tracking, corrective action when needed
- **Risk Management:** Backup plans for vehicle failure, driver absence, temperature excursions
- **ODH Communication:** Single point of contact for contracting officer, issue escalation, reporting
- **Financial Management:** Invoice processing, payment to subcontractor (Net 30), billing to ODH

**This is DDI's contract. We own the performance, the risk, and the relationship with ODH.**

### 4.2 Subcontractor Role (Field Operations)

**[Subcontractor Name]** is a Columbus, Ohio-based medical courier with [X] years of experience in temperature-sensitive specimen transport. They provide:

- **Daily courier operations:** Pickup and delivery per ODH schedule
- **Temperature-controlled vehicles:** [Number] vehicles equipped with monitoring systems
- **Trained drivers:** [Number] drivers with OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen certification and chain of custody training
- **Vehicle maintenance:** Regular inspections, temperature system calibration, backup equipment
- **Real-time communication:** Immediate notification to DDI of any issues or delays

**Subcontractor works FOR DDI. ODH works WITH DDI.**

### 4.3 Temperature Control & Monitoring

- All vehicles equipped with temperature-controlled compartments (refrigerated or insulated with active cooling)
- Real-time temperature monitoring with data logging (digital thermometers with alerts)
- Backup cooling systems (ice packs, dry ice, secondary refrigeration) for redundancy
- Temperature excursion protocols:
  - Immediate notification to DDI and ODH
  - Assessment of specimen integrity
  - Replacement pickup if needed
- Daily temperature log review by DDI quality assurance team
- Monthly temperature compliance report to ODH

### 4.4 Chain of Custody Protocols

- **Pickup:** Driver verifies specimen labels, counts, and condition. Photo documentation. Signature and timestamp.
- **Transport:** Specimens stored in locked, temperature-controlled compartments. No unauthorized access.
- **Delivery:** Driver verifies delivery to authorized BPHL personnel. Signature and timestamp. Photo documentation.
- **Documentation:** Chain of custody form for every delivery. Digital records maintained for 3 years.
- **Discrepancies:** Immediate notification to DDI and ODH. Investigation and corrective action within 24 hours.
- **Audit:** DDI conducts monthly chain of custody audit (random sample review).

### 4.5 Quality Assurance Plan

**Driver Training (Annual):**
- OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030)
- Chain of custody procedures and documentation
- Temperature monitoring and excursion response
- Specimen handling and safety protocols
- Customer service and communication
- Emergency procedures (spills, accidents, vehicle failure)

**Performance Metrics (Tracked by DDI):**
- On-time delivery rate (target: 99%+)
- Temperature compliance rate (target: 100%)
- Chain of custody accuracy (target: 100%)
- Customer satisfaction (ODH feedback)

**DDI Oversight:**
- **Daily:** Delivery confirmations, issue tracking
- **Weekly:** Performance review with subcontractor (on-time %, temperature compliance, issues)
- **Monthly:** Quality audit (random spot checks of chain of custody, temperature logs)
- **Quarterly:** Performance report to ODH (metrics, trends, corrective actions)
- **Immediate:** Corrective action for any deficiencies (retraining, process changes, equipment upgrades)

### 4.6 Risk Mitigation

**Vehicle Breakdown:**
- Backup vehicle on standby at subcontractor facility
- Immediate notification to DDI and ODH
- Alternative courier dispatched within 1 hour
- Specimens transferred to backup vehicle with temperature continuity

**Driver Absence:**
- Backup driver roster maintained (minimum 3 drivers)
- Cross-training for all routes and procedures
- Same-day replacement coverage guaranteed

**Temperature Excursion:**
- Immediate notification protocol (driver → subcontractor → DDI → ODH within 15 minutes)
- Backup cooling deployment (dry ice, ice packs, secondary refrigeration)
- Specimen integrity assessment by BPHL personnel
- Replacement pickup arranged if specimens compromised
- Root cause analysis and corrective action within 48 hours

**Weather/Road Closures:**
- Alternative route planning
- Early departure to account for delays
- Real-time communication with ODH if delays expected
