## COULD WE PARTNER WITH A MOVER?

Theoretically yes, but:
- The mover would need to be the prime (they have the license, staff, fleet)
- Our value-add is minimal — we'd just be a pass-through
- Margins would be thin on labor-based moving services
- Not worth the complexity for a small community college contract

---

## BOTTOM LINE

This is a service contract that requires being an actual moving company. We're a product resale and government contracting firm. Skip this and focus on equipment/product bids where our model works.
