## TYPES OF TRANSPORTATION CONTRACTS

### 1. Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ)
- Multi-year framework contract (base + option years)
- Agency issues task orders as freight needs arise
- **Most common and most valuable** — steady revenue stream
- Example: "Provide freight transportation services for DLA Distribution Center, 5-year term"

### 2. Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA)
- Pre-negotiated rates for ongoing freight
- Agency calls you when they have a load
- Smaller scale than IDIQ but easier to win

### 3. One-Time / Project-Based
- Equipment relocation
- Base closure/consolidation moves
- Disaster response logistics
- Construction material delivery

### 4. Household Goods (HHG) — Military Moves
- Military PCS (Permanent Change of Station) moves
- Managed through USTRANSCOM/SDDC
- **Huge volume** — 400,000+ military moves per year
- Requires specific certifications (TSP)

## SUB-BROKERING MODEL

**You will sub out the day-to-day brokering/dispatch work.**

### What the Sub-Broker Does:
- Receives load requests from the agency
- Matches loads with carriers from TruckingPlanetNetwork
- Negotiates carrier rates
- Manages pickup/delivery scheduling
- Tracks shipments
- Handles POD (proof of delivery) documentation
- Communicates with agency contacts

### What You Do:
- Hold the EDWOSB contract
- Invoice the government

- Quality oversight
- Contract compliance
- Relationship management with contracting officer
