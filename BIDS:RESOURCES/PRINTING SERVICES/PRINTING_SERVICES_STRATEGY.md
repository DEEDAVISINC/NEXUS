# PRINTING SERVICES — Bid Strategy

**Category:** Service Brokering (EDWOSB Prime + Print Shop Sub)
**Business Model:** Win printing contracts as EDWOSB prime, subcontract to local print shops
**NAICS Codes:** 323111 (Commercial Printing, except Screen and Books), 323113 (Commercial Screen Printing), 561439 (Other Business Service Centers)

---

## WHY THIS FITS

- **Every government agency prints** — forms, reports, mailers, ID cards, signage
- **You don't need a press** — local print shops do the work, you manage the contract
- **EDWOSB advantage** — print shops are rarely diversity-certified
- **Michigan has active programs** — City of Detroit Supply Schedule + State prequalification
- **Low risk** — print jobs are defined scope, predictable cost
- **Recurring revenue** — annual contracts with ongoing orders

## PROFIT MODEL

**Example: City of Detroit Annual Printing**
- Monthly orders: business cards, forms, flyers, reports
- Sub cost: $1,500/month = $18,000/year
- Contract price: $2,700/month = $32,400/year
- **Gross Profit: $14,400/year (44% margin)**

**Example: Federal Agency Print + Mail**
- Quarterly mailings of 5,000 pieces
- Sub cost: $0.75/piece x 20,000 = $15,000
- Contract price: $1.50/piece x 20,000 = $30,000
- **Gross Profit: $15,000/year (50% margin)**
