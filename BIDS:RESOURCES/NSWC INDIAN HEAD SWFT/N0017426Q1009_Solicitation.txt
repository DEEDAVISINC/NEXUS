SCOPE OF WORK:

The Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division (NSWC IHD) requires Secured Web Fingerprint Transmission (SWFT) fingerprint collection services for personnel requiring background investigations. The contractor shall provide fingerprint capture and electronic transmission services at existing contractor locations nationwide.

REQUIREMENTS:

1. The contractor shall be certified to use the Secured Web Fingerprint Transmission (SWFT) system as administered by the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA).

2. The contractor shall provide electronic fingerprint capture using Live Scan equipment at existing contractor locations throughout the United States.

3. All fingerprint transmissions shall comply with OPM (Office of Personnel Management) standards and SWFT requirements.

4. The contractor shall provide a web-based fingerprint collection system accessible to government points of contact for scheduling and tracking submissions.

5. The contractor shall support FD-258 fingerprint card processing and Electronic Fingerprint Transmissions (EFT) for all federal investigation types.

6. The contractor shall have the capacity to process up to 900 personnel per year, not to exceed 40 scans per month on average.

7. All fingerprint data shall be handled in accordance with applicable federal privacy laws and security requirements.

8. The contractor shall maintain active SAM.gov registration and hold an active CAGE code.

9. The contractor shall provide services at locations convenient to the personnel requiring fingerprinting, covering nationwide geographic areas.

10. Quality control: The contractor shall maintain a rejection rate below 2% for all electronic fingerprint submissions.
