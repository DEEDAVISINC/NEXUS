Company Overview

Dee Davis Inc (DDI) is an SBA-certified Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business (EDWOSB) headquartered in Troy, Michigan. We provide specialized professional and technical support services to federal agencies, with a focus on medical facility support, health and safety compliance, and regulatory services. Our Michigan base positions us for rapid, reliable support to Detroit-area VA medical facilities.

Service Approach — Radiation Safety
  • Qualified Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) staffing for VA medical facilities
  • NRC and state regulatory compliance management
  • Radiation protection program development, implementation, and oversight
  • Personnel dosimetry monitoring and ALARA program management
  • Radioactive materials license management and amendments
  • Equipment surveys, calibration oversight, and quality assurance
  • Staff radiation safety training and continuing education
  • Incident investigation, reporting, and corrective action
  • Waste management and disposal coordination
  • Audit preparation and regulatory inspection support

Why Choose Dee Davis Inc?

As a Detroit-area EDWOSB, DDI offers the VA both socioeconomic contracting value and local operational advantage. Our team includes board-certified health physics professionals experienced in medical facility radiation safety programs. We understand VHA radiation safety requirements and can provide seamless RSO support to the John D. Dingell VAMC with minimal transition time.
