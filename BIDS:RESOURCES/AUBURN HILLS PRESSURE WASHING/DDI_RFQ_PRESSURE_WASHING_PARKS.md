## 🎯 SCOPE OF WORK

### **GENERAL REQUIREMENTS:**

DEE DAVIS INC is seeking quotations from qualified pressure washing subcontractors to provide **HOT WATER PRESSURE WASHING SERVICES** for a municipal parks contract in Oakland County, Michigan.

Work will be performed on an as-needed basis throughout the season. The municipal client will determine quantities and timing based on field conditions. Subcontractor must perform services **within 1 week** of receiving request from DEE DAVIS INC.

### **ITEMS TO BE CLEANED:**

Subcontractor will use hot water pressure washing equipment to clean the following:
- Pavilions and gazebos
- Concrete pads and sidewalks
- Trash receptacles
- Picnic tables
- BBQ grills
- Playground equipment
- Bleachers
- Drinking fountains
- Signage and kiosks

**CRITICAL:** All cleaning must ensure items are clean without causing any damage to surfaces. Subcontractor will use additional cleaning chemicals on grease stains or other residue, ensuring such chemicals do not cause damage to surfaces.

## 🚛 EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENTS

### **REQUIRED EQUIPMENT:**

✅ **HOT WATER pressure washing equipment** (NOT cold water - this is mandatory)  
✅ Standard width pickup truck (e.g., RAM 1500 or similar)  
✅ Single axle towable hot water pressure washing trailer (or equivalent)  
✅ Extension hoses to reach remote service areas  

**Equipment Constraints:**
- Must NOT cause damage to pavement
- Must fit down service roads and sidewalks
- Must minimize damage to turfgrass
- Water supply will be provided on-site

**Example Equipment:** MITM AW-9000-0006 trailer or equivalent (reference only, not required)
