3.1 EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED EQUIPMENT:

Hot Water Pressure Washer:
• HOT WATER capability REQUIRED (minimum 200°F)
• Minimum 3000 PSI
• Minimum 3.5 GPM
• Diesel or gas-powered acceptable
• Trailer-mounted or truck-mounted acceptable
• Equipment must be in good working condition
• Backup equipment available in case of breakdown

Water Supply:
• Subcontractor responsible for water supply
• May use municipal water (at locations with access) or bring water
• Water storage tank if needed at locations without water access
• Proper connections and hoses

Additional Equipment:
• Surface cleaners (for large flat areas)
• Various pressure washing wands and nozzles
• Extension wands (for high areas)
• Personal protective equipment for all workers
• Safety barriers/caution signage

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.2 CLEANING METHODS

Approved Methods:
• Hot water pressure washing (primary method)
• Use of eco-friendly cleaning solutions (approved types only)
• Surface cleaners for large flat areas
• Low-pressure application for delicate surfaces
• Soft washing techniques where appropriate

Prohibited Methods:
• Cold water only (insufficient for required cleaning)
• Harsh chemicals that could damage surfaces or harm environment
• Excessive pressure that damages equipment or surfaces
• Methods that pose safety risks to public

Cleaning Solutions:
• Biodegradable, eco-friendly products required
• No harsh acids or caustic chemicals
• Products safe for use around children and playgrounds
• Products that do not harm vegetation
• MSDS sheets must be available for all products used

Surface-Specific Guidelines:
• Playground equipment: Low-pressure, safe cleaning solutions only
• Metal surfaces: Hot water with appropriate detergent, no damage 
  to paint/coatings
• Concrete: Standard pressure washing acceptable
• Wood surfaces: Lower pressure to prevent damage
• Rubber surfacing: Follow manufacturer guidelines

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.3 ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

The subcontractor must comply with all environmental regulations:

Water Management:
• Minimize water usage
• Prevent wastewater from entering storm drains (if required by 
  local regulations)
• Containment systems if needed
• Proper disposal of contaminated wash water

Chemical Use:
• Only approved, eco-friendly cleaning products
• Proper storage and handling of all chemicals
• MSDS sheets available on-site
• Spill containment and cleanup procedures in place

Site Protection:
• Protect landscaping and natural areas
• Cover storm drains if needed
• No damage to turf or plantings
• Restoration of any damaged areas

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.4 SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

The subcontractor must maintain safety standards at all times:

Worker Safety:
• All workers trained in pressure washing operations
• Personal protective equipment (PPE) worn at all times:
  - Safety glasses/goggles
  - Gloves
  - Non-slip footwear
  - Hearing protection (if needed)
• OSHA compliance for all operations

Public Safety:
• Work areas barricaded during operations
• Caution signs posted
• Public kept at safe distance during work
• No unattended equipment
• Immediate cleanup of any trip hazards

Equipment Safety:
• Equipment properly maintained and inspected
• Electrical safety (GFCIs if electric equipment used)
• Proper fuel storage and handling
• Fire extinguisher on-site
• Emergency procedures in place
