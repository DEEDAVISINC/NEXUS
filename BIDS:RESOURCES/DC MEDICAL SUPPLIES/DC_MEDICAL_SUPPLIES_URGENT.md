**For BPA (recurring revenue):**
- Don't price too low (need margin for long-term)
- Industry standard: 15-25% margin on medical supplies
- Consider volume discounts for larger orders
- Build in contingency for price increases

- Volume discount (over $5K): 25% off list
- Priority delivery: Standard rate
- Emergency delivery: Add 10%
