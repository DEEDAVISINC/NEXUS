## 🔍 WHAT IS DCSS?

**DCSS = DC Source Selection**
- DC Government's preferred vendor list
- Pre-qualified contractors for specific categories
- Similar to GSA Schedule but for DC Government
- Requires application and approval process
- Usually category-specific (medical, IT, construction, etc.)
