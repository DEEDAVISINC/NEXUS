## 🚀 HOW TO GET MBE/DBE/WBE CERTIFICATIONS:

### **Michigan MBE Certification:**
**Agency:** Michigan Minority Business Development Council (MMBDC)
**Website:** mmbdc.com
**Process:**
1. Submit application
2. Provide proof of woman ownership (51%+)
3. Provide financials
4. Site visit (sometimes)
5. Certification valid 1 year

**Cost:** $150-$350
**Time:** 30-60 days

---

### **Michigan DBE Certification:**
**Agency:** Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT)
**Website:** michigan.gov/mdot → Business Programs → DBE
**Process:**
1. Apply through UCP (Unified Certification Program)
2. Prove economic disadvantage
3. Prove woman ownership
4. Financials review
5. Certification valid 3 years

**Cost:** Free
**Time:** 60-90 days

---

### **Michigan WBE Certification:**
**Agency:** Various (WBENC, MWBE, local agencies)
**Process:** Similar to MBE
**Cost:** $150-$500
**Time:** 30-90 days
