## THE THREE LEAD TESTING MARKETS

### 1. BLOOD LEAD LEVEL (BLL) TESTING — Medical

**What it is:** Finger stick or venous blood draw to measure lead exposure in children and adults.

**Funding sources:**
- CDC Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention Program (CLPPP) — cooperative agreements to all 50 states
- State health departments
- Medicaid reimbursement
- Michigan universal testing law (effective April 30, 2025)

**2026 funding:** $100M+ federal + substantial state funding

**Contract flow:**
```
CDC / State Health Dept
    ↓ (contracts)
DDI (TPA — event coordination, outreach, scheduling)
    ↓
Certified Phlebotomists (DDI's network)
    ↓
CLIA-Certified Lab (Quest, Labcorp, CRL)
```

**Reimbursement rates (Michigan):**
- $221.74 per nurse home visit
- $110.00 per community health worker visit
- Per-test: $75-$150 in event settings

**DDI advantages:**
- Existing phlebotomist network (drug testing)
- TPA model already proven
- Wayne Metro CAA experience
- MDHHS Community Partner status
- Cause We Care nonprofit for outreach

**Certifications needed:**
- CLIA lab partnership (Quest, CRL, Labcorp already in network)
- State-specific requirements vary

---

### 2. LEAD-BASED PAINT (LBP) TESTING — Housing/Environmental

**What it is:** Inspection and risk assessment of pre-1978 housing for lead paint hazards. Includes XRF testing, dust wipe sampling, soil sampling.

**Funding sources:**
- HUD Lead Hazard Reduction Grant Program — $273M+ available (2026)
- HUD Lead Hazard Reduction Demonstration Program
- HUD Healthy Homes Supplements
- State and local housing agencies
- Private property owners (rental compliance)

**2026 funding:** $273M+ (federal) + state/local matching

**Homes to be treated:** 23,600 units funded in 2026

**Per-unit funding:**
- Lead Hazard Reduction: $14,000/unit
- Healthy Homes Supplement: $4,000/unit
- Radon Testing/Mitigation: $3,200/unit

**Contract flow:**
```
HUD
    ↓ (grants)
Cities / Counties / States / Tribes (grantees)
    ↓ (contracts)
DDI (coordinator / prime contractor)
    ↓ (subcontracts)
Certified Lead Inspectors / Risk Assessors / Abatement Contractors
```

**DDI advantages:**
- Wayne Metro CAA liaison experience (lead-based paint awareness)
- EPA Lead-Safe Firm application pending (FLPP)
- Cause We Care community outreach
- Contract management expertise

**Certifications needed:**
- EPA Lead-Safe Firm (RRP Rule) — APPLICATION PENDING
- State lead contractor/inspector certifications (sub out or obtain)
- EPA Lead Inspector/Risk Assessor certification (sub out or obtain)

---

### 3. LEAD IN WATER TESTING — Environmental/Infrastructure

**What it is:** Testing drinking water for lead contamination in schools, childcare facilities, and municipal water systems. Includes lead service line inventories and replacement.

**Funding sources:**
- EPA Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) — $3 BILLION (2026)
- EPA WIIN Grant Program (schools/childcare)
- Bipartisan Infrastructure Law funding
- State environmental agencies

**2026 funding:** **$3 BILLION** (largest of the three sectors)

**Scope:**
- 4 million lead service lines to be replaced nationally
- 49% of funds as grants (no repayment required)
- Every water system with lead lines = potential contract

**Contract flow:**
```
EPA DWSRF / WIIN Grants
    ↓
State Environmental/Health Agencies
    ↓
Water Systems / School Districts / Childcare Facilities
    ↓ (contracts)
DDI (outreach, sampling coordination, lab liaison)
    ↓
EPA-Certified Water Testing Laboratory
```

**DDI advantages:**
- TPA coordination model
- Community outreach capabilities
- School/childcare facility relationships (drug testing, fingerprinting)

**Certifications needed:**
- EPA-certified water testing lab partnership
- State-specific sampling certifications (varies)

## MICHIGAN OPPORTUNITY — IMMEDIATE

### Universal Blood Lead Testing Law (Effective April 30, 2025)

**Requirements:**
- ALL children tested at 12 months and 24 months
- Testing by age 72 months (6 years) if no previous test
- Additional testing ages 4-5 in 82 designated high-risk cities
- Testing if living in pre-1978 homes
- Testing within 3 months of identified risk factors

**82 High-Risk Cities/Townships include:**
- Detroit, Flint, Pontiac, Highland Park, Hamtramck
- Grand Rapids, Lansing, Saginaw, Kalamazoo, Battle Creek
- Plus 72 more designated areas

**MDHHS Contact:**
- Phone: 517-335-8885
- Email: MDHHS-CLPPP@michigan.gov

**Opportunity:** Local health departments experiencing capacity issues are seeking partners. DDI's TPA model + phlebotomist network + community outreach = perfect fit.

## PRICING MODEL

**Updated June 2026** — web-validated market research. Old internal mobile rates ($125–$200 all-in, phlebotomist cost $50–$90) were **below published market**. Nobody credible mobilizes for $50/draw; published collection-only floor is $70–$225+ (Michigan through national premium vendors).

**Market benchmarks (external sources, June 2026):**
- Precision Diagnostic Solutions (lead-specific mobile): $65–$100/test + $750 half-day / $1,400 full-day event
- Michigan mobile phlebotomy: $60–$115/visit (collection only; lab separate)
- National mobile premium: $100–$225+ per visit
- King County WA community BLL program: **$900,000 / 3 years** (public buyer scale)
- Michigan Medicaid 83655 lab assay only: **$10.03** — NOT mobile program pricing

## COMPETITIVE MOAT

**Why DDI wins in lead testing:**

1. **TPA model already proven** — Drug testing infrastructure transfers directly
2. **EDWOSB certification** — HUD and state contracts favor small/diverse businesses
3. **Michigan presence** — Universal testing law creates immediate local opportunity
4. **Community health experience** — Wayne Metro, Cause We Care, MDHHS partnership
5. **Existing lab relationships** — Quest, CRL, Labcorp just add lead services
6. **Contract management expertise** — Same model as all other DDI services

**The play:** DDI doesn't need to become a lead testing company. DDI coordinates lead testing the same way it coordinates drug testing — TPA model, qualified subcontractors, EDWOSB prime.
