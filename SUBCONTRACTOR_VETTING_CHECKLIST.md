# SUBCONTRACTOR VETTING CHECKLIST

**Copy this checklist into every bid folder that requires a subcontractor. Complete ALL sections before engaging.**

---

## BEFORE YOU CONTACT ANYONE

**Do your homework FIRST. Do NOT just start calling.**

- [ ] I fully understand the Scope of Work for this contract
- [ ] I've read the Quality Assurance plan (if applicable) and know what the sub needs to deliver
- [ ] I've researched this service type enough to speak intelligently about it
- [ ] I have my positioning ready — see below
- [ ] I've made a list of 3-5 prospective subs (not just grabbing the first one)

### Your Positioning (What You Say When You Call)

**DDI IS the contract. You are NOT "bidding on something and looking for help."**

**Say this:**
> "Hi, this is Dee Davis with Dee Davis Inc. We provide [drug testing / grounds maintenance / courier / transportation] services and we're expanding operations in [region]. I'm looking for a qualified local partner to support our work in the area. Do you have a few minutes?"

**When they ask how they fit:**
> "DDI manages the client relationships, compliance, and reporting. We're looking for a partner who handles the field operations — [testing / driving / maintenance / etc.]. We pay you directly, Net 30."

**NEVER say:**
- ~~"We're bidding on a government contract"~~
- ~~"There's an RFP for drug testing in Georgia"~~
- ~~"We need a sub for a federal solicitation"~~
- ~~"The contract is with [agency name]"~~

**To them, YOUR COMPANY is the contract. They work for DDI.**

## PILLAR 2: PAST PERFORMANCE & USASPENDING CHECK

### USASpending Verification (CRITICAL — Do This FIRST)
Look up the sub's legal name at usaspending.gov BEFORE contacting them.

- [ ] Searched USASpending.gov by legal company name
- [ ] **Do they win their own government contracts?** YES / NO

**If YES — STOP. DO NOT USE THIS SUB.**
A sub that already wins federal contracts doesn't need you as a prime. They will learn your client, your pricing, and bid directly. Find a sub that does commercial/private work and wants government exposure through YOU.

- [ ] Number of federal contracts found: _____
- [ ] Types of contracts they've won: _______________
