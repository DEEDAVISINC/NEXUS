## THE MODEL: Contract Management Firm (Prime/Sub) — Powered by Proprietary AI

This is DDI's model across ALL sectors and ALL services. No exceptions.

### How It Works:

1. **DDI identifies the opportunity** — NEXUS scans SAM.gov, state portals, 22+ vendor portals, RSS feeds — automatically, every 2 hours
2. **DDI wins the contract** — GPSS generates ProposalBio-amplified proposals, capability statements, and pricing intelligence in hours, not days
3. **DDI sources the fulfillment** — Automated subcontractor sourcing with USASpending verification, supplier RFQ management, 6-pillar vetting framework
4. **DDI manages everything** — ATLAS PM for project orchestration, COMPASS™ for quality assurance, VERTEX for financial tracking, PRISM for field service dispatch — all integrated, all automated
5. **DDI delivers to the client** — One point of contact, one invoice, one accountable firm backed by Fortune 500-level technology infrastructure

The client never deals with the sub. DDI is the face, the contract holder, and the manager. The technology ensures continuity at every stage.

## SBA LIMITATIONS ON SUBCONTRACTING (13 CFR 125.6)

**CRITICAL: These rules apply when DDI wins a contract through a small business set-aside (EDWOSB, WOSB, SBE, 8(a)).**

| Contract Type | DDI Must Perform (Own Employees) |
|--------------|--------------------------------|
| Service contracts | At least **50%** of personnel costs |
| Supply contracts | At least **50%** of manufacturing cost (or non-manufacturer rule) |
| General construction | At least **15%** with own employees |
| Specialty trade construction | At least **25%** with own employees |

### What Counts as DDI "Performing":
- Project management and oversight
- Contract administration and compliance
- Quality assurance and inspections
- Client communication and reporting
- Scheduling and coordination
- Invoice processing and payment management
- Risk management and problem resolution
- Proposal and bid preparation (pre-award)

### What Does NOT Count:
- Just receiving money and passing it to a sub
- Adding no management value to the contract
- Having no DDI personnel involved in execution

### How DDI Stays Compliant:
1. DDI always has a designated Project Manager on every contract
2. DDI performs all compliance, QA, invoicing, and client management
3. DDI maintains documentation of hours/costs for management activities
4. For larger contracts, DDI may hire W-2 employees for specific roles
5. Track and document DDI's personnel costs vs. sub costs on every contract

### If the Contract is NOT a Set-Aside:
Full and open competition contracts have NO subcontracting limits. DDI can structure the fulfillment however makes business sense.

## DDI AS SUBCONTRACTOR (Second Lane)

DDI doesn't only prime — DDI also pursues subcontract opportunities under larger primes.

**Why this matters:**
- Large primes NEED EDWOSB/WOSB/MBE subs to meet their Small Business Subcontracting Plans
- DDI gets contract revenue without the overhead of winning the prime
- DDI builds past performance that strengthens future prime contract bids
- Lower risk — the prime carries the primary contract liability
- Opens doors to contracts DDI couldn't prime alone (large IDIQ vehicles, full & open, etc.)

**DDI's value as a sub:**
- EDWOSB/WOSB/MBE/SBE certifications help primes meet their subcontracting goals
- DDI can sub for specific service lines (drug testing, fingerprinting, notary, transportation, etc.)
- DDI brings its own sub network — prime gets DDI + DDI's fulfillment partners
- DDI handles its scope independently with minimal prime oversight needed

**How to find sub opportunities:**
- SubNet (SBA subcontracting network)
- Prime contractor small business liaison officers
- SAM.gov subcontracting plan searches
- Direct outreach to large primes in DDI's service areas
- Teaming arrangement databases
- APEX Accelerator matchmaking events

**Pricing as a sub:**
- DDI charges the prime its standard agency rates (or negotiated sub rates)
- DDI still manages its own fulfillment partners under its scope
- The model within DDI's scope is the same — DDI manages, partners execute
