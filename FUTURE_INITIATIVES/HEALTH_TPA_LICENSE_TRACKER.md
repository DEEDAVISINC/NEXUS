# HEALTH INSURANCE TPA LICENSE TRACKER
**Target:** End of 2026
**Purpose:** Future expansion into health insurance claims administration

---

## WHAT THIS IS

A **Health Insurance TPA License** allows a company to administer self-funded health plans — processing medical claims, managing pharmacy benefits, coordinating provider networks.

**This is DIFFERENT from DDI's current TPA work** (drug testing, NEMT, HAVEN). This would be a new service line requiring significant infrastructure investment.

## RECOMMENDED APPROACH

### Option A: License Only (Low Investment)
- Get licensed in 2-3 states
- Pursue small contracts or subcontract work
- Build experience before major investment
- **Cost:** ~$10K

### Option B: Full Operation (High Investment)
- License + infrastructure + staff
- Compete for municipal/employer contracts
- Multi-year buildout
- **Cost:** $500K+

### Option C: Acquisition
- Acquire existing small TPA with licenses + systems
- Faster path to market
- **Cost:** Varies widely

## WHY CONSIDER THIS

**Pros:**
- Recurring revenue from claims administration fees (PEPM)
- Sticky contracts (multi-year, painful to switch)
- Leverages DDI's existing compliance/admin strengths
- Diversification beyond current service lines

**Cons:**
- Significant infrastructure investment
- Highly competitive market (established players)
- Regulatory complexity
- Different skill sets than current DDI operations
