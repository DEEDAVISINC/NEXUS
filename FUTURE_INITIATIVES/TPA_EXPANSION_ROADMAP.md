## DDI'S TPA MODEL

DDI is a **Contract Management TPA** — not a service provider. The model:
- **DDI = Prime** (contract holder, compliance owner, program manager)
- **Subs = Execution** (drivers, collectors, adjusters, providers)
- **Zero assets** — no buildings, no equipment, no clinical staff
- **Compliance-heavy** — DDI's core strength
- **Recurring revenue** — monthly PEPM or per-transaction fees

## STRATEGIC NOTES

1. **Same buyers, more services** — Most of these TPAs sell to the same buyers DDI already targets (government, healthcare, employers). Cross-sell opportunity.

2. **EDWOSB advantage** — Many government TPA contracts have diversity preferences. DDI's certifications are a competitive advantage.

3. **Compliance is the moat** — DDI's core strength is compliance management. Every TPA on this list is compliance-heavy.

4. **Zero-asset model scales** — Unlike providers who need facilities/equipment, DDI's TPA model scales with coordination, not capital.

5. **Recurring revenue compounds** — Monthly PEPM and per-transaction fees create predictable revenue streams.
