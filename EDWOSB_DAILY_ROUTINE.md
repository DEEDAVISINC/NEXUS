# EDWOSB OPPORTUNITY DAILY ROUTINE

**Time Required:** 30 minutes every morning  
**Best Time:** 8:00-8:30 AM (before bid hunting gets crowded)  
**Goal:** Never miss a good EDWOSB opportunity

---

## ☕ EVERY MORNING (30 MINUTES)

### **8:00-8:15 AM - SAM.GOV CHECK (15 min)**

**Step 1: Check Email Alerts (2 min)**
- Open SAM.gov daily alert email
- Scan subject lines for keywords:
  - "EDWOSB", "WOSB", "equipment", "supplies", "BPA"

**Step 2: Quick SAM.gov Search (10 min)**
- Go to sam.gov/content/opportunities
- Click your saved search: "EDWOSB $100K-4M"
- Filter: "Posted within last 24 hours"
- Scan results:
  - ✅ Click any that look promising
  - ✅ Download RFP if interesting
  - ✅ Add to tracking spreadsheet

**Step 3: Note Deadlines (3 min)**
- Any upcoming deadlines this week?
- Any proposals due soon?
- Update calendar/reminders

---

### **8:15-8:25 AM - MICHIGAN SIGMA (10 min)**

**Step 1: Login to SIGMA**
- sigma.michigan.gov
- Click "Solicitations"

**Step 2: Quick Search**
- Status: Open
- Posted: Last 7 days
- Keywords: "equipment" OR "supplies" OR "WMBE"

**Step 3: Scan Results**
- Any $100K+ contracts?
- Any WMBE diversity requirements?
- Add interesting ones to spreadsheet

---

### **8:25-8:30 AM - UPDATE TRACKING (5 min)**

**Review Your Pipeline:**
- Any deadlines approaching?
- Any decisions needed today?
- Any follow-ups required?

**Quick priorities:**
- What to work on today?
- Any quotes needed from suppliers?
- Any proposals to write?

## 💡 WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU FIND A GOOD ONE

**If opportunity looks REALLY good:**

**Immediately (5-10 min):**
1. Download full RFP
2. Create folder: `/EDWOSB OPPORTUNITIES/[Name]/`
3. Add to tracking spreadsheet
4. Star/flag for follow-up
5. Calendar reminder: 1 week before deadline

**Same day (30 min):**
1. Read executive summary
2. List all items needed
3. Quick Google search: Can I source these?
4. Rough cost estimate
5. Go/No-go decision

**Next day (if pursuing):**
1. Contact suppliers for quotes
2. Start proposal outline
3. Review evaluation criteria
4. Plan submission timeline
