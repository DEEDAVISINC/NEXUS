## DDI BUSINESS MODEL

**Dee Davis Inc. is a contract management firm — NOT a supplier or service provider.**

DDI wins contracts, sources qualified subcontractors/suppliers to perform the work or provide the goods, and manages the entire project: compliance, invoicing, quality assurance, reporting, coordination, and delivery.

**This means DDI can compete on virtually ANY contract category.** The question is never "can DDI do this work?" — it's "can DDI find a partner to fulfill this and manage the project?"

---

## HOW THIS WORKS
- "Est. Advertise Date" = when the RFP drops (NOT the deadline)
- DDI must respond to the RFP once it's posted
- **PREP STARTS 3 MONTHS BEFORE** advertise date (identify fulfillment partners)
- **RESPOND WITHIN 2-4 WEEKS** of RFP posting (typical Choice Partners window)
- Check https://www.choicepartners.org/current-rfps regularly for live RFPs
