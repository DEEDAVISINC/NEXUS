## 1. Program Proposal Summary

**Full document:** `SHIELD Program Proposal — Wayne County Lead-Safe Navigation`

- **Model:** DDI operates as Third-Party Administrator for all mandatory childhood BLL testing and follow-up navigation — starting Wayne County, expanding across SE Michigan
- **Structure:** Multi-year program. Year 1 in Wayne County, Year 2 adds Oakland, Year 3 adds Macomb + Genesee. No time-limited trial — families are served continuously from day one.
- **Staffing:** 3-5 CWC navigators, 1 supervisor, 1 program director (Dee Davis)
- **Cost to MDHHS:** No new budget allocation required. DDI bills MCOs, Medicaid FFS, and private insurers directly through existing reimbursement structures.
- **Year 1 volume (50% capture):** ~19,730 tests, ~809 elevated cases, ~5,919 NEMT trips
- **Technology:** SHIELD — live system with caseworker intake, navigator workspace, MDHHS partner portal, family status tracker, automated service verification
- **Performance accountability:** 7 KPIs tracked in real time through the MDHHS portal — continuous visibility, no manual reporting

## 3. Vendor Sourcing — What DDI Needs from MDHHS

These are the introductions and confirmations DDI needs to activate all 9 service lines in Wayne County. DDI will establish contracts and manage vendor relationships — MDHHS just makes the connection.

### High Priority — Cannot Operate Without These

| Need | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Certified lead abatement contractors** | Wayne County. DDI will sub-contract 1-2 contractors for remediation coordination. Are there state-funded programs (e.g., MiLeadSafe Home Lead Services) families can access directly? |
| **Nurse home visit agencies** | Wayne County. Licensed home health / visiting nurse agencies currently used by LHDs or CLPPP for developmental screening, BLL follow-up clinical assessment. DDI will sub-contract. |
| **Emergency housing partners** | Wayne County. Temporary housing for families displaced during lead remediation. Hotels, shelters, transitional housing partners used by LHDs. |
| **MSHDA regional representative** | SE Michigan. MSHDA programs for lead-affected families, HUD referral pathways. |

### Medium Priority — Accelerates Operations

| Need | Details |
|------|---------|
| **CLPPP referral pathway** | Confirm the process for CWC navigators to submit CLPPP referrals. Provide CLPPP point of contact in Wayne County for warm handoffs. |
| **Get Ahead of Lead enrollment** | Confirm CWC navigator access to the filter distribution enrollment system. Identify the supplier pipeline Aimee referenced during the 4/23 meeting. |
| **MCO contract leads** | DDI is credentialing with Molina, UHC, Meridian, HAP, Priority Health, McLaren, Aetna Better Health. MDHHS introductions to MCO NEMT and CHW contract leads would accelerate the process. |

### Not Requested from MDHHS

- Funding or grants
- IT infrastructure or staffing
- New legislation or policy changes
- Manual reporting obligations

## 4. Questions for MDHHS

These are billing and provider enrollment questions where DDI needs MDHHS input or confirmation.

### CHW Billing Structure

| Question | Context |
|----------|---------|
| Can DDI (Type 2 NPI) bill as the organization with CHW-certified CWC navigators as rendering providers? | DDI needs to confirm the billing structure before hiring and deploying navigators at scale. |
| What is the current Medicaid reimbursement rate for CHW services in Michigan? | DDI is verifying through CHAMPS but wants MDHHS confirmation. |
| Does DDI need to add a healthcare taxonomy code (e.g., 171400000X) to bill CHW services? | DDI's current CHAMPS taxonomy is 347E00000X (Transportation Broker). |

### Attending Provider NPI

| Question | Context |
|----------|---------|
| Does the referring physician's NPI travel with the CLPPP/LHD referral? | CHW claims require an attending provider NPI. DDI needs to know if this comes with the referral or if DDI needs to contract with an FQHC or physician as the ordering provider. |

### LHD Referral Pipeline

| Question | Context |
|----------|---------|
| How will CLPPP/LHD referrals be routed to DDI? | SHIELD has a caseworker intake portal (`/refer`). DDI can also accept referrals via secure email, fax, or direct Airtable integration. What works best for Wayne County LHDs? |
