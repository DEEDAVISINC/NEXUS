## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Occupational health services is a **$563 billion market by 2030**. DDI already serves employers with drug testing — the same clients need physicals, audiograms, vision tests, respirator fit testing, and medical surveillance. This is a natural extension of the existing TPA model.

**The Play:** DDI coordinates occupational health services the same way it coordinates drug testing — schedule appointments, track compliance, manage results, bill the client. Clinics and mobile units do the actual exams.

## SERVICE LINES

### 1. DOT PHYSICALS (CDL Medical Exams)
**Requirement:** All commercial motor vehicle (CMV) drivers must pass DOT physical every 2 years (or annually for certain conditions)

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| DOT Physical Exam | FMCSA-compliant medical exam | Schedule, track, report |
| Medical Examiner Certificate | 2-year certification | Expiration tracking |
| Drug Testing (combo) | Pre-employment + physical | Already doing |
| FMCSA National Registry | Certified examiner verification | Verify providers |

**Providers:** Certified Medical Examiners on FMCSA National Registry

---

### 2. PRE-EMPLOYMENT PHYSICALS
**Requirement:** Many employers require physical exam before hire

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-employment Physical | General health assessment | Schedule, results |
| Job-specific Physical | Matched to job demands | Coordinate with employer |
| Return-to-Work Physical | Clearance after injury/illness | Schedule, track |
| Fitness for Duty | Assess ability to perform job | Coordinate |

**Providers:** Occupational health clinics, urgent care, mobile units

---

### 3. AUDIOGRAMS (HEARING TESTS)
**Requirement:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95 — Employees exposed to 85 dB+ for 8 hours must have annual audiogram

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline Audiogram | Initial hearing test | Schedule, track |
| Annual Audiogram | Yearly monitoring | Expiration alerts |
| Standard Threshold Shift (STS) | Track hearing loss | Alert employer |
| Hearing Conservation Program | Full compliance program | Manage |

**Providers:** Audiologists, certified occupational hearing conservationists (COHC), mobile audiometry

---

### 4. VISION SCREENINGS
**Requirement:** DOT, OSHA, employer policies

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Vision Acuity Test | 20/40 or better requirement | Schedule, track |
| Color Vision Test | Required for some jobs | Coordinate |
| Depth Perception | Required for forklift, crane operators | Coordinate |
| Peripheral Vision | Safety-critical roles | Coordinate |

**Providers:** Optometrists, occupational health clinics, mobile units

---

### 5. PULMONARY FUNCTION TESTS (PFT / SPIROMETRY)
**Requirement:** OSHA requires for employees exposed to respiratory hazards

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline Spirometry | Initial lung function | Schedule, track |
| Periodic Spirometry | Annual or per OSHA schedule | Expiration alerts |
| Respirator Clearance | Medical clearance for respirator use | Coordinate |

**Providers:** Respiratory therapists, occupational health clinics

---

### 6. RESPIRATOR FIT TESTING
**Requirement:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134 — Annual fit test for respirator users

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Qualitative Fit Test | Pass/fail (saccharin, Bitrex) | Schedule, track |
| Quantitative Fit Test | Measured fit factor | Schedule, track |
| Annual Re-fit | Required yearly | Expiration alerts |

**Providers:** Certified fit testers, industrial hygienists

---

### 7. TB TESTING
**Requirement:** Healthcare workers, schools, certain government jobs

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| TB Skin Test (TST) | Mantoux tuberculin test | Schedule, read results |
| TB Blood Test (IGRA) | QuantiFERON, T-SPOT | Schedule, track |
| Annual TB Screening | Required for many healthcare jobs | Expiration alerts |

**Providers:** Clinics, nurses, pharmacies

---

### 8. TITERS & VACCINES
**Requirement:** Healthcare workers, government contractors

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Hepatitis B Titer | Immunity verification | Schedule, track |
| MMR Titer | Measles, mumps, rubella | Schedule, track |
| Varicella Titer | Chickenpox immunity | Schedule, track |
| Flu Vaccine | Annual requirement | Coordinate |
| COVID Vaccine | Per employer policy | Track |
| Tdap | Tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis | Track |

**Providers:** Pharmacies, clinics, mobile units

---

### 9. MEDICAL SURVEILLANCE
**Requirement:** OSHA standards for hazardous exposures (lead, asbestos, benzene, silica, etc.)

| Service | Description | DDI Role |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline Exam | Before hazard exposure | Schedule |
| Periodic Surveillance | Per OSHA schedule | Expiration alerts |
| Exit Exam | When leaving hazardous work | Schedule |
| Biological Monitoring | Blood/urine for exposure | Coordinate with lab |

**OSHA Standards Requiring Surveillance:**
- Lead (1910.1025)
- Asbestos (1910.1001)
- Benzene (1910.1028)
- Silica (1910.1053)
- Cadmium (1910.1027)
- Formaldehyde (1910.1048)
- Bloodborne Pathogens (1910.1030)
- Noise (1910.95)
- Respiratory Protection (1910.134)

**Providers:** Occupational medicine physicians, industrial hygienists

### Order Flow (Same as Drug Testing)

```
1. Client requests service (API, portal, phone)
2. PRISM creates order
3. AI matches to qualified provider (clinic or mobile)
4. Provider receives order, confirms appointment
5. Service performed
6. Results uploaded to PRISM
7. AI validates results (pass/fail, expiration dates)
8. Results delivered to client
9. VERTEX invoices
10. Compliance tracking (expiration alerts)
```
