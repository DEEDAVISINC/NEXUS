## WHAT IS IAC

**IAC = Indirect Air Carrier**
- TSA certification under 49 CFR § 1548
- Required to handle air cargo in the supply chain
- Positions DDI for pharma/medical air freight logistics
- Differentiator for MMCAP, Cardinal Health, and high-security delivery contracts
