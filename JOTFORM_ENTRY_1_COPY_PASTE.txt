VALUE-ADDED RESELLER (VAR) MODEL:

Dee Davis Inc. is a certified Value-Added Reseller. On every product-based government contract, DDI sources products directly from manufacturer-authorized distributors and wholesale suppliers at competitive pricing, then adds procurement management, compliance documentation, quality assurance, logistics coordination, and delivery management before fulfilling the contract to the end client. This is how DDI delivers products across every category — medical supplies, industrial equipment, IT hardware, office products, safety equipment, electrical components, plumbing supplies, vehicles, parts, and any other commodity. DDI does not manufacture products. DDI sources, procures, quality-checks, and delivers them with full contract compliance built in. The VAR model combined with our EDWOSB certification gives government agencies a diversity-certified, compliance-ready, single-source procurement solution. Agencies get the exact products they need from the right manufacturers, delivered on time, with all documentation handled — and they get to count the contract toward their small business and woman-owned contracting goals.
