## How an IDIQ Actually Works

1. **Agency issues IDIQ solicitation** — e.g., DHS needs IT modernization over 5 years, $500M ceiling
2. **8 companies win spots** — "Hunting license" — eligible for all task orders under vehicle
3. **Agency issues task orders** — "Cloud migration for Office X" — $2M; "Cyber assessment for Program Y" — $5M
4. **Only those 8 companies compete** — Short proposals, 7–14 day turnaround, award in 30–60 days
5. **Your entry point:** Be one of the 8 (Path A) OR team with one of the 8 as a sub (Path B)

## Why Most Task Order Proposals FAIL

1. **Copy-paste from full & open proposals** — Different format required
2. **Ignore evaluation criteria** — Miss what agencies actually score
3. **Write about THEMSELVES instead of the MISSION** — Focus on client needs
4. **Wrong pricing structure** — T&M vs FFP vs Cost-Plus
5. **Submit late** — Task orders move FAST (7–14 day windows)

---

## Anatomy of a Winning Task Order Proposal

| Section | What It Needs |
|---------|---------------|
| **Technical Approach** | Plan for THIS specific task. Methodology, deliverables, milestones. Specific to SOW. |
| **Staffing & Management** | WHO does the work. Qualifications, org chart, PM identified. Real people — not TBD. |
| **Past Performance** | Similar work. Same size, scope, complexity. Federal preferred. |
| **Price/Cost Volume** | Match contract type. T&M = loaded rates + hours. FFP = firm number + breakdown. |

## DoD Procurement Toolbox — Contract Opportunities Intelligence

**Source:** [DoD Procurement Toolbox](https://dodprocurementtoolbox.com/site-pages/contract-opportunities) + "Guidebook for Publicizing Notices in Contract Opportunities" (Version 3.0, April 2025)

### What COs See When They Post Opportunities

Understanding the CO's workflow gives DDI an edge — we know what fields they fill out, what options they toggle, and how notices flow from Sources Sought → Presolicitation → Solicitation → Award.

### Notice Types (How DoD Categorizes Postings)

| Notice Type | What It Means | DDI Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Special Notice** | Announces conferences, business fairs, long-range procurement estimates, draft solicitations | Monitor — early signal of future buys |
| **Sources Sought** | RFIs and market research — government wants to know who can do this | **RESPOND IMMEDIATELY** — cap statement + email (per presolicitation rule) |
| **Presolicitation** | "We're about to buy this" — advises suppliers of scope and invites info | **RESPOND IMMEDIATELY** — get on IVL, email CO |
| **Solicitation** | Formal RFP/RFQ — must be related to presolicitation notice | Full bid response per workflow |
| **Combined Synopsis/Solicitation** | Shortened timeline — combines synopsis + solicitation for commercial items | Fast turnaround required — prioritize |
| **Award Notice** | Contract was awarded — must relate back to solicitation | Track for debrief, recompete timing, and incumbent intelligence |
| **Justification** | J&A for Other Than Full & Open Competition | **CHALLENGE WITH EDWOSB** — sole source challenge opportunity |
| **Consolidate/Bundle** | Agency plans to bundle requirements ($8M+ for DoD) | Watch for small business impact — potential protest opportunity |

### Critical Data Elements COs Must Enter

| Field | Why It Matters to DDI |
|---|---|
| **Notice ID (PIID)** | This is the solicitation number — use it in all correspondence |
| **Federal Organization (DoDAAC)** | Identifies the buying office — research their past awards on USASpending |
| **Set-Aside Classification** | FAR 19.102 — determines if EDWOSB/WOSB/SB set-aside applies |
| **NAICS Code** | Must match DDI's registered NAICS — verify before responding |
| **PSC (Product Service Code)** | Narrows the service category — use for targeted SAM.gov searches |
| **Place of Performance** | Geographic match assessment — local vs. regional vs. national |
| **Interested Vendors List (IVL)** | **If enabled, ADD DDI TO THE IVL IMMEDIATELY** — CO sees who's interested |
| **Point of Contact** | Primary CO + optional secondary — these are who we email |

### Interested Vendors List (IVL) — Critical Intelligence

- COs decide whether to enable IVL on each posting
- COs decide whether to let vendors see OTHER vendors on the IVL
- **If IVL is enabled → DDI must add itself IMMEDIATELY**
- When "Allow Other Vendors to View" is YES → DDI can see competitors
- IVL visibility is especially useful when subcontracting is expected — potential teaming partners show up here
- **Only logged-in SAM.gov users can add to IVL** — DDI must stay logged in and active

### Notice Lifecycle (How Opportunities Flow)

```
Sources Sought → Presolicitation → Solicitation → Award
```

- Each notice RELATES to the previous one (same solicitation number)
- Information carries forward — data from Sources Sought prepopulates the Solicitation
- **DDI advantage:** Responding at Sources Sought stage means the CO already knows us when the Solicitation drops
- COs cannot change notice type after creation — they must create a new related notice

### Controlled vs. Public Attachments

| Type | Access | DDI Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone can view | Download and review immediately |
| **Controlled** | Requires Joint Certification Program (JCP) certification | **DDI should get JCP certified** — access to export-controlled technical data |

### JCP Certification (Future Action Item)

- Joint Certification Program via DLA: https://www.dla.mil/Logistics-Operations/Services/JCP/
- Gives access to controlled (CUI) attachments on SAM.gov postings
- Required for DoD contracts involving export-controlled technical data
- Uses DD Form 2345 — Data Custodian designation
- **Recommendation:** DDI should pursue JCP certification to access opportunities competitors can't see

### Operational Takeaways for NEXUS

1. **IVL is a weapon** — Every posting with IVL enabled = DDI adds itself. This puts DDI's name in front of the CO before the bid is even due.
2. **Notice relationships tell the story** — Track solicitation numbers across Sources Sought → Presolicitation → Solicitation → Award to follow the full lifecycle.
3. **JCP certification = competitive moat** — Most small businesses skip this. Getting certified gives DDI access to controlled attachments competitors can't see.
4. **COs must use the same solicitation number across related notices** — Use this to search SAM.gov history and find prior postings for recompetes.
5. **Combined Synopsis/Solicitations move fast** — These skip the presolicitation stage. NEXUS should flag these for rapid response.
6. **Justification notices = sole source challenge opportunities** — Every J&A posted is a chance to submit DDI's EDWOSB capability as an alternative.
