## 1. BRAND FRAMEWORK — THE FOUR PILLARS

All DDI/CWC programs now operate under a unified brand philosophy:

**Stability. Accessibility. Capability. Equitability.**

| Pillar | Definition | DDI/CWC Services |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Stability | The foundation is secure | Housing TPA, drug testing compliance, family navigation, recovery support |
| Accessibility | You can get to what you need | DEPOINTE NEMT, credentialed courier network, specimen courier |
| Capability | You have the tools to succeed | MI Bridges digital navigation, NEXUS systems, CWC navigators, occupational health |
| Equitability | You have the right to receive it | CWC community trust layer, Medicaid enrollment, benefits coordination, program advocacy |

CWC is the equitability layer. DDI is the stability, accessibility, and capability engine. This framework should appear on the website, in all program narrative headers, and in funder-facing materials.

## 4. MULTI-STATE LANDSCAPE RESEARCH

### Lead Screening (CLPPP)

- CDC funds 62 state and local CLPPP programs through cooperative agreement EH21-2102
- Current funding cycle runs through September 2026
- Every state has a CLPPP — the model (CDC cooperative agreement → state health dept → local health departments → community partners) is the same everywhere
- DDI/CWC's SHIELD model is replicable to any state with this structure

### Healthcare Access / HRSN (Section 1115 Waivers)

- 25 states have approved HRSN 1115 waivers as of February 2026
- Michigan received HRSN waiver approval in January 2025 — one of the last before Trump administration rescinded HRSN guidance in March 2025
- Existing approved waivers are preserved — new approvals unlikely under current administration
- This means the 25 states with existing approvals are the opportunity window

### North Carolina — Gold Standard Model

- Healthy Opportunities Pilots: $650M authorized, 29 evidence-based services delivered through community-based organizations
- Results: Reduced ER visits by 22 per 1,000 for 12+ month participants, saved $85/member/month
- Structure maps directly to DDI/CWC: NC "Network Leads" = CWC role, NC "Human Service Organizations" = CBOs needing TPA support = DDI role, NC "Prepaid Health Plans" = MCOs = DDI's existing MCO relationships
- Featured in JAMA, March 2025

### Top Multi-State Expansion Targets (Future)

| State | Lead (CLPPP) | HRSN Waiver | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| North Carolina | Yes | Yes ($650M) | Gold standard, proven model |
| New York | Yes ($60M lead registry) | Yes (SCN networks) | Social Care Networks building statewide |
| Ohio | Yes | Yes | Borders Michigan, could serve from Troy |
| Maryland | Yes (capacity building) | Yes | Montgomery County already identified as DDI NEMT target |
| Pennsylvania | Yes | Yes (Jan 2025) | Philadelphia/Pittsburgh lead burden |
| Illinois | Yes (Chicago separate) | No | Chicago lead crisis ongoing |

### Risk Flag

Trump administration rescinded HRSN guidance March 2025. Existing approved waivers preserved. New HRSN waiver approvals unlikely until policy shifts. Focus on the 25 states with existing approvals — especially Michigan.
