## WHAT OPOs NEED FROM COURIER VENDORS

**Service Requirements:**
- 24/7/365 availability
- STAT/emergency response (under 60 min)
- Temperature-controlled transport (organs, tissue, blood)
- Chain of custody documentation
- Airport logistics coordination
- Backup vehicle availability
- Real-time GPS tracking

**Credentials Required:**
- DOT/MC authority
- Commercial auto insurance ($1M+ liability)
- Cargo insurance (temperature-controlled)
- Workers compensation
- Background-checked drivers
- HIPAA compliance training

**Delivery Types:**
- Organ transport (heart, liver, kidney, etc.)
- Tissue recovery delivery
- Blood bank shipments
- Lab specimen runs
- Surgical equipment transfers
- Medical team transport

## KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS

### OPO Transportation Landscape

**Primary Vendor:** Most OPOs contract with **NORA (Nationwide Organ Recovery Transport Alliance)**
- NORA operates in 31 states
- Provides ground and air organ/tissue transport
- Phone: 855-554-6672 | Website: noratrans.com

**DDI Opportunities:**
1. **Backup Vendor Status** — Get on approved vendor lists for when NORA is unavailable
2. **NORA Subcontractor** — Become regional subcontractor for NORA in Michigan, Georgia, Florida, Texas
3. **Direct OPO Contracts** — OPOs with in-house transportation need backup/overflow vendors

### Vendor Registration Reality

**Finding:** OPOs do NOT publish vendor registration publicly. It's relationship-driven.

**How to Get On Approved Vendor List:**
1. Call OPO main line → Ask for "Operations Manager" or "Logistics Coordinator"
2. Email capability statement emphasizing backup/overflow services
3. Submit vendor application (each OPO has own process)
4. Provide: Insurance certificates, DOT/MC authority, NPI, background checks
5. Get approved for backup vendor calls
